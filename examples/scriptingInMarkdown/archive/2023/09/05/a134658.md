Just for fun the other day I asked ChatGPT for [150 words](https://scripting4.wordpress.com/2023/09/04/christopher-lydon-and-amanda-palmer/) on Christopher Lydon and Amanda Palmer, wondering if it would find the connection between them. It wrote a paragraph profile on each, but did not discover that Palmer had [written a song](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QM2IrTbWJxY) about Lydon. Note -- Google's search function totally made the [connection](https://www.google.com/search?q=amanda+palmer+christopher+lydon).
