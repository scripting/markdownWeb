I wanted to see if my [What Makes a Weblog a Weblog](http://blogs.law.harvard.edu/whatMakesAWeblogAWeblog) piece made it across, and it did. And even better, when I found a [link to it](http://blogs.law.harvard.edu/whatMakesAWeblogAWeblog) on my blog from [5/30/2003](http://scripting.com/2003/05/30.html) and clicked it, I held my breath, and it redirected correctly. This is one of those moments that gives you hope that the open web may still make it after all.
