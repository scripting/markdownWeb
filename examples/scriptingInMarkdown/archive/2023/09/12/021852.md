When I was leaving Cape Cod a couple of weeks ago, I stopped to charge my Tesla at a [place](https://www.google.com/maps/place/22+Meetinghouse+Ln,+Sagamore+Beach,+MA+02562/@41.781639,-70.5408017,230m/data=!3m1!1e3!4m6!3m5!1s0x89e4c9923ec94abb:0x335d7a8eef5da98b!8m2!3d41.781616!4d-70.540223!16s%2Fg%2F11csf2z60h?entry=ttu) that had a Dunkin' Donuts, so I went in and got a big ice coffee and a Sourdough Breakfast Sandwich, something I had never tried. For some reason it looked good to me.

![Dunkin' Donuts sourdough breakfast sandwich.](https://imgs.scripting.com/2023/09/12/dunkinDonutsSourdoughSandwich.png)

Well it was great! Since then I've had two more and I just love the sandwich. Seems like an incredible bargain. Two once-over eggs, a bunch of bacon and melted cheese in a sourdough sandwich. Really tasty and filling.

I don't often review food, so you know it's pretty good. :smile:
