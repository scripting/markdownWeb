![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2022/06/17/charlieTheTuna.png)A couple of weeks ago I found and fixed an error in [PagePark](http://pagepark.io/) that made [FeedLand](https://docs.feedland.com/gettingstarted.md) and [Drummer](http://drummer.land/) fail. The apps were offline until I could restart the server, and that sometimes meant hours of downtime. That no longer happens. But now there's a different problem, sometimes I go to [my news page](https://feedland.org/?river=true&screenname=davewiner) on feedland.org and for a minute or so there's no response. Eventually I give up and try again in a new tab. Same thing. Eventually the problem clears and the server returns to it's normal performance. I'm starting to think about how I might isolate this problem. My theory is that the problem is in the [appserver](https://github.com/scripting/appServer) layer, not in FeedLand itself, because when it happens nothing is returned. If it were at a higher level, the lockup would be in the code that gets a timeline or feedlist or whatever, so you would see a menu at the top of the screen. But it's always the news page this happens on, come to think of it. Maybe I should start a [thread](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/issues/270) on this in case other FeedLand users who are also developers (please) might have some ideas.
