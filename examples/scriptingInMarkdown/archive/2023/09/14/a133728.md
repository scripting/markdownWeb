My father liked to say: "When in trouble, when in doubt, run in circles scream and shout." It's originally from [The Caine Mutiny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Caine_Mutiny), he must've gotten it when he worked at IBM. He was always interested in new ideas, new ways to approach problems. But you never get a new idea when you're freaking about how you need a new idea now.
