The nice thing about ChatGPT is that when you want to know something like how to [hard-reload any popular browser](https://chat.openai.com/share/7555aac2-f312-403f-953a-9b812de37f80) for advice to a user, or know what [Writing-First workflow](https://chat.openai.com/share/fb172a67-2d89-41bf-8b3e-743cb738e620) is -- you get a complete answer without any of the junk writing you get from a commercial journalism site. And it seems not to forget (or bury) the one piece of information you came there for.
