Much of the story of [The Matrix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Matrix) is the question of whether [Neo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neo_\(The_Matrix\)) is [The One](https://chat.openai.com/share/2e24d0bf-f77f-4b17-9a14-1b4ca0ae2df2). Much of the [best dialog](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EUN1ClT9i9w) in the movie is about that. In the world I live in today we're looking for The One for social media, the open platform that can replace the silos. The feeling is there is now a chance to do that, it wasn't there before and it may not be there again.
