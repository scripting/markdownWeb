![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2023/10/15/yellowjacket.png)At lunch today we were joined by a few dozen [yellowjackets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowjacket) at an outdoor [noodle bar](https://www.yumyumnoodlebar.com/) in [Woodstock](https://www.google.com/maps/@42.041383,-74.1183356,3a,75y,107.09h,90.03t/data=!3m6!1e1!3m4!1sgxaBip7R1VVGYc0B_w3kzw!2e0!7i16384!8i8192?entry=ttu). They don't sting, but they sure are annoying. A waitress put out a [little dish of honey](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IQy3J-DgPmw) to draw them away from us and our food. Recalls a [piece](http://scripting.com/davenet/1996/08/28/BeeSeason.html) I wrote in the early blogging days (1996) about bees in my Woodside (California) [yard](https://www.google.com/maps/@37.418835,-122.2464066,3a,75y,84.33h,88.05t/data=!3m6!1e1!3m4!1sr4AgqxIWlZl-PwqYjkBfXw!2e0!7i16384!8i8192?entry=ttu).
