[Drummer](http://drummer.land/)'s native file format is [OPML](http://opml.org/) and OPML is the format for reading lists. This has led to some very nice synergies.

Example: A script you can add to your [Scripts menu](http://docserver.scripting.com/drummer/scripting.opml#1627490175000) that asks for the URL of a feed, and inserts a node into your outline.

var url = dialog.ask ("URL of feed")

var theFeed = rss.readFeed (url)

op.insert (theFeed.title)

op.attributes.addGroup ({type: 'rss', xmlUrl: url})
