![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/10/04/goldenGirl.png)People are really down on [paving cow paths](https://www.google.com/search?q=paving+cow+paths). But of course we think about whether it's the best way to do something, but the fact that users chose a way is important. Because our software is meant to empower them. I don't like software developers who think of themselves as above the users. Not a constructive way to do our art, imho.
