Every year around this time I start thinking about [Blogger Of The Year](https://www.google.com/search?q=site%3Ascripting.com+%22Blogger+Of+The+Year%22). And there's no doubt who it will be this year, but no spoilers. Also around this time each year we start thinking about which movies will be in this year's [NakedJen Film Festival](https://www.google.com/search?q=site%3Ascripting.com+njff), a tradition on Christmas Day every year when all the best pictures come out. Recent years the NJFF as its fans lovingly refer to it has been a virtual event, thanks to streaming, BitTorrent, COVID and the fact that Jen moved to [SLC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salt_Lake_City), though we have held them in realspace in NYC and SLC since then.
