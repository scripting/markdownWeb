Steve Algernon found this MORE ad from the 80s and sent it along.

I had been looking for it a few years ago, now I want to be sure next time, it'll be easy to find. Steve used to work at Living Videotext, [where](https://www.google.com/maps/place/117+Easy+St,+Mountain+View,+CA+94043/@37.3924404,-122.067555,122m/data=!3m1!1e3!4m6!3m5!1s0x808fb73b44beac6f:0x75582b6a9e573bd9!8m2!3d37.3923338!4d-122.0674987!16s%2Fg%2F11b8v422sp?entry=ttu) this product was made. He went on to Apple, where he did system software. He was the one who got XML-RPC and RSS into the Mac OS in the early web days. _Thanks Steve_ for all of it. :smile:
