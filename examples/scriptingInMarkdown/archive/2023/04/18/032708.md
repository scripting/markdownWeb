I asked Amazon Music to play some songs for me.

The first they chose was [Long Distance Love](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UaQ3VGeQ8Uw) by Little Feat.

What a perfect song. Driving beat. Lowell George poetry.

_No matter what I do._

_Even pray to heaven above._

_All I ever get from her is Long Distance Love._

Next [song](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/It%27s_Raining_\(Irma_Thomas_song\)) was a [hit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WJjDmpyWfBs) by [Irma Thomas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irma_Thomas) who we used to see at the [Maple Leaf](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Maple+Leaf+Bar/@29.9542913,-90.1416967,15z/data=!4m6!3m5!1s0x8620a53fb836c8ed:0x1982e3718f7b6dcd!8m2!3d29.9489368!4d-90.1320837!16zL20vMDlsaDN0) in New Orleans when I was a student there.

Have you ever heard her? The clearest voice.
