The Washington Post did some kind of [study](https://wapo.st/3AsTRnv) (free link, no paywall) to discover the sources behind "AI like ChatGPT" -- and they have a dialog you can use to find out how often certain sites are used in [Google's C4 dataset](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/Documenting-the-English-Colossal-Clean-Crawled-Dodge-Sap/40c3327a6ddb0603b6892344509c7f428ab43d81?itid=lk_inline_enhanced-template) (whatever that is). So of course I checked to see how much data from [scripting.com](http://scripting.com/) is included. The answer: 710K tokens, which is 0.0005% of all tokens. [Screen shot](http://scripting.com/images/2023/04/23/scriptingquotes.png). Some comps: [daringfireball.com](http://daringfireball.com/) had 610K, [seths.blog](http://seths.blog/) had 750K and [kottke.org](http://kottke.org/) had 590K.
