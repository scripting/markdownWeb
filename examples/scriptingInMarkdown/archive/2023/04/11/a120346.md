I got an [air fryer](http://scripting.com/publicfolder/chatgpt/2023/04/11/air-fryer-explained.html) and had a chance to try it on a [rib-eye steak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rib_eye_steak) yesterday, and omg it was sooo good. It took six minutes to cook, and then a few minutes to "rest" and it was as delicious as a steak off the grill, but without any of the work. It's easy to use, just plug it in and press some buttons. Not sure what I'll use the toaster oven for now.
