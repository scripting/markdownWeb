I often forget to be blown away by [how much house](https://www.zillow.com/homes/for_sale/70914849_zpid/) you can get for how little in the Hudson Valley. I like this [location](https://www.google.com/maps/place/16+Carol+Anns+Way,+Saugerties,+NY+12477/@42.0487971,-73.9631682,14z/data=!4m6!3m5!1s0x89dda74fd5e05e57:0xbb95c4cbecd5e8b1!8m2!3d42.0487971!4d-73.9451438!16s%2Fg%2F11c4t4b963), real close to the [river](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_River), and [Saugerties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saugerties,_New_York) is a great town, small, quiet, but has everything you need right there. And not as sprawling as [Kingston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingston,_New_York). Still old, and charming.
