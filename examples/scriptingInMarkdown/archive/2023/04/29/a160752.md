![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2023/04/29/raisins.png)Succession spoiler, don't read. Before the finale, there's going to be a presidential election. Current [ATN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox_News) management is backing the [Nazi](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZsHYod9tdsY). But it looks like Shiv and probably Tom are going to run ATN, and we know Shiv wants [someone else](http://scripting.com/publicfolder/chatgpt/2023/04/29/moderate-candidate-not-specified.html). And also don't forget, the oft-overlooked Roy, [Connor](https://succession.fandom.com/wiki/Connor_Roy), is running for president. It might not matter who's Logan's successor, the show might be about who is the [The Raisin's](https://www.reddit.com/r/SuccessionTV/comments/si0qg8/who_is_the_raisin_based_upon/) successor. It's hard to imagine the Swede cares, but we know for a fact that Shiv does. And in this, she's not like a Roy kid, she's a _[serious](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LZTaXjt2Ggk)_ person, who's been kept on the sideline too long because of her gender. A Roy might end up in the White House, a new [raisin](https://www.distractify.com/p/who-is-the-raisin-in-succession), and whoever it is will be in Shiv's pocket? _That_ would be a fine ending.
