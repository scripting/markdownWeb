![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2022/03/21/beetle.png)I've gotten a couple of responses from people who say they don't get what [Two-Way-RSS](https://www.google.com/search?q=%22two-way+RSS%22+site%3Ascripting.com) is. Click the link to see all the times I've used the term on the blog. Maybe that'll help. In case you still don't get it, it's a writing system that not only publishes RSS, but accepts it as input. So I can write using my own editing tools, and publish through yours. I want to publish anywhere people want to read my stuff, and I refuse to copy/paste my writing into edit boxes. The first system I really wanted this for was Substack, although I would have liked Facebook or Medium to have it a long time ago. Yes I know there are ways to pipe RSS into systems, I've written plenty of them. But there's no substitute for a content system supporting it as a feature.
