Basic questions to ask of any would-be successor to Twitter.

*   Is there a character limit in Blue Sky?
    
*   Does it support simple styling: bold and italic.
    
*   [Links](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperlink#How_hyperlinks_work_in_HTML)?
    
*   Titles?
    
*   Can you edit posts?
    
*   Does it support enclosures, for podcasting?
    
*   How about two-way RSS, nice to have. Hook up to simple networks.
    

My criticisms of Blue Sky, having never tried it.

*   It's invite-only. Really? On an open protocol?
    
*   It's founded by Jack Dorsey, a nice enough fellow, who should sit down after the huge mess he left us with Twitter.
