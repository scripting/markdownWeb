![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2023/04/03/cupid.png)If I were going to write a _Rules for Love Makers_ document, like the [one](http://this.how/standards/) I did for Standards Makers, I'd make the first rule: If you're going to be in a love relationship with this person, and you can't visualize them as your best-best friend, keep looking. It has to be someone you can go to with any problem, no matter how personal, involving them or someone or something else, and be totally confident they'll help.
