BTW, I read in a news article the other day that I developed iPodder, the first podcasting client in 2004. This is not true. The first podcasting client was [Radio UserLand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_UserLand) in 2001. I did not write [iPodder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juice_\(aggregator\)), it was a community effort.
