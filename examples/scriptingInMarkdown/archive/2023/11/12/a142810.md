![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2023/04/24/cheshireCat.png)Some news. I've been working with [Automattic](https://automattic.com/) for most of this year on a new version of [FeedLand](https://docs.feedland.com/gettingstarted.md) that runs in their cloud. The advantage is it will scale like the most popular websites, as [WordPress](https://wordpress.com/) does. There have been lots of internal changes in the software, but at the same time, it still runs on a $10 a month [Digital Ocean server](https://feedland.org/), and on desktops as well. The next FeedLand works at all these levels, for a person, a workgroup and the world. Obviously, lots more to say about all this. :smile:
