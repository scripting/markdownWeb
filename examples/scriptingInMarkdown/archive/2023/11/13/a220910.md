You know I don't always like the same soda. Sometimes it's [Diet Coke](https://www.coca-cola.com/us/en/brands/diet-coke), and other times, [Diet Dr Pepper](https://www.drpepper.com/s/products/diet-dr-pepper). But for the last year or so the drink of choice around these parts is [Fresca](https://www.coca-cola.com/us/en/brands/fresca-sparking-soda). But you know what's funny. No matter what my favored drink is, it's always the one you can't get at the local [supermarket](https://stores.hannaford.com/ny/west-hurley/8444). Always sold out. Very weird.
