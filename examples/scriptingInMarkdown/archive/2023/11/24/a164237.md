Maybe the most American holiday of all. But it's also a time to be jolly and kind, nice not naughty, doing our part to consume at ever-increasing rates to keep the US economy humming. And it's a time for love, one hopes. Even so it's time to put [Santa](https://chat.openai.com/share/325a58fa-1957-47f3-833f-f34b4151721b) in his place, in the [margin](https://imgs.scripting.com/2023/11/24/santaInTheMargin.png) of Scripting News! I did my part today, buying a nice curved monitor for my Mac mini, and a new smaller TV for the den. Socks and a couple of nice shirts.
