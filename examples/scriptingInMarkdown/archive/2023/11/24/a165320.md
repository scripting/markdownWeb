I'm working on a new version of the Scripting News home page, the first since 2017. There will be more tabs, that run down the left margin instead of the top. It'll have the [content](http://scripting.com/homepage.html) of Scripting News in the first tab, as it works now. And the [linkblog](http://feeder.scripting.com/returnlinkbloghtml?url=http://data.feedland.org/feeds/davewiner.xml) will be the next tab. Then the tabs from [news.scripting.com](http://news.scripting.com/) followed by the About page [outline](http://scripting.com/publicfolder/scripting/aboutpage.opml). And lots of room for growth, for new ideas for writers and readers. As always I use my own site to experiment with new web ideas.
