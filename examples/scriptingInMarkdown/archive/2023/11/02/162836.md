I'm helping my friends at Automattic make [Doc's blog](https://doc.searls.com/) easier to read, esp on tablets which is where I end up reading most of his writing.

I also never liked the [picture](https://imgs.scripting.com/2023/11/02/docblogpic.png) someone put on Doc's blog. I guess if you squint it kind of looks like Doc, but that isn't how I see him.

[This picture](https://www.flickr.com/photos/scriptingnews/292425043), which I took at a nice [breakfast place](https://www.google.com/maps/@37.8909372,-122.2875094,3a,75y,313.36h,69.63t/data=!3m6!1e1!3m4!1suaMTkzX9fdF13_oSiid1mw!2e0!7i16384!8i8192?entry=ttu) in Berkeley on [Nov 8, 2006](http://scripting.com/2006/11/08.html), is much more what I think of when I think of Doc.

So is it fair to use a picture that's 17 years old? Sure! Most of the stuff on his blog was written then or before.
