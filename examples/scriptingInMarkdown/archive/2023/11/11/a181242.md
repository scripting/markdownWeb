Nobody thinks of social media apps like Bluesky, Mastodon, Threads, etc as part of a content management system. But if they are somewhat [textcasting](http://textcasting.org/) oriented, and have feeds that are properly configured, then yes -- you can have people write for publication using such a well-equipped social network.
