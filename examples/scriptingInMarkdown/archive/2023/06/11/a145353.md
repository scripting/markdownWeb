I was [asked](https://mastodon.social/@shoq/110516792922874801) on Mastodon what role [Aaron Swartz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aaron_Swartz) played in developing RSS, since he is often credited with co-developing it. This is [what I wrote](https://mastodon.social/@davew/110520163594030698). "There was a lot of confusion about what RSS was until RSS 2.0 came out and the NYT adopted it. There was a thread based on RDF, and Aaron was credited as a contributor to that. I would ask those people what he did. I never worked with him. I saw some of his posts on their mail list and met him a few times at conferences. He was a precocious young person, obviously smart, but he didn't seem interested in what I was working on. That's about it."
