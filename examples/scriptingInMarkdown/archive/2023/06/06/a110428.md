When I was little, maybe first or second grade, my [grandfather](http://scripting.com/images/2023/06/06/grandpaAndLittleDave.jpeg) bought me a handheld [transistor radio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transistor_radio). It was the 1960s. I listened to [WABC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruce_Morrow#Radio_work), the Beatles and all the great new rock and roll. My grandpa said I’d see so many amazing things in my life. To him the radio was a futuristic miracle, like AI today. Anyway I just asked [Alexa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_Alexa) to play [You Can’t Hurry Love](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fQ7uXX9K7Sk) by the Supremes, one of the songs I listened to on the radio he gave me 60 years ago. I realized just now he was so right, I have indeed seen many amazing things in my life.
