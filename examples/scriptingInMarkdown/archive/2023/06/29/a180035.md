Another great ChatGPT use. I have a mental block with [regular expressions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_expression). I know when to use them, but I just can't construct them. I used to be able to do it, a long time ago. Anyway, now I describe the problem to ChatGPT and it [gives me back](https://chat.openai.com/share/57928125-b33a-4961-b860-482d35459fe7) the expression. I don't even have to formulate the question very carefully, it figures it out. Now I can relax about it.
