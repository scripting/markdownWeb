![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2023/06/22/swagger.png)I'm was looking for a new show to binge the other day when I saw a [tweet](https://twitter.com/arrington/status/1670835821857980418) from an old Silicon Valley friend [Mike Arrington](https://www.google.com/search?q=site%3Ascripting.com+arrington), who now is a [crypto VC](https://techcrunch.com/2021/07/21/michael-arringtons-next-act/) in Miami, recommending an [AppleTV](https://tv.apple.com/us/show/swagger/umc.cmc.63e208601mwndrxpmguc5stbo) show, [Swagger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swagger_\(TV_series\)). That was good enough for me to give it a try, that and it got [excellent ratings](https://www.metacritic.com/tv/swagger) via Metacritic. I'm now most of the way through the first season, it's really good, kind of a cross between [Friday Night Lights](https://www.metacritic.com/tv/friday-night-lights) and [The Wire](https://www.metacritic.com/tv/the-wire), with a bit of [Atlanta](https://www.metacritic.com/tv/atlanta). Takes place in Baltimore, the story centers on a 14-year-old star-to-be and his life in the same city as The Wire, during the early days of Covid, based on the life story of NBA star [Kevin Durant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Durant). An urban version of Friday Night Lights, which I had been working my way through, loving the first two seasons but the show got pretty bad by the third imho. Season 1 of Swagger is very good. I'm on episode 7. Season 2 starts tomorrow.
