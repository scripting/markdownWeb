Newsletter apps have had their moment, now WordPress has one, and wonderfully it has inadvertently plied open the [lock-in](http://scripting.com/2022/12/30/173742.html?title=substackLockin) that Substack imposes, by making everyone use their editor. WordPress has a time-tested API, so I could theoretically write my newsletters in [Drummer](http://drummer.land/) and push them up to WordPress which would then send them out.
