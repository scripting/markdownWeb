I introduced a problem in [FeedLand](https://docs.feedland.com/gettingstarted.md) when [keyboard navigation](http://data.feedland.org/changenotes/2022/12/28/162013.html) in the Feed List feature was added. Basically, if you had a [Feed List](http://feedland.org/?username=davewiner) displayed, an "ask dialog" wouldn't receive your keystrokes, or it would appear to freeze. This dialog appears whenever FL asks for a string, for example to enter a string to search for, or to enter the URL of a feed to subscribe to. I believe I found the problem and fixed it. It's [explained here](https://github.com/scripting/feedlandSupport/issues/163).
