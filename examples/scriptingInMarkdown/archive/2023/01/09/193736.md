When you Like something in FeedLand it is added to two feeds:

*   A Like feed for all FeedLand users.
    
*   A Like feed for the person who did the Like.
    

This is [where](http://data.feedland.org/likes/davewiner.xml) my personal Likes feed is.

And the feed for all [users of my FeedLand](http://data.feedland.org/likes/all.xml).
