I really like [Winning Time: The Rise of the Lakers Dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winning_Time:_The_Rise_of_the_Lakers_Dynasty).

Almost finished with the ten episodes of Season 1 on [HBO Max](https://www.hbo.com/winning-time-the-rise-of-the-lakers-dynasty).

I like it for a bunch of reasons.

1.  I like [John C. Reilly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_C._Reilly) who plays [Jerry Buss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerry_Buss) the new owner of the Lakers when the season starts. He also btw is really good at audiobooks. I'd listen to any book he [narrates](https://www.audible.com/search?searchNarrator=John+C.+Reilly). He's Mr Affability. And perfectly cast for the role of Buss.
    
2.  I like the style, it's a little weird at first, but it's clever, and they break the [fourth wall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourth_wall) a few times, to good effect.
    
3.  I like that the style reminds me of one of my favorite movies, [Any Given Sunday](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9rFx6OFooCs), also a sports movie.
    
4.  I like sports movies/shows. And I especially like basketball. It's my favorite sport.
    
5.  I like the story how [Pat Reilly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pat_Riley) became the coach of the Lakers and led them to dynasty.
    
6.  I like how Kareem became friends with Magic.
    
7.  I like that the guy who played [Spencer Haywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spencer_Haywood) is the same actor who played [Avon Barksdale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avon_Barksdale) in [The Wire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wire).
    
8.  I like that [Sally Field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sally_Field) plays Jerry Buss's mother! She's awesome as always.
    
9.  I like all the actors and roles. That's unusual. With such a huge cast you'd think there would be one impossible to watch character, but no, they're all interesting.
    
10.  I like that they will have lots of potential seasons, next up [Phil Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phil_Jackson) and how he became the next dynastic coach of the Lakers. And don't forget [Kobe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kobe_Bryant) and [Shaq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaquille_O%27Neal) who probably won't show up until Season 3 or 4.
     

I gave it the highest rating [on Bingeworthy](http://bingeworthy.io/tv/?id=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.metacritic.com%2Ftv%2Fwinning-time-the-rise-of-the-lakers-dynasty). It didn't get great reviews. When I finish it, I'll read some of them to see what I missed. ;-)
