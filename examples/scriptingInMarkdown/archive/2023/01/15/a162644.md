One of the pioneers of an [idea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zettelkasten) called [zettelkasten](https://www.google.com/search?q=zettelkasten+arno+schmidt) was [Arno Schmidt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arno_Schmidt), who also happens to be [my great uncle](https://www.google.com/search?q=site%3Ascripting.com+%22arno+schmidt%22). Quite independently, just before he died, I started working on [software](http://scripting.com/dwiner/outlinersProgramming.html) that makes it work without the index cards, on a personal computer.
