![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2023/01/08/fender.png)I bought a [2015 Mac laptop](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B082XG7VBP/ref=ppx_od_dt_b_asin_title_s00?ie=UTF8&th=1), used, for $600 via Amazon. I need it because I use [Frontier](http://scripting.com/2021/01/04/140651.html?title=whatFrontierIsAbout) as an integral part of my development process, all my build scripts run in Frontier, and while they could be converted to JavaScript, they'd be more complicated and it's a lot of work, and it's worth spending $600 to save all that work. Someday I want to do it because I'd like to use Drummer and its built-in scripting language for the same purpose. I learned a lot about why people choose older Macs. It reminds me of [pre-CBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fender_\(company\)#Sale_to_CBS) Fender guitars, which still sell at a [premium](https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw=Pre-cbs%20Fender%20Guitars&norover=1&mkevt=1&mkrid=711-153677-346401-4&mkcid=2&mkscid=102&keyword=&crlp=632052376936_&MT_ID=&geo_id=&rlsatarget=dsa-1456167871416&adpos=&device=c&mktype=&loc=1023511&poi=&abcId=&cmpgn=18786426719&sitelnk=&adgroupid=144636292002&network=g&matchtype=&gclid=CjwKCAiA8OmdBhAgEiwAShr403s3aqhTbcuAgTH_yfHqEEFmps2tzdZFrHidJRllPpY8VuLIH0mcYRoCf2IQAvD_BwE), even though Fender sold to CBS in 1965. Maybe Macs peaked? Anyway -- I should get the "new" computer next week, and I'll let you know how it goes. BTW, my main desktop, the one I'm writing this post on, is a [2017 iMac Pro](http://scripting.com/images/2023/01/08/macAboutScreen.png). I love the machine. I hope it works forever.
