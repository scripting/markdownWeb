![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2021/12/24/dontLookUp.png)If you want to watch me update my blog outline you can do it [here](http://scripting.com/code/testing/concordreader/?url=http://drummer.scripting.com/davewiner/blog.opml). It seems to update in real time. I must have written this thing, but I don't remember doing so. Looking at the JavaScript console, it's quite sexy, hooked into the websocket protocol supported by [Drummer](http://drummer.land/), which is of course where I am editing this mofo. When I let up on the keyboard for a second or two it sends an update. It's not updating on every keystroke; that would be wasteful. It's fun to watch while I type in one window and watch the update in the other one.
