![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/11/13/rssCoffeeMug.png)[RSS](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html) is a thing like [roadways](http://scripting.com/liveblog/users/davewiner/2015/12/06/0582.html) and paths of [rivers](https://www.nps.gov/vick/learn/nature/river-course-changes.htm), they change very slowly. Think about [qwerty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/QWERTY) keyboards. That's what we're talking about here. Agreements between products to interop. RSS is just like the [gauge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Track_gauge) of rails, or always driving on one [side](https://www.rd.com/article/why-drive-on-different-sides-of-the-road/) of the street. A convention that makes progress possible.
