I posted a link to a [Salvador Dali work of art](http://archive.thedali.org/mwebcgi/mweb.exe?request=record;id=1780;type=101) that Twitter thought might be bad so they blocked it.

Someday Google will do the same for a blog post like that because they own the web the same way Twitter owns tweets.

Then we will see that the question in 2023 wasn't whether HTTPS is good or not, rather it's whether it's good if [Google owns the web](http://this.how/googleAndHttp/).
