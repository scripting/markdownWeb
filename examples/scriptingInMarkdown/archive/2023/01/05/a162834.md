If you've been reading this blog since the beginning you know about [Fractional Horsepower](https://www.google.com/search?q=site%3Ascripting.com+%22fractional+horsepower%22) servers. It's an important idea. That software you think of as running on huge servers in the cloud also can be deployed on much smaller systems, including behind a firewall on a personal computer. [Radio UserLand](http://scripting.com/images/2021/08/15/radioUserLandScreenShot.png) was a server that ran on the desktop, yet very few users knew that. It just ran in the browser, there was a little cloud component to act as a proxy, to do the kinds of things you can't do from a desktop.
