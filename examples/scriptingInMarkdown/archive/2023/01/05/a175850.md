I wasn't very entertained with [The Menu](https://www.metacritic.com/movie/the-menu) on HBO but now I find myself thinking about [it](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Menu_\(2022_film\)) a lot, not deliberately. Weird. So I watched it again, and on the second viewing it came together. I've had a similar experience with [There Will Be Blood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/There_Will_Be_Blood). I didn't like it on the first viewing, but watched it again, and it became one of my favorite movies.
