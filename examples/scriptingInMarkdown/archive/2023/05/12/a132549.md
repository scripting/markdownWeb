![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2021/09/05/goneWithTheWind.png)I think I'm going to start a social network called [Pissing In The Wind](https://www.hover.com/domains/results?utf8=%E2%9C%93&q=pissinginthewind). The sub-title would be "As If Anyone Cares." Because that's what we do. We make statements. We ask questions no one is listening to. We serve as [substrate](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/substrate) for people to post spam. I know I'm exaggerating. I just posted a [thread](https://staging.bsky.app/profile/did:plc:oety7qbfx7x6exn2ytrwikmr/post/3jvju7twmk22d) on [Blueski](https://www.hover.com/domains/results?q=blueski), and by the end I remembered no one would get this far. The top message got two Likes. It did make me feel good to write it, for a moment, but it was quickly forgotten.
