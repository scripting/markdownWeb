![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2021/09/17/simpsonsBeeGuy.png)There's only one public voice in the US, and that's journalism. And since journalism doesn't cover itself, we never look at their motives when discussing the news.

If we did, we'd have no trouble [parsing](https://www.theguardian.com/media/2023/may/12/trump-town-hall-cnn-staff-rages) the CNN "town hall" presentation of candidate Donald Trump. Paraphrasing what [Les Moonves](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/news/general-news/leslie-moonves-donald-trump-may-871464/), former CEO of CBS said about Trump in 2016. "\[Trump\] may not be good for America, but \[he's\] damn good for CNN."

You can raise the price of ads with Trump. That's obviously what CNN hopes to do. Trump, who has been deprecated by Fox, needs a mainstream outlet. There's no mystery why all the people in the town hall audience were Trump supporters, that obviously was a Trump condition. He probably chose the reporter, the questions, and came prepared to get talked about, which appears to be happening.

I didn't see it, I was watching [basketball](http://scripting.com/2023/05/10.html#a122516) when he was on. I tried listening to MSNBC last night talk about this and was nauseated. They're treating it as a news event.

That's pretty much all there is that's newsworthy about the event imho.
