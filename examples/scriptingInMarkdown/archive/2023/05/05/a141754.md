![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2023/05/05/heavenCanWaitSmall.png)Elon Musk and Jack Dorsey are perfect illustrations why there's no point in accumulating vast wealth. There's absolutely nothing a person can do with the money. But it _is_ fun to destroy things, in games like [Angry Birds](http://scripting.com/stories/2010/06/18/whyAngryBirdsIsSoMuchFun.html). In the real world, actual people get hurt. No matter how heartless you pretend to be, all that pain accumulates, you end up feeling it. Maybe that'll be the moral of [Succession](http://scripting.com/2018/08/06/124120.html). The plot becomes something like [Heaven Can Wait](http://scripting.com/2023/02/26/230852.html), where they take Tom's challenge of giving up or setting aside their vast wealth, for love.
