![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2022/06/28/wimpy.png)[ChatGPT](https://chat.openai.com/chat) has an important new feature, now you can [share links](https://chat.openai.com/share/ab54d41b-904c-4b8d-8d8e-d074e8c2b104) to conversations you had with the bot. I've had that [ability myself](http://ai.scripting.com/2023/05/26/cleveland-indians-baseball-team.html), hacked together with a bookmarklet. I know how important it is to be able to share this stuff easily. Now everyone will get to do this so we can learn more easily from each other, and more easily share what I think is a new form of literature, collaborations between humans and AI bots.
