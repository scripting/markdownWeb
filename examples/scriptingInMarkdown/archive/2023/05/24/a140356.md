BTW, I know that a lot of people can't get on Bluesky. But if you know me, I'm not just doing this for one social net. The point is that once we have something wonderful running on one, we have something to show to people on others. And I'm on Mastodon and Twitter still (see [below](http://scripting.com/2023/05/24.html#a133305)). Both have APIs and could conceivably get the same features we've added to Bluesky. That's what [textcasting](https://textcasting.org/) is all about, it flattens out the differences between the networks at a higher level than tweets. Optional titles, styling, links, enclosures, editable, no length limits.
