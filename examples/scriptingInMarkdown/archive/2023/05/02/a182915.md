![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2023/05/02/marktwain.png)People aren't thinking, imho, clearly about the dangers of AIs. The science fiction stories about AI were written long ago by people who never used the tech. Before there was social media, before someone like Trump was elected president using a product called Twitter. Yet all we're hearing from journalism and the pundits, including the CS professors, are rehashes of what Isaac Asimov, Philip Dick and Arthur C Clarke wrote about in the 1960s.
