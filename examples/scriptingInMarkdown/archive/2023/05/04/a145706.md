![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/06/16/tbl.png)Podcast: [The internet of social media apps](http://scripting.com/2023/05/04/theInternetOfSocialMediaApps.m4a). How to make it possible for there to be lots of social media apps that we all use, without turning us all into copy/paste robots. We've been here before, with the internet itself and [TCP/IP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_protocol_suite) and the web with [HTTP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HTTP). The same process will yield a result that's good for social media users and developers, and will allow social networks to evolve without being dominated by the Silicon Valley cartel. We've been down that road, and we now know [where](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elon_Musk) [it](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Zuckerberg) [ends](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Dorsey)._16 minutes._
