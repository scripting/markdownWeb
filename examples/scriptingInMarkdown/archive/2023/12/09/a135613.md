After listening to Thursday's [Countdown podcast](https://podcasts.apple.com/il/podcast/ive-got-bidens-daisy-ad-and-trump-is-disqualified-12-7-23/id1633301179?i=1000637762872) I'd like to read a short book about the presidency after the Lincoln assassination, leading to the [impeachment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impeachment_of_Andrew_Johnson) and trial of his successor, Andrew Johnson. This is where the [14th Amendment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourteenth_Amendment_to_the_United_States_Constitution) came from. Some days Olbermann really nails it, this was one of those episodes, left me wanting more. This may be [the book](https://www.amazon.com/Impeached-President-Andrew-Johnson-Lincolns/dp/1416547509), I'm still looking for suggestions. I am not a history scholar, just an interested citizen.
