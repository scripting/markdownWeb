There's an interesting recent development in the world of feeds and blogs -- blogs that are only feeds. Designed to be read in feed readers of all sizes and shapes. I have a few of these myself. My [linkblog](http://data.feedland.org/feeds/davewiner.xml) for example. Also, FeedLand provides an outbound [feed to every user](https://feedland.com/?myfeed), which meets all the qualifications of a feed-only blog.
