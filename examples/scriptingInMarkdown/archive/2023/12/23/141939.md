We can now subscribe to Bluesky feeds in [FeedLand](https://docs.feedland.com/gettingstarted.md) --

*   In the [Subscribe sub-menu](https://imgs.scripting.com/2023/12/23/subscribeScreenShot.png), choose _To one feed._
    
*   Enter the URL of a Bluesky profile page. For example, here's a [link](https://bsky.app/profile/neilhimself.neilgaiman.com) to Neil Gaiman's profile.
    
*   Click OK.
    

When this feature came out [yesterday](http://scripting.com/2023/12/22.html#a193746) it didn't work with FeedLand because we were looking for full URLs pointing to a feed in their feed discovery link and they provided a relative URL.

The fix was in the [feedHunter package](https://github.com/scripting/reallysimple/tree/main/demos/feedhunter), it now looks for relative URLs and makes them full before trying to find the feed.

I think I understand why they used a relative URL, they're getting ready to federate and it's probably simpler for them to just specify a relative URL.

On the other hand, I think this is going to be a problem -- in the world of feeds, software usually expects full URLs.
