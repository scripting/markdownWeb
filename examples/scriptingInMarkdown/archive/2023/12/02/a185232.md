![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2023/11/30/curly.png)This is the stupidest thing ever. We've had a bit of trouble with the implementation of [rssCloud](http://scripting.com/opmlHowto/dave/rssCloud/walkthrough/publisher.html) on the new Automattic-hosted server, the pings to coming from one server in particular. We did finally get to the bottom of it and all of a sudden it's working. Except for [Scripting News](http://scripting.com/rss.xml). I added debugging code, stared at server logs, and scratched my head, until I decided to check if I was subscribed to the [freaking feed](https://feedland.com/?feedurl=http%3A%2F%2Fscripting.com%2Frss.xml). I wasn't. Once I fixed that little oversight, it's all [working](https://imgs.scripting.com/2023/12/02/itworked.png), nice and fast. Sometimes the bug is in your brain not in the software. :smile:
