![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2020/08/12/indianaJones.png)WordPress.org has a [site](https://wordpress.org/photos/) where people share Creative Commons licensed photos. We asked them to support image enclosures (aka artcasting) in [their feed](https://feedland.com/?feedurl=https%3A%2F%2Fwordpress.org%2Fphotos%2Ffeed%2F). They just did. And as a result their flow [looks outstanding](https://feedland.com/?river=https%3A%2F%2Fwordpress.org%2Fphotos%2Ffeed%2F) on FeedLand, which totally supports this format. This is a bell-ringer event, this is what we're about -- building up and out from where we are in the land of feeds. We can do a lot if we work together.
