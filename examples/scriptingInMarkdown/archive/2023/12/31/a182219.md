![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2022/04/15/theEggman.png)[Andrew Hickey](https://bsky.app/profile/andrewhickey.bsky.social) is this year's [Blogger of the Year](https://www.google.com/search?q=site%3Ascripting.com+Blogger+of+the+Year). He's a prototypical blogger, exactly the kind of person I hoped would [create](http://scripting.com/2022/04/13/165056.html?title=sourcesGoDirectExplained) their own media having profound positive impact on people's lives. People like me. His [500 Songs](https://500songs.com/podcast/) podcast, which I've written about [many times](https://www.google.com/search?q=site%3Ascripting.com+500+songs), focuses on a song in every episode and tells you how the pieces came together to create it. When a new episode comes out I greedily savor it, like a great [bagel](https://www.google.com/search?q=site%3Ascripting.com+bagel) from Zabar's or a new [Tarantino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quentin_Tarantino#Filmography) movie. Two recent episodes, one about the [Grateful Dead](https://500songs.com/podcast/episode-165-dark-star-by-the-grateful-dead/) and the other about [Hey Jude](https://500songs.com/podcast/episode-171-hey-jude-by-the-beatles/), changed my views of each of the groups, in the first case confirming an intuition, and in the other showing me how naive my idea of how a song comes together. I am envious of how the musicians work together, it may be a cuthroat industry, but it seems incredibly collaborative compared to how tech works, where we all pretty much ignore each other. Hickey isn't just a podcaster, he's also a blogger, and he does it in an unusual place, [on Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/andrewhickey.bsky.social). He has things to say and that's of course what [NBB's](http://scripting.com/stories/2009/11/24/naturalbornBlogger.html) are all about. You may not care for their opinions, but you will know what they are. Anyway, if you like the Beatles, I highly recommend the [latest episode](https://500songs.com/podcast/episode-171-hey-jude-by-the-beatles/), and if you like popular music, [subscribe](https://500songs.com/feed/podcast/) to [500 Songs](https://feedland.com/?river=https%3A%2F%2F500songs.com%2Ffeed%2Fpodcast%2F). And if you know of any podcasts like this, please let me know. I always want to add to my podcast reading list.
