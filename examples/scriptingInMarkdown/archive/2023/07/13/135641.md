When you're viewing a feed list, like [this](https://feedland.org/?username=davewiner), when a feed updates, instead of inserting it at the top and forcing everything down, it displays a button. Click the button to see the new stuff.

![View updated feeds button.](https://imgs.scripting.com/2023/07/13/viewUpdatedFeedsButtonScreen.png)

We already had a similar feature on timeline pages.
