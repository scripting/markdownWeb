I don't love writing, I [said](https://bsky.app/profile/did:plc:oety7qbfx7x6exn2ytrwikmr/post/3k2nksufoti24) to Elizabeth Spiers on Bluesky. I'm more driven to write. I’m a [natural born blogger](http://scripting.com/stories/2009/11/24/naturalbornBlogger.html) or NBB. We write because that’s what we do. The first impulse is to write. First thing I want to do when I sit down to work is write and when I’m thinking about more things to do, I’m usually writing about it.
