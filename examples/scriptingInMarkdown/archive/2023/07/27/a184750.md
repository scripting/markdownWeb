In general, if there's a change in [FeedLand](https://docs.feedland.com/gettingstarted.md) or [Drummer](http://drummer.land/), you want to be sure you're getting the latest client software. Normally the browser caches the code, but you can force it to get the latest versions. In Chrome/Mac with Cmd-Shift-R. Obviously it's different in different browsers.
