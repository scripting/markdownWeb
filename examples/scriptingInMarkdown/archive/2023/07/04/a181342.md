Journalism should care about Google breaking the open web as much as they care about Musk breaking Twitter because, in capitalism, Musk has the right to break Twitter, he owns it, but [Google doesn’t own the web](http://this.how/googleAndHttp/). I think the reason journalism will report on Musk breaking Twitter and not Google breaking the web is that they need a person to report on, and Google has never really had a personality. The two founders are quiet, and I don't think I've ever heard the current CEO speak a word. So they're unreportable?
