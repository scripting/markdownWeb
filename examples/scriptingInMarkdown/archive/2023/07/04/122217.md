We now [know](https://techcrunch.com/2023/07/03/threads-instagrams-twitter-competitor-is-expected-july-6/) that Facebook's new social network, Threads, [will be available](https://www.techmeme.com/230703/p25#a230703p25) to the public on Thursday. The day after tomorrow.

There's lots of discourse on the net about _What's To Be Done_ about this big event. Based on decades of experience with such launches, here's what I say.

1.  No matter what we do right now it'll probably have no effect on the Facebook rollout.
    
2.  It's better to listen, study and learn, size up the product. We don't know what it is. Wait till we find out.
    
3.  Call them Facebook, not Meta. They're trying to run away from their legacy, but it's very relevant. People should know it's coming from Facebook not "Meta." When I see Meta my mind reads "Mets."
    
4.  After the dust has settled, what if anything should be done may be more apparent.
    
5.  Remember, Bigco's launch with big thunder but the products often flop. Don't get sucked into the hype.
    
6.  Whatever you say or do now, viewed from a few weeks from now, will seem silly and over-reactive. Keep on truckin.
    
7.  Let's study the product, discover what can be done with its APIs (assuming it has some) and keep an open mind. It's possible that some good can come from this.
    
8.  Don't depend on journalists to study it. They tend to report on the press releases, and amp up the fear.
    
9.  First and foremost -- don't panic. :smile:
