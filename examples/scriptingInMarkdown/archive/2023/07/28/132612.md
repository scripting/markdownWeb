BTW, I find I don't write screeds on Twitter any longer.

If I want to piss in the wind I either do it elsewhere or don't do it at all.

I use Facebook more now, in addition to Bluesky, but I wonder why I use it because it's behind a very high silo-wall. I only realized today if say Substack were doing this, it would so irritate me that I wouldn't use it. I don't know why I give Bluesky a pass on this.

Mainly Masto is interesting again because [Liza Sabeter](https://www.google.com/search?q=liza+sabater) aka [blogdiva](https://mastodon.social/@blogdiva) resurfaced, and chose Mastodon to be her stage. She's so smart and irreverent and also incredibly dedicated to her truth. She's enough of a reason for me to check in on Masto.

I also write on [my blog](http://scripting.com/) regularly and seem to appreciate it more these days perhaps because it doesn't shut down on me, it seems to care whether I like it or not.

PS: That's kind of a joke because I have always written my own blogging software going back to 1994.

PPS: Of course I wrote this screed [on Twitter](https://twitter.com/davewiner/status/1684918163018747904), proving once again that it's even worse than it appears.

PPPS: Facebook's folly -- [aka](http://scripting.com/2023/07/06/134151.html) [Threads](http://scripting.com/2023/07/05/232331.html) -- is of no significance whatsoever to me at least.
