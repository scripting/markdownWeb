How the web should work:

1.  To ChatGPT -- list ten great [songs](http://ai.scripting.com/2023/07/01/whosongs.html) by The Who.
    
2.  To ChatGPT -- send that list to Amazon Music.
    
3.  To Amazon Music -- play the songs ChatGPT sent.
    

Not a technological problem, it's a standards problem. Easily solved by the elusive "working together."
