![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2020/12/27/wall-e.png)When I was a grad student, in the [middle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1977) of the last century, there were two kinds of terminals in the computer lab, ones that could only display uppercase characters, and the coveted [Hazeltine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hazeltine_1500) terminals that could do also do lowercase. A real luxury item. I feel the same way as we approach the middle of the next century when I get to use boldface text or a bit of italic in a tweet. I laughed out loud when some text I pasted from my blog went through with its boldness intact. We have a long way to go to [get back](http://textcasting.org/) where we were.
