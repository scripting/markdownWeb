![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2020/05/25/strongman.png)[New FeedLand gesture](https://github.com/scripting/feedlandSupport/issues/208). It was a complicated gesture to see all the text of a too-long-to-fit item in FeedLand. Then it hit me, just click on the freaking text to make it bigger and then click again to make it small again. Much easier to do, and what else would a click mean?? Here's a quick [video demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qCLIuN9Rl8s). ;-)
