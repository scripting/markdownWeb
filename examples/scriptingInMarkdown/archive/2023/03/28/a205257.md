My new (today!) chatbot, programmed with the [archive](http://scripting.com/publicfolder/chatgpt/scriptingNewsSource/scriptingInMarkdown.zip) of my blog, going back 28+ years, is up and I've been playing with it, and am as impressed as I hoped I would. An [example](http://scripting.com/images/2023/03/28/whoInventedRss.png?nodialog), I asked who invented RSS? Can you imagine how much I’ve written about RSS in 20+ years? The first part is boilerplate, could have come from any site, but the second part was a very concise, thorough and interesting synopsis of years of _my writing_ about feeds. The chatbot did very good work.
