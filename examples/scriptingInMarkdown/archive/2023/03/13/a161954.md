![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2023/03/13/littleRedBook.png)I was fascinated with [Communist China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_People%27s_Republic_of_China) when I was a kid. A forbidden place. Hardly anything came out, except culture created out of Communist Party lies. I wrote letters to the Chinese Consulate in New York asking for everything they had. They sent a huge poster of [Chairman Mao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mao_Zedong) and colorful books with gorgeous drawings of Chinese kids doing [amazing things](http://scripting.com/images/2023/03/13/chairmanMao.png) because they studied the teachings of Chairman Mao. I wasn't stupid, I understood this was propaganda, but I loved the way they did it -- as a genre. This is [what I wrote](http://scripting.com/images/2023/03/13/autographFromSixthGrade.png) in one of my best friends' autograph book when we graduated sixth grade and went on to junior high.
