![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2023/03/27/successionPoster.png)I watch [Succession](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Succession_\(TV_series\)), the HBO drama. Spoilers follow. Last night was the first episode of the last season. At the end, two characters who are married came to the conclusion they'll divorce. A sad moment, beautifully acted. The scene ends with them lying on a bed together, clothed, far apart, but holding hands. I thought, if you're going to do it, this is the way. Let there be at least a little love in the last moment.
