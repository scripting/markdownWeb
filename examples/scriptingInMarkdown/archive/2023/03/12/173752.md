what would a chatgpt-like twitter be?

i type a query, and the bot responds.

then you can type a query that builds on mine, starting a new thread, which other people can build on.

the thread to the first tweet forms a stack, a context for each subsequent reply?

what comes out at the end is pure singularity?

i [asked](http://scripting.com/publicfolder/chatgpt/2023/03/12/chatgpt-like-twitter-platform.html) chatgpt and it says no, it would not be a singularity because "ChatGPT-like models are limited to generating responses based on their training data and do not possess true consciousness or self-awareness."
