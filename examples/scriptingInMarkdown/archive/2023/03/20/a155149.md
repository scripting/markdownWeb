A cool feature for FeedLand users. When you _Like_ an item, it's automatically added to two RSS feeds -- one for [your likes](http://data.feedland.org/likes/davewiner.xml), and one for the [likes of all users](http://data.feedland.org/likes/all.xml) on your FeedLand.It's basically a single-gesture linkblog. There's a lot of that in FeedLand, where things have been factored to a point where they couldn't possibly be simpler. Of course since they're standard [RSS](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html) feeds, you could follow them from any feed reader.
