![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/10/04/goldenGirl.png)The other day I was [kvelling](http://scripting.com/images/2023/03/02/kvelling.png) about [how great ChatGPT was](https://twitter.com/davewiner/status/1630759941433589761) at finding [feeds for news sites](http://scripting.com/images/2023/03/02/paywalllessfeeds.png) that didn't have paywalls. Until I noticed that some of the sites [most definitely](http://scripting.com/images/2023/03/02/oops1.png) did [have paywalls](http://scripting.com/images/2023/03/02/oops2.png). So it's not perfect. It's a totally 1.0 kind of thing. If you don't know what that means, it might be a good time to re-read [Microserfs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microserfs).
