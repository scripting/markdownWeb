My personal chatbot is a good writer, composition-wise. I asked it [what kind of food I like](http://scripting.com/images/2023/03/30/food.png), and it answered with a story about me and my longtime bud Dave Jacobs and how we both like Chinese food. It's been a long time since I've taken a writing class, but I think that would get a good grade. Find a way to tell a story, rather than just reciting facts. Make it about people, and people will read it.
