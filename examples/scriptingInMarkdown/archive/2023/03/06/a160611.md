[Last night's Knicks game](https://nypost.com/2023/03/05/immanuel-quickley-carries-knicks-over-celtics-in-double-ot/) was wonderful. As the Nets gave up on their buy-a-championship nightmare, the slow-growth Knicks approach has been going great. I'm sure the Knicks would've signed KD or Kyrie if they had offered, but so thankful they diss'd the Knicks instead. Idiots. Now the normal order has been restored. Hopefully the Knicks continue to add pieces slowly and let home-grown stars like [IQ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immanuel_Quickley) continue to grow. It's intoxicating to watch him get the chance to excel as in last night's game, and completely exceed all reasonable expectations. The whole team is fantastic. Our loyalty to the team has paid off.
