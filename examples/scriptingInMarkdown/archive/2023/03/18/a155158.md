Another important feature, [every user has their own feed](https://feedland.org/?userfeedsnews=true), which you can edit in FeedLand, of course. To write a post, click in the edit box at the top of the page, type some text, click the Post button. It should show up in the list of user-feed posts, below. If you want to edit one of your posts, click on the text, make [the changes](http://scripting.com/images/2023/03/18/editingAPost.png), click the Update button. Of course there's an [RSS feed](https://feedland.org/?feedurl=http%3A%2F%2Fscripting.com%2Ffeedland%2Fuserfeeds%2Fdavewiner.xml) for each user, which you can subscribe to in FeedLand or any other RSS-compatible application (there are many, as I'm sure you know).
