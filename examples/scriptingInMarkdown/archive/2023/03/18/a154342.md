![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2023/03/18/accordionGuy.png)Almost everything you do on FeedLand is public. You can read anyone else's feed list. Example, [Ken Smith's list](https://feedland.org/?username=KenSmith). If you're signed in, to subscribe to a feed just click the feed's checkbox. You can also see [who's been on](https://feedland.org/?userslist) recently, when they signed up, how many feeds they subscribe to, and how many pages they've viewed. You can also see the feeds that have recently been [subscribed to](https://feedland.org/?log). The idea is to make feed discovery as easy as it possibly can be. We steal (and improve on) ideas from social media apps that didn't exist last time I took a fresh look at feed systems. I don't think of FeedLand as a feed reader -- it's more of a feed management system, imho.
