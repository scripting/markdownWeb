![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2020/03/19/beet.png)It seems like the [transition](https://github.com/scripting/feedlandSupport/issues/176) of [FeedLand](https://docs.feedland.com/gettingstarted.md) from Twitter-based identity to email-based identity has more or less worked. As part of the transition, and please this is only for people who read Scripting News, so don't pass it on -- membership in [feedland.org](https://feedland.org/) is now open. It had been closed since Dec 12 when I started working on the transition. You only need an email address to sign up, not a Twitter identity.
