![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2021/06/14/strongman.png)I get that people don't like that ChatGPT will return incorrect results. But as a software developer, I can experiment with the product with that caveat, and imagine its uses once the quality of the results are better, however they achieve that. I don't have to wait to think, in other words. With that disclaimer, I searched for docs about how to do a hard-reload in popular browsers. Those docs are hard to find, and when you find one it's heavily monetized. So instead, I asked ChatGPT to write a [docs page](http://scripting.com/publicfolder/chatgpt/2023/03/19/new-chat.html) to answer this question. Looks pretty good to me! No bullshit in the page either.
