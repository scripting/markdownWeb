We should [retroactively](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/retroactively) free Americans who were enslaved prior to the [13th Amendment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thirteenth_Amendment_to_the_United_States_Constitution). They should be thought of today as full citizens of the United States to whom unthinkable harm was done.
