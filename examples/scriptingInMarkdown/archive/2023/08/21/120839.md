As you may know, I've been reviewing projects that I haven't looked at in a while. Latest is [DocServer](https://imgs.scripting.com/2023/08/21/docserverScreen.png?nodialog), the online docs for Drummer's built-in [scripting](http://docserver.scripting.com/drummer/scripting.opml) language. If you use(d) Frontier, you'll recognize both the format of the pages and the names of the verbs. Here are the docs for [date.secondsSince](http://docserver.scripting.com/?verb=date.secondsSince) and [gitHub.upload](http://docserver.scripting.com/?verb=github.upload) to give you an idea of the range of functionality, from simple data manipulation to providing glue for powerful web apps that have well-thought-out APIs. This idea goes back to the late 80s when we were pushing scriptable Mac apps over [excessive integration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lotus_Jazz). Another idea that caught on, slowly, over time until now we take it for granted.
