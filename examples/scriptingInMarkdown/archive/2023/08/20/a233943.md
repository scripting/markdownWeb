![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2022/07/14/loveRss.png)Now that social media apps like Mastodon have [RSS](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html) feeds, we had to figure out how to display them in FeedLand. Here's an example, Matt Haughey's [feed](http://xoxo.zone/@mathowie.rss) on Mastodon, as [viewed in FeedLand](https://feedland.org/?river=http%3A%2F%2Fxoxo.zone%2F%40mathowie.rss). These posts, as you [can see](https://imgs.scripting.com/2023/08/20/titlelessPostsInFeedland.png), have no titles. So we bold-up the first sentence, or if it's too long, a big part of it. They look different from titled posts, but now that we've lived with this for a few weeks, I think it works. Might still do some tweaks.
