![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2023/07/05/pinkyFacingLeft.png)I've finished the first pass of my review of the [FeedLand docs](http://docs2.feedland.org/readme.md). I converted them to Markdown, with the source hosted [on GitHub](https://github.com/scripting/feedlandSupport/tree/main/docs), to make it easy for people to see the changes over time, and to make bug reports. I want to get more systematic in working on FeedLand. When you're reading a docs page, there's a command in the menu that opens the same page in GitHub. If you're a FeedLand user, you may want to read the new docs, it's always a good idea I find to review the docs of products I use. You often see things you didn't pick up on the first time around.
