The Rolling Stones are one of the best bands to Peloton to. A steady strong beat is essential. My favorite Stones song for workouts is [Can't You Hear Me Knocking](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=va66PwKQM1w). It's also a great song to just listen to. I find it's impossible for your body not to rock with the beat.
