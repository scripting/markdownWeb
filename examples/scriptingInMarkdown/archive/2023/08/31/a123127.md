![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/06/23/mrNatural.png)There's an autumn feel in the air today. A bit cooler, and it isn't very humid. Nice breeze. Funny thing is I so totally looked forward to summer, and it's almost over, and I should feel sad, but right now the changing season actually feels good. Just shows that there's nothing logical or predictable about [body chemistry](https://www.google.com/search?q=site%3Ascripting.com+body+chemistry). These feelings come from evolution and natural selection. It's time for the season to change, so our body feeds us chemicals that makes us feel like this is a good thing.
