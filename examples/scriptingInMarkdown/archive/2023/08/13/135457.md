![Joni Mitchell, Neil Young, Rick Danko, Van Morrison, Bob Dylan and Robbie Robertson in The Last Waltz. ](https://imgs.scripting.com/2023/08/13/lastwaltz.png)

I got this picture from a [Facebook post](https://www.facebook.com/groups/197624643745929?multi_permalinks=2599965136845189&hoisted_section_header_type=recently_seen) on Woodstock and the 60s.

My generation of software developers totally fucked this up. Everyone went their own way. Nowhere near enough working together. There was some collaboration however with people in the news world, and we created media that empowered millions of individuals to publish. We got stuff done, but we could have done much better.

The next generation should look for ways to all play on the same stage, make each other sound better, inspire each other to _their_ greatness. To show, visibly, what we can accomplish by working together.
