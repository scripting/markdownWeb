I did some searches on [Tina Weymouth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tina_Weymouth), the bass player for the Talking Heads. In an [interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rY_BfWwbCrw) with her husband, he said everyone's eyes were on her when they performed, I thought I was the only one. When I listen to a Talking Heads song with a strong beat (a lot of them) I think of her. That's what she represents, the driving power of the group is a woman. Try listening to [this song](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXdVgILd38U) and look at [images](https://www.google.com/search?sxsrf=AB5stBgMSi6KCAVa4-pV7pTOKKxwIwrtQw:1691244562327&q=tina+weymouth&tbm=isch&source=lnms&sa=X&ved=2ahUKEwi6mfb62MWAAxW2kIkEHQGiDhMQ0pQJegQIDRAB&biw=1663&bih=1151&dpr=2) of her.
