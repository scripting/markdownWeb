One of the funny things about [XML](http://scripting.com/2023/02/10.html#a020506) is that they pitched developers saying "Now you can create your own formats!" I'm sure they meant it, but once [RSS](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html) was established, they tried to replace it with an incompatible format. Luckily, the [W3C](https://www.w3.org/) wasn't as powerful as Apple or Google, and their format didn't replace [RSS](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html). But, imho, when you push an open tech and someone takes you up on it, you should help, not fight them, ie if you really meant it.
