A few days ago I [asked Brent Simmons](http://scripting.com/2023/02/06.html#a153119) where we go to talk about ideas for making our products work together in interesting new ways. Where do we go to congregate? I just watched a [fantastic](http://scripting.com/2023/02/19.html#a160614) [documentary](https://www.amazon.com/Laurel-Canyon-Season-1/dp/B0882JGTTV) about Laurel Canyon in Hollywood in the 60s and 70s, near [Sunset Strip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunset_Strip), which had lots of bars, for all kinds of music and the musicians who made the music. Lots of famous names. People who created a generation of pop music, with lots of collaboration. This is a necessary component for tech. When I first arrived in Silicon Valley in 1979, I kept looking for the pulse of the place. Where do people meet up. And within hours I found [Ken's House of Pancakes](https://www.almanacnews.com/square/2007/05/09/what-are-your-memories-of-kens-house-of-pancakes-in-menlo-park) on [El Camino](https://www.google.com/maps/place/888+El+Camino+Real,+Menlo+Park,+CA+94025/@37.4524062,-122.1802984,17z/data=!4m6!3m5!1s0x808fa4b21d83e8ef:0xd20e5a3bace86209!8m2!3d37.4525122!4d-122.1802369!16s%2Fg%2F11b8v7w2wj) in Menlo Park [was](https://www.almanacnews.com/morgue/1999/1999_03_17.kens.html) a good place. There was [Siam Garden](https://www.yelp.com/biz/siam-garden-menlo-park) also in Menlo Park. [Chef Chu's](http://www.chefchu.com/) in Los Altos. [Rossotti's](https://www.alpineinnpv.com/) in Portola Valley and of course [Buck's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buck%27s_of_Woodside) if you like VC. For a few years, my yard in Woodside was a frequent party place. I am amazed by all the people who came to [BloggerCons](https://www.google.com/search?q=bloggercon). Okay so now we're on the web, we've been here for 30 freaking years. Where do you go if you have an idea you want to share ideas with other developers, who may not develop for the same platform, or work for a large company. This is missing. At one point the [discussion group](http://static.userland.com/userLandDiscussArchive/msg000001.html) on this blog served that purpose, but it was taken over by trolls, of course. But nowadays we have less crazy ideas about "free speech" and know how to ruthlessly dispose of trolls. I'd like to figure this out.
