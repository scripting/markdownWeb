I want to see all the comments I've posted in reverse chronologic order. Where is that command? Then it hit me, they need [ChatGPT](https://chat.openai.com/chat) as the UI of their product. Let's give it a try. "In Facebook, how do I see a reverse chronologic list of all the comments I've left on other people's posts?" [Here's the result](http://scripting.com/images/2023/02/28/chatgptguide.png). I tried it, got the list, edited a message. This solves a huge design problem in software. How to simplify complex software so people can feel more in control.
