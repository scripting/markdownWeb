![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/12/12/cowboy.png)[James Cridland](https://james.cridland.net/) of [Podnews](https://podnews.net/) created a [modern feed](https://tradesecrets.archive.podnews.net/index.xml) with the podcasts from Trade Secrets, the show [Adam Curry](https://www.google.com/search?q=site%3Ascripting.com+%22adam+curry%22) and I did in 2004-2005 during in the first wave of podcasting. I've loaded it [into FeedLand](http://feedland.org/?river=https%3A%2F%2Ftradesecrets.archive.podnews.net%2Findex.xml). And James loaded it into [Podfriend](https://web.podfriend.com/podcast/trade-secrets4). It's great to have this stuff resurrected in a form that people interested in the formation of podcasting, journalists and historians we hope, can learn from. Thanks James! I hope he does the same with my [Morning Coffee Notes](http://morningcoffeenotes.com/) podcast, which precedes Trade Secrets, and documents developments in the summer of 2004.
