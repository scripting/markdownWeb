I can now settle the age-old debate about what is and isn't a podcast. If at the end of your show, and in promos, you say "Available where ever you get your podcasts," then it's a podcast! If you don't say it, then it ain't a podcast! To illustrate, here's the [shortest podcast ever](http://scripting.com/2023/02/01/availableWhereEverYouGetYourPodcasts.m4a).
