<item>s in Mastodon feeds do not have <title>s. It's time for all feed readers to not treat this as an error. Here's a [screen shot](http://scripting.com/images/2022/11/16/itemsWithNoTitlesAreLegalRss.png) that illustrates. Also repeating the text where the title goes is not useful or correct, and if your reader does that, it should be fixed. RSS is going places now, and if you don't go with it, people are going to notice.
