I [replied](https://twitter.com/davewiner/status/1589628912723951618): We would sue them. Americans are individuals. If that means anything, I must be the ruler of myself. There is a border, beyond which no one goes w/o my permission, including the government. That border is my body. Even a Republican should understand that.
