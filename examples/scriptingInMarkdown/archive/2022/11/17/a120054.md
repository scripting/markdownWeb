In 2014 I wrote a piece [explaining](http://scripting.com/2014/06/02/whatIsARiverOfNewsAggregator.html) what a River of News aggregator is. The first qualification is not true in FeedLand. We can handle full text, but it doesn't interfere with scrolling because only the first part is automatically displayed. There's a [little downarrow](http://scripting.com/images/2022/11/17/littleArrow.png) in the lower right corner which reveals the full text if you want to see it.
