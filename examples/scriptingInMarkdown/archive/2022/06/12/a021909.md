![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/09/14/rssCoffeeCup.png)The twenty-year review of [RSS 2.0](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html) is now officially underway, with the release of the [reallysimple](https://github.com/scripting/reallysimple) package. I know not many people are paying attention yet, but this is the beginning of a new stack of open source feed tools. By the time we get to Sept 18, a nice new [barn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barn_raising) should be raised, and RSS can begin its second twenty years with some of the broken windows fixed and a fresh coat of paint.
