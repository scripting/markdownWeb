![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2020/04/19/coronavirus.png)I had a Covid scare today. A friend who is visiting was staying in a house where someone got Covid the night before they came to my house. We both took Covid tests, and both were negative. So I feel pretty good about dodging this. It's the first time I took a test. I play it very safe re Covid. I have too many risk factors to want to take too many risks, and as far as I'm concerned the pandemic is still raging. In the meantime, if you get Covid, get the [latest treatment](https://www.yalemedicine.org/news/13-things-to-know-paxlovid-covid-19). I can't imagine why someone who gets infected doesn't, but it happens apparently.
