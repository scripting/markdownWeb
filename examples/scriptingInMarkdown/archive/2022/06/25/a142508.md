[The Constitution](https://twitter.com/davewiner/status/1540694811199406082) is like an operating system. The one we have was designed for slavery. But then a few decades after the [Bill of Rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Bill_of_Rights) we changed our mind, and decided not to have slavery. It's like going from [character-based](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Text-based_user_interface) to [GUI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graphical_user_interface). but we never wrote a new OS.
