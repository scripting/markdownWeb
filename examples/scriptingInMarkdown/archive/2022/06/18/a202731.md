The goal of the [reallysimple](https://github.com/scripting/reallysimple) project is to have lots of devs doing lots of experimentation with [RSS](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html) at the same time, and having all their work be totally compatible. To start a new developer process that avoids the pitfalls of the RSS environment of the last 20 years.
