Scienceites have an effectionate name for ourselves, but it's not really printable. Let's see if I can telegraph it. We didn't have a football team, but we did have a math team. We had a computer in the early 70s when I went there, which was amazing. A block away was [DeWitt Clinton High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DeWitt_Clinton_High_School) which was all-boys and definitely had a football team. We were under no illusions about who would win if we fought, so they didn't pick on us. Sort of an honor thing, I guess. Anyway we called ourselves (and as far as I know still do) The Science Fa\*\*ots. Maybe they _don't_ say it these days, this was before political correctness. :smile:
