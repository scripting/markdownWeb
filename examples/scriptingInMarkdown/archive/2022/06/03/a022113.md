![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2022/06/03/atlanta.png)Atlanta [season 3 episode 4](https://www.avclub.com/atlanta-review-season-3-episode-4-the-big-payback-1848761087), which I just watched, is brilliant. There's one point in the episode when a white man [explains slavery](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-6jL8hXUfa0) from the point of view of today's blacks, and you realize as it makes sense, the words were probably from a black writer. The power of being able to have the ideas you want white people to understand come from the mouth of a white actor. They get to insert their truth into the script the way Woody Allen inserts [Marshall McLuhan](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jRcMsqCbzWk) into the script of Annie Hall.
