Come to think of it, Twitter and RSS have a lot in common. Both are roughly contemporary pioneering efforts on which today's social media and news worlds are built. Both are a bit worse for wear and tear, to [quote](http://scripting.com/images/2022/08/14/wearAndTear.png) the [Rolling Stones](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJBkL9_Lfm4). But they're still here! We'll make love. Twitter and RSS all the way.
