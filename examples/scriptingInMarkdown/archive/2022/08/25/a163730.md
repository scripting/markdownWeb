Sometimes in programming you have to set up a test that might take a minute or two to run. While I'm waiting today I ask my speaker to play [Oye Cómo Va](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J7ATTjg7tpE) by Santana as my waiting music. It gives me something lively to groove to while the gears in the cloud mesh and produce the test result. It's like the [song they play](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B3lLYOGDsts) while the contestants write down their Final Jeopardy questions.
