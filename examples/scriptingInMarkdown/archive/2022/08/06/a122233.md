Wow I miss [Summer Streets](https://twitter.com/SummerStreets/status/1555873107667189761). On three Saturdays in August, [in NYC](https://www1.nyc.gov/html/dot/summerstreets/html/home/home.shtml), they close Park Avenue and 4th Avenue below it, all the way down to the Brooklyn Bridge -- and it's just for bikes and pedestrians. It's what NYC would be like if there were no cars.
