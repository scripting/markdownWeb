A reader thanked me for the [tip](http://scripting.com/2022/08/05.html#a141224) about Olbermann. "With Maddow only one day a week, it's nice to know there is still a good, daily source of my kind of Snark and News available." My response: You're very welcome. But I think when you listen to Olbermann you'll realize as I did that Maddow wasn't cutting it. She stepped back because she lost her edge, she must've known it. I also think the MSNBC management must have been forcing her to not say the whole truth. Olbermann for now at least is unchained. But his show is owned by a [big corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IHeartMedia) so he'll probably eventually go stale too. But for now -- he's got it.
