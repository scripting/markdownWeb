I made a trip [down to NYC](http://scripting.com/2022/08/05.html) a couple of weeks ago, and picked up a fresh bagel with cream cheese and lox at Utopia Bagels in Queens. It was heaven.

I don't imagine I'm going to get that good a bagel in Ulster County, but what about a fresh bagel? I went to Lox of Bagels in Saugerties last Tuesday, they had bins full of bagels that _looked_ fresh and there was a long line of people waiting to be served so it seemed promising. But the bagels were old and stale. Yck.

Okay I'm going to go to every bagel place in the area until I find one that's decent. Or maybe you can save me the time??
