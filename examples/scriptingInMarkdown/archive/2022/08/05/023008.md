Today was the first time I went to a [Tesla supercharger](https://www.tesla.com/supercharger).

I thought I could make it home with 6% to spare, but my Tesla insisted that I should play it safe and get a charge at the charger in [New Paltz, NY](https://www.tesla.com/findus?v=2&bounds=41.866847319736465%2C-73.89163051440431%2C41.62811734881771%2C-74.28198848559572&zoom=12&filters=store%2Cservice%2Csupercharger%2Cdestination%20charger%2Cbodyshop%2Cparty&search=New%20Paltz%2C%20NY%2012561%2C%20USA). Turns out it was at a place I knew well, the Plaza Diner. When I was 17 I hitchhiked up to New Paltz every Friday that summer and often would end up there on my way.

![The Plaza Diner, New Paltz, NY.](https://imgs.scripting.com/2022/08/05/plazaDiner.png)

I was amazed at how fast their charger runs. It almost felt like filling up a gas-powered car. I was also impressed that there was _nowhere to piss._ How can you have a place like this with no way for a human to evacuate while the car fills up. I was tempted to just do it, but I held on and waited till I got home.
