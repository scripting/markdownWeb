I would never try to reinvent Twitter in terms of [RSS](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html), but I do want very much to get the two networks joined. Twitter does what it does very well, and RSS does something different, also very well. The philosophy of the internet tells us we should get them to peer.
