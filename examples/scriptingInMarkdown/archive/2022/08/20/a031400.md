Turns out I did have something to say. I'm watching a great HBO series called [My Brilliant Friend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Brilliant_Friend_\(TV_series\)). It got the highest [rating](https://www.metacritic.com/tv/my-brilliant-friend) on Metacritic, and it is deserved. Almost like watching [Jennifer Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Lawrence) (who I love) in her [first movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winter%27s_Bone), and realizing there's going to be a lot more of this and being happy to be around to see all of it. There are three seasons, I'm only on episode 4 of season 1. I'm sure there will be a lot more to say about this. In the meantime I'm just enjoying a beautiful film as a TV series about two children growing up in Naples in the 1950s.
