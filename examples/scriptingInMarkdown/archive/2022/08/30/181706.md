I have never liked the Mets mascot [Mr Met](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mr._Met) and now there's Ms Met are they married or what?

Guess so, her actual name is _Mrs_ Met.

Get creative, or who needs a mascot?

You could have Mayor Koch as a mascot, in an outfit with an oversized head, walking around asking [How Am I Doing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UeERjHfk9cY)?

Or a cranky old Met like Casey Stengel.

![](http://scripting.com/images/2022/08/30/caseyStengel.png)

Or Bella Abzug, or the Guardian Angels, Patti Smith?

I mean who came up with Mr Met?
