[Interop](http://scripting.com/2021/12/16/153331.html?title=myProductIsInterop) is the [Hippocratic Oath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hippocratic_Oath) for developers.

Whenever possible [make](http://this.how/standards/) [your](http://scripting.com/davenet/2000/11/30/bootstrapping.html) product [interop](http://scripting.com/2014/03/21/theWebIsMadeOfInterop.html) with products that [came before](http://essaysfromexodus.scripting.com/priorArtDesignMethod).

First do no lock-in.
