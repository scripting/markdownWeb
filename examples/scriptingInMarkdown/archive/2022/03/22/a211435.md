The nicest thing in [Wordle](http://daytona.scripting.com/?q=Wordle&collection=scriptingnews) is when type your next word and the letters, one by one, all come back green. The worst thing is when you have a 12-day streak, you feel compelled to do it the next day and the day after and on and on.
