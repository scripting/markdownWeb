Two Grateful Dead songs.

[Mama Tried](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MP4gy0TBDfU), written by Merle Haggard, but I like the Grateful Dead version better.

And I turned twenty-one in prison doin' life without parole.

No one could steer me right but Mama tried, Mama tried.

Mama tried to raise me better, but her pleading, I denied.

That leaves only me to blame 'cause Mama tried.

[Me and My Uncle](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U5Ngb6CgUOI), another Dead song that says that uncles can have a lot of influence too.

Love my uncle, God rest his soul.

Taught me good, Lord, taught me all I know.

Taught me so well, that I grabbed that gold.

I left his dead ass there by the side of the road, yeah.

Seems perhaps we have a little more mercy for mama. :boom:
