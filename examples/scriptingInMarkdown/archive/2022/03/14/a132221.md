![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2022/03/14/blueMonday.png)A summary of the VC business model. Take an idea that was developed on the open internet, without lock-in, then fund a startup to do the same thing, with lock-in and don't change the name. In other words take something like [podcasting](http://scripting.com/2021/12/21/124205.html), which thrives because users and publishers have choice and there are no gatekeepers, and lock it up and still call it podcasting.
