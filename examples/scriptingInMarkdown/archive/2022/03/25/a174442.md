The idea that software is a process was the big idea in [We Make Shitty Software](http://scripting.com/davenet/1995/09/03/wemakeshittysoftware.html) in September 1995. Here's a [screen shot](http://scripting.com/images/2022/03/25/shittyQuote.png) of the relevant portion. I got a lot of shit from devs who said they don't make shitty software, but in fact, they do. All devs do. Those that don't know they do don't know to set up a process that accounts for the shittiness of their software. A corollary -- some of the shittiest software is made by devs who think their software _isn't_ shitty.
