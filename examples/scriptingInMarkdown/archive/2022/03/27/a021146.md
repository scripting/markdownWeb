[Elon Musk](https://twitter.com/davewiner/status/1508122967024320514): If you do a new social network, please make sure it supports posts with optional titles, simple styling, links, and [RSS](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html) in and out. These are the things that are missing that make it impossible for networks to interop. And if you want free speech [interop](http://scripting.com/2021/12/16/153331.html?title=myProductIsInterop) is a must.
