![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/08/05/loveRss.png)Now is a time for news sources that might be cut off to start using [RSS](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html) seriously because that would allow others to mirror their content, and make it possible to get it back online with just a [DNS](https://www.google.com/search?q=DNS+how+to+edit) change. People thought we were being too idealistic when it was said that a benefit of syndication is that it helps route around censorship. Now in 2022, that's a very real thing. Such an inexpensive insurance policy to help make sure the news gets out.
