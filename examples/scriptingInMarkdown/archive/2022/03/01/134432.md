The monthly ritual is partially done. I have archived the [outline](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/blob/master/blog/opml/2022/02.opml) for February in the GitHub repo.

And as a reminder to myself and other Drummer users -- this is what I did for the Daytona part of the rollover.

daytona.removeOutlineRefs ("http://drummer.scripting.com/davewiner/blog.opml")

daytona.ping ("https://raw.githubusercontent.com/scripting/Scripting-News/master/blog/opml/2022/02.opml")

And, amazingly -- it worked. Here's a [search](http://daytona.scripting.com/?q=ukraine&collection=scriptingnews) for Ukraine and it finds the refs from February. They moved from the main blog.opml file to the archive file.
