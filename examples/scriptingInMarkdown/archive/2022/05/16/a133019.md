Twitch did an amazing job, turning off the [livestream of the Buffalo shooter](https://www.cnn.com/2022/05/15/business/twitch-livestream-buffalo-massacre/index.html) in two minutes. The current governor of NY, [Kathy Hochul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathy_Hochul), says that isn't good enough. As usual, NPR completely mangled the story. They should get reporters who have some idea of what livestreaming is before reporting on it.
