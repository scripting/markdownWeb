I don't know how McCartney came up with the idea for [this song](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N6XB2STqyKY), but I imagine he goes into a junk store and takes a look around. Then he goes home and [writes](https://www.azlyrics.com/lyrics/paulmccartney/junk.html) a song about what he saw. "Candlesticks, building bricks, something old and new. Memories for you and me."
