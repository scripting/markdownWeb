Ukraine had to deal with what we're [going through](https://twitter.com/jeffjarvis/status/1521145126390796288) now. Their political system was undermined by Putinites, proclaiming their right to speak, but they were undermining their democracy like the Putinites here are today. They solved the problem by being hardcore about it. Free speech is not more important than survival of democracy. We will have to make that choice too, until we do, we'll be stuck where we are.
