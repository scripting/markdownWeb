![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/09/02/mrFrog.png)We now have a list of [OPML-compatible apps](http://opml.org/compatibleApps.opml). It's a beginning, there are a bunch of apps that are not on the list. It's not just for editors, you can use OPML to pass outlines to apps that render outlines, for example, as a blog (that's Old School). There will be more of those. BTW, of course the [list](http://opml.org/compatibleApps.opml) is an outline, and the [OPML version](http://opml.org/compatibleApps.opml?format=opml) is public, so you should be able to open it in any of the apps on the list.
