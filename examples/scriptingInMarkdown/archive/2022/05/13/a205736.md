You know that [scene](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jRcMsqCbzWk) in [Annie Hall](https://www.metacritic.com/movie/annie-hall-1977) where Woody Allen gets into an argument with a Columbia professor about [Marshall McLuhan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marshall_McLuhan), who is waiting off-camera to support Allen. McLuhan says to the professor: "You know nothing of my work. How you ever got to teach to teach a course in anything is totally amazing." Allen looks at the camera: "Boy if life were only like this." Well anyway, on Twitter, I am regularly pulled out from behind a poster to support someone who thinks [RSS](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html) is doing fine. I'm supposed to play the role of McLuhan. I would enjoy that once, but not all the time for decades. :boom:
