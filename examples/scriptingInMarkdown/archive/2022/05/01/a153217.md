I'm [trying to figure out](https://twitter.com/davewiner/status/1520786270728990721) why Wordle's reports get mangled when they come through the Twitter API. I find when I paste them into a local text file they [look pretty bad](http://scripting.com/images/2022/05/01/wordleMangle.png) too. Update: It appears it has nothing to do with the Twitter API, rather [the fonts](https://twitter.com/davewiner/status/1520791215414947847) that are used to render the page.
