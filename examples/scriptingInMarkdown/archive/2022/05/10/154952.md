If you aren't into [OPML](http://opml.org/) and [RSS](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html) you can skip this post. :smile:

I have yet to organize all the bits going into my next project, the one I've been working on all year (so far). It doesn't even have a codename yet, which is unusual for me, I usually come up with them at the beginning. It will eventually have one. Anyway at this time I don't have a good place on GitHub to put the bits, the data and scripts, so I'll hang them off this post on my blog until a better place shows up.

I wanted a really good example of an OPML subscription list for the RSS-related project. The canonical example is the collection of feeds for the NY Times. They have them all assembled on a [single page](https://www.nytimes.com/rss). I did a view source, and saw that it's good HTML. I selected the part of the HTML with the feed list, and opened it in an HTML source editor, and cleaned it up until it validated.

Then in [Frontier](http://scripting.com/2021/01/04/140651.html?title=whatFrontierIsAbout), I wrote a [script](http://scripting.com/publicfolder/misc/nytFeedsOpmlList/frontierScriptSource.opml) to read the file into an [object database structure](http://scripting.com/publicfolder/misc/nytFeedsOpmlList/scratchpad.nytstruct.fttb), and then wrote a script to create a [flat OPML subscription list](http://scripting.com/publicfolder/misc/nytFeedsOpmlList/flatlist.opml) with the data. I opened the outline in [Drummer](http://drummer.land/), and [organized it](http://scripting.com/publicfolder/misc/nytFeedsOpmlList/nytFeeds.opml) the way they have it organized on their site. I then opened the outline in my feed reader app and copied a few of the feeds from the NYT list, pasted them into the list for the test app, and it worked. :smile:

Now we also have a current, complete and well-organized [list](http://scripting.com/publicfolder/misc/nytFeedsOpmlList/nytFeeds.opml) of the NYT feeds. It should prove useful in the work to come.
