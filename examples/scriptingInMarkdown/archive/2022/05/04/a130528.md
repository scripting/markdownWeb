The Supreme Court protects the rights of Americans. They’re not a religious body, they are not chartered to adjudicate religious questions, because there is no religion attached to our government. That part is in [the text](https://constitution.congress.gov/constitution/amendment-1/) of the Constitution, for our textualist friends.
