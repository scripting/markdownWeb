![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2021/05/30/nancy.png)It was always possible to edit subscription lists in [Drummer](http://drummer.land/), its native format is [OPML](http://opml.org/) which is the same format used for subscription lists for [RSS](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html) applications, but a feature was added and a [howto written](http://editsubscriptionlists.opml.org/) that makes it easier. You can start with a list exported from your feed reader, or write one from scratch to import into your reader.
