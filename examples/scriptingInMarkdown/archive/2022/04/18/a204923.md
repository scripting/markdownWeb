![](http://images.scripting.com/archiveScriptingCom/2009/05/06/loverss.gif)We're going to need curated lists of feeds, like people do curated lists of Twitter users. If we're going to make the most of the coming reboot of [RSS](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html), we should be prepared to help people get the immediate benefit without doing a lot of upfront work. Having wonderfully thoughtful collections of feeds, based on real expertise, would be a great welcome wagon for new users. I can help with the presentation.
