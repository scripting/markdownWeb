There's a new trend in journalism where editors [turn away from Twitter](https://twitter.com/jayrosen_nyu/status/1516093318974824449), thinking of it as a waste of time. Another theory is that this is where the news happens and turning away from Twitter would be like a journalist of the 80s turning off their phone.
