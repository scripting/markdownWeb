I had a small package delivered today via Fedex containing a SIM that I was going to use in my phone. But the package was opened, and there was nothing inside. This seems to be happening a lot these days. Hackers get jobs at Fedex or UPS and grab stuff as it's in transit. PS: Fedex [apologizes](http://scripting.com/images/2022/04/09/fedexApologizesForAnyInconvenience.png) for any inconvenience when they should be apologizing for completely failing at the job they were hired to do.
