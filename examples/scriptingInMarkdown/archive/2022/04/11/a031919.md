"In my movie" is a mode of living that's detached from reality, in memory. We're all doing it all the time. Each of us is the star in our own movie, the center of existence, when in fact we're just one of [7.9 billion humans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_population) on an [insignificant planet](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wupToqz1e2g) orbiting a boring star, in a galaxy that's one of a [two trillion galaxies](https://bigthink.com/starts-with-a-bang/how-many-galaxies/) in the known universe. Each of us are, in fact, the star of nothing that matters.
