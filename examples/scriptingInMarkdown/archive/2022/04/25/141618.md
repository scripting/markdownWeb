It's two-way

1.  It's great if your app supports outbound RSS.
    
2.  Even better to support inbound RSS, esp if your software acts as an aggregator or distributor.
    

[Substack](https://substack.com/) is a great example of an app I wish supported [inbound RSS](https://www.google.com/search?q=%22inbound+RSS%22).
