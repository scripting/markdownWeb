Because the weather has been delightful here the last few days, I'm afraid I got no work done this week. I basically gave up today and spent time in the sun, reading, listening to music, went for a walk, did some shopping, took a long nap in the afternoon. I guess I was overdue for some downtime. It is a wonderful time of year, winter truly is over. We will still get some coldish weather, no doubt, but the glory of [Catskills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catskill_Mountains) [summer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fuz5TKzaJoE) is coming soon. It's the _best_ place to be in the summer.
