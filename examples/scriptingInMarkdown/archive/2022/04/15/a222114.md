I love all the Beatles songs, but get the most, these days, out of John Lennon's songs. I learned that Lennon only partially completed his songs, leaving a lot of the work to [George Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Martin). Keep that in mind when you listen to [I Am The Walrus](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t1Jm5epJr10) (my favorite Beatles song) and [Strawberry Fields](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HtUH9z_Oey8), as examples. The orchestral bits are pure Martin.
