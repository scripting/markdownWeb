![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2022/04/15/theEggman.png)Like many fans of the Grateful Dead I thought Bertha was about a fan in the Dead office named Bertha, [but](https://www.dead.net/features/greatest-stories-ever-told/greatest-stories-ever-told-bertha) it's the other way around, the fan was given the name of the song. On the other hand, the Beatles song, [Martha My Dear](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RXawa90YU2s) _is_ [about](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martha_My_Dear) Paul McCartney's Old English Sheepdog [Martha](https://www.google.com/search?q=martha+mccartney). Speaking of McCartney, I learned recently that he co-wrote the fantastic Elvis Costello song [Veronica](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g-rF4COOd9c). It's definitely got the McCartney feel to it, another spin perhaps on [Eleanor Rigby](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HuS5NuXRb5Y), which is _not_ a good song to Peloton to, btw, imho.
