It's so funny -- not really -- that Scott Love is gone, and one of our many slogans was this: Boca or Bucha, which meant that we'd either succeed in overthrowing IBM in the personal computer industry, headquartered in [Boca Raton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boca_Raton,_Florida), or we'd be on the red-eye to [Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bucharest) to begin our exile.

Why [Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bucharest)? It was where my [father](http://daytona.scripting.com/?q=leon%20winer&collection=scriptingnews) was born, and at the time, in the mid-80s, was known to be a hellish place, run by a totalitarian dictator, [Nicolae Ceaușescu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolae_Ceau%C8%99escu), a monster.

And why would we [travel](https://www.kayak.com/flights/SJC-BUH/2022-05-07/2022-05-14?sort=bestflight_a) on a [red-eye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red-eye_flight)? We were taking a lot of them to go to pointless meetings with [IBMers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_IBM#1975%E2%80%931992:_Information_revolution,_rise_of_software_and_PC_industries) in [Boca Raton](https://www.google.com/maps/place/IBM+Boca+Raton/@26.4024866,-80.1167947,15z/data=!4m2!3m1!1s0x0:0x7cb5808417a4b1a8?sa=X&ved=2ahUKEwi2m9ekkoL3AhWDjIkEHTbDDD8Q_BJ6BAhPEAU).

[Scott](http://daytona.scripting.com/?q=scott%20love&collection=scriptingnews) died in an accident late [last year](http://scripting.com/2021/11/08.html#a205642). Ceaușescu was overthrown and [executed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trial_and_execution_of_Nicolae_and_Elena_Ceau%C8%99escu) in 1989, a couple of years after Scott and I parted ways. IBM sold their PC business a few years later, they didn't need our help to be driven out. And now [Bucha](https://www.bbc.com/news/av/61025328) has become famous as the [site](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Bucha,+Kyiv+Oblast,+Ukraine,+08292/@50.54608,30.1845498,13z/data=!4m5!3m4!1s0x472b31bdf94125bb:0x51c08a27332520fa!8m2!3d50.553333!4d30.213517) of a great atrocity in the Russian-Ukrainian war.

Now we can write the epitaph of something that started as a joke between two young friends fighting for the cause of software that helps you think and now has become anything but funny. I have no idea what this means, or if it means anything at all. But I wish Scott were here to share this story with.

BTW, you know how it is when you start to come to terms with the death of a friend, and you think, right now Scott is going to send me an email saying it was all a big joke. He architected quite a few of those at Living Videotext back in the day. But it's not going to happen, is it?
