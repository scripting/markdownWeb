Once I got [FeedLand](https://docs.feedland.com/gettingstarted.md)'s implementation of [rssCloud](http://this.how/rssCloud/) working with WordPress and [Andrew Shell's server](https://github.com/rsscloud/rsscloud-server), I created a very [simple demo app](https://github.com/scripting/reallysimple/tree/main/demos/clouddemo) in Node that just does the rssCloud functionality, so anyone can test with our implementation, and see exactly what's going on.

It also provides a reference [implementation](https://github.com/scripting/reallysimple/blob/main/demos/clouddemo/clouddemo.js) for people just getting started with rssCloud.

At some point I need to rewrite the [walkthrough](http://walkthrough.rsscloud.co/). It's not very well done imho (I am the author).
