![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2020/03/19/beet.png)[Podcast](http://scripting.com/2022/12/09/toBradDeLongReWtfHappened.m4a): I was cc'd on a Masto [thread](https://mastodon.social/@mjgardner@mastodon.sdf.org/109484684585038468) re wtf happened to the blogosphere. I started writing, and couldn't keep up with my mind, so I opened up my iPhone and started talking about what happened and what we should and shouldn't do if we want to rebuild the war-torn landscape of blogging and feeds, that still exists. I addressed this to [Brad DeLong](https://braddelong.substack.com/p/old-style-blogging-should-be-new) one of the earliest bloggers, and a user, not a developer, as in "users and developers party together." We can still have it all, imho -- but we need users to work with the developers. 15 minutes.
