A product idea for a new AI bot.

A confessional.

You tell it what pisses you off and it tells you what you can do to work around the problem instead of being stopped by it.

For extra $ it will connect you with people with a similar grievance who want to [get it done](http://scripting.com/2016/05/04/1237.html).
