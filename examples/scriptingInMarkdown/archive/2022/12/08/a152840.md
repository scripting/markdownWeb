WordPress has a feed reader built-in and a plugin that displays feed items in the right margin of a blog's home page. It uses the \[No Title\] approach. Please if it doesn't have a title, just leave it blank. Here's a [website](https://odd.blog/2022/11/06/how-to-add-your-blog-to-mastodon/) that illustrates, and a [screen shot](http://scripting.com/images/2022/12/08/wordpressnotitle.png).
