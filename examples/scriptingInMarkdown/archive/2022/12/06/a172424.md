![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2022/12/06/jimiHendrix.png)The most interesting episode so far in the [500 songs](https://500songs.com/) series is the one about [Hey Joe](https://500songs.com/podcast/episode-147-hey-joe-by-the-jimi-hendrix-experience/) as performed by [Jimi Hendrix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimi_Hendrix). I'm not listening to them in any particular order. I felt kind of bad about it, but I got over it. One idea he talks about was there was an idea of white drugs and black drugs. I can vouch for that. I was blown away a few years ago to find out _everyone_ smokes weed. I thought it was just a white boomer thing. Everyone does it. The sound of eyes opening. I asked Alexa to play me some Hendrix music. First she played Hey Joe which I'd been bingeing on. Then came his [opening](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4TgbWOy6Buk) at Woodstock. And then [All Along the Watchtower](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TLV4_xaYynY). It was really emotional for me. It's already been a heavy duty emotional day. Let's go deeper! After that he asks [Are You Experienced?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HfZHGJxv3qQ) Hey for such a young man, he [died](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimi_Hendrix#Death,_post-mortem,_and_burial) at 27, he was very self-aware and surroundings-aware.
