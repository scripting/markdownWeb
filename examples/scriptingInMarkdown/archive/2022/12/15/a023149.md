![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2021/09/07/mrMeeseeks.png)A note to [FeedLand](https://docs.feedland.com/gettingstarted.md) users. There's a new feature where you can associate an email address with your account. Choose _Connect with email_ in the [Tools menu](http://scripting.com/images/2022/12/15/toolsMenu.png). This could serve as a backup in an emergency if Twitter goes down. It's important to create that connection as soon as possible, _before_ an outage.
