I noticed that [Twitter promotes](https://twitter.com/TwitterDev/status/1546615344780914688) a [Python toolkit](https://www.tweepy.org/) for API support. I have a [package](https://www.npmjs.com/package/davetwitter) for Node.js, and it does a lot of extra stuff, like merging Twitter threads into a single tweet. I also have [Twitter support](https://github.com/scripting/docServer/blob/main/docs/pages/twitter.md#) in the scripting language in [Drummer](http://docserver.scripting.com/drummer/scripting.opml) and that's even easier.
