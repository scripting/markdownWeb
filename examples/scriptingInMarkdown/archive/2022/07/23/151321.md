I hate [Hawley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Hawley) but he's onto [something](https://www.npr.org/2021/11/11/1054615028/is-masculinity-under-attack-sen-hawley-wants-to-defend-the-men-of-america).

The Dems probably unintentionally do things to push male voters away. He, and the Repubs are milking that for all it's worth.

You can argue the righteousness of it, but it's bad politics to alienate a whole gender. Bad for winning elections, assuming that's what the Dems want to do.

Also by making him [famous](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?q=hawley&geo=US) you're helping him, even if you say nasty shit about him.

Ask [George Lakoff](https://twitter.com/GeorgeLakoff). People don't care whether you [like him](https://www.axios.com/2021/11/07/josh-hawley-masculinity-porn-video-games) or [not](https://www.rollingstone.com/politics/politics-news/josh-hawley-masculinity-obsession-1254776/), but when you push him, you're creating a bigger space in their minds labeled X where X is the name of the asshole you're writing about.

I'm doing it right now, I know it -- can't think of a way of avoiding it and still make the point.

I bet the Dems could pick up 10% more male voters just by doing a little marketing saying the Dems care.
