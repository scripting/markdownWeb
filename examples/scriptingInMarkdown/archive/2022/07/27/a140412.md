Yesterday on my linkblog I included a link to the NYT obituary for [Tony Dow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Dow), who played the older brother on [Leave It To Beaver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leave_It_to_Beaver), a [show](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jtm1XTVqPz0) people of my generation grew up with. This morning I got an email from [Steve Garfield](https://twitter.com/stevegarfield), a longtime reader of this blog with a note that the NY Times had [retracted](http://scripting.com/images/2022/07/27/editorsNote.png) the obit, because Mr Dow is still alive. I always try to check these things out but until now thought the NYT was authoritative.
