An unsolved problem as far as I know -- how to tell that two different URLs are actually the same feed? I know the [Atom rel="self"](https://stackoverflow.com/questions/48138258/how-do-you-add-atomlink-with-rel-self-to-an-rss-document) element is an attempt to solve that problem, and it is fairly widely supported because the [default feed validator](http://feedvalidator.sourceforge.net/), written by one of the Atom guys kind of [requires](https://stackoverflow.com/questions/48138258/how-do-you-add-atomlink-with-rel-self-to-an-rss-document) it (if you look closely it doesn't, but people miss that).
