![My Model Y thinks I'm a 94.  ](https://imgs.scripting.com/2022/07/06/94.png)

I got a 94 from my car, presumably out of 100.

I lost points for "unsafe following," something I abhor, but I guess I must _occasionally_ actually do, lesson learned, I'll try to do better.

But I contest the hit for "hard braking." I know [exactly when that I happened](http://scripting.com/2022/05/14.html#a142227). I had driven to [Phoenicia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoenicia,_New_York) to pick up a [pizza](https://www.yelp.com/biz/brios-pizzeria-and-restaurant-phoenicia) at [Brio's](http://brios.net/) and on the drive home, I had to hit the brakes, hard -- to avoid killing a [wild turkey](https://www.google.com/search?q=wild+turkey+on+the+road) who was slowly making its way across the highway. The pizza went flying, along with a fair amount of sauce and cheese, soiling the carpet in front of the passenger seat on my almost new Model Y, which is scary for a new car, for fear it'll [attract rodents](http://scripting.com/2020/08/30/152537.html?title=howToFightMiceInCars).

But I digress. I hit the brakes to save the turkey. And now I learn because of that fukcing turkey, no [Full Self Driving beta](https://www.teslarati.com/tesla-fsd-beta-1-million-drivers/) for Uncle Davey. Oy. Fuckin turkeys, they'll get you every time.
