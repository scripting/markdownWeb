The problem with the [Supreme Court decision](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dobbs_v._Jackson_Women%27s_Health_Organization) is that while the Constitution doesn’t specifically name abortion as a right, in order to implement a ban on abortion a state will have to violate all kinds of other rights that _are_ specifically mentioned in the US Constitution and probably the state constitution as well.
