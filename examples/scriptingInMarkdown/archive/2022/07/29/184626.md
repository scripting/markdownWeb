I'm always getting on about how to do a great bug report, I thought I should show you a perfect one.

_A small UI thing I noticed was I clicked the X in the News Box to dismiss the box and then opened the settings again and the "Show news items in a small box at the top of the screen." was already checked when I expected it to be unchecked. Clicking the Ok button returned the News Box._

It has all the required elements.

*   What they did.
    
*   What they expected to happen.
    
*   What actually happened.
    

It doesn't have to be a formal process, or particularly long. Just the facts, as [Jack Webb never said](https://www.cheatsheet.com/entertainment/dragnet-jack-webbs-joe-friday-never-actually-said-just-facts-maam-comedian.html/). The facts the developer needs to find and fix the problem.

I also like the part up front where they say "A small UI thing..."
