[An RSS-related question](https://twitter.com/davewiner/status/1547962785492979713). Suppose I have a subscription to the NY Times (I do). And I'm using a traditional feed reader to browse their top stories. A [mailbox](https://twitter.com/davewiner/status/1547962792585506817/photo/1)\-style reader. Here's a [link](http://feeder.scripting.com/?template=mailbox&feedurl=https%3A%2F%2Frss.nytimes.com%2Fservices%2Fxml%2Frss%2Fnyt%2FHomePage.xml) to just that. Why can't I have the full text of the article in the right panel of the reader? Remember, I'm a paid-up subscriber, so I'm not trying to pirate their content, I'd just like to read it along with the other pubs I care about without a lot of ads and other stuff. I suppose I'd have to pay more for it?
