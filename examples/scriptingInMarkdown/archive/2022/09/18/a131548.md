[September 18, 2002](http://scripting.com/2002/09.html#When:10:25:22AM). It was low-key, I was fully expecting the roof would cave in and rioters would break down my door. I announced [here](http://scripting.com/2002/09.html#When:10:25:22AM) on Scripting News that the final RSS 2.0 spec was out. But! -- no one had a problem with it. I guess we were ready to go to the next stage, with a [clear way forward](http://scripting.com/2002/09.html). And [RSS](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html) would go on to [eat the world](https://a16z.com/2011/08/20/why-software-is-eating-the-world/), to steal a phrase from a well-known tech pioneer. More on this later. And more software and interesting ideas in the days to come. :boom:
