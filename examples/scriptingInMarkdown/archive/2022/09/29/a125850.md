[Last night's baseball](https://www.nj.com/sports/2022/09/eduardo-escobar-powers-mets-to-walk-off-win-as-braves-fall-out-of-1st-place-tie.html?fbclid=IwAR3k6EbUbRRzjD9l0f_3gb6EaeEf27TB4LkZy_ZBmXiys1bHioaYOItSbeI) was pretty intense. The Mets and Braves were playing at the same time in different cities, tied for first place in the NL East, as both games went into extra innings. I was sure the Mets were going to lose because they kept doing the exact wrong thing. But the hero of September 2022 for the Mets, a man who is not known for heroics, the third baseman [Escobar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eduardo_Escobar), came to the plate in the 10th inning and hit the ball into left field, and the shortstop, Lindor, who was on second base, came around third, as the ball was coming to the plate, but the throw was off and the shortstop scored and there was [pandemonium](http://scripting.com/images/2022/09/29/escobar.png) everywhere. And meanwhile in the other game, the Braves were losing, so as they say it was a win-wn. But I'm not sure I can stand the stress of the post-season that's starting in a week, but actually feels as if is already underway.
