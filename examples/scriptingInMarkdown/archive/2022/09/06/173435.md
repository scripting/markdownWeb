![Mom, dad and their perfect American kids.](https://imgs.scripting.com/2022/09/06/theIdealAmericanFamilyOfThe20thCentury.png)

When I was young, this was considered the ideal [20th Century](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/20th_century) life.

My mother had this in her head, that if we were better children, or whatever, that her life would be like this.

Occasionally I would tell her that she was no [June Cleaver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/June_Cleaver) herself.

We were a post-war refugee family trying to live up to what was [expected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_race) of us.

What a crock of shit that was.
