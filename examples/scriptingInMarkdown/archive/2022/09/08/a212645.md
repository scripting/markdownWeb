[People aren’t wearing masks](https://twitter.com/davewiner/status/1567766704385581057), even at the pharmacy, the person who gave me my booster said she didn’t need one because she just got over her second bout with Covid. What? Unreal. Then it came to me, this is normal. It’s why even when the temperature in California is over 100 in lots of places, we're still pumping carbon into the atmosphere. Pakistan is drowning, and [Lake Powell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Powell#21st_century_drought) is drying up. Business as usual. What was abnormal was the brief period when many made sacrifices to fight Covid. Now we just shrug it off, I guess?
