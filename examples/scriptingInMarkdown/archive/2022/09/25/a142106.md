![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2022/09/25/winjberg.png)I got a comment from a reader, a journalist -- asking why images of writers from [Puck](https://puck.news/) are in the right margin of a [few](http://scripting.com/2022/09/24.html#a132801) [recent](http://scripting.com/2022/09/23.html#a172129) Scripting News pieces. At first they thought my site had been hacked. No, I put them there. [Julia Ioffe](http://scripting.com/2022/09/22/154250.html?title=hopeAllIsWell#a154254) got me started. I love head shots esp ones that stop at the chin. These were especially beautifully drawn head shots, in the spirit of the ones for that [Dutch news org](https://decorrespondent.nl/) that hyped everyone into thinking they had figured out how to make news work in North America. Anyway I often put images in the right margins, it's an artistic thing, they set a mood and often they have nothing to do with the content they're adjacent to. They sometimes make pieces wrap better, to avoid egregious [widows or orphans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Widows_and_orphans).
