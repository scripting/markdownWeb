Okay here's a problem. Axios has a feed. Here's the [url](https://api.axios.com/feed/). When I view it in the browser it shows up fine. Even [view source](view-source:https://api.axios.com/feed/). If I bring it to the [W3C feed validator](https://validator.w3.org/feed/check.cgi?url=https%3A%2F%2Fapi.axios.com%2Ffeed%2F), it can read it and says it's a valid RSS feed. But when I try to open it in my feed reader I get a 403 Forbidden. I assume they're only allowing some services to read their feed? I've never seen this behavior before. Anyone [have an idea](https://twitter.com/davewiner/status/1575124429490855941) what's going on? (Update: In this [thread](https://twitter.com/ScottHansonDE/status/1575156930779152385) Scott Hanson has the answer. They serve through CloudFlare, and they require a [CAPTCHA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CAPTCHA) for requests coming from Digital Ocean, and my servers are there. With that info we were able to add a route-around, so now we're receiving Axios news in our feed system.)
