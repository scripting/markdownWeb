[Twitter tip from Andy Piper](https://dev.to/twitterdev/getting-to-the-canonical-url-for-a-tweet-85e). If all you have is the ID of a tweet, how to get its url? If you look at a Twitter url, there's a screenname in there. So how do you find the name of the person who posted tweet whose id is 1574769537244958720. Turns out you don't have to. Make up a name like _mamamia,_ and form the url like [this](https://twitter.com/mamamia/status/1574769537244958720). It'll find the tweet for you and redirect to the actual users screen name. Nice little hack.
