It's nice to hear that as [Font Awesome](https://fontawesome.com/) nears the release of version 6, apparently they have fulfilled the promise of [not breaking users](http://scripting.com/2020/02/16.html#a180941) of previous versions. It's nice when a developer empathizes with their users, even when there isn't an serious competition. I [wrote](https://twitter.com/davewiner/status/1486059557407313921) to [Dave Gandy](https://twitter.com/davegandy/status/1486056856510513154), the CEO, the best upgrade instructions are: "Heh the best upgrade guide is 'do nothing, it already works.'”
