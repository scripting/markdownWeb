![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2022/01/29/strawberryField.png)Today's songs: [Strawberry Fields Forever](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HtUH9z_Oey8) and [Penny Lane](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S-rB0pHI9fU). Both were about places in Liverpool. McCartney wrote Penny Lane, the [street](https://www.google.com/maps/@53.385517,-2.922201,3a,75y,218.78h,90t/data=!3m7!1e1!3m5!1suxfEgVSsvPtN86HT8-23eg!2e0!6shttps:%2F%2Fstreetviewpixels-pa.googleapis.com%2Fv1%2Fthumbnail%3Fpanoid%3DuxfEgVSsvPtN86HT8-23eg%26cb_client%3Dmaps_sv.tactile.gps%26w%3D203%26h%3D100%26yaw%3D205.606%26pitch%3D0%26thumbfov%3D100!7i16384!8i8192) Lennon grew up on. Strawberry Field is also a [real place](https://www.google.com/maps/@53.380399,-2.8838764,3a,75y,137.11h,69.2t/data=!3m6!1e1!3m4!1sdvEAm-XFTRmkmqi-ZRC25g!2e0!7i16384!8i8192). Lennon wrote the song while he was away from the group in Spain, acting in a [movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/How_I_Won_the_War) and pretty lost. The two songs were released together on a [double-a-side single](https://www.beatlesstory.com/blog/2016/11/24/song-facts-strawberry-fields-forever-penny-lane/).
