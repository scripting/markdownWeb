Back in the 80s at Living Videotext, I imagine us having a conversation about which of us would be first to die. That said, it wouldn't surprise me if [Scott Love](http://daytona.scripting.com/?q=scott%20love&collection=scriptingnews) wasn't the first, I didn't stay in touch with most of the people who worked there. But Scott was certainly the first of those who I did. I wrote about [his passing](http://scripting.com/2021/11/08.html) in November, but I didn't have any pictures of him from Living Videotext. My brother sent me a couple, so here they are.
