I changed the [header graphic](http://scripting.com/images/2022/01/12/goldenSpike.png) on Scripting News to an artist's rendering of the completion of the [Transcontinental Railroad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_transcontinental_railroad) at [Promontory Summit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promontory,_Utah), Utah on May 10, 1869 and the ceremonial driving of the [Golden Spike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_spike). I always loved the imagery of this event. A cooperative venture, one team building from east to the west, and the other from west to east, meeting at a middle point, and thereby creating one great thing. Working together. Built during the Civil War.
