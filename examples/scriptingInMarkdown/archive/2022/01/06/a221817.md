I like the [Microsoft Mouse](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B088MGT2WP/ref=ppx_yo_dt_b_search_asin_title?ie=UTF8&th=1) with the scroll wheel, having adopted it in the last month. But it has a cheap feel, and mouse pointer freezes a lot. If I'm going to use a scroll wheel mouse eight hours a day every day, I am willing to buy a [better product](https://www.amazon.com/Scroll-Wheel-Mouse-Mac/s?k=Scroll+Wheel+Mouse+for+Mac).
