![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2021/11/21/tesla.png)I was driving the [Tesla](http://daytona.scripting.com/?q=tesla&collection=scriptingnews) on a mountain road yesterday, when I came upon a family of deer in the middle of the road. They took no notice so I stopped and just watched, until a car came in the other direction and they scattered as it got close. I have a theory on why this happened, but don't want to spoil the puzzle for you.
