Reading this [piece](https://every.to/superorganizers/the-fall-of-roam) how Roam disappointed [Dan Shipper](http://danshipper.com/) illlustrates the need for a standard format for hierarchic notes, so that lots of tools can develop. We'll never have one system that serves all note-taking purposes. We must make evolution not require starting over.
