I like the idea of a [block protocol](https://www.joelonsoftware.com/2022/01/27/making-the-web-better-with-blocks/) that makes compatible writing tools possible, [my product is interop](http://scripting.com/2021/12/16/153331.html?title=myProductIsInterop), but I use straight JavaScript, no toolkits, frameworks, no TypeScript. I feel strongly that packages that want to tie things together should be written in straight JS, that's why I chose that as my development platform many years ago. Actually I'd prefer if we stuck to the JS we were using in 2013. :boom:
