I fixed a bug. There's an [encoding mismatch](https://github.com/scripting/opmlPackage/issues/6) between text coming from Twitter through its API, which is encoded in UTF-8, and text in OPML files which is encoded in ISO-8859-1. In outlines that only contain tweets the fix is simple.
