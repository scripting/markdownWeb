[McCartney began writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Here,_There_and_Everywhere#Inspiration_and_background) Here, There and Everywhere at Lennon's house in Weybridge while waiting for Lennon to wake up. He recalled: "I sat out by the pool on one of the sun chairs with my guitar and started strumming in E. And soon had a few chords, and I think by the time he'd woken up, I had pretty much written [the song](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xdcSFVXd3MU), so we took it indoors and finished it up."
