Update on the [iPhone 13 Max](http://daytona.scripting.com/?q=iPhone%2013&collection=scriptingnews). It did not arrive yesterday. For a few hours last night the UPS report went back to saying there was an emergency that supposedly prevented the delivery. But this morning it's back on the truck. I [posted](https://twitter.com/davewiner/status/1491938203296751616) about it on Twitter after yesterday's no-show, the fifth such day, and got a response from Apple support. I gave them the order number via DM. They wanted lots of details but what did I need to say beyond "I paid the money, I want the phone, or refund the money." For a company of Apple's size this shouldn't be hard unless it's a common occurrence.
