I highly recommend reading the [Starting Up](http://docs.feedland.org/firstThings.opml) doc. It shows how to find feeds to subscribe to, read the news, and find other users. FeedLand is all about people, feeds and news. It's not like any other feed reader that you've seen -- in fact it's very useful in conjunction _with_ feed readers, thanks to the OPML subscription list standard.
