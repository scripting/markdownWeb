Having listened to and loved the most recent episode of [500 Songs](https://500songs.com/) about [the Turtles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Turtles) [and](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZxQDsM3Wvh8) [their](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JeAtre3Bxg8) [songs](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9ZEURntrQOg), my friend said now you have to start with the first. I looked all over the web and I couldn't find a list. So I looked into their [RSS feed](https://500songs.com/feed/podcast/). It's huge but they're all there, all the episdes. How am I going to parse this? Then I remembered I have it in my database. So I wrote a little [query](http://scripting.com/images/2022/10/02/query.png) to get me the _enclosureUrl_ of all items from that feed, and [it worked](http://scripting.com/publicfolder/misc/500SongsEnclosureUrls.html). I love it when this happens.
