FeedLand can also generate feeds, not just read them. For example, when I _like_ an item in a feed I'm reading in FeedLand, it's automatically added to a [public feed](http://xmlviewer.scripting.com/?url=http://data.feedland.org/likes/davewiner.xml) of the items I've liked. Every user gets one of these feeds. I've subscribed to the feed in [Feedly](http://feedly.com), since it's RSS, it [works](http://scripting.com/images/2022/10/27/feedlyScreen.png). Of course you can also subscribe to it [in FeedLand](http://feedland.org/?feedurl=http%3A%2F%2Fdata.feedland.org%2Flikes%2Fdavewiner.xml).
