In [FeedLand](https://docs.feedland.com/gettingstarted.md) there are three ways to view a feed and a menu to switch between them. To try this out, go to your Feed List (choose _My feed list_ in the first menu) and click on the title of one of the feeds. This takes you to its _Feed Info_ page, one of the three views.

**Feed Info page**

1\. Here's a link to the [Feed Info page](http://feedland.org/?feedurl=https%3A%2F%2Ffeeds.arstechnica.com%2Farstechnica%2Findex) for Ars Technica. This page has basic info about the feed, when it was added and who added it. It says when it was last checked, last updated (a new item appeared in the feed), how many items we've retrieved from the feed, how long it took to read the feed (an indication of how healthy its server is) and most important, who is subscribed to the feed. When you click on a user's name, you're taken to their feed list ([example](http://feedland.org/?username=gwthompson)), where you may find other feeds to subscribe to. This is what we mean about FeedLand being made of people (and feeds and news too, of course).

If you look in the menu bar, you'll [see](http://scripting.com/images/2022/10/27/viewMenuScreen.png) there's a _View_ menu. It gives you a way to switch between the three views. For the next step choose _View as mailbox._

**Mailbox view**

2\. The [Mailbox view](http://feedland.org/?mailbox=https%3A%2F%2Ffeeds.arstechnica.com%2Farstechnica%2Findex) presents a feed as if it were a collection of email, with the titles and descriptions in the left column and the full text of each item in the right column. You'll find this familiar if you use most other feed readers, it's the most common way of presenting feed content. I [patterned](http://essaysfromexodus.scripting.com/priorArtDesignMethod) my mailbox reader after the one in [NetNewsWire](https://netnewswire.com/), designed by a former colleague, [Brent Simmons](https://www.google.com/search?q=site%3Ascripting.com+Brent+Simmons).

**River view**

3\. To see the [River view](http://feedland.org/?river=https%3A%2F%2Ffeeds.arstechnica.com%2Farstechnica%2Findex), choose _View as river_ from the View menu. In this view you see the feed contents presented as a [river of news](http://scripting.com/2014/06/02/whatIsARiverOfNewsAggregator.html) -- a format I've used in all my feed products going back to [My.UserLand](http://my.userland.com/) in 1999. The River view is different because it presents just the items from one feed, not a set of feeds. The full text of the item is not displayed by default, but you can see it by clicking on the down arrow in the lower right corner of each item, if it has text that is hidden.
