![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2022/10/17/sticky.png)Right now my favorite song in creation is [Can't You Hear Me Knocking](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gz5mI6tqm_Q). It's got everything, but most important, a driving bass and drums and a steady beat. It's always in my Peloton playlist, every time -- it's when I drive the bike fastest and steadiest, with a huge grin on my face. And it's two songs in one. First it's the hit as conceived by [Jagger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mick_Jagger) and [Richards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keith_Richards). But then it just goes into a jam, not part of the song, but they loved it so much they kept it. It's got a Santana sound. I never seem to get tired of it. The lead on the second half is [Mick Taylor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mick_Taylor) not Keef.
