I added a new feature to \[\[FeedLand\]\] today. There's now a white-on-orange XML icon in the upper right corner of each news product. [Screen shot](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/1686843/194099839-d458927b-8b0b-46d8-af83-f27c00f81764.png) and [example](http://nbariver.com/). The icon links to the OPML subscription list for the feeds on the page. This came from a [question](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/issues/239#issuecomment-1268027417) on the thread, basically asking "Where are the feeds." I love those kinds of questions, they help direct the development of a product. My perspective is so muddled, I've been living with this product for nine months already. I can't see what it looks like to someone seeing it for the first time.
