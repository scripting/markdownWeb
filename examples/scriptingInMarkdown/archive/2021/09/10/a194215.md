![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2020/04/10/tree.png)[This page](http://scripting.com/2001/09/11.html), 20 years ago tomorrow. I was living on [Manzanita Way](https://www.google.com/maps/place/425+Manzanita+Way,+Woodside,+CA+94062/@37.4190408,-122.2473884,17z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m5!3m4!1s0x808fa438d2e58d87:0x6c0dbc392cc6bf79!8m2!3d37.4190408!4d-122.2451997) in Woodside, CA. Woke up early as I always did in those days. If you [scroll to the bottom](http://scripting.com/images/2021/09/10/scriptingNews911.png), the day started out normally. A link to a friend's blog hosting service, a Wired story about Hollywood, a new [book](https://www.amazon.com/Breaking-Windows-Fumbled-Microsoft-Hardcover/dp/B010CKSFT0) about Microsoft, then at 6:15AM, we get the news of the first plane. Then a reader finds a web cam on the Empire State Building pointing at the [WTC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Trade_Center_\(1973%E2%80%932001\)). Then a second plane hits. And from there the [story develops](http://scripting.com/images/nyTimesHomeBombing7AM2.gif). There was a personal side to it, my father taught at [Pace University](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Pace+University/@40.7112562,-74.0073645,17z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m5!3m4!1s0x89c258fe30a7b447:0x8a0a55f0a5fd1f1f!8m2!3d40.7112562!4d-74.0051758), which is across from City Hall, very near ground zero. He was okay, but had to [walk](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Pace+University,+One+Pace+Plaza,+New+York,+NY+10038/29-31+170th+Street,+Queens,+NY/@40.7336612,-73.9707111,12z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m14!4m13!1m5!1m1!1s0x89c258fe30a7b447:0x8a0a55f0a5fd1f1f!2m2!1d-74.0051758!2d40.7112562!1m5!1m1!1s0x89c28a8078136589:0xcdf1c8651455269a!2m2!1d-73.7949205!2d40.7696434!3e2), like many thousands of others, from lower Manhattan to Queens. My mother saw the whole thing from a rooftop in Brooklyn.
