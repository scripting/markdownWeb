I saw a [snarky thing](http://scripting.com/images/2021/09/05/snarkySlogan.png) in a friend's Facebook feed that said women don't need men telling them what to do with their bodies. A recent [Gallup poll](https://news.gallup.com/poll/244709/pro-choice-pro-life-2018-demographic-tables.aspx) shows that 43 percent of American women are pro-life and 45 percent of men are pro-choice. I thnk everyone should be pro-choice, but it's wrong to blame men for the current situation.
