[Omar Little](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omar_Little) is the most likeable character on \[\[The Wire\]\]. [Marlo Stanfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marlo_Stanfield) is the coldest. For some reason I think of Marlo as [Matt Mullenweg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Mullenweg). I know that's weird. Matt is a very nice fellow. But I guess the parallel is that I am [Avon Barksdale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avon_Barksdale) to his Marlo Stanfield? The Wire is good, but it's [Season 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wire_\(season_1\)) that's great. The remaining four seasons kind of coast a little after the spectacular creative success of the first.
