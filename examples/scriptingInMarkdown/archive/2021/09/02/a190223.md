[Roe v Wade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roe_v._Wade) was decided in 1973, 48 years ago. I was 18 that year. I was on the edge of awareness for what it meant. I don't remember a time when abortion was not legal, not in a way that could have affected me. We're all going to learn about it now.
