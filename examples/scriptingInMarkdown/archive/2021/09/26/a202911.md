The basic problem (imho) is that we all have been raised to believe that _getting ahead_ is the thing to do with our lives. It's deeply ingrained over many generations. We're still doing that, even though the three big crises we're going to be dealing with for the rest of our lives. demand a different impulse -- _working together._
