I'm [re-watching](http://scripting.com/2021/09/12.html#a171236) \[\[The Wire\]\], after the [death](https://www.nytimes.com/2021/09/06/arts/michael-k-williams-dead.html) of [Michael K Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_K._Williams), the actor who played [Omar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omar_Little). He was right about [type-casting](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RpST8KE3vSI). His one great role was Omar. Once you get a part that good, and play it so well, and everyone else is that good, you just don't get to do that twice.
