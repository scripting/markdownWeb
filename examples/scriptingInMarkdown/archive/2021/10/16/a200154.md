![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/08/30/butHerEmails.png)I have a thing. If they're [forecasting rain](http://scripting.com/images/2021/10/16/weather.png), showers or even a thunderstorm, and it's not raining when I want to go out for a ride, I go out anyway. Worst that can happen is I get soaked, I reason. And I usually make it home in time. And often as soon as I walk in the door it starts to pour. That's exactly what happened today. I'm feeling very oxygenated and strong, writing this post in my bike clothes, and outside it's pouring cats and dogs. Amazing.
