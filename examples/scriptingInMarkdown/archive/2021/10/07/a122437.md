It's a fitting moment to flip the switch, because \[\[Drummer\]\] will open the doors for a new _fluid_ style of blogging, based on outlining. To create a post, you click the [big plus icon](http://scripting.com/images/2021/10/07/bigplusicon.png) and start typing. When you're ready to publish, choose a menu command. No more slogging your way through Wordpress menus or a static site generator. It all happens in a second or two. And making a change to your blog is as easy as making the change in your outline, and publishing -- again -- just one command and a couple of seconds. Welcome to the world where \[\[tools for thought\]\] meets publishing. Blogging at the speed of thought. Where nothing gets in your way.
