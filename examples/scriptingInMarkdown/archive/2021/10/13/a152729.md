To people who wanted to use a custom domain for their [Drummer](http://drummer.land/) blogs, we now have an [answer](https://github.com/scripting/drummerSupport/issues/67). There's a new head-level attribute for your blog.opml file that tells the CMS where the links should point. It works on my [test blog](http://clueless.lucky.wtf/) for the clueless newbie.
