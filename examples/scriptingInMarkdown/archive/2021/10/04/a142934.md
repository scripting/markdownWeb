![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/08/06/hippieBus.png)I had this thought when I was driving into San Francisco in 1979 -- I bet I know a bunch of people here, but I have no way to find them. At some point between then and now, that vision was achieved. Add that to the [list](http://scripting.com/2021/09/17/153104.html?title=facebookIsAtLeastEightThings) of things Facebook is other than Mark Z. I think it's time for people with a sense of the foundations Facebook is built on speak up and say what it represents. The press, given the benefit of the doubt, has no clue.
