I recommend [The Power of the Dog](https://www.google.com/search?q=The+Power+of+the+Dog). It's a little bit of [There WIll Be Blood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/There_Will_Be_Blood) and a little [Brokeback Mountain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brokeback_Mountain). Beautifully presented, great acting, took a while to realize that the hardass cowboy was Benedict Cumberbatch. I have nominated it as the first movie of this year's [NakedJen Film Festival](https://www.google.com/search?q=NakedJen+Film+Festival). [88](https://www.metacritic.com/movie/the-power-of-the-dog) on Metacritic.
