If you want to understand the mindset of VC, see the movie [Don't Look Up](http://daytona.scripting.com/?q=Don%27t%20Look%20Up&collection=scriptingnews), which is becoming an extremely useful landmark. Not all VCs are so slimy, I know a few who are highly principled. But they are all in the ROI business, and the only return that matters is $ to investors.
