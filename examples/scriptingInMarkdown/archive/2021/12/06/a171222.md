What got me going on this was a [tweet](https://twitter.com/pierce/status/1467874084608520195) from the editor of Protocol, about the pain in switching from different outliners, and a [subsequent tweet](https://twitter.com/pierce/status/1467876578814271489) in the thread where he said the answer was a [long way off](http://scripting.com/images/2021/12/06/longway.png). Emphatically it is not a long way off. This isn't that new. We were doing outliners 40 years ago. These problems have been solved. Let's work together, users and developers.
