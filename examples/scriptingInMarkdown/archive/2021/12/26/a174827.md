![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2021/12/26/theGreat.png)If you have [Hulu](https://www.hulu.com/series/the-great-238db0d4-c476-47ed-9bee-d326fd302f7d) and are looking for a great [binge](http://bingeworthy.io/tv/?id=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.metacritic.com%2Ftv%2Fthe-great), I highly recommend [The Great](https://www.metacritic.com/tv/the-great). It's a farce and it's really funny and sexy, super-cute and sad, in the same way that [Don't Look Up](http://scripting.com/2021/12/24.html#a143342) is sad. Human nature is all [fucked up](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BdPmNM0IF7Y&t=36s). That seems to be a recurring theme. Heh. But it is so much fun, kind of like the way [Beetlejuice](https://www.metacritic.com/movie/beetlejuice) is fun -- and there are two full seasons so there's a lot to binge. _Huzzah!_
