I did the initial Scripting News collection on Daytona with the [current archive](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/tree/master/blog/opml), started in May 2017. It's in OPML, so it was ready to be imported into the database as-is.

I have now converted the previous months, August 2016 through May 2017, from a JSON-based format to OPML, and published the result [in the GitHub repo](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/tree/master/blog/opmlOtherSources/2016-2017), and also imported this archive into Daytona.

Along the way I found another eight months of blogging that's in the same format. I plan to convert those as well, and flow the result into Daytona.

I plan to march back in time, looking for OPML versions of the blog, or OPMLizable data. The goal is to see how much of the 27 years of Scripting News I can get into Daytona.

I also published the [Node app](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/tree/master/blog/opmlOtherSources/2016-2017/chatlogToOpml) that converts chatLog.json text to OPML.

PS: I recorded a [video demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GWbsrjRrDOw) of the tool I used to edit my blog in this period. The software is 1999.io.
