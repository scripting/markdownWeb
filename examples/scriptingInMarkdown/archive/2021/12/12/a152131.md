![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2021/12/12/zen.png)Already [Daytona](http://docserver.scripting.com/drummer/search.opml) has improved my writing. I think I could be writing a book now, a memoir, a [Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenence](https://www.amazon.com/Zen-Art-Motorcycle-Maintenance-Inquiry/dp/0060589469), my [Island Book](http://daytona.scripting.com/?q=island%20book&collection=scriptingnews). A new dimension has opened up. Until now writing has been more or less Of The Moment. As Google has punished me for not supporting their various [hijacks](http://this.how/googleAndHttp/) of the web, the writing has become more ephemeral, kind of like Twitter -- once written -- hard to find again. With Daytona all of a sudden my [memex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memex) has memory. My writing is upgraded a billion percent.
