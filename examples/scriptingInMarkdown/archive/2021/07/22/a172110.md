Falling in love with \[\[Google Reader\]\] and then giving up on open \[\[RSS\]\]-based news after it was cancelled is like falling in love with [The Monkees](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZcXpKiY2MXE), and giving up on music when their show was [cancelled](https://www.quora.com/Why-was-The-Monkees-show-cancelled). Long term the open wholesome tech ecosystem is where the breakthroughs come from. When the users learn to accept that tech companies just harvest these developments, don't manage them or develop them, or care about them, then progress will accelerate, but not until then. The power is strong, but you have to use it.
