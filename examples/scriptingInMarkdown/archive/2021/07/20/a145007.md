![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2021/07/20/johnDillermand.png)In \[\[Denmark\]\] they have a children's show about a [man with a huge penis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Dillermand) that he uses to help people. At first it's a little shocking to this American, but why shouldn't we think of a \[\[penis\]\] as something friendly and useful. I was brought up to think of my penis as something shameful, dirty, to hide, to apologize for. I can't imagine how I got those ideas, but they were and are there in my infant brain. Eventually we learn to have fun with our penises. Hopefully. Why not start as a kid? Thanks to \[\[John Oliver\]\] for his incredible little [tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A51mJjFyG_w).
