Something they don’t teach us in school in [my country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) is that when we [pledge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pledge_of_Allegiance) allegiance to the [flag](http://scripting.com/images/2021/07/26/flag.png) of the United States of America, we are really pledging allegiance to each other. Because that’s all there is, the people. No dear leader, no state church, just us.
