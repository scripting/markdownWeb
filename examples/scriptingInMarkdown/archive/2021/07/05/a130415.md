It's really weird [how they do](https://web.archive.org/web/20190815000000*/https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html) the [RSS 2.0 spec](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html). They're sampling it every day, as many as 17 times a day. [Screen shot](http://scripting.com/images/2021/07/05/rss2archiveorgsampling.png). It's a static page for a format that's been frozen since 2002. A few samplings a year would be sufficient. As with the OPML spec, their archive starts in 2016, but the doc has been there at since 2003.
