![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2021/05/02/taylorOnTucker.png)I'm really confused about what's up with [archive.org](https://archive.org/). As I [reported](http://scripting.com/2021/06/29.html#a131345) on Tuesday, they clearly aren't archiving this blog in any meaningful way. I write this blog with history in mind. I put things here and on the [linkblog](http://scripting.com/?tab=links) because I want to be sure historians in the future will find at least one link to this stuff. If archive.org isn't backing it up, that's a big problem. And it raises an even bigger question -- what else aren't they archiving? And how do they decide what to archive and not to archive? Do they run the JavaScript on a page to get a rendering? If not, there's a huge amount of our knowledge that isn't being recorded. Are they trying to be neutral or do their friends and people who donate get preferential treatment?
