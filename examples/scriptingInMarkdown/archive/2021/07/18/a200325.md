[Memeorandum](https://www.memeorandum.com/) has been a regular [stop](http://scripting.com/images/2021/07/18/memeScreenShot.png) for me for news for decades, is now mostly links to news that's behind paywalls. I couldn’t possibly pay for most of them, too many subs to manage, and I don’t read enough of any individual pub to justify subscribing. I do subscribe to NYT and Washington Post. But I don’t get enough value from either, honestly. This continues to be a [lousy situation](http://scripting.com/2020/08/06/133617.html?title=moneyForNews). The pubs only talk about how news is broken from their point of view, but the readers point of view matters as much, if not more.
