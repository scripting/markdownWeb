Both forms of revenue, advertising and subscriptions, [for news](https://www.nytimes.com/2021/07/27/business/media/buzzfeed-vox-media-vice-group-nine-ipo.html), are unworkable. Take down the paywalls, let me pay per article, then when I'm spending $100 a month, then and only then, offer me a sub, as a way of saving money.
