![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/06/03/tinaTheBearNurseFromBojackHorseman.png)[Where I live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulster_County,_New_York) some people call city folk "cityots" -- not sure I'm spelling it right. It's supposed to be an [amalgam](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/amalgam) of "city" and "idiot." Weird thing, almost everyone who lives here is from the city. It's the [biggest city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City) in the country, it can't help but exert its influence.
