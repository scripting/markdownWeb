Twitter CEO Jack Dorsey's [thread](https://twitter.com/jack/status/1349510771928010753) from the Wednesday about the "significant ramifications" of banning Trump from Twitter. In the thread he talks about Twitter funding an [open decentralized standard](https://twitter.com/jack/status/1349510780043988992) for social networks called Blue Sky. I remember when they announced this in 2019, I think I offered to help then, and I offered again today. Creating an open distributed system at Twitter's scale is an ambitious undertaking.
