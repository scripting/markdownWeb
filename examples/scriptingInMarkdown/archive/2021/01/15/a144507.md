There are, as far as I know, two places to get a Covid vaccine in Ulster County, the [county itself](https://covid19.ulstercountyny.gov/vaccine-resource-center/) in Kingston and the [Village Apothecary](http://drnealsmoller.com/vax) in Woodstock. Neither are making appointments now, but you can register to be notified when they are. If you know of others, send me a link and I'll try to post it here or in the [linkblog](http://scripting.com/?tab=links).
