I [lost](http://scripting.com/2021/01/29.html#a215557) one of my AirPods. I can replace it for $89, or try something new, which is what I'll do. I'll let you know. In the meantime, I solved the problem with the crappy UI for the LG screen I bought [last month](http://scripting.com/2020/12/14/173714.html?title=myNewTv). I bought the top-of-the-line [Roku device](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B08Q34DRLZ/ref=ppx_yo_dt_b_asin_title_o01_s00?ie=UTF8&psc=1), plugged it in, and it gives the same great picture, and lets me control everything from the much more [elegant Roku remote](http://scripting.com/2019/04/17/144031.html). I loaded on all the apps I need, including Fubo, which is not available for the LG, and after a few hours I realized why the Roku remote is so superior. There are so few buttons, and they're big enough, that I don't have to look at the remote to operate the UI. This is important for a TV where you use it in a dark room, ideally. Roku is the best. People say it's dated, I think it's really that it works, and that's what I want. ;-)
