![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2021/01/24/bush.png)[Rosental Alves](https://journalism.utexas.edu/faculty/rosental-alves) wrote to tell me about a [CDC site](https://vsafe.cdc.gov/) that tracks people who have been vaccinated. "V-safe is a smartphone-based tool that checks in on you after your COVID-19 vaccination. Your participation helps keep COVID-19 vaccines safe — for you and for everyone." This answers the question I asked [yesterday](http://scripting.com/2021/01/23.html#a205638), why aren't they staying in touch with vaccinated people. There is a system in place, apparently, I just didn't know about it. Now I do, and so do you. :boom:
