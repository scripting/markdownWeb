Embarrassing mistake. Yesterday, in a [longish piece](http://scripting.com/2021/04/10/142746.html?title=shouldNetflixHaveASchedule) about bingewatching, I gave Kazuo Ishiguro credit for writing the novel Howard’s End. He actually wrote [Remains of the Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Remains_of_the_Day). There is a connection. Both were made into movies starring [Emma Thompson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Thompson) and [Anthony Hopkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Hopkins). I corrected the piece, but here's a [screen shot](http://scripting.com/images/2021/04/11/theError.png) of the error. BTW, interestingly, I received an [email from Netflix](http://scripting.com/images/2021/04/11/netflixemail.png) last night suggesting I watch Howard's End. Is it a coincidence, or does their algorithm read my blog? That would be amazing if it did. (I'm sure it doesn't but it's an incredible source about my interests.)
