![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/09/01/mrFrog.png)Follow-up to yesterday's [braintrust query](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/issues/206). Apparently [WordPress still has an API](https://developer.wordpress.org/rest-api/), there's a [Node package](https://github.com/WP-API/node-wpapi) to interface to it, and even better, they still have the [XML-RPC interface](https://codex.wordpress.org/XML-RPC_Support) and support the [Metaweblog API](https://codex.wordpress.org/XML-RPC_MetaWeblog_API). So it should be possible to have WordPress support in [Drummer](http://drummer.land/) and use the outliner to create and edit posts on a WordPress website, and who knows where that goes. What's especially cool about it is that in [December 2019](http://scripting.com/2019/12/) I released a new [reference implementation](http://xmlrpc.com/) of XML-RPC in the form of a client and server for Node, in JavaScript of course, and a client that runs in the browser. So now there's a good reason to add [XML-RPC](http://xmlrpc.com/) support to Drummer, which I totally want to do.
