I've been reading [Pale Blue Dot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pale_Blue_Dot_\(book\)) by [Carl Sagan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Sagan). It's a great book, but already dated. The missions he talks about for the future have already happened. Our understanding of [Saturn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturn), for example, has greatly increased since 1994. Now that [Cassini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cassini%E2%80%93Huygens) has visited, so much [more](https://stfc.ukri.org/news-events-and-publications/features/10-things-we-ve-learned-from-cassini-huygens/) is known. Sagan of course is not alive to do this for us. But it seems someone should. Next up I'm going to re-read [Cosmos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmos:_A_Personal_Voyage).
