![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2020/08/09/gary.png)I [propose](https://twitter.com/davewiner/status/1387115927188221952) that the Democrats publish what they think would be fair districting for every state, including ones that Republicans control. That way we can see how they deviate from fairness. Give journalism a means to compare. Help people understand gerrymandering. Another possibility -- maybe someone like Nate Silver could put together fair redistricting maps for all the states. It'd be an interesting idea to approach it as a non-partisan thing, using the best information and data tools we have. ([Update](https://twitter.com/jasonSaldo/status/1387121440789606401): They [did](https://projects.fivethirtyeight.com/redistricting-maps/) it last time around.)
