If you use [Little Outliner](http://littleoutliner.com/) you should [download your files](http://this.how/littleoutliner/versions.opml#1586885920000) regularly, as a backup. It's saved my ass more than once. The _Download My Files_ command is in the Tools menu. You quickly get a zip archive of all your files.
