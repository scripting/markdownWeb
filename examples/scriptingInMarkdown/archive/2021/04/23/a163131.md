The other day I [reported](http://scripting.com/2021/04/18.html#a140711) that I had the project I'm working on "stabilized" after major brain surgery. In software it's always a good idea to be prepared for the worst possible outcome. In this case, I forgot that I hadn't deployed the new version to a server, and when I did that, nothing worked. [Edge cases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_case). It is once again "stabilized." But the list of new stuff to do is growing, not shrinking. And who knows what broke in the last round of changes.
