The nicest thing about the Apple credit card is how incredibly easy it is to pay your bill.

1.  Pick up iPhone.
    
2.  Log in (with my thumb).
    
3.  Open wallet app.
    
4.  Click the pay button.
    
5.  Thumb thing again.
    

That's it.
