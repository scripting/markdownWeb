The pandemic is very much not over. [Italy is locking down over Easter](https://mobile.reuters.com/article/amp/idUSKBN2B41FM). We all know what's happening. And again, the US is largely going exactly the wrong way. We're mistaking feelings for reality. It feels like spring is here, and it's time to emerge from hibernation. It's the wrong impulse. If you use your mind, you can see the feelings are wrong. Adults do the right thing anyway. Look at Italy. Smart.
