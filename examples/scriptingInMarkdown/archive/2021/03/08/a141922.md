![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/08/02/moscowMitchButton.png)It took a while for me to get how common [gaslighting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaslighting) is. If someone says, in a discussion, that you're upset, they're gaslighting. It's wrong because: 1. They have no insight into how you feel. 2. They're changing the subject. Gaslighting is insidious.
