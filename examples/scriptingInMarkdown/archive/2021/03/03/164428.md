I think some of my friends might enjoy a little thing I put together a while back called [thesaurus.land](http://thesaurus.land/).

It's a word [navigator](http://thesaurus.land/?word=navigator). Enter a word in the text box, click the button and you get a list of synonyms. You can then double-click on any of them to see its synonyms, on and on as long as you like.

I love words, maybe you do too.

PS: Here's a [video demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3ySSzmaoRzo) I did in 2016.
