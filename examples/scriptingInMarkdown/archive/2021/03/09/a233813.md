The new [header graphic](http://scripting.com/images/2021/03/09/bronxScienceEntrance.png) on my [blog](http://scripting.com/2021/03/09.html) is a picture of the entrance to the [Bronx High School of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronx_High_School_of_Science), where I went many years ago. The legend was [the](https://thesciencesurvey.com/photography/2020/04/14/the-bronx-science-mural/) [mural](http://scripting.com/images/2021/03/09/mural.png), showing great scientists of the past, was what we got instead of a pool. We also noticed that the test tube in [Marie Curie's](https://www.haydenplanetarium.org/tyson/photos/2002-10-city-of-stars/bronx-high-school-science.php) hand was actually floating in air. Maybe after all these years they've fixed that bug. :smile:
