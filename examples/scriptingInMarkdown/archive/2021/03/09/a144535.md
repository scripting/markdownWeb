[Audible](https://www.audible.com/) has a collection of books that members can listen to for free. So I downloaded a bunch of classics I read in school to see if I wanted to read any of them again. 1984. Brave New World. And [The Adventures of Huckleberry Finn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adventures_of_Huckleberry_Finn). I started reading it, and the racial slur that got [Donald McNeil](https://donaldgmcneiljr1954.medium.com/nytimes-peru-n-word-part-four-what-happened-in-peru-2a641a9b5e83) fired is all through the first chapter. A dozen times probably. It's really hard to listen to, so I stopped reading it.
