![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2020/04/19/coronavirus.png)[One year ago today](http://scripting.com/2020/03/05.html) I recorded a [podcast](http://scripting.com/2020/03/05/aDeadlyVirusIstakingOverTheWorldAndTheUnitedStatesDoesntHaveAGovernment.m4a) about how the world was about to change and how Americans weren't ready for it. And for a few hours I replaced the contents of the home page of my blog with a simple statement in very [big letters](http://scripting.com/urgent/2020/03/05.html): "A deadly virus is taking over the world and the United States doesn't have a government." A year later, and the virus did take over the world, and thankfully the US now has a government, yet it is very damaged by the traitorous behavior of the ousted president. But we're doing better now.
