Highly recommend [The Making of Sgt Pepper](https://productionadvice.co.uk/the-making-of-sgt-pepper/). Interviews with George Martin, the three surviving Beatles (in the early 90s). Many others. This album was the turning point from the Beatles being a touring phenom, to pioneering a new creative direction for pop music. It's inspiring, we need a similar turn in software. (It's also available [on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/dave.winer.12/posts/1478913278982739) if that works better.)
