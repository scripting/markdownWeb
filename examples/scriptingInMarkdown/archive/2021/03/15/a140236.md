I found a way to save trees. I bought a pile of [dish towels](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B00JCG1V42/ref=ppx_yo_dt_b_search_asin_title?ie=UTF8&psc=1) and put them on the kitchen counter where the paper towels and paper napkins used to be. When they get dirty, I throw them in the washing machine with the clothes. And re-stack them. And they're nicer to use than napkins.
