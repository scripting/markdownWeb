![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2020/04/16/mrWhiteBreakingBad.png)There's a [long thread](https://twitter.com/benedictevans/status/1373215191031222274) on Twitter today where people from Microsoft are reminiscing about the days when they put Netscape down, and then got in trouble with the DoJ. I remember those times differently, and very well. Microsoft was in no danger. They could have let Netscape grow, it never would have hurt them. For all of Netscape's bluster about [device drivers](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=14610341), they had a really bad codebase, and a chaotic team that while blessed with a product that the web was growing around, had a long way to go before they could compete with Microsoft.
