[Enoch Choi](https://twitter.com/enochchoi) has appeared [on this blog](https://www.google.com/search?q=site%3Ascripting.com+%22enoch+choi%22) many times, he's a heroic [doctor](http://scripting.com/2020/03/24.html#a165220), natural-born [blogger](http://scripting.com/manila/bloggerCon/2004/10/19.html), he works to save lives, always, and now, is caring for Covid patients in the [Santa Clara Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Clara_Valley) in California.

He's also an [advocate](https://actionnetwork.org/forms/call-for-a-community-centered-response-to-violence-against-aapi-communities) for [Asian-Americans](https://twitter.com/search?q=%23StopAsianHate&src=typed_query), and now his son Aidan, who I watched grow up on Facebook, is an [artist](https://achoi2022.wixsite.com/website), and has a couple of pieces about an [assault](https://www.bbc.com/news/world-asia-china-56453806) on a Chinese-American grandmother, who fought back and sent her attacker to the hospital, in San Francisco last week.
