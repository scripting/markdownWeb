![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/09/02/teamworkWins.png)I think all apps should be scriptable, whether they run on the desktop or in the cloud. See the use-case in this [tweet](https://twitter.com/davewiner/status/1367477718976061441). Eli wants a feature in Zoom. He has to wait for them to give it to him. If the app were scriptable, any developer could write it.
