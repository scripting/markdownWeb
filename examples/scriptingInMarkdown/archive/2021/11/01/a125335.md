Mark my words -- outliners are destined to be in the middle of everything. That‘s why I’ve invested so much in [OPML](http://opml.org/), the open format designed for sharing outlines. It's the native file format of [Drummer](http://drummer.land/). We don't need to have another giant Facebook in the middle of everything controlling everyone. Much better if it's distributed. That's what's made possible by open formats.
