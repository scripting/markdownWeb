There's a [dichotomy](http://scripting.com/2013/09/10/twoWaysOfLookingAtAnOutline) in outliners -- 1. The outliner as a document, like a spreadsheet or word processing file. A file format. 2. The outliner as a file system, a container for documents, a way of organizing them. Drummer, used as a blogging tool is a solid #2.
