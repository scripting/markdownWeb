I added [The Great](http://bingeworthy.io/tv/?id=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.metacritic.com%2Ftv%2Fthe-great) as a favorite on my [BingeWorthy profile](http://my.bingeworthy.io/davewiner/). I'm well into Season 2. It's a pseudo-history dramedy in the spirit of [Succession](http://bingeworthy.io/tv/?id=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.metacritic.com%2Ftv%2Fsuccession) or [The Death of Stalin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Death_of_Stalin). It's about a [Russian empress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine_the_Great) who overthrows her [husband](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_III_of_Russia) the emperor. As they say, hilarity ensues. :smile:
