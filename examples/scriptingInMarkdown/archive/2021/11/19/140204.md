![Here she is, my new blue Tesla Model 3.](https://imgs.scripting.com/2021/11/19/teslaModel3.png)

Drives great.

A bit hard to get in and out of.

Very comfortable to drive.

Quiet.

Wish it had Apple CarPlay.

It's charging now in the garage.

It's as powerful a car as I've ever driven, like my 2007 BMW 535i.

Most of the Tesla people at the [Mt Kisco store](https://www.tesla.com/findus/location/service/mtkisco) were assholes, but they finally gave me someone who talked like a New Yorker. Most of the Tesla people have the same smarmy attitude that they're doing you some kind of favor to sell you a hugely expensive car, like at an Apple store. The guy we finally got talked to us like human beings who love computers and are excited to own a new kind of car.

Thanks to Peter Politi for taking the trip with me.
