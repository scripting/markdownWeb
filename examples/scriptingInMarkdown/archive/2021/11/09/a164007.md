I put about twenty [Tom Petty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Petty) songs on my iPhone and have been [listening](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1lWJXDG2i0A) to [them](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xqmFxgEGKH0) on and off. The thing about Tom Petty is his songs are the kind that you sing in your head all the time days and days awake or in dreams. To have twenty Petty tunes co-resident means you're living the Tom Petty lifestyle, [waiting](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uMyCa35_mOg) for it to pass, but kind of hoping it doesn't. It's kind of like [Rickrolling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rickrolling) but with really good music.
