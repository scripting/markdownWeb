![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2021/11/09/2001.png)I'm spending a couple of days [baking](https://github.com/scripting/drummerRFC/issues/7) GitHub into [Drummer](http://drummer.land/) via scripting. We used to call this a "scriptable app" on the Mac, when apps were [Filemaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FileMaker) and [Quark Xpress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/QuarkXPress), the [Finder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finder_\(software\)), stuff like that. Then when [Frontier](http://scripting.com/2021/01/04/140651.html?title=whatFrontierIsAbout) moved to the web, the apps were [Eudora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eudora_\(email_client\)), [Netscape](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netscape), [MSIE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_Explorer) and [MacHTTP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MacHTTP), then Google and [Manila](https://www.google.com/search?q=site%3Ascripting.com+manila&rlz=1C5CHFA_enUS743US747&oq=site%3Ascripting.com+manila&aqs=chrome..69i57j69i58.4100j0j4&sourceid=chrome&ie=UTF-8), then WordPress and Twitter. Along the way came new protocols -- [XML-RPC](http://xmlrpc.com/), [RSS](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html), [OPML](http://opml.org/). Drummer already has excellent scripting support for [Twitter](http://docserver.scripting.com/?verb=twitter.addGroupToList). I made this a priority to have one really good scriptable app when Drummer came out. Now I'm working on the second biggie. I wrote [yesterday](http://scripting.com/2021/11/08.html#a143815) that GitHub could be [MS-DOS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MS-DOS). What I meant is that it's basic low-cost storage for everyone with a GitHub account that's about as easy to work with as MS-DOS was. For early adopters, what we used to call power users. It's an amazing resource. And that made me think of MS-DOS because both are owned by Microsoft and storage is basically what MS-DOS did, and btw, MS-DOS is the one product they didn't use as a weapon when they were trying to usurp the web, so it has a nice taste to it, still. I have [started a thread](https://github.com/scripting/drummerRFC/issues/7), on GitHub of course, narrating what I've got working. Watch this space.
