There should be a limit on the number of times a team can foul one player. When a team exceeds the limit, the team being fouled gets to choose a player on the fouling team who is fouled out of the game. Do it again, lose another player. [Hack-A-Shaq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hack-a-Shaq) isn't good for the game. There's no reason a power dunker like [Giannis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giannis_Antetokounmpo) has to be a good foul shooter.
