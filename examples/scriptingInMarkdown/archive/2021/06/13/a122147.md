[GitHub](https://github.com/scripting?tab=repositories) is an incredible free to use resource, reliable, has an excellent [API](https://docs.github.com/en/rest/overview) and through [webhooks](https://docs.github.com/en/developers/webhooks-and-events/webhooks/about-webhooks) is even real time. I think it could be a strong basis for [interop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interoperability) between various outline generators and consumers.
