![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/09/10/drummer.png)This is important. In the conversations I've had recently with users and devs in the nascent \[\[Tools For Thought\]\] community, people talk in terms of importing and exporting outlines, but this emphatically is not good enough in 2021. We need a live connection. So when you hook your outline up to a rendering server, the connection persists. When you make a change, the worker app on the server has the changes and is ready to re-render, without you doing anything. I can't manually import a file every time there's a change. \[\[Web sockets\]\] are built into all browsers, it's simple and standardized tech. Let's use it. There's at least one public storage system, \[\[GitHub\]\], that supports real time notification. Again, let's use it. Instead of going back to the beginning each time a new generation comes along, let's start with the work and knowhow of previous generations. I'm here to help.
