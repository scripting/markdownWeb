When your “worth” rises, that’s not income. You don’t pay income tax until you sell the asset whose worth increased. [Journalism](https://www.propublica.org/article/the-secret-irs-files-trove-of-never-before-seen-records-reveal-how-the-wealthiest-avoid-income-tax) almost never explains this. That’s why if your net worth rises by say $1 billion, you might not pay any income tax.
