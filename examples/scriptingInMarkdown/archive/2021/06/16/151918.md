I noticed is that most of the new outliners are dark mode apps. White text on black background.

Until recently it had never occurred to me that my outliner should have a dark mode too, so I've been working on that for [Drummer](http://drummer.land/) in the last few days. Getting pretty close to having it working, then I have to go back and review everything to see if a bit of color here and there would be nice. I notice that other dark mode apps make use of color in an accenting way. I like that. You almost don't notice it until you look for it.

![Dark mode Drummer screen shot.](https://imgs.scripting.com/2021/06/16/drummerScreen2.png)

Let me just say \[\[CSS\]\] is the most awkward possible way to do this kind of programming. I yearn for \[\[QuickDraw\]\], the UI tech we used in the 80s on the Mac. Everything was straightforward and done in a real programming language as opposed to CSS.
