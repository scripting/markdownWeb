![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/06/11/pizza.png)The new [Instant Outlines](https://github.com/scripting/instantOutlines) toolkit. Over the last few days I've been tweeting and blogging like a fool about [why](https://github.com/scripting/instantOutlines#simple-use-case) importing and exporting outlines from one app to another isn't enough. We can do much better. In the background I was working on a [new toolkit](https://github.com/scripting/instantOutlines) for developers who want to be part of a network of outline producer and consumer apps based on \[\[OPML\]\] and \[\[web sockets\]\]. With [source code](https://github.com/scripting/instantOutlines/blob/main/demo/code.js) of course, and a [demo app](http://scripting.com/code/instantoutlinesrepo/demo/) to prove it works. All my outliners support the feature as well, it's the basis for sharing outlines. This is my vision for an open ecosystem based on outlining. It's a big part of what I [dreamt](http://scripting.com/dwiner/outlinersProgramming.html) of when I started UserLand in 1988.
