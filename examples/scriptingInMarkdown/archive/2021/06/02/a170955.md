If you [click this link](http://littleoutliner.com/?url=http://instantoutliner.com/pg), their outline will open in LO2. Seeing their outline open in my world is revealing to say the least. This is like joining two cultures. The Mac outlining culture with the "other" culture (to me) which derives from [Org-mode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Org-mode) (i am told) and wikis. I can see some interesting cross-fertilization happening.
