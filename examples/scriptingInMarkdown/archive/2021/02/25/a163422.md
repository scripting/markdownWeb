Twitter has a newsletter service. I signed up to see how it works. [You can subscribe](https://www.getrevue.co/profile/dave-winer/issues/weekly-newsletter-of-dave-winer-issue-1-79432), but there probably will only be this one issue. Functionally it's very similar to the others. You have to use their editor. I already have an editor that's customized for the way I work, so there's no way I'm using their editor. I already have [email distribution](http://scripting.com/email/) of my own. It's ridiculous. They must not understand how writers work. We find the tool that's right for us and it becomes part of our bodies. Too much world domination as a strategy in tech, as always. That's how we get situations like this.
