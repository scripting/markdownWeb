Imagine if the only sound the human voice could make was this: [oy](http://scripting.com/2021/02/17/oy.m4a). We'd develop a whole language around various intonations and combinations of oy. Give it a try. Sing [Jesus Christ, Superstar](https://www.youtube.com/watch?t=90&v=IvVr2uks0C8&feature=youtu.be), but replace all the words with combinations of oy. Try not to laugh.
