![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2021/05/03/diegoRivera.png)Yesterday I [wrote](http://scripting.com/2021/05/02.html#a213217) about the fan art accounts on Twitter, and what they've made possible. I now have my app running and downloading images from the initial 26 accounts. I've uploaded the [first batch of images](https://github.com/scripting/artDownloader/tree/main/data/images) to a [GitHub repository](https://github.com/scripting/artDownloader). I plan to update them as long as there's interest in this project. I've also included the [source code](https://github.com/scripting/artDownloader/blob/main/app/artdownloader.js), with [instructions](https://github.com/scripting/artDownloader#running-the-app), in case you want to run it yourself. I'm sure there will be problems that need fixing, if you spot anything, post an [issue](https://github.com/scripting/artDownloader/issues) in the repo for the app. I love this, it started as a warm-up project, but quickly got a life of its own.
