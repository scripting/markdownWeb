I was invited to [Newsgeist](https://twitter.com/newsgeist?lang=en) this year, and accepted.

It's a virtual conference, so no travel is involved.

The conference is split in two parts, Ignite and Discussion. Each set of sessions is split into four groups, for various time zones around the world.

The [official calendar](http://scripting.com/images/2021/05/18/newsgeistSchedule.png) is hard to figure out because they don't translate all the times into my home timezone which is Eastern Time in the US. So I did a little table with the times in my timezone. You can easily translate to yours, I hope.

**Ignite sessions**

APAC -- May 17, 5AM ET

LATAM -- May 18, 4PM ET

EMEA -- May 19, 11AM ET

North America -- May 20, 5PM ET

**Discussion sessions**

APAC -- June 14, 10:30AM ET

LATAM -- June 15, 9:30AM ET

EMEA -- June 16, 4:30AM ET

North America -- June 17, 11:30AM ET

The only hard ones to figure out were the APAC times, which are in [SGT](https://www.timeanddate.com/time/zones/sgt), which is 12 hours ahead of Eastern time.

If you see errors, let me know! ;-)
