[Art Show](http://artshow.scripting.com/) update: With over [10K works](https://github.com/scripting/artDownloader/tree/main/data/images) of art in the [repo](https://github.com/scripting/artDownloader), I needed to slim down the [art.json](https://github.com/scripting/artDownloader/blob/main/data/art.json) file that the [web app](http://artshow.scripting.com/) loads on startup. It was getting too big, and was only getting bigger. Now there's data for 1000 works of art in the file. After a day or two you might want to reload the page in case there's a new random selection in art.json.
