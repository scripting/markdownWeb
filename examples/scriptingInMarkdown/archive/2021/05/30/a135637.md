![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2021/05/30/spikeLeeKnicksFan.png)I listened to a [New Yorker interview with Spike Lee](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/spike-lee-on-the-knicks-resurgence/id1050430296?i=1000523437231) about this year's Knicks. There was a surprise, he didn't watch the NBA bubble last year. I didn't either. It was too sad. The NBA now, finally, is back. And having the Knicks do so well this year, and it being the immediate post-pandemic season, it's like both happened at the same time and one was the cause and the other was the effect, though it's impossible to say which is which. I would like to hear [Spike Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spike_Lee) ramble about the Knicks, for an hour or so, or really as long as he wants.
