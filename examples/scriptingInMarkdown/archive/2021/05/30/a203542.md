If the Knicks end up losing, the moment their fate was decided was when they gave Julius Randle the [Most Improved Player](https://www.si.com/nba/2021/05/26/julius-randle-son-surprises-him-most-improved-player-award) award. Just before the first game of the playoff series. Who decided that timing? Tempting fate. God'll always [get you for that](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kpSw5tREOxQ). If I were advising Randle, I'd urge him to throw the award into the [East River](https://www.tripsavvy.com/how-to-walk-across-the-williamsburg-bridge-nyc-4158670) before Game 5.
