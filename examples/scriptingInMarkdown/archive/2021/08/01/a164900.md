![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2021/08/01/purpleFlowers.png)I watched [The Color Purple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Color_Purple_\(film\)) on HBO last night. I'm looking for ways to learn what black people say about the black experience in America through history. It was a weird choice, because, even though the actors are black, it was a [Steven Spielberg movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Spielberg_filmography), like ET or Jaws. Always upbeat, even when a husband is raping his sister-in-law. The weirdest thing to have Indiana Jones type music playing while Oprah Winfrey is being beaten by a mob of white men. So bizarre. But I loved all the actors, esp of course Whoopi Goldberg, and there was at times a bit of suspension of disbelief, but the Spielbergishness of the movie was very awkard.
