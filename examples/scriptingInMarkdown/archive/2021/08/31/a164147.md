![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2021/08/31/mrMet.png)As a lifetime \[\[Mets\]\] fan, I'm glad the [players pointed out](https://www.nydailynews.com/sports/baseball/mets/ny-mets-celebrate-his-thumbs-down-fans-20210829-rjt54obqunbrjma3w33557cevm-story.html) that other fans are being assholes. I'm glad because I want to let the players know that I used to mow Mets' players lawns in the 60s. I went to games with my parents, uncle, grandfather, brother. We had a slogan "Shea Stadium Rules" which meant that even if we had our differences underneath it all we're still rooting for the Mets. So-called "fans" who dis the team when they lose really are Yankees fans and they should just go to the Bronx and shut the fuck up. The [Mets philosophy](https://www.google.com/search?q=site%3Ascripting.com+mets+philosophy) stands, the [Mets](http://scripting.com/2018/06/24/181734.html) are the Mets, win or lose or whatever.
