Over the past couple of days I binged season 1 of [The Chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Chair_\(2021_TV_series\)) on [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/title/81206259). I liked it. It's quick, only about three hours. It's a Sorkin-like show, like [The West Wing](http://bingeworthy.io/tv/?id=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.metacritic.com%2Ftv%2Fthe-west-wing) or [The Newsroom](http://bingeworthy.io/tv/?id=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.metacritic.com%2Ftv%2Fthe-newsroom), fast dialog. Fun and really pulls on the heartstrings. There's cute smart little girl, and a timely plot of a second-tier Ivy League school that reminded me that campuses can be interesting places to hang out. I gave it a 4 of 5 [on BingeWorthy](http://bingeworthy.io/tv/?id=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.metacritic.com%2Ftv%2Fthe-chair-2021).
