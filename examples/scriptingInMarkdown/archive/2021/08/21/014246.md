You know what Zuck should work on, getting a network together for people who are interviewed on TV, so the quality gets a huge upgrade, and make the UI dead simple. I can't believe none of the big tech companies see this opportunity. The quality is uniformly awful.

No one knows if people want to sit in virtual reality [conference rooms](https://www.nbcboston.com/lx/facebook-wants-us-to-put-on-a-vr-headset-to-go-to-work-not-everyone-is-happy/2471118/), but we know for sure that TV is built around a new way of discussion, and there's no prospect for this changing. So someone ought to capitalize on the vacuum.
