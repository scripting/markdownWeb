I keep beating [this](http://scripting.com/2021/08/24.html#a211637) [drum](http://scripting.com/2021/04/02/153208.html?title=aProposalForNewsletterTools), because it's important.

This morning [Thejesh posted](https://twitter.com/thej/status/1431230072103440389) a link to [Buttondown's API](https://api.buttondown.email/v1/schema). I like it -- it's really simple. But it could be even simpler! :smile:

First a summary of why this is important. I want to use my own editor to write a newsletter and publish it through a commercial newsletter service. I don't want to use their editor. Why? Because as a writer, I have the best editor for me. And I don't like being a copy-paste-bot. My writing has too many ways to get out to make it possible to do that for all of them. And I want someone to go first, to make it easy to publish without forcing me to be creative in their world, with their inadequate (for me) editor.

So to further the discussion -- here's how I would do the API .

1.  The user, through the app's user interface, enters the URL of an RSS feed. That interaction is of course authenticated.
    
2.  When a new item appears in the feed, publish it.
    
3.  For extra credit, accept a simple unauthenticed ping message with the username, which tells your server to check the feed. That way your server doesn't have to check as often.
    

Voila, super simple API.
