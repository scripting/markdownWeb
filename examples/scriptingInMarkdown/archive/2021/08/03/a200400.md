I went to a [farm produce stand](https://www.yelp.com/biz/migliorelli-farm-stand-phoenicia) on 28 in [Mt Tremper](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Migliorelli+Farms/@42.0329671,-74.2749173,15z/data=!4m5!3m4!1s0x0:0xdd7ed88ac0c706ec!8m2!3d42.0329439!4d-74.2749978) yesterday to get fresh local peaches, just now coming into season, and everyone was masked up. I think we're doing the wise thing in NY state. It's easy to put a mask on, much easier than being intubated and dying. The peaches are the best part of the year except for the apple harvest. On the other hand at the hardware store, I was the only one wearing a mask. So maybe it's not _all_ peaches yet.
