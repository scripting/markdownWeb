This [article](https://www.washingtonpost.com/climate-environment/2021/08/05/change-ocean-collapse-atlantic-meridional/), while shocking, doesn't explain how devastating it will be if the [Gulf Stream](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulf_Stream) disappears. The Gulf Stream is why [the UK](https://www.thesun.co.uk/news/2569640/what-is-the-gulf-stream-where-is-it-how-does-it-affect-uk-weather-and-why-does-it-cause-heavy-snow/#:~:text=How%20does%20the%20Gulf%20Stream,to%205%20%C2%B0C%20cooler.) has a temperate climate, even though it's at the same [latitude](https://earth.google.com/web/@46.01634833,-6.80705831,-3927.74817016a,9514760.87145209d,35y,0h,0t,0r) as [Hudson Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Bay) in Canada.
