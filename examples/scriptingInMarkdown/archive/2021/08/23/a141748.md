![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2021/08/07/rickAndMorty.png)I'd say [BingeWorthy](https://tv.bingeworthy.org/) didn't work. [Nice software](http://scripting.com/images/2021/08/23/bingeworthyScreen.png), but the goal was to keep an easy to find [public list](http://my.bingeworthy.io/davewiner/) of shows I recommend, and those that my friends recommend. A couple of weeks ago I discovered, entirely on my own, [Rick and Morty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rick_and_Morty). I loved it from the first scene in the first epsiode. I started telling everyone I know how great it is, and with few exceptions they said "old news Dave." They loved the show since it started in [2013](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Rick_and_Morty_episodes#Season_1_\(2013%E2%80%9314\)). Why did I struggle for years to find something great to watch when Rick and Morty was out there. I'm sure there are a dozen other delicious shows I don't know about, and it's only getting worse. What is the answer? It seems like a simple problem to solve, yet it evades an answer.
