There’s this great line at the end of an episode of [Law & Order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_%26_Order). Jack McCoy says the person who was convicted got a tough sentence. The DA, an old man, says when he gets out he’ll be 35. “I’d take it,” he said. When a young cute person mocks others’ pain, I think of this story.
