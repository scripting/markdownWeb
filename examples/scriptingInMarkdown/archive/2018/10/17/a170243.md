![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2018/10/17/toaster.png)In Battlestar Galactica, the humans call the [Cylons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cylon_\(Battlestar_Galactica\)) [toasters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toaster), to remind them they are machines not people. But we know they’re actors, and the actors are actual people, pretending to be machines that look human. It creates an interesting conflict that they explore from every angle.
