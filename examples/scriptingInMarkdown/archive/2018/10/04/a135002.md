![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2018/10/04/maniac.png)Some TV series are special, like dessert, like a rich layer cake with lots of sweet frosting and butter. You eat the slice slowly, savoring every bite, and even so it disappears too quickly. [Better Call Saul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Better_Call_Saul) is like that. Some episodes of [Game of Thrones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_of_Thrones). The entirety of [The Wire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wire). And lately, [Maniac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maniac_\(miniseries\)) on Netflix was savory in that sense. NakedJen is watching it now. Hope she loves it. I just saw a [note](https://twitter.com/DanDotLewis/status/1047845366681554944) from a friend on Twitter saying he had watched four episodes of Maniac and was giving up. I know the feeling. I went through that with [Castle Rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castle_Rock_\(TV_series\)). The first episodes were great but then I lost interest. I did manage to muddle through, because I wanted to know how it ended. Then there are shows that everyone loves that I can't even get started with. [Peaky Blinders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peaky_Blinders_\(TV_series\)), for example. Or [The Expanse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Expanse_\(TV_series\)). I cannot figure out wtf is going on in that one. I feel if I can just gain traction I'll love it. But I never have been able to.
