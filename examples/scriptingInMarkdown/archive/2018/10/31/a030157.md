Somehow [gab.com](https://gab.com/) got [knocked off](http://scripting.com/images/2018/10/31/gabHome.png) the web, by GoDaddy or Joyent or a combination thereof. [I wanted to know how](https://twitter.com/davewiner/status/1057791349708283904) this happened, and if people agree that Gab should go, where is the line? It can't be that Nazis use their service, because Nazis use everything. I think the ACLU and the EFF should be involved here, and try to come up with some rules for all parties, so we don't have to wing this every time.
