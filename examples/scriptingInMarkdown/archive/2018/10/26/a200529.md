![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/12/20/shrek.png) I read a post this morning by [Evan McMullen](https://twitter.com/davewiner/status/1055890611105087493), a conservative Republican saying "the next political tribe in America will care about everyone." I commented "sign me up." Then someone [said](https://twitter.com/bob2cleo/status/1055890152256618497) "That's the Democrats." And I thought "That's bullshit." You want to keep losing elections, keep demonizing white men. There's a lot of resentment building, the Repubs have figured out how to tap into it. On the other hand McMullen's theoretical party, by design, will care about everyone.
