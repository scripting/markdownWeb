Another possible [River5](https://github.com/scripting/river5) problem. For the last two days the NYT Daily podcast hasn't shown up on [Podcatch.com](http://podcatch.com/). I was willing to write it off as a one-time glitch yesterday, but when it happened again today, I'm starting to get concerned. Here's the [feedvalidator](http://www.feedvalidator.org/check.cgi?url=http%3A%2F%2Frss.art19.com%2Fthe-daily) link to the feed. As you can see it's saying it's not valid, but I also fed it through my own [feed debugger](https://github.com/scripting/nodeFeedDebug), which exactly mimicks what River5 does with feeds, and it seems okay with it. I'm starting a [thread](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/issues/62) for this.
