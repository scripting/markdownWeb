Writing and publishing threads on Twitter is a big deal. Twitter is adding the feature to the core product, but [pork.io](http://pork.io/) is more of an editor, it automates a bunch of things that Twitter makes you do by hand, such as wrapping tweets, numbering them, adding hash tags. It also creates an RSS feed of your threads, so they can be shared in other environments.
