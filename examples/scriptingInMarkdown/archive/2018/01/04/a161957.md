I still use [Frontier](http://scripting.com/2021/01/04/140651.html?title=whatFrontierIsAbout) for little [utility scripts](http://scripting.com/images/2018/01/04/removeRobots.png). Nothing else is as easy. And with the perspective of all these years, I realize we managed to create a structured Algol-like scripting language. It has all the right primitives, and makes the right tradeoffs to be able to do quick development, without worrying about much of the things you have to worry about in lower-level systems.
