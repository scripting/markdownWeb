As you may know I've been searching for a show to binge, settled on [Travelers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Travelers_\(TV_series\)), and I pretty much hate it, in the middle of season 2. It's taking the place of watching Law & Order reruns, a show I can space out while reading on my iPad or thinking about stuff. But in my search for something to involve my mind, I stumbled across Martin Scorcese's [documentary](https://www.rollingstone.com/music/news/amazon-details-martin-scorseses-grateful-dead-documentary-w461551) about the Grateful Dead. I got through the first episode, and _loved_ it. And it ended on such a high note. It isn't about creating a massive work to be remembered by, Jerry totally saw through that. Anything you leave behind is bullshit. Just have fun, and you're achieving life's purpose. Made me wish Jerry were running the world instead of the [orange blob](http://scripting.com/images/2018/01/02/theOrangeBlob.png) sitting in the Oval.
