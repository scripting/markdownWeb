Journalists [still think](https://twitter.com/unclegrambo/status/954010704490713088) they were/are bloggers. Reporters using blogging tools is not blogging. Bloggers are reporters' sources. [They say](https://www.newyorker.com/culture/cultural-comment/the-end-of-the-awl-and-the-vanishing-of-freedom-and-fun-from-the-internet) blogging is over because their professional CMSes caught up? They're in a [bubble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reality_distortion_field). A profession that should be _good_ at listening only actually listens to itself.
