![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2018/01/16/librarianActionFigure.png)Just realized, the reason [librarians](https://mcphee.com/products/librarian-action-figure) must love the web, and linking, is that you can provide a complete bit of complex information without being overwhelming. It's the same reason I like coding in an outliner. There's no cost for being verbose, just tuck the verbosity under a headline and leave it collapsed. Until the day you wonder wtf is going on here. You can hide little crumb trails for later discovery. Links work the same way.
