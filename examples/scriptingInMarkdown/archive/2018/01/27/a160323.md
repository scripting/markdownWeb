[Julia Evans](https://jvns.ca/), a tech blogger from Montreal, [posted](https://twitter.com/b0rk/status/957278161544335360) a list of tech blogs she follows, on GitHub in [OPML](https://gist.github.com/jvns/26dbe9b84694362d9fc6c6fccbd05048#file-tech-blogs-opml) and [Markdown](https://gist.github.com/jvns/26dbe9b84694362d9fc6c6fccbd05048). I love the way this is going. It's perfect. I haven't heard of _any_ of the blogs. I suspect that's what's happening. Lots of pockets of bloggers here and there, not connected together because of the huge roar or Twitter and Facebook. It's like setting up a bunch of music venues on the shore of a raging river.
