![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2018/01/03/kellyAnneBastard.png)This is how I knew.

I was watching MSNBC on Election Night. They had [Kellyanne Conway](https://21stcenturyfaketoys.tumblr.com/post/157898390903/00152) on. She was blaming someone, I forget who, for Trump's losing. Chuck Todd observed, after the interview, see the blame game is already starting.

We all let out a sigh of hopefulness. Only to have it shattered in an hour or two. But there was no doubt she was so sure they were going to lose she didn't wait until the results were in to start bashing whoever it is she was ready to bash.
