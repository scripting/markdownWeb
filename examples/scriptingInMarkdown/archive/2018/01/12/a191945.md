![](https://imgs.scripting.com/XMLRPCMAN.gif)[XML-RPC](http://xmlrpc.com/) started in 1998, which means it's about to be 20 years old. I think this is the first [post](http://scripting.com/davenet/1998/02/27/rpcOverHttpViaXml.html#5) about it. Not very specific. We were already working on it, but we hadn't yet hooked up with the people at Microsoft. From a quick scan it looks like the actual protocol we standardized on didn't come out publicly until June. Pretty sure we had something working internally at UserLand in March or April.
