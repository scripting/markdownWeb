![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2018/06/27/officer.png)I made a few small improvements to the modal image dialog on this blog. Now if the [anchor](http://pics.scripting.com/0129/0001.jpg "My first flat-screen monitor.") tag linking to the image has a title [attribute](http://pics.scripting.com/0050/0006.jpg "Dave Jacobs bought a hot new sports car, many years ago."), that is displayed in the lower left corner of the dialog. At first I thought it should be at the top, but that's not the role that [titles](http://scripting.com/static/412/Dscn1048.jpg "Lunch w Tim O'Reilly when he got a call from Jeff Bezos.") play in anchors. It's a parenthetical comment.
