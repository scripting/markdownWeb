If you want a reason to be optimistic, check out yesterday's [Daily podcast](https://www.nytimes.com/2018/06/29/podcasts/the-daily/supreme-court-labor-unions-janus.html?rref=collection/column/the-daily&action=click&contentCollection=podcasts&region=stream&module=stream_unit&version=latest&contentPlacement=1&pgtype=collection). A new Supreme Court decision said membership was no longer mandatory in government employee unions. This was thought to be a blow to unions. Turns out it may _revitalize_ them, as the membership, newly empowered, drives the unions, instead of being taken for granted. See how [#4 kicks in](http://scripting.com/2018/06/30/122337.html) to change #1. And you can be sure conservatives are taking note.
