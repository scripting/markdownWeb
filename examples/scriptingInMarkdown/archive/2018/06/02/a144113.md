My _this.how_ pages now have the date posted at the top of the page, instead of when they were last updated. The mod date is at the bottom of the page. [Example](http://this.how/standards/). I edited the default outline [template](http://fargo.io/code/pagepark/templates/opml/template.txt) for [PagePark](http://pagepark.io/) to do this, so if you're running PagePark and using it to view OPML files, you'll get this change too. You can override it by providing a custom template.
