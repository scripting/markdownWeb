When a president lies as much and as boldly as Trump does, not only does the press have to [call him on it](https://www.teenvogue.com/story/thigh-high-politics-donald-trump-lying-journalists-need-to-call-him-out), but new forms of journalism have to develop around this new norm, to report on why he's lying. We can have a robust discussion about that.
