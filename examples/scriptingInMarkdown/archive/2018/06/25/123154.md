When I travel I come back with ideas.

I should do it more often.

On the last [trip](http://scripting.com/2018/06/20.html), the question was, as it often is, what should we do for a bright future with publishing technology. I think that's been topic number one for me for at least 30 years, maybe my entire life, going back to the womb.

So here's what I came back with.

If I had my [druthers](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/druthers), I'd have a team of writers, bloggers, journalists to work with, and I'd do a lot of experimenting with news creation and reading ideas. Rapidly try new approaches until we hit on one(s) they all loved. Then we'd know where to go from there.

That's it.
