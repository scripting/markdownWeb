Hitler became [chancellor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adolf_Hitler%27s_rise_to_power#Chancellor_to_dictator) of Germany in 1933 at [44](http://scripting.com/images/2018/06/28/hitler.png). Trump became president of the United States in 2016 at 70. There are of course other differences. In order to have a global empire Hitler had to go to war, which he ultimately lost. Trump was elected president of a country that already dominated, economically and militarily, and had by far the largest military and nuclear weapons.
