What inspired the idea. In all the talk on MSNBC last night about the president [pardoning himself](https://www.npr.org/sections/thetwo-way/2018/06/04/616724965/giuliani-president-trump-has-constitutional-authority-to-pardon-himself) not once did anyone ask if there was a benefit to American people in the president being able to do so. Seems the journalism, if you can call it that, has lost the tune. The tune is this: A government of the people, by the people and for the people. Part of [Gettysburg Address](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gettysburg_Address).
