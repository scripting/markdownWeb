![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2018/06/05/hitchHikersGuide.png)20-minute [podcast](http://scripting.com/2018/06/05/lovingGithubUsers.m4a) on lessons learned from Microsoft's acquisition of GitHub. Show notes follow. The Battlestar Galactica quote: "All of this has happened before and all of this will happen again." [Douglas Adams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_Adams) wrote [The Restaurant at the End of the Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Restaurant_at_the_End_of_the_Universe). Microsoft's first CFO was [Frank Gaudette](http://community.seattletimes.nwsource.com/archive/?date=19930423&slug=1697384). Microsoft's 2017 sales [were](https://www.marketwatch.com/investing/stock/msft/financials) approx $90 billion. Bottom-line: Advice to MS, add a feature that allows users to redirect from their GitHub repos, and consider a "loyalty dividend" for pre-Microsoft GitHub users, and establish a new tradition in tech acquisitions, a windfall for users.
