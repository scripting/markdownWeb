I tried an experiment to see how Twitter displays thread structures.

I entered a [subset](http://scripting.com/images/2018/07/09/statesSubset.png) of the standard [states outline](http://this.how/states/states.opml). [United States](https://twitter.com/Twittergram/status/1016328508128288768) is at the root. It has one sub, [Far West](https://twitter.com/Twittergram/status/1016328530328805377). And from there I list the six states, and provide detail under [Nevada](https://twitter.com/Twittergram/status/1016328618899902464).

I'm wondering what the algorithm is. Because sometimes it shows nodes at multiple levels, and how they choose them isn't clear.
