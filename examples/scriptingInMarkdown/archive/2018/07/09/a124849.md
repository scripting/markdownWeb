For years I was really confused about what blogging is, after Twitter and Facebook. In May last year I decided I [wanted](http://scripting.com/2017/05/05/iWantMyOldBlogBack.html) my old blog back, the one I started in 1997. Now, a year later, I realize it really works. The 280 char limit in Twitter helps because I can cross-post shorter posts there. Here's a [screen shot](http://scripting.com/images/2018/07/09/blogScreenShot.png).
