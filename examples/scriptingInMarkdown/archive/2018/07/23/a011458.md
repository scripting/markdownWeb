One thing you can be sure of, when a lot of reporters get laid off, [you'll hear about it](https://mediagazer.com/180723/p10#a180723p10). It's not true of a lot of other things. Big news happens, no coverage. It's not just because we're losing reporters, the ones that we have don't integrate well with the rest of the world.
