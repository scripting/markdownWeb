![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2018/07/23/webIsDeadFreeGift.png)Apparently tomorrow is the day Google will start flagging sites that use HTTP, the [standard web protocol](http://this.how/googleAndHttp/), as "not secure." Curious to see how people react. BTW, [this link](https://www.cnet.com/news/chrome-warns-of-not-secure-sites-to-cut-web-surveillance-tampering-faq/) has auto-playing video. It may be "secure" but it's also obnoxious. This blog and all my other sites use HTTP. I don't see that changing. I expect this will make writing for the web more of a chore. That's life I guess. I don't want Google to be able to mold the web to its needs. I never signed on to being a [Google developer](http://scripting.com/2018/06/13/142326.html), and never would. Basic rule: Google is a guest on the web, as we all are, and guests don't make the rules.
