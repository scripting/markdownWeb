For my friends in journalism -- eventually Facebook, Google and Twitter will have to push the far right off their services, per your [wishes](https://qz.com/1329967/facebooks-fight-against-bad-content-is-as-chaotic-as-ever/). But that won't be the end of the tectonic [plate shifts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plate_tectonics) in news. The right will form their own services, where your product will be excluded, and will still apply pressure on the public companies to treat your work as suspect, and they will have to give them what they want. At that point you must have control of your own distribution.
