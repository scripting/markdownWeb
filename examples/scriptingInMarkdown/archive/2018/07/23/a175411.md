[Yesterday](http://scripting.com/2018/07/22.html) I wrote that Frontier does not have hoisting. This is incorrect. It does. I found it in the docs written in [1996](http://scripting.com/frontier/manual/chapter07.html) and [2008](http://scripting.com/opmlHowto/dave/editorOpmlOrg/helpMenu.html#commandsInTheOutlinerMenu), and tested it with the two built-in verbs, [op.hoist ()](http://docserver.userland.com/op/hoist) and [op.dehoist ()](http://docserver.userland.com/op/deHoist). My memory is imperfect. What I should have said is that I didn't use the feature, even though it was there.
