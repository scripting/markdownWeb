![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2018/05/17/mrNatural.png)It takes courage to write a [blog post](https://werd.io/2018/life-on-the-darkest-timeline) like this from Ben Werdmüller. Whenever we write, imho, we're saying something simple. This is what I see. These are my values. This is what it's like to be me. By showing yourself, you become vulnerable, and that has a cost. Better to be safe. But sometimes you have so much to say, the pain is so great, it has to come out. One thing I've discovered in a life of ups and downs -- it's true -- it's darkest just before dawn. :boom:
