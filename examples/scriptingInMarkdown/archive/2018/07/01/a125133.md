The best defense against [Putin and Trump](https://www.theatlantic.com/politics/archive/2018/07/the-great-russian-disinformation-campaign/564032/) is to teach every student the basics of journalism. Not just how to [detect](https://twitter.com/ctonk144/status/1013384894976798721) fake news, but how to write a news story. If we do it now, we'll be glad we did in a few years.
