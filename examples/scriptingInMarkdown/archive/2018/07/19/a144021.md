The Democrats have a new campaign [slogan](https://www.politico.com/story/2018/07/18/house-democrats-midterms-campaign-slogan-730907) "For the people." It's close. But it betrays an attitude that separates you from us. Better to get down in there. Luckily there is a [very American idea](https://www.loc.gov/item/48034353/) that fits. "We the people..." I'd leave the elipses in the expression to let memory remind us that we remember who we are.
