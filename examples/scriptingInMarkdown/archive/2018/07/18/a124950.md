I know this is like pissing in the wind, but here's an idea for a demonstration that might impress the Repubs in Congress. In every one of their home districts, people march to their polling place, next Saturday or the Saturday after that. Carrying signs that say _We Know How To Vote,_ with the name of their congressperson on it. Go out of the way to recruit Republican-looking voters. Make sure the TV cameras are there.
