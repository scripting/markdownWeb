So many security schemes are based on the control of phone numbers. But it’s relatively easy to [hijack](https://motherboard.vice.com/en_us/article/a3q7mz/hacker-allegedly-stole-millions-bitcoin-sim-swapping) phone numbers. They're also the weak link in most [2FA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-factor_authentication) systems.
