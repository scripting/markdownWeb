The other day I was [looking](http://scripting.com/2018/07/09/143533.html) for the canonical [states.opml](http://hosting.opml.org/dave/spec/states.opml) file, the one that I use to test structures of outlines with. I found what appears to be the original copy, dated 12/2/2000. It's now [accessible](http://this.how/states/states.opml) through this.how, which is served by PagePark, using its built-in ability to render OPML files. I'm rebooting the [worldoutline](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=site%3Ascripting.com+worldoutline&t=h_&ia=web) capabilities but with an open source scalable stack. :rocket:
