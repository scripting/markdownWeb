I haven't heard this mentioned elsewhere, but there are parallels to where we're at now and at the beginning of World War II.

Russia, like Germany had been defeated and humiliated. Out of that rose a strongman, who has territorial ambitions in Europe.

As in the 1930s, the US is isolationist. This time we have a [defense pact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NATO), but it seems like we're going to withdraw from that.

What it means for Europe is that they're heading for government like that in Russia, and without a strong adversary, an unchecked Russia won't stop before it runs over all of Europe.

Here are the relevant dates.

*   The [Treaty of Versailles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Versailles) was in 1919 and the first territory grab by Germany was the annexation of [Austria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anschluss) in 1938. 19 years.
    
*   The collapse of the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissolution_of_the_Soviet_Union) was in 1991, and the annexation of [Crimea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annexation_of_Crimea_by_the_Russian_Federation) was in 2014. 23 years.
