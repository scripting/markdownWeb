[Matt Groening](https://www.nytimes.com/2018/07/18/arts/television/simpsons-matt-groening-apu.html?action=click&module=MoreInSection&pgtype=Article&region=Footer&contentCollection=Television) still isn't listening. Apu _is_ lovable. The [problem](http://scripting.com/2018/04/10/221756.html) is not political correctness, or people being offended, it’s that Apu creates problems for Indian-Americans because there are so few South Asian characters on TV. Apu dominates. I'm sure the Simpsons and Groening didn't intend it, but it's happening just the same.
