In the great [spaces vs tabs debate](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=spaces+vs+tabs+debate&t=h_&ia=web), as a programmer for over 40 years, I use neither. I program in an [outliner](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=what+is+an+outliner+site%3Ascripting.com&t=h_&ia=web), a kind of editor that understands structure. The display of the structure is something the editor does for me. It doesn't use spaces or tabs.
