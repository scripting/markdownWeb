![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2018/12/26/kiddingPromo.png)I had no way of knowing how well the two shows I've been bingeing fit together. They are [River](https://www.netflix.com/title/70260350), which is the story of a crazy grieving brilliant cop who learns to accept his insanity, and [Kidding](https://www.sho.com/kidding), a crazy grieving Mr Rogers type TV actor. They're both struggling with pretty much the same issue. What were the chances. And both are quite good, if you're looking for a time-consuming holiday binge.
