Yesterday on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/davewiner/status/1075541500027650048) and [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/dave.winer.12/posts/862996047241135?notif_id=1545264878217485&notif_t=feedback_reaction_generic) I asked for advice about cars.

_I am quite tall. Esp in the torso. So it’s hard for me to find a car I fit into. My head is usually crammed against the roof. Poor me. Okay I’m going to buy a car soon, and would like it to have ample headroom. Any ideas?_

Lots of great advice came back which I will summarize here.

*   Subaru, specifically the Forrester.
    
*   Toyota Rav4.
    
*   VW Golf and Jetta.
    
*   Kia Soul (I rented one of these a few months ago, loved it. Lots of room and it's fun to drive.)
    
*   Honda Pilot.
    
*   Mazda CX-5.
    
*   Honda Element, but they no longer make them.
    
*   Hyundai Sonata.
    
*   Chevy Impala.
    
*   Toyota Highlander.
    
*   Ford C-MAX.
    
*   Honda Accord.
    
*   Denali.
    
*   BMW i3.
    
*   Scion XB.
    
*   Kia Niro.
    
*   Tesla 3.
    
*   Chrysler 300.
    
*   VW Beetle.
    
*   Nissan Maxima.
    
*   Dodge RAM 1500.
    
*   Volvo XC90.
