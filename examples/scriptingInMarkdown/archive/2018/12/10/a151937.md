[Civil](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=Civil+blockchain+journalism&t=h_&ia=web) was a recent [example](https://www.nytimes.com/2018/11/01/style/blockchain-journalism-civil.html) of an effort to launch a new model for journalism that received a lot of hype and unconditional endorsements. But the journalists who were supposed to be paid say they aren't being paid. And the investors who were hoping to support journalism and were motivated by greed (investor greed is perfectly appropriate) aren't happy either. Tech is never a panacea. If people are hyping a technology as one, they're selling [snake oil](http://scripting.com/davenet/1996/06/03/ofmiceandmenus.html), and you should keep your hand on your wallet.
