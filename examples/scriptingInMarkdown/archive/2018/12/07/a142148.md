![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2018/12/07/drummerboy.png)[Chris Beard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Beard_\(executive\)) at Mozilla wrote a [piece](https://blog.mozilla.org/blog/2018/12/06/goodbye-edge/) that I totally agree with about the problem of Google dominance of the open web. I could have written that piece myself. In fact in a way I [did](http://this.how/googleAndHttp/). But Mozilla hasn't listened any better than Google. Maybe if we want to keep the thread of a free web alive, we should listen better, and _help each other._ That was the original spirit of the web, not the dominance of big companies who don't feel obligated to listen to and work with individuals. To me, Mozilla looks as big as Google looks to them.
