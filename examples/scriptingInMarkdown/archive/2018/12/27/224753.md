Today I [learned](https://jsfiddle.net/ha9g2w5j/) that testing two dates for equality in JavaScript does not work as a reasonable person might expect.

var d1 = new Date ("March 12, 1994");

var d2 = new Date ("March 12, 1994");

alert (d1 == d2);

The answer in the alert is false.
