Do you ever wonder, while watching a nature documentary, what it must feel like to be the [prey](https://twitter.com/koopa_kinte/status/1078875929437450241) who's being eaten while they're still alive? Do you think they feel pain? Perhaps they feel an overwhelming sense of purpose, well-being. To know that they're being of use? It's hard to know. But I think that's where [this impulse](https://www.washingtonpost.com/crime-law/2018/12/22/hes-jailed-life-after-murdering-his-wife-kids-so-why-do-women-send-him-love-letters/?fbclid=IwAR3mLXP66MoEtDCX_YuU3ocToF2bqWE0KhJHPNHGyoxMtftf_28Eng3uO1c&utm_term=.2782a0c79042) originates. We all know at some point we will be overwhelmed and dragged under, we'll die. And I'm guessing it isn't an entirely unpleasant experience.
