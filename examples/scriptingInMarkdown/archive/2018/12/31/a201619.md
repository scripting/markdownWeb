![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/11/15/kristaps.png)[18-minute podcast](http://scripting.com/2018/12/31/whyTheNewCommentsAreSoGood.m4a) about why the new comments are so good. Two main reasons. 1. It's inverted from a spam point of view. You comment in your Twitter flow. I get to read your comment because I follow the [RSS feed](http://xmlviewer.scripting.com/?url=http://scripting.com/comments/rss.xml) and the [#scriptingnews](https://twitter.com/hashtag/scriptingnews) tag. If I think what you said is valuable I can _like_ it, or RT it or follow you. So there's a way for me to amplify your flow, but it's my choice. 2. Twitter's new 280-char limit works really well, as [noted](http://scripting.com/2018/12/31/155551.html) earlier. It's enough to express an idea, but not enough to write a blog post. Perfect.
