![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/12/20/shrek.png)Reminder to myself and others who read this blog. Use the feature here on Scripting News more. It's a way to comment on what's going on here, without using Disqus (which in its latest iterations has become really offensive).

Twitter, with its 280 character limit is now a good commenting system wrt my [Comment Guidelines](http://guidelines.scripting.com/). Keep it short, provide a link, don't use comments to write blog posts.

It's funny, I didn't write much about the comments feature when it came out on [12/16](http://scripting.com/2018/12/16.html) and [subsequent](http://scripting.com/2018/12/17.html) [days](http://scripting.com/2018/12/18.html). I did start a [thread](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/issues/106) on the repo, but there isn't much there.

Comments are [much-discussed](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=site%3Ascripting.com+comments&t=h_&ia=web) over the years on [this blog](http://scripting.com/).

Also there's an [RSS feed](http://xmlviewer.scripting.com/?url=http://scripting.com/comments/rss.xml) for comments.
