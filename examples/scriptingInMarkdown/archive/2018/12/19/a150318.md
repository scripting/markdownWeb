[Jason Pontin asks](https://twitter.com/jason_pontin/status/1075383209356091393) how journalism is self-dealing and corrupt. Not all corruption is about money. Journalism exterts control and influence. Gatekeeping. They keep lots of ideas out of the news, esp ones they find uncomfortable. Show me an op-ed in the NYT about the future of journalism that says they should compete with Facebook. Or a public editor who is a member of the public? Also any industry that feels they should be above examination, and has been exempt for so long, must be _filled_ with corruption. And of course if there's no corruption, there's no harm in looking, right?
