![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2018/09/20/forever.png)I watched the 8-episode [Forever](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forever_\(2018_TV_series\)) series on Amazon. I really liked it, totally worth watching. It's a love story, but anything beyond that would be a spoiler. Episode 6 is the oddball, it's totally standalone, you can watch it first if you want, it won't spoil anything. The two characters don't appear in any of the other episodes. It's an artistic flourish you see in shows like Breaking Bad or Better Call Saul, and in this case while the rest of the series is excellent, episode 6 is killer.
