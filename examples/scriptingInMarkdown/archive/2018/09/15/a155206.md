![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/10/31/bojack.png)I injured my right shoulder 25 years ago in a skiing accident, it was worth it, I was in top shape and showing off, and crashed spectacularly. I could have had it fixed with [arthroscopic surgery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthroscopy), but opted not to. The cost, every so often my shoulder goes lame and is extremely painful. It's always (so far) been solved with rest and patience. I think I must have hurt it when I took [the fall](http://scripting.com/2018/08/30.html#a021556) a couple of weeks ago, which otherwise appears to be healed. So it's been hard to sleep. Luckily there's a new season of [Bojack Horseman](http://scripting.com/2015/08/30/whyYouMustWatchBojackHorseman.html) to feast on. It's made the pain of my shoulder disability a lot easier to bear.
