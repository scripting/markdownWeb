![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2018/09/18/sadElephant.png)My own opinion. Kavanaugh will not be confirmed. I expect [Christine Ford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christine_Blasey_Ford) to be a compelling witness. Reporters will interview [classmates](https://www.vox.com/policy-and-politics/2018/9/17/17870642/mark-judge-brett-kavanaugh-friend-christine-ford) of hers and of Kavanaugh's. Some Repubs on the [Judiciary Committee](https://www.judiciary.senate.gov/) understand enough about the issue to be far more [sensitive](https://www.motherjones.com/politics/2018/09/lindsey-graham-kavanaughs-christine-ford-accusations/) than they were with [Anita Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anita_Hill). But [some](https://nypost.com/2018/09/17/orrin-hatch-says-kavanaugh-accuser-may-be-mixed-up/) will not, and this will spark rage at Republicans including from within their own base. A lot of [women](https://www.thenation.com/article/the-reasons-why-white-women-vote-republican-and-what-to-do-about-it/) vote Republican. There's a decent chance a handful of Repub senators will quit the party, or even become Democrats. As I said [yesterday](http://scripting.com/2018/09/17.html#a030123), governing has become a burden for the Repubs. They know how to bully, they know how to [obstruct](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merrick_Garland), but this is far more complex. They won't be able to hold their coalition together. Think of it as an intra-party wedge issue for the Repubs.
