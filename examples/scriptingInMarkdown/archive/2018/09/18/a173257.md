It's not well known that [Aaron Swartz](http://www.aaronsw.com/) made a contribution to RSS 2.0, wrt the <cloud> element. We were initially only supporting XML-RPC and SOAP protocols, but [Aaron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aaron_Swartz) suggested also supporting HTTP-POST. It was a good idea, and it came during the comment period for RSS 2.0, so it was possible to add it, so I did.
