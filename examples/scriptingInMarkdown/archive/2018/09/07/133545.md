An analogy for what the NYT did by publishing the [anonymous op-ed](https://www.nytimes.com/2018/09/05/opinion/trump-white-house-anonymous-resistance.html).

1.  Suppose I run an airline.
    
2.  Some guy shows up at the gate with a package, asks if we'll put it on the plane for him and transport it to the plane's destination.
    
3.  I open it, look inside, see a couple of soaked rags, and say OK.
    
4.  I ask for ID because I want to know who is sending the package.
    
5.  When the plane gets to its destination, they open the package. It contains a deadly toxin, it poisons everyone within 100 miles, they all die.
