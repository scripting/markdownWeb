People think it's my fight. They're wrong. It's our fight. If you don't help, it won't happen. And so far it seems to me we're headed for a Trump-like loss on the web. You'll miss it when it's gone. I was sure that Google's trying to own the open web wouldn't stop with [attacking HTTP](http://this.how/googleAndHttp/). They didn't even wait a month before launching the next attack on web addresses. There was no resistance, why should they wait?
