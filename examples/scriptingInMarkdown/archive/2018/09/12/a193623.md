![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2018/09/12/vietCongFlag.png)One idea pundits and politicos have trouble with is the difference between voters and parties. When [Susan Collins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Susan_Collins) blames Democrats for the campaign against her, she's missing that it's voters doing it, not the other party. A big difference. It's like fighting the [Viet Cong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viet_Cong) when you think you're fighting the army of North Vietnam. It's what they call an [asymmetric war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asymmetric_warfare). The people can probably bring down one senator if we really focus on it. We should do just that to open the eyes of the press and the politicos.
