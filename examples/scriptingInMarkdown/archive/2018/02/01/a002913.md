![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2018/02/01/willieMays.png)We need a new kind of domain, perhaps a _Historic Domain,_ like a [retired number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retired_number) for a sports team. Should there ever be another [gawker.com](http://gawker.com/)? I don't think so. The content that's there now should be there for the foreseeable future. I don't know what the criteria should be for preservation but Gawker is clearly a domain that should be around next year and the year after that. See this [NYT piece](https://mobile.nytimes.com/2018/02/01/business/media/gawker-archives-press-freedom.html?smid=tw-nytimes&smtyp=cur&referer=http://m.facebook.com) for background.
