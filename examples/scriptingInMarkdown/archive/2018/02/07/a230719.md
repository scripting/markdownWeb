I often complain about podcasts that hide their [RSS](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html) feed. It's only fair to call out one for doing it well. Here's a new [podcast](https://www.wnycstudios.org/shows/trumpinc) from WNYC. Over on [the left](http://scripting.com/images/2018/02/07/trumpIncHomePage.png), impossible to miss, is a Subscribe button. Click it, and see a [list of choices](http://scripting.com/images/2018/02/07/menu.png), one of which is [via RSS](http://feeds.wnyc.org/trumpinc). It links to the [podcast feed](http://feeds.wnyc.org/trumpinc), ready to subscribe to, which I have done in [Podcatch.com](http://podcatch.com/).
