Doc Searls an old radio guy [wrote about podcasting](https://web.archive.org/web/20041001072412/http://garage.docsearls.com/node/view/462) in the same month of September 2004. He followed this with [another piece](https://web.archive.org/web/20041105123652/http://garage.docsearls.com/node/view/469) in October, explaining that podcasting is not radio. October was an [incredible month](http://scripting.com/2004/10.html), lots of adoption, new aggregators, tools, an audio version of weblogs.com, and signs of the grief that was about to happen, starting to happen.
