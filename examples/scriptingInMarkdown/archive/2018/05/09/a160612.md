![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2018/05/09/shrug.png)With tech billionaires so visible in the news these days we can all see the conundrum clearly. There really is nothing good you can do with the billions. You still are a person, you still are the person you were before you had the billions. There's no escaping yourself, I know I've tried, it doesn't work. There is no _better_ when it comes to people. We're all slightly different variants of the same theme.
