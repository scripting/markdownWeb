From the _How Far They've Fallen_ category, if you [search](https://www.google.com/search?safe=off&source=hp&ei=fFcQW52LMI6d5wLgs77IBQ&q=infinity&oq=infinity&gs_l=psy-ab.3..35i39k1l2j0i20i263k1j0i67k1l2j0j0i20i263i264k1j0i131k1j0j0i67k1.2761.4520.0.5728.10.9.0.0.0.0.110.800.5j3.9.0....0...1.1.64.psy-ab..1.9.976.6..0i131i20i264k1.176.hTKlkvgRICA) for _infinity_ on Google, you get a [page of ads](http://scripting.com/images/2018/05/31/googleSearchForInfinity.png) for a car named Infiniti. Even more interesting, if you [search](https://www.google.com/search?q=define+google&rlz=1C5CHFA_enUS743US747&oq=define+google&aqs=chrome..69i57j0l5.1927j0j3&sourceid=chrome&ie=UTF-8) for _define Google_ on Google, you don't get the mathematical definition either. In this case, they are probably correct, the name of their company has usurped the [mathematical](https://whatis.techtarget.com/definition/googol-and-googolplex) definition.
