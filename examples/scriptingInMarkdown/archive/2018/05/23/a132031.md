![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2018/05/23/clapperBook.png)I couldn't sleep after last night's [excellent game](https://www.sfgate.com/warriors/amp/Warriors-collapse-in-4th-quarter-as-Rockets-even-12936022.php?__twitter_impression=true) between the Rockets and Warriors, so I tuned into the midnight re-broadcast of Maddow. She had an ex-CIA guy on as [the big guest](https://www.msnbc.com/rachel-maddow/watch/james-clapper-on-russia-they-swung-the-election-to-a-trump-win-1239786051725?playlist=associated), he has a [book](https://www.amazon.com/Facts-Fears-Hard-Truths-Intelligence-ebook/dp/B076GPMDTS/ref=sr_1_1?s=books&ie=UTF8&qid=1527082847&sr=1-1&refinements=p_27%3AJames+R.+Clapper) out. He says he believes, now that he's retired, that the Russians did influence the outcome of the election. Rachel paused, as if some big moment had passed. She seemed to be saying, aha now at last the truth has come out. I thought this is so laughably ridiculous. Of course _we all knew_ that the Russians influenced the outcome of the election. This. Is. Not. News. Stop being so ridiculous Rachel. Let's move beyond what we all know. We all know what happened in 2016. Now let's put up some defenses against what's happening _now._
