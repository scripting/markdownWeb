![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2018/05/04/dorothy.png)My [mom](http://www.legacy.com/obituaries/nytimes/obituary.aspx?page=lifestory&pid=188139452) was a quilter, so I inherited a bunch of her quilts ones she made over many decades. There is a philosophy to quilting, which I observe as an outsider. The work is done very much with the idea of creating a legacy. I have a friend, Sheila, in Seattle who is a quilter. I wanted her to have one of Eve's quilts, even though they had never met. I thought of this because she posted a [picture](https://www.facebook.com/photo.php?fbid=10216286318295172&set=a.1604799764457.241313.1370027423&type=3&theater) of her latest quilt on Faceboo. We hooked up in Seattle this week, talked about parents, and of course quilting. I don't know much about quilting, but I find it fascinating. Anyway, I just rolled over the archive for this blog, saving an [OPML file](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/blob/master/blog/opml/2018/04.opml) of the writing I did in April, along with all the [other months](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/tree/master/blog/opml). In a way my blog archive is like mom's quilt collection. A hope to pass something into the future saying _this is who I was._
