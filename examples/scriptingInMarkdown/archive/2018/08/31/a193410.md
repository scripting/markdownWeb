The [Village Voice](https://www.villagevoice.com/) [is](https://www.nytimes.com/2018/08/31/business/media/the-village-voice-closes.html?partner=rss&emc=rss) [shutting down](http://gothamist.com/2018/08/31/village_voice_is_officially_dead.php). A New York [institution](http://evgrieve.com/2018/08/rip-village-voice.html), it was [born](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Village_Voice#Early_history) the same year I was. One nice thing is that they are keeping half the staff, 15 to 20 people, to figure out how to [archive the site](http://scripting.com/images/2018/08/31/villageVoiceArchive.png). Any time a publication does this, we should offer to help, to learn from the experience, and develop a base of knowledge and best practices. I need to figure out how to do that for my blog. I want to do that sooner than later. Tick tock, the [runway](http://scripting.com/2018/03/03/135246.html) isn't getting any longer. ✓
