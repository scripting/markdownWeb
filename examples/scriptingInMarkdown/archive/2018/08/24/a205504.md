A young [Paul Simon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Simon) [explains](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vzDp2d5b_o8) how he [wrote](https://variety.com/2018/music/news/the-graduate-soundtrack-50th-anniversary-mrs-robinson-1202663554/) [the](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Graduate_\(soundtrack\)) [music](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9C1BCAgu2I8) for [The Graduate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Graduate) in an interview with Dick Cavett. He was really sweet and intense and oozed creativity. Earlier today I listened to a podcast interview with [Robbie Robertson](https://www.npr.org/sections/allsongs/2018/08/08/636328129/the-bands-robbie-robertson-looks-back-on-50-years-of-music-from-big-pink) from [The Band](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Band), remembering how they did [Music from Big Pink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_from_Big_Pink), recorded 50 years ago in a [house](https://www.vrbo.com/3970069ha) in [Saugerties, NY](https://www.google.com/maps/place/56+Parnassus+Ln,+Saugerties,+NY+12477/@42.0776522,-74.0517061,177m/data=!3m1!1e3!4m5!3m4!1s0x89dda86a2f92ff0b:0xe993fcc7401d9732!8m2!3d42.0778051!4d-74.0512407) (near Woodstock). I love to hear how creative people do their thing.
