I've written here many times about the [distinction](http://scripting.com/2014/03/03/whyBloggingIsAnAmateurThing.html) between the terms blogger and journalist. In a Twitter thread, [Lora Kolodny](https://twitter.com/lorakolodny) makes a distinction between [journalist](https://twitter.com/lorakolodny/status/1026499041792647168) and [reporter](https://twitter.com/lorakolodny/status/1026499347763027968). I hadn't realized there might be a difference. Here's what she says.

_Journalist_ (besides sounding hi-falutin) includes people who aren't reporters. Editors, producers, photographers, columnists, podcasters, vloggers and bloggers may not all be doing the primary research or interviews for a piece, but are all responsible for accuracy of it.

_Reporters_ do the primary fact finding, interviews, field work, etc. But by no means are reporters the only journalists. It's not a commutative property. If reporter then journalist. But not if journalist then reporter.

My own two cents. I'd love to reserve the term blogger for people who write about their own experiences, not for pay, the "unedited voice of a person." I think of bloggers as sources in the journalism world.
