Yesterday I [asked](http://scripting.com/2018/08/14.html#a194320) [about](https://twitter.com/davewiner/status/1029453411262054400) bike-mounted speakers, and got lots of great advice. For some reason a [search](https://www.amazon.com/s?url=search-alias%3Daps&field-keywords=bike+speaker) on Amazon yielded nothing but crap, but if you know what you're looking for you get some pretty nice stuff. Rex Hammock recommended [JBL Flip](https://www.amazon.com/JBL-Waterproof-Portable-Bluetooth-Black/dp/B01MSYQWNY). And Jason Gilman recommended [Clearon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B01MXDNA2O/ref=cm_sw_r_cp_ep_dp_-L1CBbV67GDBM). Roland Tanglao [said](https://twitter.com/rtanglao/status/1029534796957933568) he loves his [UE Roll 2](https://www.amazon.com/Ultimate-Ears-Wireless-Bluetooth-Waterproof/dp/B01FFV59PY), and I have one of those, but it didn't occur to me it could be bike-mounted. The bungee that's built in works fine on a bike, and I took it for a spin just now and it's perfect. Great sound. Totally loud enough to be heard over NYC street noise, with great frequency range and thumpin base to keep the wheels turning. A really great answer. Davey's a happy cyclist. Thanks everyone for the great advice. I want to try all of these speakers.
