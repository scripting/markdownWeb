![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2018/07/01/loveRss.png)If you want to read feeds of any kind in a Node app, my [feedRead package](https://github.com/scripting/feedread) is the easiest way to get something up and running super quick. Simple example code for reading a feed over [the web](https://github.com/scripting/feedRead/blob/master/examples/readurl/read.js) or from a [local file](https://github.com/scripting/feedRead/blob/master/examples/readfile/read.js). I [use](https://github.com/scripting/reader/blob/master/davereader.js) it in [River5](https://github.com/scripting/river5) so it's been extensively burned in [with](http://river5.scripting.com/) all manner of feeds.
