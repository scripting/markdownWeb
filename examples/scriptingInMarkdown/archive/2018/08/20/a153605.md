Journalism has lots of problems, but the really big one is that distribution is a mess. There needs to be an [EU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Union) of news. And a [euro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euro). It's no longer an idea for the future, it's a model that should have been implemented long ago, and we should be growing based on it.
