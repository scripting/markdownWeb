![](http://scripting.com/2016/01/30/strongman.png)Thinking about where I am in re a [Linux version](http://scripting.com/2018/08/21.html#a144138) of Frontier. I just realized that my collection of [GitHub projects](https://github.com/scripting?tab=repositories) amount to the built-in verb set of Frontier. There are big missing pieces, and they aren't tied together in an object database, but the functionality is there. I am often reminded of this, when writing a utility script that would take minutes in Frontier, and I'm already on Day Two in JavaScript. For example, here's the [JS code](https://github.com/scripting/utils/blob/master/daveutils.js) for the [daveutils](https://www.npmjs.com/package/daveutils) package. I even use the same names as in Frontier, but without the Frontier syntax.
