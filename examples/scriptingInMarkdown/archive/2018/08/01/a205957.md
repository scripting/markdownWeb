A big downside of ceding economic leadership to China is that their norms of freedom will become the norms of the world. Once Google's search engine [censors](https://theintercept.com/2018/08/01/google-china-search-engine-censorship/) access to the net in China, it's just a matter of time before they do it in the west too.
