![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/11/26/wonderWoman.png)Open source developers need as much support as the users of their products. With that in mind, I want to congratulate the developers of [jsDelivr](https://www.jsdelivr.com/). I just [adapted](http://scripting.com/2018/04/14/152222.html) my project to use it as a CDN. The process was hard work, but made much easier by the docs and design of jsDelivr. That part was a breeze. They tell you just what you need to know when you need to know it with no extra fluff. This is really rare. Good work! :boom:
