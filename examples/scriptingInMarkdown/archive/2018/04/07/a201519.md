[The rule of links](http://bloggercon.scripting.com/ruleoflinks.html): "Linking is an art. It's a choice. You don't link from every word or even every noun, or from the subject of every sentence. But when a reader reasonably would want to know more about the subject, the Rule of Links says you should link to it."
