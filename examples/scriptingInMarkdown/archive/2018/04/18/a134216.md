When I was growing up [in NYC](https://www.google.com/maps/@40.7552947,-73.8758418,3a,75y,67.55h,88.27t/data=!3m6!1e1!3m4!1s7JDIf4Nq9rc0G2SHUe2f8w!2e0!7i13312!8i6656), before the EPA, they'd burn garbage in incinerators, in the morning, as I walked to school. It was choking. My mom sent me out clean, by the time I got to school there'd be burnt garbage in my hair and on my clothes.
