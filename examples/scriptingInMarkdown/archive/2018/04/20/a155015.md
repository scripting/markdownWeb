For such a famous place, it's [weird](https://twitter.com/davewiner/status/987357745165762561) that Silicon Valley doesn't have a landmark. Paris has the Eiffel Tower. NYC has the Empire State Building, London has Big Ben. I remember arriving in Silicon Valley in 1979 and driving around looking for it and finding nothing but suburbs. I guess that's why [Buck's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buck%27s_of_Woodside) was so popular. It certainly wasn't the food. :boom:
