I finished all sixteen episodes of [Bablylon Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babylon_Berlin). It was quite a ride. And at the end I'm not sure exactly what happened. I needed this [summary](http://www.vulture.com/2018/02/babylon-berlin-netflix-how-it-ends.html) to convince me that I did/do. Would I recommend it? It's a roller coaster. Dark. Some scenes are hard to watch. The [song](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=30PPdLTLlko) is addictive. You fall in love with the characters. Would I watch it again? Not sure. But the ending isn't exactly Singin' in the Rain. 💃🕺
