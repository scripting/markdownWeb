Another thing. I loved the [Wired article](https://www.wired.com/story/rss-readers-feedly-inoreader-old-reader/) about the RSS revival. But I hope it doesn't create the kind of hyper atmosphere we had in the early days. We moved way too fast and created chaos. I want to move slowly and do tech that can last for another 20 years, and hopefully without all the fits and starts and major steps [backwards](https://www.wired.com/2013/06/why-google-reader-got-the-ax/).
