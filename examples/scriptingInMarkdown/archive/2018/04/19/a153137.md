![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2018/01/26/falloutShelterGuy.png)Anyway not only is blogging not xxx, but look at all the new features here on [Scripting News](http://scripting.com/). Many of these things have never been done in blogs before, or for that matter on news org sites. You only believe it's xxx if you [ignore](http://thesaurus.land/?word=ignore) everything outside your little piece of the blogging world (which for many is limited to their own blog). Just as we need to branch out and share innnovations in the [land of RSS](http://scripting.com/2018/04/18.html#a214730), we must do that for blogging too, so that when people say it's xxx, we can _show them_ how it's anything but. You want blogging to thrive? It can, if you're willing to look, and tell the story.
