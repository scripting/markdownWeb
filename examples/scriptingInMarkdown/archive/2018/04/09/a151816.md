![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2018/04/09/loveRss.png)If [RSS](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html) was a startup and had a CEO that TechCrunch needed access to, or was backed by VCs, or even a big tech company, they would temper their [judgment](https://techrunch.com/2018/04/07/rss-is-undead/). Instead it's a punching bag, a technology [piñata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pi%C3%B1ata). But people love RSS. It's still here, ready to bring you the news. And honestly they probably wouldn't beat on it if it weren't threatening someone. :heart:
