I changed the way the thumb works in [Like](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/issues/102). Now, when you click the thumb, it fills in, when you click it again it toggles to being open. It used to flip to down. This was confusing. Now it doesn't flip, it's always up. I think this feels better, burns fewer braincells.
