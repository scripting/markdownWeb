So what does a _Like_ mean [here](http://scripting.com/2018/11/17.html) on Scripting News? It's a way to tell me that you saw what I wrote and found it likeable. It doesn't mean you necessarily agree. You're also registering your presence to other people who read this blog. Maybe it's more like a ping? Hmmm. I know the Like icon doesn't show up in your feed reader (maybe that can change) but it may be worth a trip to [my blog](http://scripting.com/) if you want to say hi to me and others who read this blog. That's what it means.
