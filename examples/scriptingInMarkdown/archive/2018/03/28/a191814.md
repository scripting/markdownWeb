Could a pardon be obstruction of justice? People say the president is free to pardon anyone. True. He is also allowed to use the phone. But if he uses the phone to threaten a witness, that's [OOJ](https://acronyms.thefreedictionary.com/OOJ). Same with a pardon. If it's purpose is to silence a witness, it's [OOJ](https://acronyms.thefreedictionary.com/OOJ).
