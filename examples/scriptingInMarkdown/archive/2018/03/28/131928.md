![](http://scripting.com/2016/01/30/strongman.png)Today is the official unveiling of feedBase.

The [home page](http://feedbase.io/) is the hotlist. Click on the name of a feed to see who subscribes. Click on a user's name to see what they follow. If you see something that interests you, click the Subscribe button and it will be added to your public subscription list.

The next thing to do is to [upload](http://scripting.com/2018/03/27/164606.html) an OPML file with your subscriptions. If you have more than one, upload both. We take care of duplicates.

Read the [docs](http://this.how/feedBase/).

And the [technical docs](https://github.com/scripting/feedBase). Including a description of the [database](https://github.com/scripting/feedBase/blob/master/docs/database.md), and the feedBase [source](https://github.com/scripting/feedBase/blob/master/feedbase.js) (it's written in JavaScript of course, runs in node.js).

If you're a developer, here's a feedBase-generated [OPML file](http://opml.feedbase.io/davewiner/main.opml) you can test with. What to do with it? Listen to the 8-minute [podcast](http://scripting.com/2018/03/27.html#a172435) I posted yesterday. It'll explain the vision. It's a bootstrap. You are part of it. :rocket:

Here's the [hotlist](http://opml.feedbase.io/hotlist.opml) in OPML.

If you have technical questions or concerns, post an [issue](https://github.com/scripting/feedBase/issues/new) on the repo.

If you have user questions, you can post them there too, or as a comment on the docs.

I still have more work to do, and there will be more features to add. Bugs to fix. Etc.
