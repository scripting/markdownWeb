![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2018/03/19/mrWhite.png)I've listened to a couple of episodes of the new [Techmeme podcast](https://techmeme.com/techmeme-ride-home-feed). I was really [excited](http://scripting.com/2018/03/06.html) to hear about it. I imagined something like the [Daily podcast](https://www.nytimes.com/podcasts/the-daily) or [Fresh Air](https://www.npr.org/podcasts/381444908/fresh-air), or any number of podcasts built around an interview with an interesting person, with the focus on tech. The Techmeme podcast takes a different approach, one more like radio news programs of the past, where the presenter, [Brian McCullough](https://twitter.com/brianmcc), reads summaries of stories that are presumably the top items for the day on [Techmeme](https://www.techmeme.com/).

Called _Ride Home,_ each episode begins with the headlines followed by what I assume is its positioning statement: "Here's what you missed today in the world of tech," followed by a synopsis of each story, read from a script. There are other podcasts that assume this voice -- notably the [Whistlestop podcast](http://www.slate.com/articles/podcasts/whistlestop.html) with CBS newsperson John Dickerson.

I want to like this podcast because I'm a longtime reader of Techmeme, and a fan of McCullough's [Internet History Podcast](http://www.internethistorypodcast.com/). I was hoping it would be like a short version of that podcast every day, a candid interview with a tech newsmaker or a reporter at one of the pubs that Techmeme covers. I will keep listening because this is potentially an important podcast.

On the other hand, I understand that this is the format they would arrive at by adapting Techmeme to a podcast. But sometimes a literal translation isn't the most useful approach. Techmeme is of course useful as a web page, refreshed many times during the day. Voice offers an opportunity to approach the mind through a different channel, to spread new ideas, more than "what you missed."

It is of course one of the podcasts available at [Podcatch.com](http://podcatch.com/).
