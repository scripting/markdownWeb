Here's a plot for a fantasy movie.

1.  The fictitious Patriot Party surprisingly wins control of Congress and the White House.
    
2.  They want to control the government forever.
    
3.  Instead of raping the country they pass laws that voters love that make them happier, healthier.
    
4.  Everyone lives happily ever after.
