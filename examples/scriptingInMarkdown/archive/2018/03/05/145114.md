It's not necessary to actually watch Knicks games because they all seem to end the same. Last night they played the Sacramento Kings, another lottery team, and you might think here's a game the Knicks might actually be able to win. [Nahh](http://www.nydailynews.com/sports/basketball/knicks/knicks-fall-kings-skai-labissiere-game-winner-article-1.3855565). At least they're consistent!

A friend who happens to be a Yankees fan is also a Knicks fan, and we talk about the team regularly. He wonders why he's a Knicks fan if they always lose. I said I know the answer. The Knicks are his [atonement](http://thesaurus.land/?word=atonement) for being a Yankees fan. Now what does this say about me, a lifetime Mets fan? Well, one thing -- winning isn't the most important thing. And that said, unlike the Knicks, the Mets do win once in long while. Even that amount of winning requires [atonement](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=define+atonement&t=hf&ia=definition), I guess.
