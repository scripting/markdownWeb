[Farhad Manjoo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farhad_Manjoo), tech columnist for the NYT [wrote](https://www.nytimes.com/2018/03/07/technology/two-months-news-newspapers.html) about a hiatus he took from social media and how much he liked it. According to CJR, he actually [didn't](https://www.cjr.org/analysis/farhad-manjoo-nyt-unplug.php) take a hiatus. Makes sense. How could a reporter not use Twitter these days? That's where news is made.
