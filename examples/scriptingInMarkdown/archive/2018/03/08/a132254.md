Matt Webb is a [new voice](https://twitter.com/genmon/status/971673956829859840) in opposition to Google's treating the web as if it were a corporate platform. I added [this](https://twitter.com/davewiner/status/971735689741176832) to his tweets: "Google is trying to force people to change. I can accept them doing that for APIs and platforms they created, but not for the web. They are a guest here, as we all are. Guests don't make the rules."
