![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2018/03/31/mrNatural.png)[8-minute podcast](http://scripting.com/2018/03/30/howMelberCoversFacebook.m4a). I often watch [Ari Melber's](https://twitter.com/AriMelber) show on MSNBC, but when they turn to Facebook I get ready to be angry because they screw the story up so badly. Zuck pitched [Medvedev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmitry_Medvedev) and China. He says he wants regulation but has lobbyists that worked against it. Great, you embarrass him, but the net-effect is they continue to destroy the open web. Why don't we work on something that really matters that isn't about Russia or doesn't feel like Watergate. Tech makes messes when companies get dominant. Finding things Facebook is [screwing](http://scripting.com/2016/09/06/facebookAlreadyHasStylingAndLinks.html) up is easy. But Melber and the rest of the folks in the mainstream press have not found it. Same with [Google](http://this.how/googleAndHttp/).
