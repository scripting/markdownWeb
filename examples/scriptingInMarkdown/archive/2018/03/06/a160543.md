![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2018/02/13/curry.png)Approx 20-minute [podcast](http://scripting.com/2018/03/06/podcastingAsJuggernaut.m4a) about podcasting as a juggernaut. Why is it a juggernaut? You don't have to get permission to start a podcast, you can follow an idea intuitively, creatively. I offer two examples, the [Internet History Podcast](http://www.internethistorypodcast.com/) and [Here's the Thing](https://www.wnycstudios.org/shows/heresthething). Two podcasts that are not like what you hear in the excellent field of podcasts that sprang from [This American Life](https://www.thisamericanlife.org/). And that's just a tiny corner of what's out there. It's huge and diverse. No one gets the whole thing. Podcasting will continue to evolve as long as it's not controlled.
