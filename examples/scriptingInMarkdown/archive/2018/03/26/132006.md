I have to take my watch off to wash my hands.

1.  I put the watch in my pocket.
    
2.  When I take it out it [says](http://scripting.com/images/2018/03/26/wrongPasscode.png): "Wrong passcode, open the Apple Watch app on your iPhone, go to Passcode, then tap Re-enable Passcode Entry to try again." Note I never entered a passcode. I'm guessing it interprets my movement as the watch is in my pocket as a human entering a passcode.
    
3.  I open the Apple Watch app on my iPhone. Locate the Re-enable Passcode entry, tap it. Nothing happens. Tap again. Nothing. Again, again. Nothing, nothing.
    
4.  Now what?
    

Believe it or not the watch has to be [erased and restored](https://discussions.apple.com/thread/7898495) from a backup. I might get around to doing that in September or October.

Hold on, after powering off and on, I get a [new message](http://scripting.com/images/2018/03/26/tryAgainIn59.png).

Epilog: After 59 minutes, it let me enter the password. Moral of the story -- never put the watch in your pocket.
