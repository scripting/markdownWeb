I've been thinking about my categories. When I say 4 out of 5, this is what I'm talking about.

1.  Worst! -- Never should have been made. A complete disaster. I need to hate on this.
    
2.  Meh -- I have some respect for it, but please, don't do another season. Homeland is in this category. Lost is halfway betw Meh and Good.
    
3.  Good -- I watched it, maybe not all seasons, maybe not all the way through. It was entertaining. I respect the program, but I'm not sure I would recommend it. Dexter is a prototype. I got really tired of it. Also Big Love. Entourage.
    
4.  Loved -- The programs I would recommend wholeheartedly. There are a lot of programs in this category.
    
5.  Best! -- There are only a few Bests. For me The Sopranos, The Wire. The West Wing. Silicon Valley.
    

My rating is the average of all seasons. For example, the first couple of seasons of Homeland were pretty good. Then it got lost, about the time F. Murray Abraham came on board. So net-net it's a Good. ;-)
