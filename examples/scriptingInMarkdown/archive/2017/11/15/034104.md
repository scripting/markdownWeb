![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/11/15/kristaps.png)The Knicks this year are fucking awesome. So much fun to watch. So many stories and rookie lessons. I love it.

The only reason the Knicks [lost](https://www.newsday.com/sports/basketball/knicks/knicks-cavaliers-lebron-james-1.14968904) to Cleveland is that LeBron fucked with their heads. If that's how he has to win a game. My fear is that he'll want to come to NY next, and the Knicks might just do a deal with him. That would be Melo 2.0. We just learned how having a non-point guard who thinks he owns the ball is poison.

This is [Linsanity](http://www.nydailynews.com/sports/basketball/knicks/daze-linsanity-5-years-jeremy-lin-ny-storm-article-1.2974623) squared. The Knicks are having fun and there's so much love on the court. It's why basketball can be such a great sport.
