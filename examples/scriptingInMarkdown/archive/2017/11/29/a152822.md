![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/11/29/racehorse.png)JY [found](https://twitter.com/jy/status/935603589283745797) another Erik Sandberg-Diment [review](http://www.nytimes.com/1986/04/01/science/personal-computers-new-software-for-making-note-scribbling-easier.html?&pagewanted=print) of one of my products, Ready!, which was a [TSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terminate_and_stay_resident_program) [outliner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outliner) shipped in 1986. We were so busy with a growing company at the time, I probably saw the review when it came out, but I was in the middle of a tornado then. Not many memories. I remember the first review, of course, always will, because it was the one that made the startup actually start up.
