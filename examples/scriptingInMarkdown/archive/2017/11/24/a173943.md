![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/11/24/deadwood.png)I'm working my way through [Godless](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=Godless+tv+series&ia=web). It's nice, but so far, three episodes in, it's not as great as the reviews say. I'd say it's about 3/5th of [Deadwood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deadwood_\(TV_series\)), which came first, was more depraved, and more entertaining. That said I was just in the part of the country that it's about, southern Colorado and northern New Mexico. I think it was filmed there too. And this gives me a clear picture of what's next for [BingeWorthy](https://tv.bingeworthy.org/), a diary, a place where I can take notes about programs as I work through them.
