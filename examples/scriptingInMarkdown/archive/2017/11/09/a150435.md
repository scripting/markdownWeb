One of the reasons 280 chars is great is that it means that links I post to Facebook can have longer descriptions. It may seem counter-intuitive, but I use [Radio3](http://radio3.io/) to post my links, to Twitter, Facebook, to my [linkblog](http://scripting.com/?tab=links) and to an [RSS feed](http://radio3.io/users/davewiner/rss.xml). I don't write different versions for each environment, so what you get is the [least common denominator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lowest_common_denominator). Which is Twitter. When they up the limit, they do it for all other platforms, at least in my world.
