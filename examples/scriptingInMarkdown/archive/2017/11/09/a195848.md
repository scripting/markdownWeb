I was invited to a future-of-news conference in [Moscow](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Moscow,+Russia/@55.7494733,37.35232,10z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m5!3m4!1s0x46b54afc73d4b0c9:0x3d44d6cc5757cf4c!8m2!3d55.755826!4d37.6172999) in 2011, all expenses paid, by a Russian news agency, [Ria Novosti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RIA_Novosti). I said yes at first, but in the end didn't go. Something didn't seem right. Soon after, their feed redirected to [Sputnik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sputnik_\(news_agency\)), then to [RT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RT_\(TV_network\)).
