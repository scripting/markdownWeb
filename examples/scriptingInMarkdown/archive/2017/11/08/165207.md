Here are the steps:

1.  Press Return.
    
2.  Enter the title of the site.
    
3.  Press Return, then Cmd-R. Enter paragraphs for the text that goes on the home page of the site.
    
4.  Click on the title headline, then choose the Edit Attributes command.
    
5.  Enter a [domain attribute](http:///scripting.com/images/2017/11/08/editatts.png) with the domain name you want to be mapped to this sub-outline. Click OK.
    
6.  Save
    

All you have to do from there is map the domain to the server. The CMS server takes care of everything else.
