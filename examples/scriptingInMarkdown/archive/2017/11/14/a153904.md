Here's the idea. I want to binge something good on Netflix or HBO or Amazon, or whatever. I ask friends for ideas. Then I pick something. It might be good. I want to systematize that. That's [BingeWorthy](https://tv.bingeworthy.org/). At first we produce a Top 100 list, because that's all we have the data for. The goal is to build a database of prefs so we can make better and better recommendations.
