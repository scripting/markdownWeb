I like yesterday's blockbuster [trade](https://www.sbnation.com/nba/2017/8/22/16186928/kyrie-irving-trade-celtics-isaiah-thomas-cavaliers) betw Boston and Cleveland. I think Cleveland got the better deal. Kyrie Irving is a solid superstar, but I don't think he drives other players to perform. Not like the guy they traded for, Isaiah Thomas. He's a force of nature. I guess the Celtics thought he would burn out fast and Kyrie will last a while and will like staying in Boston. Cleveland is destined to be volatile. But it'll be interesting to watch Thomas and LeBron team up. Two very different kinds of players.
