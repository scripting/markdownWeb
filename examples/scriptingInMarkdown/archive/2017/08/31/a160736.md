[Quartz asked](https://qz.com/1065263/a-houston-residents-striking-plea-to-the-media-were-not-an-event-to-be-coolly-analyzed/) for ideas about how to cover the hurricane and its impact on Houston. Set up newsrooms in places where the victims are and let them write the news. Make sure their story gets through _directly_ to people outside Houston. Their thoughts are potentially very interesting. I sometimes have to turn off the sound on TV accounts because the words are so insipid. Usually from paid reporters. But I watch because the images are so astonishing.
