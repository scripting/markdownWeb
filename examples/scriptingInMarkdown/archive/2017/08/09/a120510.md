Ever get a phrase stuck in your head and you find yourself saying it over and over silently, sometimes, and audibly others? Today, for me, that phrase is the name [Millie Dresselhaus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mildred_Dresselhaus). There's a [commercial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sQ6_fOX7ITQ) on TV that imagines that everyone is saying her name all the time in weird ways and places. She's a real person. Unfortunately it turns out [she died](https://www.nytimes.com/2017/02/23/science/mildred-dresselhaus-dead-queen-of-carbon.html?_r=0) earlier this year. I am sorry.
