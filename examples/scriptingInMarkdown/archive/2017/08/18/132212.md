My longtime friend Stan Krute took this [picture](http://siskiyoulife.com/2017/Fair/121878_2000_max_captioned.jpg) of three young contestants waiting to be judged [at](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Siskiyou+Golden+Fair/@41.7070062,-123.191682,9z/data=!4m5!3m4!1s0x0:0x1136dfc372999fd7!8m2!3d41.7070062!4d-122.640134) the [Siskiyou County Golden Fair](http://sisqfair.com/).

![A picture named chickens.png](http:///scripting.com/images/2017/08/18/chickens.png)

When I saw the picture on Facebook I wasn't sure if it was a [Norman Rockwell](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=Norman+Rockwell&t=h_&ia=web) painting. Stan really captures a sense of pride and anticipation. Human qualities, in young people, and their chickens.
