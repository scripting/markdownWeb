![](http:///scripting.com/images/2017/08/13/typrionLannister.png)Game of Thrones took a turn to idiocy. In one moment the Mother of Dragons didn't want to finish Cersei off, now she's sold on Jon Snow's vision of the army of the dead attacking from the north. What changed her mind? We never saw it happen. Was it the note from the three-eyed Raven? Who knows. After last week's [glorious battle scene](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pE2wcBeyNdk), they shot their wad. Back to the story which consists mostly of reunions and weird pairings of characters doing really really really really stupid things.
