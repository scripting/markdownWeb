![](http:///scripting.com/images/2017/08/01/tree.png)There's [more](http://pitchfork.com/thepitch/the-soundcloud-you-loved-is-doomed/) [talk](http://www.billboard.com/articles/business/7881154/soundcloud-trouble-layoffs-sale-acquisition) [about](https://www.nytimes.com/2017/08/01/magazine/if-soundcloud-disappears-what-happens-to-its-music-culture.html?partner=rss&emc=rss) SoundCloud failing, or [changing](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2017-07-29/soundcloud-is-said-near-deal-to-sell-stakes-to-private-equity) radically. Which raises again the basic question behind creative work on the Internet -- when are we going to start building structures designed for longevity. With an economic model other than founders-get-rich. We should be thinking about a creative environment that builds on its achievements, and creates a usable archive.
