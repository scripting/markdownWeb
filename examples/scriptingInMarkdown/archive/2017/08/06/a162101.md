![](http:///scripting.com/images/2017/08/06/wonderWoman.png)I've been modularizing my [Node](https://www.w3schools.com/nodejs/) work using [NPM packages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Npm_\(software\)). Murphy-willing I'm going to be writing each of them up, in turn, [here](http://scripting.com/2017/08/) on Scripting News. You'll recognize the functionality as the kind of stuff you need to build DaveLand on the Internets in Node. The first one up is a really good OPML-to-and-from-JavaScript package. Naturally this stuff will mostly be of interest to developers and people who might want to become one.
