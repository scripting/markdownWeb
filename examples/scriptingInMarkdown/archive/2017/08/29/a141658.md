I think of the [Daily podcast](https://api.nytimes.com/podcasts-rss/zKq6WZZLTlbM?apikey=18736e07a8184bf2a3f1a9ed57ed5152) as the _NYT podcast._ It's really good enough to be thought of that way. And you can tell that the people he interviews, who are all [NYT](http://nytimesriver.com/) reporters, feel that way too. It wasn't like that at first. I don't think they knew why they were doing it. Now I think the Daily must be catching on inside the [NYT](https://twitter.com/nyt). The reporters are all very different people. Yesterday's was especially illuminating. I love seeing a venerable old newspaper transition to another great new medium. To help define that medium. It continues to be a great podcast. Mazel tov.
