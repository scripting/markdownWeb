![](http:///scripting.com/images/2017/08/14/funky.png)Here's the third in a [series](http://scripting.com/2017/08/07.html#a093801) of new NPM packages I've produced in the last few months. This one is called [githubpub](https://github.com/scripting/githubpub). It's close to the [ideal](http://scripting.com/2017/08/13.html#a094412) for the [#indieweb](https://twitter.com/search?q=%23indieweb) storage system. You put your web content in GitHub repositories, and they are served directly from there from the public API, with a domain name associated with each site. It's not as easy as Dropbox, and requires a bunch of setup, probably not something for end users, but a bunch of the pieces are there and work.
