It's getting close to the Emmies. How do I know? I'm seeing pitches for the nominees. Shows I love like Fargo and Game of Thrones. And Saturday Night Live. Of all the SNL nominees my two favorites by far are Kate McKinnon and Tom Hanks. McKinnon did so [many](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=Kate+McKinnon+snl&t=h_&iax=1&ia=videos) great skits, as did Hanks (as a guest host) but the one I liked the best, the one I totally adored, was Tom Hanks as the redneck on [Black Jeopardy](http://www.nbc.com/saturday-night-live/video/black-jeopardy-with-tom-hanks/3333590?snl=1). Not just because it was funny but also because it was generous at a time when generosity is in short supply.
