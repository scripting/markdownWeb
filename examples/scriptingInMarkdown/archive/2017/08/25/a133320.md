![](http:///scripting.com/images/2017/08/25/powell.png)Gruber [exposes](https://daringfireball.net/2017/08/wading_through_accuweathers_bullshit_response) a lie by Accuweather re their iOS app. This illustrates something that's missing in tech journalism: We need a general way of debunking tech bullshit, as [Politifact](http://www.politifact.com/) does for politicians. Tech companies get away with it because journalists worship the icons of tech. They can do no wrong. And they're cute, like baby squirrels. The tech gods know you think this about them and fully take advantage of it. Also journalists aren't confident in their understanding of technology.
