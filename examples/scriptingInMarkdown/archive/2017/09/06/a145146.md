The most shocking realization when I did [jury duty](http://scripting.com/davenet/1996/03/01/juryduty.html) was that I was the jury. I had spent so much time reading and watching courtroom dramas that it snuck up on me that the body of the trial was in my own body. After years of tweeting at and about presidents, I don't think _El Orange_ has yet realized he's the guy in the hot seat.
