[2FA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-factor_authentication#Mobile_phone_two-factor_authentication) is not as good as people think because the second factor is not very secure. It's pretty easy to [hijack](https://blog.kraken.com/post/219/security-advisory-mobile-phones/amp/) your phone. This weakness has been used to [steal Bitcoin](https://www.forbes.com/sites/laurashin/2016/12/20/hackers-have-stolen-millions-of-dollars-in-bitcoin-using-only-phone-numbers/amp/).
