![](http:///scripting.com/images/2017/09/03/mrToad.png)[Steely Dan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steely_Dan) was a favorite of mine in a wonderful and uplifting period of my life, when I was a grad student in [Madison](https://www.cs.wisc.edu/) in the mid-late 70s. I was finally an adult, in love, lots of friends and accomplishments everywhere. Firing on all cylinders. One of the most prime periods of my life. I guess I'm going to listen to a lot of Steely Dan in the next few weeks. Kind of the way we listened to a lot of Prince last year. Donald Fagen [wrote](https://twitter.com/search?q=donald+fagen+on+walter+becker&ref_src=twsrc%5Egoogle%7Ctwcamp%5Eserp%7Ctwgr%5Esearch) about his friendship with [Walter Becker](https://www.nytimes.com/2017/09/03/arts/music/walter-becker-dead-steely-dan.html?_r=0). We all were friends with him through their creativity.
