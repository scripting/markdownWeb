I just spent a couple of hours trying to figure out why the tabs on the [new home page](http://scripting.com/) don't work on an iPhone. I decided to ask you all if you had any ideas, so I [recorded a video](https://twitter.com/davewiner/status/907980895688314880) to demo the problem, and outline my theory. Also took out the sidebar, and replaced it with a directory in the About outline. It was responsible for flakiness elsewhere. I'll probably add it back in a year after I've forgotten the problems it caused. (It's even worse than it appears.) Here's a [place](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/issues/26) to discuss.

Update: [Sahil Khan figured it out](https://twitter.com/sahilk/status/907982259591954432). The element #idPageTopText is in front of the tabs, invisibly. It's not a problem on the desktop because there's more space. The fix was easy. Thanks to Mr Khan. (More proof that you often can't see what's right in front of you. In this case literally.)
