I worry what will happen when and if NPM or GitHub add business models to the services they provide. I pay GitHub a monthly fee, to help be somewhat sure [my repos](https://github.com/scripting?tab=repositories) are not filled with ads or made to comply with some rule I don't support. It's so funny to see the press just begin to tune into the power tech companies have to interfere with our creative work. I've been ringing this bell for a very long time.
