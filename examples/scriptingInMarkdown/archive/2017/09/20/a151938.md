![](http:///scripting.com/images/2017/09/20/jayRosen2.png)[Jay Rosen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jay_Rosen) has been is considering cross-posting to Medium. On Twitter he [asked](https://twitter.com/jayrosen_nyu/status/910508236423405569) if he should point to the Medium versions of his post or the [blog](http://pressthink.org/) version. I thought I'd answer on my [blog](http://scripting.com/2017/09/20.html#a111938). It wouldn't make much difference to me. I read his blog through his feed, and I dislike going to Medium, because of the interstitial dialog that pops up before I can read a piece. So I guess it would annoy me to see a Medium link to Jay's posts, but I'd get over it.
