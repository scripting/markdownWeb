One item he didn't mention -- as [Nixon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1972_Nixon_visit_to_China) was able to open American relations with China because he was such an outspoken [anti-communist](https://www.washingtonpost.com/opinions/nixons-great-decision-on-china-40-years-later/2012/02/10/gIQAtFh34Q_story.html?utm_term=.6d97df88e5a5), Trump might be able to get Congress to do this because of his rep as anti-immigrant. The reasoning goes "If it's ok with Trump it must be ok."
