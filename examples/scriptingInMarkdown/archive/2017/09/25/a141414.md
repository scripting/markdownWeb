![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/09/02/mrFrog.png)BTW, to continue yesterday's [thread](http://scripting.com/2017/09/24.html#a094703) about geeks with good hearts. There are tech community people journalists can and should rely on. The press has assumed that we all work at big companies, but the truth is most of the ones you can trust do not. To my friends in journalism, could you please, next chance you get, explain to your peers that there are people who want to help without locking you in. Thanks.
