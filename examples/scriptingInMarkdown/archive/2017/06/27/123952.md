![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/06/27/cowgirl.png)According to Dropbox today is the last day to use [Fargo](http://fargo.io/).

A frequently asked question: _How do I export my outlines?_ It's a trick question. You don't. They are sitting in your Dropbox folder in a standard open [format](http://dev.opml.org/spec2.html), supported by any reasonable outliner. They will still be there tomorrow after Fargo stops working.

BTW, it couldn't hurt to copy them out of your Dropbox folder into a backup somewhere. Better safe than sorry. :cat:

A [reminder](http://scripting.com/2016/08/24/fargoTimesOutOnJune2017.html), they changed the [API](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Application_programming_interface), and today is the last day the old version of the API works. I decided not to maintain Fargo, so when the old API is turned off Fargo will stop working.

If you like the outliner in Fargo, it's available in another package, [LO2](http://littleoutliner.com/). The outliner is also available as [open source](https://github.com/scripting/concord), so if you want, perhaps you can get another developer to make it work with Dropbox.

Fargo is a great product, but what I hoped would happen with it, has not happened. It is tightly integrated with the Dropbox platform, which has not taken off as a developer ecosystem. I wish it had, but the fact is that it hasn't. At some point you have to cut your losses. That's part of development too.
