I love threads like this. John Dickerson (CBS News) [asks](https://twitter.com/jdickerson/status/873525476282818561) what would be a good book for a 15-year-old boy. Huge number of great suggestions. My own: [Time Enough For Love](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_Enough_for_Love), [Confederacy of Dunces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Confederacy_of_Dunces), [Sirens of Titan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sirens_of_Titan) (really any Vonnegut book), [Thinner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thinner_\(novel\)), [Catch-22](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catch-22), [Enders Game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ender%27s_Game). And of course the must-read for every literate American coming of age: [Catcher in the Rye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Catcher_in_the_Rye).
