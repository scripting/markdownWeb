![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/06/23/mrNatural.png)[Richard MacManus](https://twitter.com/ricmac) keeps on truckin. There's nothing more powerful than a persistent and curious user who's relatively fearless.

In a follow-up [post](https://richardmacmanus.com/2017/06/22/openness-rivers-indieweb/) I learned that there is an IndieWeb-approved feed reader called [Woodwind](https://github.com/kylewm/woodwind). That's good news. [RSS](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html) and related technolgies, [including](https://github.com/kylewm/woodwind/issues/66) [OPML](https://github.com/kylewm/woodwind/issues/7) import and export, are essential components of the open web.

BTW, to Richard, I wrote up my [rules for standards-makers](http://this.how/standards), based on experience re what (imho) is important and what works and doesn't. Another item for your consideration.
