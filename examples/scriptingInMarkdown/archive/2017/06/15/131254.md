![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/06/13/weaselsRippedMyFlesh.png)Here's an [item](http://scripting.com/images/2017/06/15/fromTheJsonFeed.png) in my river that came from the Scripting News [JSON feed](http://scripting.com/rss.json). It's fair to say that [River5](https://github.com/scripting/river5) now supports [RSS-in-JSON](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/blob/master/rss-in-json/README.md).

It was more work than I thought it would be. It's not that the [code](https://github.com/scripting/reader/blob/master/davereader.js#L769) assumes XML, it just wasn't organized for variable syntax. It is now. So when they invent the thing that comes after JSON, it'll be somewhat easier to adjust.

Having spent two full days wrestling with this I have to say I wondered more than once if it was worth it.

I haven't got a plan for how to support JSON-based feeds that have different semantics from [RSS](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html). Truth be told, their semantics will have to map on to RSS, as Atom semantics do. I don't wonder whether that exercise is worth it, it's total make-work. A lot of work with _no gain_ in features, performance, interop.The kind of [firedrill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fire_drill) that BigCo's make developers do.
