My titled posts appear in [riverBrowser](https://github.com/scripting/riverBrowser)\-rendered rivers as outlines. Which has a problem. Since clicking on the top line expands the post, there was no direct link to the item. Just fixed that by [adding](http://scripting.com/images/2017/06/15/permalink.png) a purple link in the right margin. Feels just right. (Probably because they date back to the [early](http://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/permalinksNewsAggregators.html) blogosphere.)
