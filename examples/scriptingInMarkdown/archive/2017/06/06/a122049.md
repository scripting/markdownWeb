![](http://scripting.com/2017/06/06/duckduckgo.png)When I link to search queries I'm using [DuckDuckGo](https://duckduckgo.com/) now. I was using Google figuring that they'd be the most long-lived search engine -- the links would likely work years from now. But it all comes down to what "work" [means](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=work&t=h_&ia=web). Google is playing [strategy tax](http://scripting.com/davenet/2001/04/30/strategyTax.html) games with search. I no longer consider it authoritative. It misses too many things that are important for reasons that have nothing to do with their value as a search result. I'm bettting that DuckDuckGo has a stronger philosophy and even if they were not to last as long as Google they're still worth supporting.
