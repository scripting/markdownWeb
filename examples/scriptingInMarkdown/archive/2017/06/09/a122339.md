One good thing that came from all the online craziness of the 2016 election is that it eliminated the pretense that everyone's intentions are good. Also that everyone is who they pretend to be. Also that people who appear to be human actually are. We dealt with that kind of mess in RSS-land in the early 00's. In the end we came through it, and [RSS](http://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html) is still working today. Our experience could contain some lessons for democracy. :smile:
