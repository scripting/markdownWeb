I didn't realize the [Moves](https://www.technologyreview.com/s/510491/every-step-you-take-tracked-automatically/) app was [bought](http://www.moves-app.com/press) by Facebook. It gets a lot of data about the people who use it. I might have thought twice, might not even have installed it, if I had known it was a front for Facebook. Funny thing, when you go to the App Store, it [doesn't tell you](http://scripting.com/images/2017/06/11/movesInAppStore.png) there either. How is a user supposed to know that this innocuous app is actually a tentacle of the Internet's octopus.
