![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/06/17/bowling.png)I just read a [piece](https://shift.newco.co/small-is-the-new-big-5e7f2c715fa1) from [Doc Searls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doc_Searls). I was lucky, I saw it because I happened to be looking at Twitter when it was in my timeline. Had the task I was doing previously taken a little more or less time I would have missed it.

Doc writes in a lot of different places. No blame, just fact. It was true of me too until about a month ago, when I re-concentrated my web writing back where it started on my [blog](http://scripting.com/).

So we need a feed for Doc that gathers together all he does into one [RSS](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html) file that our feed readers can scan so no matter where he posts we'll pick it up.

Idea: Maybe this is something that could be done with Wikipedia? :bulb:
