1\. Readers.

2\. Audience.

3\. Viewers.

4\. Consumers.

When you use those terms to describe your relationship to the rest of the world, be careful, because you might be overlooking value that other people can share. You have become a gatekeeper. A bottleneck. A problem.

Better terms:

Experts.

Constituents.

Taxpayers.

Patients.

Professionals.

Scholars.

Workers.

Students.

Friends.

Children.

Doctors.

Teachers.

Think of others as participants. And think of ways you can get new value from them. Believe me, we're all _aching_ to help make the world better, in ways beyond being part of a crowd. It really needs all of us on board. If you're thinking exclusivity, then you're in the way.
