1\. I am an outliner.

2\. Their stories are outlines.

[Evidence](https://www.axios.com/dc-shooting-congressional-republicans-baseball-practice-2441543433.html). Look at how beautifully organized this piece is. It's like a [dossier](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=dossier&t=hz&ia=meanings). Here's the info you need, presented in a way that's easy on your mind.

So many stories on other sites take forever to get to the point. Here, they give you the organization of the story in a skimmable form. I know where we're going before I read a word.

It builds trust. I more than like it, I love it.
