The other day I wrote a [piece](http://scripting.com/2017/07/11.html#a094345) about how I prefer blogging on the open web because if I have an _Aha!_ idea, over [here](http://scripting.com/2017/07/13.html#a033236), I can implement it. If you click the wedge next to this [item](http://scripting.com/2017/07/13.html#a033236) you can read the full text of that post, without leaving this post.

That's the feature I figured out. One of the nice things that [Old School](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=%22old+school%22+site%3Ascripting.com&t=hq&ia=web) does is create a [JSON file](http://scripting.com/items/2017/07/11/a094345.json) of every post on the site. You can derive its address from the permalink. It took all of today to get the new feature working. I could have waited 100 years and they would never have implemented it in Facebook. They don't listen to me. But _I_ listen to me. :bowling:

I have ideas where this feature could go, but for right now it's really cool just to have the feature. Let's see if I use it. (No guarantees. I don't always use the features I implement.)

PS: The new feature is called _xrefs._
