I didn't discover [cargo shorts](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=cargo+shorts&t=h_&ia=products) until people started dissing them. They are useful. I need pockets esp when I'm out bike riding. If you don't like em, you don't know what you're missing.

And at my age, I don't worry too much about how I look. I'm pretty much invisible anyway. And if you still have a problem with my wearing cargo shorts, you seriously need to get a life. :heart:
