![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/07/10/mao.png)People might not like a world dominated by China. For example, the Internet is heavily [censored](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_censorship_in_China) in China. They also have the [death penalty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital_punishment_in_China). Trials are short, constitutional protections non-existent. The dominant country tends to spread its values to the countries it dominates. They are in a position to demand it. The United States has been in that position for all the lives of everyone alive today. We don't know any other way. We are spoiled and lazy and are trying desperately to pay the price for it.
