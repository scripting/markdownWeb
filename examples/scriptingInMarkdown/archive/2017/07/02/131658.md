This raises the question why a political leader hasn't emerged that puts his or her personality into their tweeting with a strong message of democracy, development and empowerment?

So far the only political leader to fully embrace the [Sources Go Direct](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=Sources+Go+Direct+site%3Ascripting.com&t=h_&ia=web) philosophy is the worst one imaginable.
