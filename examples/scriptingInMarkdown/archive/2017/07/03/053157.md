![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/07/03/emiliaClarke.png)Acting is amazing. I've just tuned into it in a whole new way. I saw a [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BXJ3sebNGeU) interview [of](http://www.rollingstone.com/tv/features/game-of-thrones-cover-story-emilia-clarke-the-queen-of-dragons-tells-all-w489550) [Emilia Clarke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emilia_Clarke), who plays [Khaleesi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daenerys_Targaryen) on [Game of Thrones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_of_Thrones).

Look at how expressive she is. Watching her talk is like watching a great musician practice. Her face is her instrument.

I've been watching Game of Thrones on HBO on and off over the weekend, they're running a bingeathon. I've been watching for the way the actors use their faces. I don't know why I never tuned into this before.
