Anthony Scarramucci, a newbie in the Trump White House, who has yet to be broken by the Cheeto-in-Chief, posted a [philosophical tweet](https://twitter.com/Scaramucci/status/213702657687293952) on June 15.

_Dance like no one is watching. Sing like no one is listening. Love like you've never been hurt and live like its heaven on earth. Mark Twain_

As we all know that was [not said](http://quoteinvestigator.com/2014/02/02/dance/) by Mark Twain, which led to an epic tweet-meme of famous things that were not said by historic figures. I contributed [my own](https://twitter.com/davewiner/status/888630020490616832) to the meme-parade.

_We all live in a yellow submarine, yellow submarine, yellow submarine. Abraham Lincoln at Gettysburg_

Oh the things we do for amusement these days. :heart:
