![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/07/07/george.png)There never was a real controversy over which form of [RSS](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html) reader is better. There are two. Mailbox style and [river](http://scripting.com/2014/06/02/whatIsARiverOfNewsAggregator.html). The correct answer as to which is better is _both._ You need both. People who thought they could live without rivers were presented with Twitter and Facebook whose timelines are rivers, although they muddy them up a bit with algorithms and ads. You need the mailbox approach when you want to see all that an individual has posted recently. Even Twitter [has](https://twitter.com/davewiner) this ability. Even Radio UserLand, in 2002, had it. In 2017 the controversy, if there ever was one, is long-settled.
