![](http://images.scripting.com/archiveScriptingCom/2005/02/21/charlesFoot.gif)[Treason](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treason) probably isn't the right word. Legally it only applies when we're at war. If Trump had aided the Taliban in their fight against the US, that would be treason. If he helped ISIS plan an attack, that would be treason. If he gave North Korea a map of safe hiding places for government officials, that might not actually be treason because we're not currently at war, but there's no doubt it's a betrayal of the country.

The word everyone's looking for is [traitor](http://thesaurus.land/?word=traitor), which is derived from [betray](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=define+traitor&t=hq&ia=definition). By accepting help from Russia to win the election, he betrayed us. He upped the betrayal by accepting the office, knowing that Russia had control over him.

1.  I am a member of [Occam's Church of Political Analysis](http://scripting.com/2017/07/14.html#a112705). Our motto: "It is what it appears to be until proven otherwise."
    
2.  Just wait, the proof is coming. I'm pretty sure you, if you're paying attention at all, know it as well.
