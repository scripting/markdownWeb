![](http:///scripting.com/images/2017/10/10/mrMet.png)Yesterday I did an interview with [Brian McCullough](http://www.internethistorypodcast.com/author/brian-mccullough/) of the [Internet History Podcast](http://www.internethistorypodcast.com/). We talked about many of the things you'd expect. When we got to podcasting, I realized his was exactly the kind of podcast I hoped would emerge when we did the work in the early-mid 2000s. Something that starts out as a hobby, a passion, an idea, and develops into real media with regular listeners. A [weblog for the ears](http://scripting.com/davenet/2003/07/31/chrisLydonsWeblogForTheEar.html) is what I called it back then. I'll let you know when the interview is available.
