![](http:///scripting.com/images/2017/10/13/fatcat.png)Yes indeed NYT, [Silicon Valley is not your friend](https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2017/10/13/opinion/sunday/Silicon-Valley-Is-Not-Your-Friend.html?_r=0) but the [open web](http://scripting.com/liveblog/users/davewiner/2016/01/07/0801.html) is. As often is the case, corporate media has trouble seeing anything but corporate tech. But before there were tech companies, there was the web, which sprung from academia. It instantly wiped out the business models the tech industry had for networking, all of which were designed to lock users in. It took a couple of decades for Silicon Valley to re-establish lock-in, at least partially because news let them take over their distribution system. It may not be too late for the [open web](http://scripting.com/liveblog/users/davewiner/2015/12/27/0724.html), but it needs a lot of help.
