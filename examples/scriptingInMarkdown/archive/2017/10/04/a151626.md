Speaking as a lifelong Knicks fan, currently somewhat in remission (hah!), I have to say Fox Sports hit the nail on the head with the [Knicks Hopeless](http://www.nydailynews.com/sports/basketball/knicks/hopeless-knicks-greet-nyc-subway-riders-due-fs1-ad-campaign-article-1.3538210) campaign. The moment of max hopelessness was reading the headline that [Tim Hardaway](http://www.ajc.com/sports/nba-fans-stunned-tim-hardaway-million-deal/3BDBrrw0u6gqnboyT23wBP/) had gotten a $71 million contract and then learning that it was the Knicks who paid that for him. Until Dolan leaves, the Knicks will continue to [sputter](https://www.si.com/nba/2016/06/22/derrick-rose-trade-knicks-worst-deals-team-history-andrea-bargnani) in hopelessness.
