[Two-factor](https://www.theverge.com/2017/7/10/15946642/two-factor-authentication-online-security-mess) actually makes your account _less_ secure. Security on phones is terrible. If your phone is hijacked, the hackers could control of any two-factor account you have. And it's easy to talk a telco into giving you control of someone else's phone. Two-factor is a very misleading name. It's not two factors, it's the _weakest of_ two factors. It's not _and_ it's _or._
