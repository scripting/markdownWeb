I changed my name to [140-char Dave](http:///scripting.com/images/2017/10/01/140chardave.png) on Twitter, not as an endorsement of the 140-char limit, the exact opposite. It's a disclaimer. You're not getting full-strength Dave, you're getting a highly abbreviated version. I favor no limit, with a simple "show more" [link](http://scripting.com/2015/10/02/whatWouldAFatTweetLookLike.html) on tweets over 140. I understand some people don't favor such a change, but imho they are wrong. :boom:
