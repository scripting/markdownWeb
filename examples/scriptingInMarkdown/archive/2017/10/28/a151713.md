An important point that I [still](http://scripting.com/2017/10/27.html#a112546) haven't made well enough is that people could stop trying to get ahead and put an emphasis on **working together** to get us back on track. There are clear tradeoffs. I'm saying this -- take the other branch. Instead of getting ahead, work with others even if it means they get ahead not you.
