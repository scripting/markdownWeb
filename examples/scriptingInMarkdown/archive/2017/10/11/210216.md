First, I wish there was a table somewhere with all the emoji and their hex equivs expressed in HTML format, like this: F4A9; But it's not so bad, you just have to do it for yourself.

1.  Look up the char on this [page](http://www.unicode.org/emoji/charts/full-emoji-list.html), and copy the "code point value", something that looks [like](http:///scripting.com/images/2017/10/11/findingEmojiCode.png) this: U+1F4A9.
    
2.  Remove the U+ part.
    
3.  Add &#x at the beginning and a semicolon at the end -- yielding: &#x1F4A9;
    

Here are the translations of a few characters:

poop == U+1F4A9 == 💩

fire == U+1F525 == 🔥

neutral\_face == U+1F610 == 😐

smile\_cat == U+1F63B == 😻

clap == U+1F44F == 👏
