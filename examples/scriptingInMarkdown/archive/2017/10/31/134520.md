![](http:///scripting.com/images/2017/10/31/bojack.png)I'm looking for a show to binge.

A TV series, like Deadwood, Fargo, The Sopranos, [Breaking Bad](http://scripting.com/stories/2010/08/29/breakingBadOhSoGood.html), [Battlestar Galactica](http://scripting.com/stories/2009/03/06/howBattlestarGalacticaEnds.html), [The West Wing](http://scripting.com/2016/05/30/1295.html), Mad Men, Six Feet Under, [The Wire](http://scripting.com/stories/2010/09/17/myFifthTimeThroughTheWire.html) — these are some of my favorite classics. More recently The Leftovers, [Bojack Horseman](http://scripting.com/2015/08/30/whyYouMustWatchBojackHorseman.html), Mr Mercedes and Mr Robot.

I did something similar with podcasts, a couple of years ago, and the resulting website, [Podcatch.com](http://podcatch.com/), has been supplying me with great listening ever since. I have a hunch something similar could be done with TV series.

As with podcasts, I promise to share what I learn about bingeable TV, but it'll be different because TV series are different from podcasts. :rocket:

I have started [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/dave.winer.12/posts/643494485857960) and [Twitter](https://twitter.com/davewiner/status/925365704236388353) threads for this, as well as an [issue](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/issues/39) on the GitHub repo. Tell me what your top all time favorite binges are, with a focus on ones other people might _not_ know about.
