![](http:///scripting.com/images/2017/10/30/laughnow.png)So, in the [Internet History Podcast](http://www.internethistorypodcast.com/2017/10/dave-winer-on-the-open-web-blogging-podcasting-and-more/), I talked a bit about the idea of "working together," and somewhat unfairly used [Marco Arment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marco_Arment) as an example of someone who wasn't receptive to my new ideas. I recorded this [10-minute podcast](http://scripting.com/2017/10/30/aPodcastForMarco.m4a) to explain what I had in mind, generally, not about specific products (it was a long time ago, the opportunity long-since passed). Most of the time when you knock on the door saying _here's a free idea,_ people think you want something. But sometimes it goes somewhere and that's when really interesting stuff happens. This is worth talking about.
