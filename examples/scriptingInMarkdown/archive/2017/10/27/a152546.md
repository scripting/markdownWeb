I've been trying to figure out how to put this idea in words. The world is teetering on the edge of nuclear war. The American president is provoking it. Meanwhile, most people are trying to get ahead as if everything was normal. Nazis marched in the street this summer, killed an innocent protestor, yet were defended by the same president. Very fine people, he [said](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=%22very+fine+people%22&t=hg&ia=news). Companies are ripping off customers, shutting down the EPA, and journalism is thinking about page views, and congress people are thinking about reelection. As if any of that has any meaning in a world where _even one_ nuclear weapon is detonated in war. I don't think people understand how much danger we are in right now.
