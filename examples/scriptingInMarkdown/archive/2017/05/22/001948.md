Examples of products of mine that have JSON file formats.

1\. In [1999.io](http://1999.io/) the entire blog is stored in a single [JSON file](http://friends.farm/users/davewiner/chatLog.json).

2\. All rivers produced by [River5](https://github.com/scripting/river5) are [JSONP](http://riverjs.org/) [files](http://radio3.io/rivers/tech.js).

3\. Historically [subscription lists](https://github.com/scripting/river5/tree/master/lists) are OPML files. In River5 I relaxed this rule and we allow either JSON or simple text files alongside the original OPML format.

Lots of good stuff in the links hanging off this post. ;-)
