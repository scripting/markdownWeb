My friend @jayrosen\_nyu calls it the _[View From Nowhere](http://pressthink.org/2010/11/the-view-from-nowhere-questions-and-answers/)._ The reality that news pretends doesn't exist. Today the reality is overwhelming, because the envelope that contains the news, the companies that own journalism, are such a huge part of the story, every story. Media has become much more pervasive in the last 20 years. That's why news has drifted further into Nowhere, and it's become more of a fantasy.
