![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/05/31/bike.png)It comes back like swimming or riding a bike. When I start the day there's a blank section of the outline where my ideas will show up. I press the big [plus](http://scripting.com/images/2017/05/31/bigPlus.png) icon to create a new section and type: _Good morning sports fans!_ I have no idea where this came [from](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Good_Morning_Sports_Fans). But I used to do it in the old days, so it feels right. Later in the day the greeting might be deleted or move. But at the top of the day it's at the top of the page.
