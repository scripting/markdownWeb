There are a bunch of changes and new features. There's a [link icon](http://scripting.com/images/2017/12/13/permaLinkIcon.png) in the upper left corner of the program box. This is the permalink to the page for that program. Previously there was no easy way to get a permalink. Also there's a menu bar at the top of the screen. The links below the program box have been moved there. I expect there will be more things to link to over time, and we needed an approach that would have some ability to extend.
