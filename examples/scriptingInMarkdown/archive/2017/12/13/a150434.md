![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/12/04/bowersWilkinsPx.png)I have these [sexy new headphones](https://www.amazon.com/Bowers-Wilkins-Cancelling-Headphones-Performance/dp/B07574TYWW/ref=sr_ph_1?ie=UTF8&qid=1513177553&sr=sr-1&keywords=bowers+%26+wilkins+px). Great sound. But I keep accidentally hitting the controls, which are not labeled. I have no idea what they do, other than they turn the music off, and I can't figure out how to turn it back on. This gets inconvenient when I'm out and about and it's _fucking cold_ outside. They should user-test these things in the conditions people really use them in.
