It's an annual tradition, started by NakedJen, to cram in as much movie watching on Christmas Day as possible. I joined the [NJFF](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=site%3Ascripting.com+njff&t=hf&ia=web) revolution in 2007 or so, and every year since have been partaking. Sometimes even in person with NJ herself who lives in a remote part of America, out west [somewhere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_United_States). This year, I will be in NYC.

The pre-festival festivities have already commenced with The Post (see review below, spoiler -- I do not recommend).

Here are some of the movies I'm thinking of for tomorrow...

1.  Molly's Game
    
    Written and directed by Aaron Sorkin, lots of stars. How bad could it be?
    
2.  I, Tonya
    
    Tonya Harding.
    
3.  Lady Bird
    
    Gets great reivews.
    
4.  Phantom Thread
    
    [Paul Thomas Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Thomas_Anderson) film, starring Daniel Day-Lewis.
    
    These two brought us one of the best films ever -- There Will Be Blood.
    
    I rate this a must-see.
    
5.  The Shape of Water
    
    Guillermo del Toro, what more is there to say?
    
6.  Bright (on Netflix)
    
    The reviewers hate it but viewers love it.
    
    Just for that, I'm in favor of it. :boom:
    
7.  All the Money in the World
    
    Ridley Scott. About the Getty kidnapping. They removed Kevin Spacey at the last minute.
    
8.  Bombshell: The Hedy Lamarr Story
    
    I always want to know more about the great Hedy Lamarr.
