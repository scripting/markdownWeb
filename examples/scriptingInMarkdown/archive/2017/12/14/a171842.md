I finished [The Crown season 2](http://www.vulture.com/2017/12/the-crown-season-2-watch-the-real-historical-footage.html) last night. Very nice. It's a little confusing as new characters are introduced without explanation. Feels like it's just getting started. So many characters to develop yet. And the Queen herself. Her majesty [Claire Foy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claire_Foy) is just amazing, and the queen's husband, played by Matt Smith, is revealed to be complex as well. I love this show. Almost ready to [give](http://bingeworthy.io/tv/?id=00352) it a 5.
