![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/12/29/napster.png)[Napster](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=site%3Ascripting.com+napster&t=hf&ia=web), one of the most culturally significant inventions, doesn't get enough credit imho. It's when the [dam](http://scripting.com/2014/12/17/whatIfTheRiaaHadEmbracedNapster.html) broke, when music programming became something we can do for ourselves. It's hard to remember how it used to work, we were so controlled by the scarcity of music. After Napster, we assume we can listen to anything we want.
