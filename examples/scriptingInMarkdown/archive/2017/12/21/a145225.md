![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/12/21/kraftParmesanCheese.png)Nieman Lab runs a [year-end series](http://www.niemanlab.org/collection/predictions-2018/) of essays by experts on the future of news. If I had a say --> In the future news will include perspectives from voters and taxpayers, people who are governed, the ideas and questions they have, without experts getting in the middle. Also "public editors" will be members of the public, not professional journalists.
