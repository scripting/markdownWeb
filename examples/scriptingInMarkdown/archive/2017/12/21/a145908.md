Really the greatest disease of journalism is its closed [topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topology). No ideas can enter. It runs off its own fumes, only. Nothing can take it off course. Same assumptions apply, always, even when discredited. Allowing non-journalists access to the flow would change that in an instant. Which is why journalists are so totally opposed to it. They do not want change.
