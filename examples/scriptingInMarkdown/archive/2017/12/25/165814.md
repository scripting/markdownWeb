A game you can play with a computer and a mouse.

1.  Pick any spot in the world where [Street View](https://www.google.com/streetview/understand/) has been.
    
2.  Zoom out to a level where the streets are a blur of blue.
    
3.  Drag the little guy into the middle of the blur.
    
4.  Let up the mouse at a random spot.
    
5.  See where you landed.
    
6.  Repeat.
    

PS: Hidden benefit. It'll drive the Google algorithm crazy. :rocket:
