Some people, esp in tech and journalism, think if you're nice to them, or help them, that you must want something from them. I have to admit that _sometimes_ I do want something, but I try to keep that decoupled from what I'm doing at the moment. No need for a quid pro quo. But I don't mind reciprocation. It's a subtle distinction. Anyway, I don't think I've ever posted this. I really actually like helping people. It makes me feel good. Esp when they do something powerful with the help. :boom:
