![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/12/19/nixon.png)Caught a bit of the [Brian Lehrer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_Lehrer) show on [WNYC](http://www.wnyc.org/). Heard a common idea, that the Trump thing is a replay of Watergate. "At this point in Watergate..." his [guest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Holtzman) was heard to say. Dear people. _This is not Watergate._ There [were](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Miller_\(political_advisor\)) [no](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Bannon) [Nazis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastian_Gorka) [in](http://www.nydailynews.com/news/national/charlottesville-suspect-james-alex-fields-indicted-10-counts-article-1.3708991?utm_content=buffera8300&utm_medium=social&utm_source=twitter.com&utm_campaign=NYDailyNewsTw) Watergate. The most offensive American at the time was [Archie Bunker](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sOnTZipv03M), who, like [Trump](https://twitter.com/seanodotcom/status/943148418855157760), was a TV character. The Mets had [won](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1969_World_Series) the World Series. The Internet had [barely](https://www.wired.com/2011/12/1219altair-8800-computer-kit-goes-on-sale/) gotten started. The [moon](https://www.nasa.gov/mission_pages/apollo/apollo11.html) landing. [Woodstock](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wyx053CNMag) had just happened. The [Beatles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Beatles) [were](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uyAAHpkJMc4) [all](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Break-up_of_the_Beatles) still [alive](http://scripting.com/images/2017/12/19/beatles.png). It was a different time. Trump is not Nixon. Nixon could be shamed. He resigned rather than put the country (and himself) through impeachment. Trump seems to revel in his own degradation and humiliation. He thrives off it. This won't go the way of Watergate. I'd give you odds. I think there will be a lot of dumbfounded people when Trump doesn't play the script the way we wish he would.
