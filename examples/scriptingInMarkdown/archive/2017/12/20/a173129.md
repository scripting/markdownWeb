![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/12/20/sonyHeadphones.png)I'm trying out another pair of (for me at least) high-end headphones, [Sony WH100XM2](https://www.amazon.com/Sony-Noise-Cancelling-Headphones-WH1000XM2/dp/B074KDJVS2). I really like these, esp the app that configures the set. I'm charging it up now, then I'll go out and try them in the real world. One thing -- they're lighter on the head than the [Bowers & Wilkins PX](http://scripting.com/2017/12/04.html#a213202), which I decided to keep. The sound was the reason. Really rich and deep and powerful. I've never owned headphones that can do this. Consistent with my motto, if the world is going to end, I might as well have some nice headphones.
