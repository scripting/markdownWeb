![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2021/03/30/grannySmithApple.png)I want to connect **RSS to AT Proto**, both ways, inbound and out. Toward that end, today I [helped Leaflet](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/issues/359), a very nice [editor](https://leaflet.pub/) for AT Proto, make their outbound RSS feed better. We should have all our best editors, everywhere, hooked up over popular web standards. And I want my posts to show up not only in Bluesky but also in [standard.site](https://standard.site/). I was able to do that with micro.blog which is the [swiss army knife](https://daytona.scripting.com/search?q=%22swiss%20army%20knife%22) of interop in the web of the 2020's. Getting independent developers working with each other is my mission, because first you have to have [interop](http://scripting.com/2021/12/16/153331.html?title=myProductIsInterop) between the people before you can get interop between the apps.
