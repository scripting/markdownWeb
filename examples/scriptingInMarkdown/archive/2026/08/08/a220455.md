Here's the [sign of success](https://pdsls.dev/at://did:plc:oety7qbfx7x6exn2ytrwikmr/site.standard.document/3mslhd5zxkg22). ([screen shot](https://imgs.scripting.com/2026/08/08/atprotoScreenshot.png).) I wrote a [post on demo.rss.chat](https://demo.rss.chat/?id=395) was automatically cross-posted to Bluesky via micro.blog. Because the post has a title, it was routed to [standard.site](https://standard.site/). What does that mean? I still don't really understand, when I do I will explain it here. But part of the answer is that we now have a [data structure](https://pdsls.dev/at://did:plc:oety7qbfx7x6exn2ytrwikmr/site.standard.document/3mslhd5zxkg22) in _atmosphere_ that [looks like](https://imgs.scripting.com/2026/08/08/atprotoScreenshot.png) an RSS item with different names for things. Are there feed readers in AT-Proto-Land? If so, can we ping one of them to say hey there's a new post in the standard.site world, so please show it to subscribers? And I keep wondering, why not just make all of Bluesky capable of handling the features standard.site calls for. Twenty years is too long to have such a limited view of what text is. See [textcasting](https://textcasting.org/) for more dogma.
