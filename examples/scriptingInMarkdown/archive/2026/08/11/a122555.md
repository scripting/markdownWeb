![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2026/05/19/reallySimpleNet.png)Apparently the nightly email didn't go out last night. The problem was an error when it tried to read the Linkblog entries for the day. I've seen reports that these errors happen when you try to read the [Links tab](http://scripting.com/?tab=links) on scripting.com. The error shouldn't kill the email sending, it should just omit the linkblog entries, and publish the rest. These kinds of problems always pop up at the worst time. Luckily yesterday wasn't a big news day, and you can read about it [on the blog](http://scripting.com/2026/08/10.html).
