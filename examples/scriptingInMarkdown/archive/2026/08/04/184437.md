![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2023/05/17/pinky.png)I was into [Ayn Rand](http://scripting.com/stories/2011/01/01/theWorldIsSocialist.html) when I was a teen until I realized that the Great Man theory was a lot of bunk written for teenagers who can't believe how stupid the adults are. The teens are right about that, the adults have no idea wtf they're doing. Never did. And yet somehow the world can support most of the humans. And the reason that works is we build systems iteratively to meet the needs for everyone and when things change, they change. We're a species-oriented animal. And that means unfortunately for the dreamers among us, [we are socialists](http://scripting.com/stories/2011/01/01/theWorldIsSocialist.html) because the world is socialist All of us. Even the people who say they aren't.
