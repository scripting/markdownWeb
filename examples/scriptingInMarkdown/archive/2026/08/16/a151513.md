![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2023/04/24/cheshireCat.png)[Brent Simmons](https://daytona.scripting.com/search?q=%22Brent%20Simmons%22) posted a [note in RSS.chat](https://rss.chat/?id=530) about how to connect with [NetNewsWire](https://netnewswire.com/). I have a fairly detailed [response](https://rss.chat/?id=531), that says [dynamic OPML](https://www.google.com/search?q=%22dynamic+OPML%22) is the way to go. I know serving it publicly will be a problem, that's why we're going to move FeedLand into position to solve that. [FreshRSS](https://freshrss.github.io/FreshRSS/en/developers/OPML.html), [InoReader](http://scripting.com/2025/12/18/135613.html) and my own [FeedLand](https://docs.feedland.com/gettingstarted.md) already support it. It feels like this is getting established. I still want a podcast client to work here. I've pitched a few of them. Will continue.
