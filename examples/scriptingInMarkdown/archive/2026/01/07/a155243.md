[Remember](https://www.cnet.com/culture/facebook-f8-one-graph-to-rule-them-all/) when [OG metadata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facebook_Platform#Open_Graph_protocol) was new? 16 years ago. It's one of those things that's widely forgotten, but still widely in use. I just wrote a [post](https://bsky.app/profile/scripting.com/post/3mbtstigya22o) on Bluesky that [demonstrates](https://imgs.scripting.com/2026/01/07/bigAppleUgh.png). That could be a new feature in WordPress, or the decision whether or not to display it could be up to the reader app (in this case Bluesky). If it were up to the reader we might need a way to signal that to the reader from the CMS. In the new WordLand, we use the image as an icon. It's completely wrong to use it as an OG Metadata image in Bluesky -- realllly embarrassing ([screen shot](https://imgs.scripting.com/2026/01/07/bigAppleUgh.png)).
