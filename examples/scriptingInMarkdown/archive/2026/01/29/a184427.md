The best description for the web I've ever heard is [small pieces loosely joined](https://www.amazon.com/Small-Pieces-Loosely-Joined-Unified/dp/0738205435). That really gets to the essence of it. The pieces stand on their own, up to a point, when they are joined to other pieces. And you can un-join and re-join them. I see a lot of things that say they're part of the web that can't do the arbitrary joining that's central to what the web is.
