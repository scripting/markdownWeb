![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2025/09/17/reallySimpleKetchup.png)Next year I have to go to [FOSDEM](https://fosdem.org/2026/). This year's conference is going on right now in [Brussels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brussels). If you're there and reading this -- say a hello for me. I realize the [piece](http://scripting.com/2026/01/30/150403.html) I wrote yesterday about the future of the social web is equally relevant now for FOSDEM. My prescription: carefully [start over](http://scripting.com/2026/01/30/150403.html#a150735) with a simple peer-to-peer service and build on that foundation. I would use [websockets](https://github.com/scripting/feedlandSocket). You will have to deal with issues of centralization, and at each point decide how much you're willing to trade off ease of use and performance for decentralization. I think you can go pretty far without stripping the gears of users, but there has to be some amount of centralization, identity and storage, being the two biggies. Please read the piece, it's short, [bulleted style](http://scripting.com/2026/01/30/150403.html), highly opinionated, and based on my experience with systems like the ones they're working on. (Who does he think he is? Just a software developer, working hard for a feature-complete web who thinks we've been stuck in a few ruts for a depressingly long time.)
