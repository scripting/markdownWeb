![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2026/10/20/guyKawasakiHeadShot.png)My friend [Guy Kawasaki](https://www.goodreads.com/quotes/391717-steve-jobs-has-a-saying-that-a-players-hire-a) has a quote from the early Mac division at Apple. "Steve Jobs has a saying that A players hire A players. But B players hire C players, and C players hire D players. It doesn't take long to get to F players. This trickle-down effect causes bozo explosions in companies." You see this over and over among the very rich in tech. The money is supposed to make them invincible, but they don't hire the best people, I guess, for fear of being found out to not be the genius that the money says they are.
