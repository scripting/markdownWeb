Maximally minimal. I got interested in what it would take to do this, and asked ChatGPT for a [plan](https://gist.github.com/scripting/c81461104f079b7a017802286853c4f1). I definitely don't agree with all of it, forget about the other formats, we're only interested in Markdown. No frameworks, not "try to avoid frameworks." None. Maximal simplicity for the code, make it easy to follow. No hidden bullshit. If you want to implement it, please do. Let me know if you have something to try.
