Automattic has created a [Stewart Brand website](https://stewartbrand.org/) to honor one of the most creative and respected people of my generation of the web. Chronicling his work is time well spent. "I'm envious," I [wrote](https://x.com/davewiner/status/2109373850731331838) in response. "The tech industry is gradually disconnecting the early web, pre-https. A hundred years from now they may have your writing and pictures, but the early web will be gone. The web as designed was very resilient, but it can’t survive deliberate attempt to destroy." I think Stewart would understand why we should help the web we loved so much, archive itself. Think about it this way. Would you rather visit the Hearst Castle at San Simeon, or read about it in a book? You'd want to do both. I want my work to survive a hundred years as it was when it was first published. This would be a good thing for us to work on, together, imho. I did the first [Long Now bet](https://longbets.org/2/) for Stewart's Long Now foundation. I always thought it was the first, now they say it's the second? Did someone pull rank? :-)
