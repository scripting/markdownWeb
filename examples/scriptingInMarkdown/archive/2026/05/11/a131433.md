![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2024/03/23/blogrollMan.png)Members of the WordPress community. Monday morning is a good time to check out [WordPress News](https://wp.feedland.org/) via FeedLand at [wp.feedland.org](https://wp.feedland.org/). You can also subscribe to the [list of feeds](https://feedland.social/opml?screenname=davewiner&catname=wordpress) this site follows in your own feed reader, and if you have a WordPress news site, please post the URL [here](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/issues/353) so we can send readers to _your blog_ too. I think there are a lot of would-be bloggers out there that need a slight kick in the pants to get going. I'm happy to provide readers if you provide the ideas. There's a lot of power in WordPress that no one knows about. Let's help other users and developers find the good stuff. If you have questions or suggestions, here's a [new thread on GitHub](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/issues/353).
