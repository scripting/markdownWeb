My recommendation for [Automattic](https://bsky.app/profile/pfefferle.org/post/3mmh6zmfkrs2r) and Bluesky.

*   Bluesky supports RSS 2.0 inbound and outbound.
    
*   Bluesky eliminates its character limit, allows bold, italic styles. Links. Optional titles. Users can edit their posts. More [here](https://textcasting.org/).
    

Automattic already fully supports RSS 2.0 in both directions, in all their products.

This gives us the most interop with the most respect for [prior art](http://essaysfromexodus.scripting.com/priorArtDesignMethod). No need to [reinvent](https://bsky.app/profile/pfefferle.org/post/3mmh6zmfkrs2r). There's nothing special about Bluesky, they can use what we've all been using for 20+ years.

It's really very simple, let's hook everything together and let the users and developers create.
