A [post](http://scripting.com/2026/05/09/135221.html) from Scripting News, automatically mirrored to a [WordPress site](https://daveverse.org/2026/05/09/having-a-good-bench/), and that flowed via ActivityPub to [Mastodon](https://mastodon.social/@scripting@daveverse.org/116544994475978236), via a relatively new feature in WordPress. Almost by accident Mastodon supports long text, styling and links -- even though their editor doesn't generate it, if it comes from the outside it will respect the styling.

Below is a post on Mastodon coming from WordPress. Masto's limits aren't enforced, and that's good.
