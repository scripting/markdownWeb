![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/12/26/frontier.png)Speaking of memorials, do you remember [UserLand Frontier](https://daytona.scripting.com/search?q=Frontier) and all the cool stuff we developed with it? Like Manila, Radio, XML-RPC, RSS, OPML, adding so many cool open features to the web. When people asked how we did all that, I said great tools. That was Frontier. [Jake Savin](https://daytona.scripting.com/search?q=%22Jake%20Savin%22), one of the 1990s UserLanders, is continuing the project to get it running on today's hardware and for today's web. He's documenting it [on his blog](https://jakesav.in/2026/05/25/a-new-frontier-old-databases-are-new-again/). I can't wait to use it. Watching him go through the process has been eye-opening. He's basically retracing all the steps it took to create it as done by four or five people over quite a few years, a long time ago. But when it's running and I don't doubt that he will get it running, it'll be fascinating to see if I remembered it correctly. If you remember Frontier fondly, I suggest you [subscribe to his feed](https://feedland.com/?feedurl=https%3A%2F%2Fjakesav.in%2Ffeed%2F) in your favorite RSS feed reader.
