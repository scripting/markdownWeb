Scoble asks [on Twitter](https://x.com/bullmancuso/status/2050926863648702490) if there are successful companies that have an open source product. There are lots of them. There are markets where users and developers won't even consider your product or service if it isn't open source. It's a trust issue. I offered an [example](https://x.com/bullmancuso/status/2050946354164195800), WordPress, which probably wouldn't have launched well if it wasn't open source.
