![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2025/09/17/reallySimpleKetchup.png)I've been teaching Claude why we [favor Markdown](https://textcasting.org/#1683751572000). "We add support for Markdown editing wherever we can, because people _like_ Markdown and they should. It makes things simple and guarantees a certain level of flexibility for their writing far beyond the standards of twitter-like systems with [tiny little text boxes](http://scripting.com/2024/01/14/031201.html). If you don't really support Markdown people figure it out right away. But the character limits and stuff like that seem more technical to users. Markdown support says clearly -- you're really on the web."
