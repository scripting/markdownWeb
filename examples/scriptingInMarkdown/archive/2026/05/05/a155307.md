It's interesting what Jeremy Herve and Matthias Pfefferle at Automattic have [created](https://activitypub.blog/2026/05/05/radical-speed-month-the-reader-meets-the-fediverse/) with the [WordPress feed reader](https://wordpress.com/reader), hooking it up to Activity Pub and AT Proto, the same way they hooked up those protocols to the standard WordPress blogging functionality (not sure how technically accurate this is). They're also supporting the Google Reader api for users of products like NetNewsWire.
