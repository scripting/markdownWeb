Apparently Substack does not implement [MCP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_Context_Protocol), which is basically the [XML-RPC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/XML-RPC) of AI. According to [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/share/69f4aae0-1778-83ea-bf7a-9986cbb4e7e5) they have a limited API that some independent developers have bridged to MCP. But as you would expect from a tight silo like Substack, the API lets you read but not write. They want you to use their editor, what they don't want is to be one of 20 distributors of your writing. They want an exclusive and they get it.
