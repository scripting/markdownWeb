Often when I use the term _RSS feeds_ it will link to this page.

In the coming weeks and months I'm going to talk a lot about [RSS feeds](http://scripting.com/2026/06/15/140536.html). I want to be clear, that it is a short hand for RSS, Atom and RDF. It makes the writing flow better, and it gives me a place to provide the technical details for people who need them.

We use the [Feedparser package](https://www.npmjs.com/package/feedparser) to read the feeds, so basically we support the same feed formats they do.
