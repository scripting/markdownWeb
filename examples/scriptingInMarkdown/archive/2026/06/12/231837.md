There's an [account](https://tomkahe.com/@GiftArticles) on Mastodon containing a flow of gift articles.

Because Mastodon supports outbound RSS, you can subscribe to it in any RSS reader.

But the RSS is not very good. Have a [look](https://tomkahe.com/@GiftArticles.rss).

So I built a little app in my new scripting language, with the help of Claude, and boom now I can read the output of the mangled feed.

I don't know what is responsible, probably has something to do with the account, and something to do with how Mastodon. But the information _is_ being communicated.

[https://giftarticles.feedland.org/](https://giftarticles.feedland.org/)
