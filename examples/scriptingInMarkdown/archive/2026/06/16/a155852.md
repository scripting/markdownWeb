Everyone wants to know things humans can do better than AI systems. One answer — relate with humans. The machines have no clue how our minds work. They act as if we're just like them. They could tell you all about it, from books they read, but they've never related with humans as humans. There's a great [speech](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8GY3sO47YYo) by Robin Williams in Good Will Hunting, where he explains how reading about something isn't the same as living it.
