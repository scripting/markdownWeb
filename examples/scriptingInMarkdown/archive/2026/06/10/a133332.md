If you run a feed reader or other form of news consuming software, you will encounter RSS 2.0 feeds that support rssCloud. This [example Node app](https://github.com/scripting/reallysimple/tree/main/demos/clouddemo) shows you how to hook into the network to get instant updates. No polling. As fast as a twitter-like system
