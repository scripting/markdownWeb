Why email newsletters made sense. Email has no character limits, can represent bold and italic, links, titles, enclosures, basically most [features of the web](https://textcasting.org/), and social media places limits on what writers can write. That's where the literate social web went, and the bloggers too. Like how birds are really dinosaurs.
