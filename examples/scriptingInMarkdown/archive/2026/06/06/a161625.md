[Star City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_City_\(TV_series\)) is very good. It's good enough that you have to watch each episode at least twice to get the idea of what's really going on. I stopped watching the show it is a sequel for, [For All Mankind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/For_All_Mankind_\(TV_series\)), because it got incredibly juvenile and sitcom-like. But Star City is serious, at least in the first three episodes.
