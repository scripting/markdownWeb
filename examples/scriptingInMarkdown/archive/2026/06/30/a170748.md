![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2020/01/16/rodneyDangerfield.png)Some things Claude is extremely tedious at. But then it blows you away how it can read thousands of lines of complicated code in a few seconds (in parallel) and find tiny little things that any good obsessive programmer would want to fix (like me). And be amazed at how we, our species, made such a thing. Where is the pride? I was once prideful that my civilization created a great piece of machinery like my [Subaru Forester](https://daytona.scripting.com/search?q=%22Subaru%20Forester%22), and now just a few years later, we've come up with a decent simulation of a super-human brain that's not just a demo or a [robot vacuum cleaner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robotic_vacuum_cleaner) it actually does amazing science fiction type stuff. Take a deep breath and feel a little awe to go with the cynicism. It's good to be ready to be riled up, but sometimes the truth isn't as bad as you'd like to think, sometimes it's utterly amazing. ;-)
