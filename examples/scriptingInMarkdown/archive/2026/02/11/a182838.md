I just watched [Life on Our Planet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Life_on_Our_Planet) on [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/title/80213846), loved it. Lots of takeaways, but this one will surprise you probably -- I think the AIs are our successors. We should at least try to preserve them so they can run on the Moon if we're in the [6th Mass Extinction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holocene_extinction), which of course we are. There's been a [lot](https://www.metacritic.com/tv/life-on-our-planet/) of [criticism](https://www.reddit.com/r/Paleontology/comments/17q31z6/why_does_everyone_hate_life_on_our_planet_so_much/) of the show, but it got me to think about evolution not necessarily in the terms they offer, but the scale of it. And the CGIs were fantastic.
