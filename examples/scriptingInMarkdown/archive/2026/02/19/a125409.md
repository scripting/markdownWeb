I would like to have an [OPML subscription list](https://daytona.scripting.com/search?q=%22OPML%20subscription%20list%22) containing the feeds of all RSS-based products. So when they update everyone can see what they did. I'd also like to encourage people to post screen shots so we can get an idea of what the product does before installing it. Maybe it's for a platform we don't use? Let's have a new practice where we all know what everyone is doing.
