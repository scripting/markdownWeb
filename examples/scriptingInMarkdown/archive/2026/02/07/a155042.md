[Doc has a idea](https://doc.searls.com/2026/02/07/endweek/) how to stop teams from [tanking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanking). Get rid of the [lottery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_draft_lottery). He's right of course. Think of the futility of tanking in the NBA when last year the #1 pick went to the [Dallas Mavericks](https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/45117355/mavericks-win-nba-draft-lottery-18-odds), who were not a lottery team with only a 1.8% chance of getting the first pick. They got a [player](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooper_Flagg) who looks to be a great star but you can't always tell if a #1 pick will turn out to be a star, sometimes [they do](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LeBron_James), but [often](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrea_Bargnani) [not](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zion_Williamson).
