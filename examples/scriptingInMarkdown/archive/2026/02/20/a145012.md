I'd like to have an OPML subscription list with feeds with news about specific feed-based products. I started a [thread](https://github.com/scripting/reallysimple/issues/21) on the [reallysimple repo](https://github.com/scripting/reallysimple) for people to post links to such feeds. Once I have enough feeds, I'll publish the URL of the subscription list. We should, in this community, of all communities, a good way to communicate about developments. Too many good ideas get lost without this.
