Highly recommend the HBO two-part [interview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mel_Brooks:_The_99_Year_Old_Man!) with and profile of [Mel Brooks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mel_Brooks) who was 99 years old when the interviews were done. Includes quotes from lots of famous comedians. And the philosophy of comedy as art. So many things to say. Why is physical humor the funniest? And the funniest of all the excerpts was the [farting scene](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1ARXuhoPsTc) in [Blazing Saddles](https://www.metacritic.com/movie/blazing-saddles/). Humans are so damned simple.
