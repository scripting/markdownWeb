Did some work on my RSS feed this morning.

1.  There was only one [source:account](https://source.scripting.com/#1405172297000) element in [my RSS feed](http://scripting.com/rss.xml) and it was to a [Twitter account](https://x.com/davewiner) I lost control of a few weeks ago, the account I had there since 2006, that at one time was in the top 10 accounts on Twitter. I changed the account to [bullmancuso](https://x.com/bullmancuso) -- one of my many testing sites on Twit, and added my [Mastodon](https://mastodon.social/@davew) and [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/scripting.com) accounts. I will check the bullmancuso account every so often, but the masto and bluesky ones are several times daily. And the funny thing is that I have far more Bluesky and Mastodon accounts than Twitter accounts. Put that in your press release. ;-)
    
2.  I updated the [JSON version](http://scripting.com/rss.json) of the RSS feed, but noticed the Masto and Bluesky accounts (above) hadn't come through, and then on further investigation I realized that I hadn't been keeping it up to date for quite some time! The rule is every time something is added to the XML version I have to also add support for it in the JSON version. This is where Claude came in handy, it gave me a list of all the updates that were needed and I did them. We make a pretty good team imho. The two versions should be in sync now.
    
3.  A note on my [JSONification](https://daytona.scripting.com/search?q=jsonified) of RSS. When I first did it [in 2010](http://scripting.com/stories/2010/12/23/throwingMoreJsonOnTheFire.html), it was on a lark. I was tired of hearing how people didn't like XML so could I please switch. So I did and published it, and I think no one used it. The idea of a JSON version of RSS is in the air again, so I brought it up to date. I want to serve it with a different https-friendly address, but probably won't get to that today.
    
4.  The reason I wanted to work on this today because I have to add RSS-generating code to [wpIdentity](https://github.com/scripting/wpIdentity), so I needed to get warmed up on that part of the world.
    
5.  The new version of the [daverss](https://github.com/scripting/rss) package is 0.6.15.
