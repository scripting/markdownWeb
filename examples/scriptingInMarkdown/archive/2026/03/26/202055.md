When I heard about [Matt's](https://ma.tt/2024/04/beeper-texts/) product [Beeper](https://www.beeper.com/download) I thought wow what if that were on the [RSS network](https://daytona.scripting.com/search?q=%22rss%20network%22).

I think RSS should be here. Makes sense doesn't it?

![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2026/03/26/beeperProtocols.png)

Why not an open independent format from nowhere that no one objects to you using and will not do anything ever to turn you off. It seems it would be fairly easy to add two-way support. :-)
