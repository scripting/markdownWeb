WordPress can now [connect via MCP](https://wordpress.com/blog/2026/03/20/ai-agent-manage-content/) for both reading and writing. This sounds like a possible alternative for the [wpcom api](https://www.npmjs.com/package/wpcom) that we're building on in [WordLand](https://wordland.social/). Sometimes it feels like everything is being reinvented. If the world would just stand still for a moment we might be able to do some building. I wonder how the advent of AI is affecting how WordPress is being developed. I know it's changing everything here.
