![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2026/03/30/bankDick.png)There's something incredibly funny about slapstick and farting. I was flipping channels the other day and came across an [old WC Fields movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Bank_Dick). I used to love them when I was a kid, but figured now, with so many many fancier forms of entertainment this wouldn't get to me, but I was laughing uncontrollably the whole way through. Later, I caught a SNL skit with a boss being surprised by her employees with a Happy Birthday celebration and started farting uncontrollably. They're indulging in body-humor thanks largely (I think) to [Sarah Sherman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Sherman) whose whole comedy schtick is about disgusting things about the human body, esp her own. The boss was played by [Ashley Padilla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashley_Padilla), another SNL superstar. Everything she does is funny, esp farting. Even now, [rewatching](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UI9M_hE_e60) the segment, I couldn't help but laughing loudly. Farts are funny. I have no idea why.
