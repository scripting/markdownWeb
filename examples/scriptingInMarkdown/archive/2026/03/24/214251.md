I’ve watched [Mozilla](https://daytona.scripting.com/search?q=mozilla) not get it for what feels like decades.

Their only legit function imho is to make the real actual web be a great platform for independent developers.

For that, start by adding [user controlled storage](http://scripting.com/2018/06/02/150528.html?title=userWebStorage) to the web, a few standard formats, and let app devs take it from there.
