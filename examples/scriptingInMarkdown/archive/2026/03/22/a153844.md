![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2020/08/29/hope.png)A bit of history. Read this [post](https://platformwars.blogspot.com/2006/03/dave-bonaparte.html) from 20 years ago by Phil Jones. That's what I was trying to do back then, just as Twitter came online. I didn't know it then but was the moment when the web stopped growing. When the VCs took over, and monetized the hell out of it. What we got in the end was Trump and Musk. We would have been smart, as a civilization, to hedge against the monopolies. If we get another chance what are we going to do with it? Will we work together this time? It's worth one more shot.
