[Bluesky](https://bsky.app/) is having trouble keeping their network running. As a developer I empathize. As a user, it's beginning to be a problem. I am using it the way I used to use Twitter, taking notes for future blog posts, sharing a few linkblog feeds, DMing with people I work with, and want/need to keep going. I know about its lock-in problems, but at this time there's no open alternative that has the same collection of users who are easy enough to find. So if it stays unreliable I have to think about what to do about that. Every time I get a [403 Forbidden](https://imgs.scripting.com/2026/04/26/403Forbidden.png), I stop and think if this is the time to write a post? This was the time.
