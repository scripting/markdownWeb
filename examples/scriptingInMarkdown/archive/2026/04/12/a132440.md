The demo for Gutenberg is at [demo.gutenberg.land](https://demo.gutenberg.land/). Easy to remember, and makes the point. If you want Gutenberg instead of [WordLand](https://wordland.social/), you can have it. Hopefully this reinforces what my goals are here. I do not want to favor any one kind of editor. I want every kind of editor here. I want there to be a web of great editors that runs on the web.
