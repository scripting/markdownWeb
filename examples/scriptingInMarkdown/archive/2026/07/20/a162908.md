![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/09/02/teamworkWins.png)[John Johnston](https://demo.rss.chat/?id=189) got [inboundRss](https://github.com/scripting/rss.chat/tree/main/examples/wordpress/inboundRss) working, so now he can automatically post to his WordPress blog from [demo.rss.chat](https://demo.rss.chat/). These are the kinds of things that "just work" when standards are used to connect to the outside world. WordPress, as I've been saying so long, has all the right hooks to be _the_ place where text and publishing meet on the web. They've been reluctant to step into that role, but I think it'll be realllly good for [Matt's company](https://automattic.com/) and the [community](https://wordpress.org/). A lot of fresh developers can come in via the web. That's how I personally approach WordPress. A place to send text so it can be widely read. A growing foundation for a community to build on, and would imho help the web enormously.
