![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2026/07/14/lukeSkywalker.png)Claude codes. It may seem as if developers have taken over [this blog](http://scripting.com/), because we have real business to do. So much new stuff to show. Today's [RSS.chat](https://this.how/rsschat/) goodie is an [example app](https://github.com/scripting/rss.chat/tree/main/examples/threadwalker) called _ThreadWalker_ that crawls the network of replies under a single post, to all levels, printing them on screen in neat hierarchic form. It was written and documented by Claude. I reviewed the code, and looks good, and honestly doesn't look like anything I would write (not bad, just different). It could be used as a starter system that says when someone replied to something you wrote, or something deeper in the tree. All this is done by reading RSS feeds. This is where we begin to explore what's possible when you use a famous and familiar format like RSS 2.0 as the basis for a social network.
