[This is something](https://github.com/pfefferle/wordpress-rss-chat). WordPress now supports RSS.chat in an interesting way, and it's surprisingly deep. Once it's cross-posted a message to RSS.chat, any comment in response will be cross-posted to the comment thread on RSS.chat and vice versa. What's great about this is that you never can tell what people will do when you are on the web. Matthias is a friend, I was on his [podcast](https://www.listennotes.com/podcasts/open-channels-fm/lessons-from-blogging-and-DavqSPdRAoM/) last year. If people use this connection and see how it works, then we'll get an idea of where to go next.
