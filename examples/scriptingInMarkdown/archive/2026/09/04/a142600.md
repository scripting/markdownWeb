![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2024/12/25/leftfacingsanta.png)My first job after college was on the 39th floor of the [Empire State Building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empire_State_Building). I wrote software for our customers, in BASIC, on a [teletype](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teleprinter) in our office, talking to our mainframe in New Jersey. It seemed magical at the time. When I hit a bug, a mystery -- you'd try this and that, and not get anywhere on something that seemed so simple, you could stare at the problem for an hour, trying desperate things, getting no closer to the fix. One night, I was the only person in the office, I thought they should not put programmers in the Empire State Building with windows that open. I'm having the same feeling with Claude. I know when this project is done, I will have Frontier, and I know what that's like. If I didn't so totally look forward to that I would never try to finish the project, I hate this mode of working with the bot. Ugh.
