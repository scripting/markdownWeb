![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2025/06/13/tomatoSoup.png)[Scott Hanson is working](https://github.com/scotthansonde/feedland-rivers) on a WordPress plugin that displays a FeedLand river — a constantly-updated stream of news items from feeds a FeedLand user subscribes to — on a WordPress page or post. It's important that news flows be part of managing a site. Help Scott make it perfect, WordPress is a very important part of the web. The [news tab](http://scripting.com/?tab=news) on my blog home page is an example.
