![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2020/03/31/kentBrockman.png)My long-overdue review of [Don't Look Up](https://daytona.scripting.com/search?q=%22don%27t%20look%20up%22). I know people say it's a bad movie, but I love it so much and it's so incredibly watchable. I could watch the whole thing from beginning to end for the fifth time and still find it entertaining. So many good jokes, and it's got the best actors and acting and best of all Jennifer Lawrence is one of the stars, and she plays a very unlikely JL character, but then she can play any character. Here's she's a totally nerdy PhD student who gets a comet named after her. She has a boyfriend who dreams of being an influencer. They laugh at everything that's so _stupid_ about our civilization, and its downfall, and near the end of the movie Leonardo gets the line that [makes it so sad](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cd0Nu3d3b4g). We did have everything and we keep blowing it, over and over. We haven't evolved enough to use the greatness we inherited.
