[Video demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iLXEkM1cXNk) of a new [LO2](http://littleoutliner.com/) feature Doc asked for. Put the cursor in the middle of a paragraph. Press cmd-return to split the paragraph at the cursor position, creating two headlines. Simple, I do this manually all the time, with copy-paste which is more tedious and error-prone. Also going to do the inverse, cmd-backspace to merge two headlines.
