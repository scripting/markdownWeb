I really liked [Jojo Rabbit](https://www.metacritic.com/movie/jojo-rabbit), even though it got mixed reviews. You start out wondering what the hell they're doing, but in the end it's a good simple little story that has a lot of meaning. A sort of lite version of [Handmaid's Tale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Handmaid%27s_Tale_\(TV_series\)). I really liked that [Rebel Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebel_Wilson) was in it, a good fit, as was the chief Gestapo guy, and Jojo's best friend. What's not to like?
