Andrew Shell opens a [thread](https://twitter.com/andrewshell/status/1216370917225517056) on Twitter for [Overcast](https://overcast.fm/), an iOS podcast client, asking why he was unable to subscribe to [Scripting News](https://scripting.com/rss.xml). Turns out Overcast doesn't like titleless posts. I don't use Overcast myself, so I can't offer specific advice, but it is not acceptable to use the file name as a title. That's not what the file name is for. I described the [use-case](https://twitter.com/davewiner/status/1216404689719365634) in a tweet (of course without a title, tweets don't have titles). Vendors tend to ignore advice on these issues, and that's kept us from growing. I used to budge for them on this issue, but [decided in 2017](http://scripting.com/2017/05/05/iWantMyOldBlogBack.html) to stop. All the compromises meant that my blog disappears. Bottom-line: I put podcasts on title-less posts. _That's never going to change._
