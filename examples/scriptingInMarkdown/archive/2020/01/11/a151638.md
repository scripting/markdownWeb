![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2020/01/11/qube.png)You want to know how bonded Doc and I are. That [day in 1999](http://scripting.com/1999/11/15.html) when we got his blog started, I saw he had a blue box in his office. I asked what it is. He didn't really know. I took it home with me, I didn't even ask permission. I realized even at the time this was [nervy](http://thesaurus.land/?word=cheeky) of me, but Doc was happy that I did. Turns out it was a [Cobalt Qube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cobalt_Qube), a revolutionary product, an [idea](http://scripting.com/weLoveOurQube.html) that seemed like the future in 1999, but sadly its future has yet to arrive. Hopefully this decade it will. (On second thought maybe it was the precursor of the [Raspberry Pi](https://www.raspberrypi.org/)?)
