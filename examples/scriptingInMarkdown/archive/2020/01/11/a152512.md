[Interesting tweet](https://twitter.com/hacksaar/status/1215995308829634560), says that it was [19 years ago today](http://scripting.com/2001/01/11.html) that the [enclosure](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/enclosuresAggregators.html) element was added to RSS, by yours truly, in an effort to add _payloads_ to RSS. I looked it up, and they are right. And through the rest of [January 2001](http://scripting.com/2001/01.html), Adam and I were trying out neat hacks that could move large (for the day) audio and video files back and forth between our offices in California and Europe. Here's the [piece](http://scripting.com/davenet/2001/01/11/payloadsForRss.html) I wrote that day explaining the idea. The site, twowayweb.com, is gone, but the article is preserved in many places, including [archive.org](https://web.archive.org/web/20010202144800/https://thetwowayweb.com/payloadsForRss).
