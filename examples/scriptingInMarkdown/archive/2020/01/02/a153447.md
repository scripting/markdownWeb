Speaking of Facebook, yesterday I posted [something](https://www.facebook.com/dave.winer.12/posts/1123011587906245) intended to be wistful and introspective that some people took as a cry for help. Funny how that works. I said this: "Honestly I never thought I’d make it to 2020." Now it's 100 percent my fault there was confusion. Going back to the 1960s, when we had [duck and cover drills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duck_and_cover) in school, we all thought for sure we'd die in a [nuclear holocaust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_holocaust) long before 2020. If you had asked me then what were the chances I'd be alive in 2020, I probably would have said "No one will be alive in 2020." But here we are. :boom:
