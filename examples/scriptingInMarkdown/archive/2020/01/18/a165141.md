There was a great New Yorker article in [September 2016](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2016/09/26/president-trumps-first-term?verso=true) that laid out what a Trump presidency would be like. We need to do that again. We're staring at the next level. What if Trump succeeds at fully taking over the government? What if he's acquitted? What will that be like?
