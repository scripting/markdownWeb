People are cackling over the design of the [Space Force uniform](https://www.military.com/daily-news/2020/01/17/space-force-offers-first-peek-new-camouflage-uniform.html). Why do they need camouflage in space? Obviously it's not really a priority. I'm a big believer in [prior art](http://essaysfromexodus.scripting.com/priorArtDesignMethod), and there's an obvious choice here. License the design of the Star Trek uniforms from [whoever](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ViacomCBS) owns them. It has the advantage of looking vaguely futuristic, and it makes Star Trek downright [prescient](http://thesaurus.land/?word=prescient). That is if the [Space Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Space_Force) ever amounts to anything.
