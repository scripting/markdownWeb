A couple of worthwhile podcasts. Brian Lehrer's [Impeachment podcast](https://www.npr.org/podcasts/767280204/impeachment-a-daily-podcast) is the adult version of what you hear on cable news. It still focuses on process and horse races but it goes into more detail, Lehrer has a sharp mind, and is a great interviewer, so you learn more. And [Planet Money](https://www.npr.org/podcasts/510289/planet-money), always good, has a [piece](https://www.npr.org/2020/01/15/796799769/episode-964-billboards) on privacy and billboards. I kept wanting to show them their own [story](https://www.npr.org/sections/money/2018/10/09/655921710/chinas-social-credit-system) about social credit in China. No longer are the concerns about privacy theoretical and narrow. It's definitely coming to the US.
