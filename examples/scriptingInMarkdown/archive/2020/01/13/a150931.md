![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2020/01/13/davesCoffee.png)I'm having the best time writing my blog these days. What makes the difference? You. I play my blog the way a musician plays the piano or guitar. I can hear what I play, but I'm most interested in what you hear. I'm not running [McDonalds](https://www.facebook.com/) or [Starbucks](https://twitter.com/), I've got a little [coffee shop](https://www.yelp.com/biz/the-mud-club-woodstock-2) in town. I serve the drinks, tell stories, decorate the place, choose the [music](http://scripting.com/davenet/1996/01/08/gottabearetha.html) and lighting, bring in guests, all to pass the time in the most Dave-friendly way I can. I look for others doing their own things, so we can perform together. Our ideas influence, are tested and improved on. I'm back in the 90s and all of a sudden there's [blue sky](http://scripting.com/davenet/1995/01/13/ahugechunkofbluesky.html) in every direction. All because, imho, in [2017](http://scripting.com/2017/05/05/iWantMyOldBlogBack.html), I decided to be myself again. :boom:
