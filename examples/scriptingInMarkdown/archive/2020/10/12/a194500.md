I was writing enthusiastically about \[%search: Clubhouse%\], then I stopped writing, because I stopped using it. At first, for no particular reason. I wasn't finding conversations that interested me, and when I started them myself, usually [nothing](http://scripting.com/2020/09/02/173236.html?title=clubhouseAfterAMonth#a174138) happened. Then came the [anti](https://www.theverge.com/2020/9/30/21495419/clubhouse-conversation-antisemitism-content-moderation)\-[Semitism](https://www.tabletmag.com/sections/news/articles/clubhouse-app-anti-semitism) [meltdown](https://www.algemeiner.com/2020/09/30/elite-tech-app-clubhouse-under-fire-for-hosting-antisemitic-discussion-on-black-jewish-relations/). It was depressing to find out that fighting for equality, to the extent that it works, would not end the hatred for my people. After that I looked at every conversation there differently. Never spoke again. I still have the app, am still a member, so this all could change, but I thought it was good to update.
