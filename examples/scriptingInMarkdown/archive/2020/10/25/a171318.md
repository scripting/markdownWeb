![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/04/17/roku2.png)[HBO Max](https://www.hbomax.com/) is like [New Coke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Coke). HBO was the [#1](http://www.tjwaldorf.com/brand-positioning-the-ladders-in-our-head/) brand. Needed to be a little more like Netflix, but really it was pretty good as it was. Now the _max_ part has coincided with it being a lot harder to use, virtually impossible if you use the popular Roku platform. At least for me. Looks like an epic mistake. At a time when the market is overloaded with lots of big brands (Disney, Apple etc) trying to diversity into a market already dominated by HBO, it's a really curious time for them to knock out their own legs.
