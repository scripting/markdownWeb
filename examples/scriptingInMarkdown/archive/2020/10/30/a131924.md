Yesterday I put out a [call](http://scripting.com/2020/10/29.html#a210605) for programs to binge. I got an overwhelming response. But nothing obvious emerged. Some were programs I had already tried, but didn't get far enough into to rate in my BingeWorthy [profile](http://my.bingeworthy.io/davewiner/). Others were comedies, and I'm just not into them now. Examples of shows I didn't have the energy to watch recently: [Schitt's Creek](http://bingeworthy.io/tv/?id=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.metacritic.com%2Ftv%2Fschitts-creek) and [The Good Place](http://bingeworthy.io/tv/?id=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.metacritic.com%2Ftv%2Fthe-good-place), both of which are very good for what they are, but I'm just not in the mood now. I'm thinking I may just re-watch something from the past that I liked and think might be worth a second look. In the meantime, the lists, one on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/davewiner/status/1321921170938204167) and one on [GitHub](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/issues/192) are there for everyone to reference. I also want to add a recommendation algorithm to [BingeWorthy](http://bingeworthy.io/tv/), but I haven't figured out how to do it yet.
