WordPress added a tweet-streaming feature, described in this [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/10/13/wordpress-can-now-turn-blog-posts-into-tweetstorms-automatically/) article. I followed their directions, and [published a thread](https://twitter.com/bullmancuso/status/1316064421844865024), based on a post [here](http://scripting.com/2020/10/12.html#a144048). The post also [exists](https://unberkeley.wordpress.com/2020/10/13/where-is-your-soul/) on WordPress.com, so it elminates the need for a separate app that turns a thread into a post. Makes me wonder if Substack and Medium are going to add this feature. Also does WordPress support email bulletins?
