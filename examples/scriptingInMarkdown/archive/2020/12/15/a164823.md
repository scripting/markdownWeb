![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2020/12/15/deanForAmericaBumperSticker.png)[The Dean campaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_Dean_2004_presidential_campaign) in 2002-04 was on the verge of turning a campaign into a social network. I was there. [Here are my notes](http://scripting.com/2004/01/19.html) from the last night of the campaign, the Iowa Caucus in 2004. I was at Dean HQ in [Burlington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burlington,_Vermont). I was blogging on [Scripting News](http://scripting.com/2004/01/19.html) and on the main Dean weblog. We also rolled out [Channel Dean](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/howChannelDeanCameToBe.html) that day, basically a linkblog managed by campaign people, linking to articles and blog posts their community would find interesting. These were all precursors to the social nets of today.
