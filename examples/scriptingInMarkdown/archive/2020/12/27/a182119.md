When you write an [outline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outline) on paper, if you feel the need to reorganize, you have to write the whole thing over. This makes the initial structure something you resist deciding on, so you never get started. Doing an [outline on a computer](http://outlinerhowto.opml.org/) is different. Since you have commands to reorganize the outline, you can use any structure that supports your list-making. Because you can always regroup things, or ungroup them., Whatever works for you, over time.
