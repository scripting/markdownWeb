Last week I [asked](http://scripting.com/2020/12/22.html#a195634) for recommendations for great audiobook listens, and got these titles from [Seth Godin](https://seths.blog/): 1. [On Immunity](https://www.amazon.com/On-Immunity-Eula-Biss-audiobook/dp/B00NI4LD6K/ref=tmm_aud_swatch_0?_encoding=UTF8&qid=1608725944&sr=8-3&pldnSite=1). "Important, timely and beautifully read." 2. The two David Deutsch [physics](https://www.amazon.com/The-Beginning-of-Infinity-audiobook/dp/B005HTYBCM/ref=sr_1_1?dchild=1&keywords=David+Deutsch&qid=1609254206&s=audible&sr=1-1) [books](https://www.amazon.com/The-Fabric-of-Reality-audiobook/dp/B07L5XWD7X/ref=sr_1_2?dchild=1&keywords=David+Deutsch&qid=1609254227&s=audible&sr=1-2) on infinity. 3. [Just Kids](https://www.amazon.com/Just-Kids-Patti-Smith-audiobook/dp/B005EJFVHY/ref=sr_1_1?dchild=1&keywords=Just+Kids&qid=1609254302&s=audible&sr=1-1) by Patti Smith. 4. [The Art of Possibility](https://www.amazon.com/The-Art-of-Possibility-audiobook/dp/B004HY9254/ref=sr_1_1?dchild=1&keywords=The+Art+of+Possibility&qid=1609254280&s=audible&sr=1-1) by Roz and Ben Zander. The last two Godin calls "the best two audio books ever."
