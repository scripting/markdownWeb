![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/12/24/santa.png)We're having a huge rainstorm in the [Catskills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catskill_Mountains). Over five inches of rain so far. All the snow from last week's storm has melted and all that water is flowing downhill. And then it's going to get cold and snow tonight. Trees are down. Power and internet went out overnight. It's a wet, soggy, grey Christmas. Of course it is. This is 2020.
