There's another reason to be in a hurry to get everyone vaccinated. As time goes by, the virus is mutating. Maybe a strain will come along that's resistent or immune to the vaccine. It makes sense that will happen faster as people are vaccinated. To work best it should happen fast. There's a lot of subtlety that is being missed in the press coverage. There is a [new strain](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2020-12-20/uk-new-strain-covid-19-more-infectious-spreads-faster-urgent/13001198) apparently in the UK now that is more communicative. Also, if history is a guide, the new strains will be less lethal. That's good news of course.
