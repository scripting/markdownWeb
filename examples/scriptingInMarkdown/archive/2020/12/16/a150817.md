![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/12/24/drummer.png)I've been beating this drum for years. We should be [teaching journalism in school](https://www.google.com/search?q=teaching+journalism+in+school+site%3Ascripting.com). Not just for journalism majors. Everyone should have a semester of training. If we had started doing that ten years ago, we'd be in a much better place now.
