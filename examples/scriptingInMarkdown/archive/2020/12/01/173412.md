![](http://scripting.com/2016/11/12/trump.png)I've heard it asked who wrote the best bit of journalism in the last decade? That answer is very easy. [Evan Osnos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evan_Osnos) in the New Yorker.

In Sept 2016, he wrote a [piece](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2016/09/26/president-trumps-first-term) about what a Trump presidency would be like. He's the only one who did. It was unthinkable at the time that he'd win.

Why this was the best.

1.  Osnos thought of doing it.
    
2.  He didn't just dash it off, he thought, really learned about the subject.
    
3.  The piece was chilling.
    
4.  The artwork also.
    

And of course Trump did win, and now we're all experts in what happened next.

Writing about the unthinkable, that's when journalism can be great.

Here's [my writeup](http://scripting.com/2016/11/12/thePressMissedTheBigStoryOf2016.html) of his piece, at the time. I called it the missed story of 2016.
