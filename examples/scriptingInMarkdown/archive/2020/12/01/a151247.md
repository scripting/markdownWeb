![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2020/12/01/podcastsAppIconApple.png)I want to delete all the episodes downloaded by Apple's Podcasts app. I've done it before so I know it's possible. Any clues much appreciated. \[On Twitter, the [best advice](https://twitter.com/obelixtwit/status/1333791755230035970), which I used, was to delete the app and re-install. It saves the subscriptions but throws out the data. I had 31GB of downloaded podcasts. I needed none of them. Podcasts aren't like music, if you don't listen to one after a week (say) the app should just delete it.\]
