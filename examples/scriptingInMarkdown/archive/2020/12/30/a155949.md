I like when news gives us lifesaving clues. For example, there’s a new more communicable form of the virus. It’s [here](https://www.cnn.com/2020/12/30/health/colorado-uk-coronavirus-variant/index.html), of course. This was inevitable, I learned from [reading](http://scripting.com/2020/08/19.html#a160302) about the [1918 pandemic](https://www.cdc.gov/flu/pandemic-resources/1918-pandemic-h1n1.html). Question: Does this invalidate current practice? Can I still go food shopping on my own? Gas up my car? Of course no one knows yet. But the question hasn’t even been raised as far as I know.
