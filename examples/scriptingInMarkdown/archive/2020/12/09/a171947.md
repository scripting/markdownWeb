![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2020/12/09/chromeIcon.png)Google changed the icon for Chrome? It's as stupid a thing to do as a cigarette maker changing the taste or packaging of their product. It's an addiction you don't even know you have until they _make_ you conscious of it. Going to the web is programmed so deep into my neurons, it's at the base of my spine, a total reflexive thing, addict behavior. Now I think about it every damn time. And the [Safari icon is next to it](http://scripting.com/images/2020/12/09/iconbar.png), and btw it _hasn't_ changed. Which browser you use is something they want you not to think about, as long as it's their browser. Maybe it's a bug, in which case never mind. But if it was deliberate, it's a stupid move. Read \[%search: Positioning%\] for the importance of what's in peoples' minds.
