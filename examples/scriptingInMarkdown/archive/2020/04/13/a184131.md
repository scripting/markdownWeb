![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2020/04/13/kimchiRamen.png)The pricing model of online pubs is as if each is publishing a whole magazine or newspaper, as they did before the web. I'm sure that's still their internal model, how their organizations work, but it's not the way we read them. We read an article here, another one there. Their pricing is utterly inflexible. It's a shame to [waste a crisis](https://www.brainyquote.com/quotes/rahm_emanuel_409199). We need information like never before. And they need money because advertising is evaporating. Can't we arrive at a compromise?
