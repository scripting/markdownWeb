BTW, the [trouble](http://scripting.com/2020/04/26/211718.html?title=dropboxLo2Outage) with Dropbox turned out to be their Linux install requires components that aren't there on a headless Ubuntu installation. When the software tried to update itself it failed, and that was [the end](http://scripting.com/2020/04/24/171231.html?title=dropboxIsKillingItself). I'm guessing not many people use Dropbox on headless Linux?
