The [next in a series](https://www.nytimes.com/2020/04/18/health/coronavirus-america-future.html) of eye-opening NYT pieces from Donald McNeil about the coronavirus. It's mostly about the long miserable slog it's going to be, with one bright spot. "In the movies, viruses become more deadly. In reality, they usually become less so, because asymptomatic strains reach more hosts. Even the 1918 Spanish flu virus eventually faded into the seasonal H1N1 flu." An [example](https://www.navytimes.com/news/coronavirus/2020/04/16/secdef-majority-of-roosevelt-sailors-with-covid-19-are-asymptomatic-flattop-still-wartime-ready/), the majority of [Roosevelt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Theodore_Roosevelt_\(CVN-71\)) sailors with Covid-19 are asymptomatic.
