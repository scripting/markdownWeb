[McConnell](https://www.stripes.com/for-mcconnell-virus-carries-echo-of-his-boyhood-polio-during-wwii-1.625609) had polio as a child, it left him disabled for life. I grew up after there was a vaccine, but just barely. The vaccine came out in [1954](https://www.history.com/this-day-in-history/children-receive-first-polio-vaccine), I was born in 1955. So there were polio victims in school when I was. There was a girl, Bridgett, who played with us in a wheel chair.
