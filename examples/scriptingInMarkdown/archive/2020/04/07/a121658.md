![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2020/04/07/arnoSchmidt.png)When I edited yesterday's piece about my grandfather [Rudy Kiesler](http://scripting.com/2020/04/06/152004.html?title=rudyKiesler), I stumbled across a reference to him as a "German Jewish communist," on a page about [Arno Schmidt](https://wikimili.com/en/Arno_Schmidt), who was my grandmother's brother, Rudy's brother-in-law, my mother's uncle and my great uncle. I thought that was super interesting. Because if you met Rudy you'd have thought he was a Republican, at least today you would. But he wasn't at all. He was super liberal. Our whole family was. It never occurred to me that he was a communist, but he might have been, esp from the point of view of Uncle Arno (who I never actually met).
