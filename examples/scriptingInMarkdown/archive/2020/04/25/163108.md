Here's a splash of cold water.

We've been sort of assuming that there will be immunity and eventually a vaccine for [Covid-19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coronavirus_disease_2019).

But it may be that neither will happen.

There isn't a [vaccine for HIV](https://www.hiv.gov/hiv-basics/hiv-prevention/potential-future-options/hiv-vaccines), for example.

It first appeared in 1984. 36 years. That would mean there would be no Covid-19 vaccine in 2056.

Something to think about and ponder.

Also there's another apocalypse lurking in the shadows, other than climate change. [Antibiotic resistance](https://www.cdc.gov/antibiotic-use/community/about/antibiotic-resistance-faqs.html).

Yeah science is a bitch. :boom:
