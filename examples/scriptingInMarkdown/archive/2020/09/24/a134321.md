![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2020/09/06/bidenHarris2020.png)Biden's campaign had a slogan, [Build Back Better](https://joebiden.com/build-back-better/), but it didn't stick. Campaigns need slogans. For example, with \[%search: ThinkTank%\] we had See What You Think. Of course Trump has many. MAGA. Mexico will pay for it. Fine people, on both sides. We'll have to see. Just a few. It's okay if a slogan translates into a concrete promise, but it doesn't have to. Obama had [Yes We Can](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jjXyqcx-mYY). That's pretty good, hard to refute and doesn't really commit to anything in particular. I liked [FDR's](http://content.time.com/time/specials/packages/article/0,28804,1840981_1840998_1840901,00.html) [Happy Days Are Here Again](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zvk_uLnv4uo). That campaign happened long before I was born, but in a sense, I remember it. The same way I guess today's kids remember the Beatles.
