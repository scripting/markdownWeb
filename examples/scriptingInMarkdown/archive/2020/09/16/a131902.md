Yesterday I was out and about doing errands, and instead of wearing my usual blue Mets cap, I was wearing my [red Wisconsin hat](http://scripting.com/images/2020/09/16/daveWithWisconsinHat.png). It has a big W on the front, and on the back it says [Badgers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bucky_Badger). I was getting some dirty looks, and at one place a woman commented that I shouldn't wear that hat because people might not understand.
