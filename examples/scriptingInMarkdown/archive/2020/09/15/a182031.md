![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/09/27/todd.png)Jon Udell was [tweeting](https://twitter.com/judell/status/1305924441809903617) about podcasting, and I had a bunch of things I wanted to say, so I talked for 22 minutes in [this podcast](http://scripting.com/2020/09/15/whyIsPodcastingSoPrimitive.m4a). I talk about the Daily podcast and Brian Lehrer, two formats that work. Why has the tech for listening to podcasts evolved so slowly? Who has the ability to innovate? Same with blogging. When was the last time there was a serious innovation?
