If I were president of the United States given infinite power for just an hour, I'd establish by decree a new holiday, _National Everyone Love Each Other Day._ On that day it would be illegal to talk about polls, or bases, or divisiveness. People matter. That's it. The patron saint of National Everyone Love Each Other Day would be [Rodney King](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Rodney_King). He was the man who, after being beaten by the police in LA in 1992, [said this](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kKj4L6GwL5Y). "Can we all just get along? Can we get along? Can we stop making it, making it horrible for the older people and the kids?" It's a good question and for one day each year we could all ask it, of ourselves and each other.
