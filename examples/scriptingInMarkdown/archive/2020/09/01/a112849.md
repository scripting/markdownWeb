In the [tabs vs spaces](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=tabs+vs+spaces&t=hk&ia=web) debate, I am firmly in the "neither" camp. Too low level a question. My editor [understands code structure](http://scripting.com/images/2020/09/01/indentation.png). I don't indent with tabs or spaces, I indent with structure.
