On the west coast, every town has a few hot tub rental places. You pay a fee, about $10 or $20 an hour, and you get a private room with a hot tub. Soak for an hour. Come out a new person. Here's the list for [Berkeley](https://www.yelp.com/search?find_desc=hot+tub+rental&find_loc=Berkeley%2C+CA). In Palo Alto there's the grand [Watercourse Way](https://watercourseway.com/tub-rooms/). And if you want the best, go to [Esalen](https://www.esalen.org/page/esalen-hot-springs) or [Harbin](https://harbin.org/). Of course you might want to wait until the fires are over. Nothing like this exists in the east. In all of New York City, the closest you could get was a communal [Russian bath](https://www.nytimes.com/2016/01/31/nyregion/after-124-years-the-russian-and-turkish-baths-are-still-a-hot-spot.html), that's like 100 years old. And no it's not the same thing. Once they legalize cannabis in NYC, they're going to need to open up some hot tub places for people to mellow out in.
