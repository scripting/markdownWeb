![](http://static.scripting.com/larryKing/images/2013/09/14/allyourbase.gif)I want to see one major publication's editorial page or opinion writer say how [Woodward](http://scripting.com/2020/09/11/135338.html?title=woodwardBetrayedUs) betrayed us, and how reporters must not do that. If you have information that can save lives, err on the side of disclosure. I was hoping that Ben Smith would do it in his weekly column, instead he went after [The Intercept](https://www.nytimes.com/2020/09/13/business/media/the-intercept-source-reality-winner.html). Reporters do that, go after safe targets, believing perhaps they'll never work with someone from The Intercept, or need a quote from the owner. As far as I know not one op-ed writer has said what Woodward did was bad.
