I have a series 5 Apple watch, reading [this review](https://www.theverge.com/2020/9/17/21440604/apple-watch-series-6-impressions-photos-video-blood-oxygen), can't see a reason to get the series 6. I already have a [pulse oximeter](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08925BSQJ?gclid=CjwKCAjwkoz7BRBPEiwAeKw3q5aZ6H8UhO9-Mvimosqj09gb1Ieg576mrwkZy5zwD6s0GzfEl1wdQRoCBssQAvD_BwE), and I take a reading once a day, and record it.
