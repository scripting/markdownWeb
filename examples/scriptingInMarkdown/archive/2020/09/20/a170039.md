People who think they can predict the near-future, look at [this video](https://twitter.com/davidfrum/status/1307722350775930888), and imagine it was a year ago, looking a year into the future. It's so frustrating when smart people quote the [pablum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pablum) they hear on the cable news shows. Those are bedtime stories. The world is far more dangerous and far less predictable. Go back and watch the shows from a year ago to see how well they did.
