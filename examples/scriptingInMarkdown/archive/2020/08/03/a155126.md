I’m watching [Schitt’s Creek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schitt%27s_Creek). It’s really funny. I was expecting it to be cringe-inducing, like [Curb Your Enthusiasm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curb_Your_Enthusiasm), but it's not. Starring [Catherine O'Hara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine_O%27Hara) who was in [Beetlejuice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beetlejuice), one of the best movies ever made. And the [geeky guy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Elliott) from [Groundhog Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groundhog_Day_\(film\)), the other best movie ever made. Schitt's Creek, like its name, is both profane and wholesome.
