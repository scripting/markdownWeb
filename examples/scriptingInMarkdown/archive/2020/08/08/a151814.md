Briefly discussed WordPress on Twitter last night with Don Park, long-time friend, ex-Automattician. I said I don't think they understand blogging there. Don says "members of WordPress community and employees of the company behind it use blogs all the time." I don't doubt it, [yet I could never do](https://twitter.com/davewiner/status/1291868787218710528) what I do with Wordpress. Far too clumsy. Not fluid. Not designed for writers. Really weak product.
