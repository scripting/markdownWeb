People who give the TV series [Fargo](http://bingeworthy.io/tv/?id=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.metacritic.com%2Ftv%2Ffargo) the highest rating are people who really know a good thing. It is one of a small number of shows in the running for Best Series Ever. The others: [The Wire](http://bingeworthy.io/tv/?id=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.metacritic.com%2Ftv%2Fthe-wire) (of course) and a show that a lot of people gave up on too soon: [Mr Robot](http://bingeworthy.io/tv/?id=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.metacritic.com%2Ftv%2Fmr-robot). Its final season was beautiful heart-filling drama. As much a vision as a story.
