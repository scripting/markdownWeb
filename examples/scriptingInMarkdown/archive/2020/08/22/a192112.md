Following up on yesterday's [hypothetical](http://scripting.com/2020/08/21.html#a174124), assuming people from blue states relocated to sparsely populated states, let's do an example. The population of [Wyoming](https://www.google.com/search?q=population+wyoming) is 579K. The population one of the boroughs of NYC, [Queens](https://www.google.com/search?q=population+queens+ny), is 2.3 million. So if 15 percent of the people in Queens relocated to Wyoming, that could yield three more non-fascist Electoral College votes, one member of the House and two Senators.
