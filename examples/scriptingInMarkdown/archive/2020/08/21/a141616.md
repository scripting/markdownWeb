[Leonard Nimoy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonard_Nimoy#Star_Trek). A perfectly [good](https://www.emmys.com/bios/leonard-nimoy) actor, capable of playing lots of roles, but because he was Mr Spock on Star Trek, you'd always think wtf is [Mr Spock](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9lWBbDGYC3o) doing on [Mission Impossible](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kg76TOodagI). [Typecasting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typecasting). Another example. [Bill Buckner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Buckner#1986_World_Series), a good first baseman with a long career. He misjudged a ball once. That becomes his whole [life story](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rpyJjecJnuI). As they say, it [is](https://twitter.com/davewiner/status/1296495392150949888) what it is.
