![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/12/21/fox.png)Such a shame to see [RSS](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html) [readers](https://lifehacker.com/the-best-rss-readers-and-news-aggregation-apps-1839030732) nuzzle up to Feedly the way they did with [Google Reader](http://scripting.com/2019/12/21/170042.html). This will not end well. Don't centralize technology whose main power is decentralization. But there's no law against it. Maybe it's a business model. And no I don't care if users want it.
