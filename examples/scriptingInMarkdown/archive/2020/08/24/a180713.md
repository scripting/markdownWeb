At times [like this](https://www.nytimes.com/2020/08/24/opinion/jerry-seinfeld-new-york-coronavirus.html) I like to say: NYC is not dead because it was never alive. Words like _dead_ should not be thrown around casually. Death is a [heavy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_\(personification\)) [word](http://thesaurus.land/?word=death), and should be reserved for real death, not an imaginary stupid metaphoric death.
