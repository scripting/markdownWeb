I went back to [UW-Madison](https://www.wisc.edu/) for a visit a few years ago and got a tour of a [new building](https://discovery.wisc.edu/) created to generate [patents](https://news.wisc.edu/tag/patents/). But it didn't appear it was used for much. Extravagant and luxurious. Very unlike a typical utilitarian campus building at a large state school. I learned they built it to recruit a professor who generated billions from patents, and I guess they felt was likely to continue to do so. The money apparently flows to the university, but the professor gets to work in a [Taj Mahal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taj_Mahal) of campus buildings. Not sure. But it was _very_ impressive, and surprising.
