[RSS](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html) makes [à la carte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%80_la_carte) software possible. [Substack](https://substack.com/) is like [prix fixe](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/prix_fixe) software. You must to use their editor to publish a newsletter. No substitutions. But what if you already have an editor you like? No [soup](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M2lfZg-apSA) for you!
