![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/12/06/dorothy.png)Twitter [subscriptions](https://techcrunch.com/2020/07/23/after-ad-revenue-drop-twitter-tells-investors-its-eyeing-subscription-options/). Fine with me. I bought $25K of Twitter stock a few years ago. I don't mind putting money behind my use of Twitter. What would I pay for? No character limit. Titles, simple styling, links. Basic [blogging features](http://scripting.com/2016/04/30/1229.html). It can carry multi-megabyte images and videos as payloads. A blog post is no big deal. Add a [More link](http://scripting.com/2015/10/02/whatWouldAFatTweetLookLike.html). People still don't click links in Twitter. That's why threads are so popular. That's why [image posts](https://twitter.com/davewiner/status/1286641900825575425) are popular. [Pave the cowpath](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/pave_the_cowpath) and let's go unlimited-length.
