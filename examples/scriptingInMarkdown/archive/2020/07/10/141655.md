I subscribed to Disney-Plus so I could watch [Hamilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamilton_\(musical\)).

Parts of it I liked, but for the most, it was very Disney. I wasn't expecting that for some reason. I was prepared to be inspired but it never happened.

I then subscribed to Hulu so I could watch [Brockmire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brockmire).

It is very good and very not-Disney. [Hank Azaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hank_Azaria), the voice for so many great [Simpsons characters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_The_Simpsons_cast_members), plays a washed up baseball play-by-play announcer who ends up in a washed up Pennsylvania town, announcing for a team called The Frackers.

It's LOL funny. Really good. And Azaria, as you might imagine, can play any character that requires character.
