The EFF started, [30 years ago today](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_Frontier_Foundation), with the right idea, defending speech on the then-nascent net. I gave them $5000 at startup, but ultimately they sold us out to the big tech companies. Now we need a new EFF to reign in the original EFF.

I blogged about my first [break with the EFF](http://scripting.com/2005/03/29.html#breakWithEff) in 2005. Subsequently I [objected](http://scripting.com/2014/05/05/#a1399316366) to how they defended podcasting from a patent troll, and ultimately the effort to give [control of the web to Google](http://this.how/googleAndHttp/), that was the absolute last straw.

They are no longer defending electronic freedom, they are protecting the rich monopolists. Ugh.
