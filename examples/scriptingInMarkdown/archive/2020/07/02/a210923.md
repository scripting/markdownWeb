![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2020/07/02/evita.png)[A 25-minute podcast](http://scripting.com/2020/07/02/dontCryForMeArgentina.m4a) I did this morning. It was mostly about what's in the [gaslighting](http://scripting.com/2020/07/02/134749.html?title=onGaslighting) post below. Expanded, and starting with an observation by [Ken Smith](https://clas.iusb.edu/english/faculty/smith.html), English prof at Indiana University, that students deserve to be shown how to use _the power tools of our culture._ Teachers should say that to them, out loud. I tried saying it loud and felt the power. Led to a story or two. The title of this podcast is [Don't Cry For Me Argentina](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KD_1Z8iUDho). Wish I had thought to play that song during the podcast. Next time. :boom:
