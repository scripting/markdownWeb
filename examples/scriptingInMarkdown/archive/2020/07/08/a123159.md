![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2020/07/08/saveMyLifeMask.png)[NakedJen](http://nakedjen.com/) works in a place where people must wear a mask, so she gives out a lot of masks every day. She's going to make custom masks that have a message. That's the first part of this story. Second part, we both started a campaign called [#savemylife](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=site%3Ascripting.com+%22save+my+life%22+%23savemylife&t=h_&ia=web), where we promote stories and ideas that save people's lives in this age where lives have all of a sudden become cheap, something our government is promoting (the cheapness, not the saving). So Jen has designed a mask that has the [#savemylife](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=site%3Ascripting.com+%22save+my+life%22+%23savemylife&t=h_&ia=web) message on it, which is kind of heavy, and to balance that she put a giddy little smiley on it, to make it more Jen-like. I said I want one, but maybe I want mine to have a picture of [MLK](http://scripting.com/2016/12/20/mlk.png) instead of the smiley. And then I would probably change the tag to [#blacklivesmatter](http://this.how/blackLivesMatter/). This is something that I, a white man, would wear with pride. But there's a problem. The purpose of my wearing a mask is to save _your_ life, not mine, in case I'm infected and am shedding. I feel contradicitons like this have to be resolved. Or do they?
