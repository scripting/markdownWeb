I have an app called [Server Monitor](https://github.com/scripting/serverMonitor) that does something simple but important. It pings each of the apps I have running once a minute to see if they're still alive. If not, SM sends me an email. I bet everyone who writes server apps has this kind of utility.

I'd like something like this for my friends. A ping goes out to each, if someone doesn't respond, I get an email.

In the age of [The Trump Plague](http://scripting.com/images/2020/07/25/trumpPlague.png), I worry about my friends. I figure it's just a matter of time...
