The _infinite scrolling_ [feature](http://scripting.com/2019/10/17.html#a161833) on the Scripting News [home page](http://scripting.com/) wasn't a good idea, because it made it difficult to reach the bottom of the page which has useful stuff on it. I just turned it off. I did a [video demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k5kRl-Qy7_I) of the feature so we can remember how it worked, and if needed I can turn it back on. And the _More_ button remains, so you can use that to scroll back, one day at a time.
