Think about ventilators.

Suppose you catch the virus today.

In two weeks you're so sick you can't breathe.

You need a ventilator.

The [EMT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emergency_medical_technician) driver won't take you to the hospital because there is no ventilator for you.

It's like planning ahead for [TP](https://www.urbandictionary.com/define.php?term=TP) but this matters more.
