![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2020/03/29/beavis.png)Dev work: A few days ago I [asked](http://scripting.com/2020/03/24.html#a204119) how to use regex to mash up paths for web requests. In the end I went another way, adding a long-wanted plug-in capability to [PagePark](https://github.com/scripting/pagePark), my labor-of-love Node-based web server. I found a really Nodey way to do it, much better imho than using [eval](https://www.w3schools.com/jsref/jsref_eval.asp) or [vm](https://www.w3schools.com/nodejs/ref_vm.asp). I [use](https://www.w3schools.com/nodejs/nodejs_modules.asp) [modules](https://nodejs.org/api/modules.html). A lot of people don't know they can be in files, not just distributed through NPM. It's a really nice way to do plug-ins, and it's made something cool possible with Little Outliner. More on that later. In the meantime I've written up the use of modules as PagePark plug-ins in the [thread](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/issues/158#issuecomment-605650163) about regex. _Still diggin!_
