![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2020/01/13/davesCoffee.png)When last night's email went out, it sent Saturday's posts, not Sunday's. This is pretty hard to debug, I'd have to recreate the conditions of various databases at midnight last night. At first I thought the problem must be related to the Daylight Savings Time switch on Saturday night, but on second thought, how could it? It doesn't really seem likely. And the relevant machines auto-updated their clocks. So for now, I'm going to wait to see what happens tonight. If the right stuff goes out, then I'll just call it an [edge case](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_case) or [cosmic rays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmic_ray) (programmerese for [Act of God](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Act_of_God)) and move on.
