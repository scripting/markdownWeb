One of the best things you can do to [love the web again](https://immerse.news/time-to-love-the-web-again-collective-wisdom-in-a-time-of-covid-19-crisis-2d359cf3f2f9) is decentralize. Start your own blog, and post links to social media sites. Then you're building on the resilience of the net, instead of concentrating on a potential single point of failure.
