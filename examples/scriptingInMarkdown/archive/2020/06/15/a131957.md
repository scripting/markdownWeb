I read that [GitHub](https://www.zdnet.com/article/github-to-replace-master-with-alternative-term-to-avoid-slavery-references/) is going to get rid of the concept of master for repositories because it's a reference to slavery. According to their [docs](https://guides.github.com/activities/hello-world/), "By default your repository has one branch named _master_ which is considered to be the definitive branch." A search of [github.com](https://www.google.com/search?q=site%3Agithub.com+master) finds a lot of uses of the word master. And in the rest of the world, there are master bedrooms and master's degrees. Courts appoint special masters. There are a lot of [synonyms](http://thesaurus.land/?word=master) in the thesaurus.
