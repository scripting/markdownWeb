![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/08/05/loveRss.png)[Colin Nederkoorn](https://twitter.com/alphacolin/status/1277822149781147648) reminds us that the NY Times, even though they've [opted out](https://nypost.com/2020/06/29/new-york-times-pulls-out-of-apple-news-partnership/) of Apple News still has a full complement of [RSS feeds](https://archive.nytimes.com/www.nytimes.com/services/xml/rss/index.html). The feeds are perfect. A headline, a synopsis and a link to the paywall'd article. I still would like to see a [way around](http://scripting.com/2020/06/16/133627.html?title=newsHasBeenUnbundled) the paywall for non-subscribers.
