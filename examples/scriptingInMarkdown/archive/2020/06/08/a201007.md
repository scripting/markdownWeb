There was so much happening so quickly during the first days of the protests. I couldn't figure out what was going on in NYC. And that's just one city. [This podcast](https://www.nytimes.com/2020/06/08/podcasts/the-daily/police-brutality-protests.html) filled in some of the blanks, if you're confused I highly recommend it. It pays to watch [The Fog of War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fog_of_War) every so often for a reminder of how confusing war is. In America, our military thought the Vietnam War was a [war against communism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domino_theory). On the other side it was a war for independence. We fought that war for years, at huge cost, and we didn't even agree on what the war was about. Think about that.
