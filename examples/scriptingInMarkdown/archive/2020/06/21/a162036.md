So they had 800K signups, with room for only 19K fans. Only 4 million live in [Oklahoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oklahoma). So the numbers were fishy. Even so the Trump campaign hyped them. They seem to have [believed](https://www.cnn.com/videos/politics/2020/06/20/trump-overflow-crowd-speech-phillip-sot-nr-vpx.cnn) them. They must've wanted to. Ethan Zuckerman [says](https://twitter.com/EthanZ/status/1274685979182485505) it was activism, it was, and it's surprising how well it worked.
