![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/08/05/loveRss.png)All you have to do is write an article that says [RSS is Dead](http://isrssdead.com/) and you'll get lots of clicks, and I'm not sure if anyone will believe you or think you're smart, but you are wrong. Those articles have been written for _decades,_ and my friends RSS is built-in, like HTTP and HTML, DNS, SMTP, POP and all the other michegas that makes up the net. The stack never gives up anything. Also since it never was alive, how can it be dead, n'est-ce pas?
