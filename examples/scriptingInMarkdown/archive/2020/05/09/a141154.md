[Braintrust query](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/issues/170): Writing my own synchronous functions in Node. The conclusion -- anything you can do from the command line in the OS can be synchronous, because they're easy to [invoke](https://nodejs.org/api/child_process.html#child_process_child_process_execsync_command_options) from a Node app. A wide gaping loophole in the design of Node, and I love it.
