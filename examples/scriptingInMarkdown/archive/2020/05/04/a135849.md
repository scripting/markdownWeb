My [SNL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live)\-awareness is spotty. I didn't learn about [Kate McKinnon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate_McKinnon) or [Bill Hader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Hader) until they had already been on the show for years. Same with [Tina Fey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tina_Fey) and [Amy Poehler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Poehler) and so many others. Well, over the weekend, I discovered the [Whiskers R We](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=whiskers+r+we+snl&t=ha&ia=videos&iax=videos) series. OMG. They're so good. Two lesbian cat rescue ladies, a different partner for McKinnon's character, [Barbara DeDrew](https://snl.fandom.com/wiki/Barbara_DeDrew). every [time](https://www.google.com/search?q=whiskers+r+we+snl&safe=off&rlz=1C5CHFA_enUS743US747&sxsrf=ALeKk00QqZkqKxzHdEIHuVllMw1PVMUFhQ:1588601142929&source=lnms&tbm=vid&sa=X&ved=2ahUKEwjiiMG2sJrpAhUrheAKHSzNBl8Q_AUoAXoECAQQAw&biw=1473&bih=1165).
