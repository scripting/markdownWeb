When you hear someone you love and think is smart and literate say Oh it's no big deal if I catch the virus, say "I love you and you're smart and literate, and please [read this story](https://thesunmagazine.org/issues/534/just-this-breath), because you're wrong about it not being a big deal." Perfect example of [save my life](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=site%3Ascripting.com+%22save+my+life%22&t=h_&ia=web) story-telling.
