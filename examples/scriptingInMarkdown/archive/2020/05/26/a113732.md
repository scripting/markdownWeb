The idea of a self-contained news product you subscribe to is a vestige of the way news used to be distributed. Now, in my Twitter timeline, or RSS [river](http://scripting.com/images/2020/05/26/riverScreenShot.png), I see links to stories all over the place. This is the way I want it. I want a mix, my choice.
