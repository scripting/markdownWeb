![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2020/04/30/voila.png)Now that the [Apple is happy](http://scripting.com/2020/04/30.html#a142426) with the [Cuomo podcast](http://this.how/cuomo/), it's really nice the way it updates so quickly in the Podcasts app on my iPhone. A few seconds after a new episode is available, the podcast moves to the [top of the list](http://scripting.com/images/2020/05/05/applePodcastsAppScreenShot.png) and it starts downloading the new episode. I wonder how they do it. Is my iPhone polling the feed constantly? Is an Apple server doing the polling and relaying a notice to all subscribed clients? Or perhaps they hook into the [rssCloud](http://scripting.com/2015/06/02/rsscloudDomains.html) functionality that the [feed](http://xmlviewer.scripting.com/?url=http://scripting.com/cuomo/rss.xml) [supports](http://scripting.com/images/2020/05/05/cloudElement.png) (part of RSS 2.0). That would be the best answer.
