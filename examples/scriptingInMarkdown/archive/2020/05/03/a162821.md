It's great to get John Naughton's [nightly email](https://johnnaughton.substack.com/). He writes frequently about [contact tracing](http://scripting.com/2020/04/21/191743.html?title=theEbbAndFlowOfLockdown), as he did [last night](https://johnnaughton.substack.com/p/saturday-2-may-2020). I don't agree with Schneier who dismisses the help mobile devices could offer, but some of the comments on [his post](https://www.schneier.com/blog/archives/2020/05/me_on_covad-19_.html) were right on, imho. More later for sure.
