I don't know why people take the shortcut of saying other people are [tone deaf](http://scripting.com/2019/09/09.html#a205150). Obviously the person who they're saying it to doesn't agree. Do they really want to argue about that? What's the point. It's like The Invasion of the Body Snatchers, where [Donald Sutherland](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wTP_SdjD5ms) is screaming and pointing at someone who is not yet possessed. Look I found a human, the accuser seems to be saying. It's not a good look.
