![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2020/02/02/kaepernick.png)The only football team I've ever been a fan of, the [San Francisco 49ers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco_49ers), is in tonight's SuperBowl. I'm angry and frustrated that the NFL won't hire former Niners quarterback [Colin Kaepernick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colin_Kaepernick). I also remember the intellect, wisdom, character, professionalism, talent and luck of the Niners of the 80s and 90s. So just for tonight, I'll be a football fan again. Go Niners! :football:
