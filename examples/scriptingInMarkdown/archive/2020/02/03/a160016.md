![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2020/02/03/jenniferLopez.png)Over on Facebook Terry Heaton [posted a screed](http://scripting.com/images/2020/02/03/terry.png) in support of Jennifer Lopez in last night's SuperBowl halftime show. To me it was a big meh. I find those kinds of [shows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Super_Bowl_halftime_shows#1960s) unimpressive, the music not interesting. I came to watch football, not dance. I'm not kidding. Even Prince, the Stones, Bruce, etc didn't get me off. I was in the kitchen during the [wardrobe malfunction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wardrobe_malfunction). But -- Terry's post is noteworthy because he ends it with this line: "America is facing the destruction of our democracy, and we're worried about this?" We should make a button out of that. Tatoo it on our forehead so we see it in the mirror. We need to be _good_ to each other, find ways to combine our power, not minimize it. Let's go Mets! and thanks for listening.
