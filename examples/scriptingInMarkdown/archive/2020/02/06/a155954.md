Tony Haile, the founder of [Scroll](https://scroll.com/) and [Chartbeat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chartbeat), had a really [good idea](https://twitter.com/arctictony/status/1225253452168781825). When the Senate votes on something consequential, such as whether to remove the president, let's have a readout that shows the number of people the Yes votes represent, by state, and the number of people the No votes represent. It would measure how far our government is from being a democracy. I would love to see this as a website.
