Randall Neff did an [Excel spreadsheet](http://scripting.com/2020/02/06/Senate-vote-2020-02-05.xlsx) for the impeachment trial. It wasn't as lopsided as I imagined. Roughly 52% to convict, 48% to acquit. The reps of a majority of Americans thought he was guilty, but not nearly enough to convict him. Here's the spreadsheet [in PDF](http://scripting.com/2020/02/06/Senate-vote-2020-02-05.pdf).
