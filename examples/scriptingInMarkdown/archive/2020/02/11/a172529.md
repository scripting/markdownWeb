This is weird. If you do a [Google search](https://www.google.com/search?q=What+makes+a+weblog+a+weblog%3F&rlz=1C5CHFA_enUS743US747&oq=What+makes+a+weblog+a+weblog%3F&aqs=chrome..69i57j69i64j69i60l2j69i61.1281j0j3&sourceid=chrome&ie=UTF-8) for _What makes a weblog a weblog?_ it links to a page on blogs.harvard.edu that's a [Miley Cyrus fan blog](https://blogs.harvard.edu/whatmakesaweblogaweblog/). It was last updated in 2008. Anyway, the [original piece](http://blogs.harvard.edu/whatmakesaweblogaweblog.html), intended to last for a long time, is still present, even though Google doesn't know about it. Written in 2003, by yours truly, it was an attempt to put a marker down. This is what we were doing way back when. To the future, you know how it turned out. We had no idea.
