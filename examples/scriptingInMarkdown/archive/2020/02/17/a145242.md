![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/07/22/rssShirt.png)I updated the [copy](http://scripting.com/images/2020/02/17/isrssdeadsnapshot.png) on the [Is RSS Dead](http://isrssdead.com/) page. I still really dislike the concept of pairing RSS with that word. But this site gives me a chance to say concisely what RSS is. Things like RSS are useful forever. Live or die isn't even a question, it's a standard, an agreed-to way to make things that are compatible. It's gives users maximum power. It's kind of a miracle that it exists. I question the motives of anyone who promotes the idea that it doesn't.
