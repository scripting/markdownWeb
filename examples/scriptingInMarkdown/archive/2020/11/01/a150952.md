![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2020/03/11/hillaryClinton.png)On Facebook, I'm getting another look at all the things I posed in the run-up to the 2016 election. One of the threads was a [screen shot](http://scripting.com/images/2020/11/01/clintonOdds2016.png) of the [538](https://twitter.com/FiveThirtyEight) graph showing the probability of a Clinton win over Trump over time. It took a real dive when [Comey](https://www.nytimes.com/2016/10/29/us/politics/fbi-hillary-clinton-email.html) announced he was [investigating](https://www.insider.com/lone-fbi-agent-reopened-hillary-clinton-investigation-2019-10) emails found on [Anthony Weiner's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Weiner) laptop. Nate Silver said, in the aftermath of the election, when people complained about the polls, that Trump had a greater than 30% chance of winning. A baseball player who hits .300 is a pretty good hitter. 30% ain't zero. That said Comey _made_ it 30%. The odds were a lot lower before his announcement.
