Very quietly [Medium](https://medium.com/) is no longer the online place-of-record, which is good, because as a money-losing tech startup they have no obligation to maintain a record. The bad news is that the [new incumbent](https://substack.com/) is yet another tech startup. If we want the net to work, we need some institutions that are not-for-profit. [archive.org](https://archive.org/) is a good backstop. But the web imho deserves more.
