Where did people get the idea that this holiday season could be like last year's holiday season? [Andrew Cuomo](http://this.how/cuomo/) says it's commercials, and I think he's right. Check out [this Etsy commercial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LS8npcVD4Ik). A feel-good holiday dinner. No masks. People of all generations together, indoors, no social distancing. Where did they get the idea that this kind of ad was appropriate in 2020? I'm sure the question came up.
