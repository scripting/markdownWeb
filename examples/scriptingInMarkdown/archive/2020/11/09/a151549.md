One of the reasons my blog doesn't work as [an RSS feed](http://scripting.com/rss.xml) is that I'm always adding links and editing text through the day. I am done by midnight before the [nightly email](http://scripting.com/email) goes out. But people who read via RSS only see the first version of a post. RSS as a format is fine, but the installed base of readers hasn't evolved at all since the beginning.
