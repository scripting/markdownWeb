When I was 17 I was too young to vote, but I really wanted [McGovern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1972_United_States_presidential_election) to win. I was campaigning for him, going door to door [where](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Far_Rockaway,_Queens) ever the campaign sent us. My father didn't have a strong opinion, so he let me have his vote. One of the nicest most memorable gifts he gave me.
