I'm loving [Better Things](https://www.metacritic.com/tv/better-things). It's smart, funny, classy, nutty, loving and lovable, and sexy. The characters are great because they all remind me of someone dear to me. It's not Le Grand Binge, it's more like an [amuse-bouche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amuse-bouche), something to [cleanse the palate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palate_cleanser), before going onto more serious fare. Right up there with [30 Rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/30_Rock), another of [my favorite](http://my.bingeworthy.io/davewiner/) shows. I'm still in Season 1 on Better Things. Savoring each episode because it makes me feel good, in times when feeling good isn't the norm.
