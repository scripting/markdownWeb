![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/06/23/mrNatural.png)[This is why](https://www.forbes.com/sites/patrickmoorhead/2020/11/21/apple-macbook-pro-13-m1-reviewwhy-you-might-want-to-pass/amp/) I don’t trust reviews from journos who Apple chooses to review their products. They overlook the flaws that make the machine unusable. And all the gushing over what? A new CPU? Please. I can't tell what CPU any machine is running, and neither can you, unless you open the [system About box](http://scripting.com/images/2020/11/24/macAboutSystem.png). My main concern about the new Macs with the new CPU is if VMWare runs on them, and if I can run [Frontier](http://scripting.com/2021/01/04/140651.html?title=whatFrontierIsAbout) in there. Otherwise forget it. I'll stick with my Intel iMac Pro I bought in 2017.
