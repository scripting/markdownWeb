I asked Michael Hunt about the status of the [Woodstock Library](https://www.dailyfreeman.com/news/elections/woodstock-fate-of-library-proposal-rests-with-absentee-ballots/article_4c324f4c-1ee4-11eb-a779-af9c43f53456.html) bond issue. It will be a nailbiter but it ain’t over."
