Why is no one talking about [Emily Murphy](https://twitter.com/GSAEmily), administrator of the [GSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emily_W._Murphy). She's the single government official who could turn this situation around, yet she [refuses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emily_W._Murphy#Refusal_to_begin_presidential_transition). Why haven't there been any interviews? She has more power in this matter than any governor, or secretary of state, or McConnell even. Later it won't be so clear, once this situation has [festered](http://thesaurus.land/?word=fester), but right now she alone has the power to turn the corner. I suggested on Twitter the other day we send her flowers with cards that say "Write the letter."
