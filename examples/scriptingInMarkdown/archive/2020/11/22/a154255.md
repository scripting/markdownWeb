An [interesting idea](https://twitter.com/aiddya/status/1329860731135782918) on Twitter, [addressed](https://mediagazer.com/201121/p2#a201121p2) to future generations: "So misinformation was free and facts were hidden behind a pay wall? And you all wondered why democracy was brought down by masses peddling conspiracy theories?" It's a fun theory but it's not true. Buzzfeed, Axios, CNN and many others, are not behind paywalls, and are just as reputable as the ones that are.
