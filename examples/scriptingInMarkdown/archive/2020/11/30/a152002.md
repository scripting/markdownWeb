[The Undoing](http://bingeworthy.io/tv/?id=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.metacritic.com%2Ftv%2Fthe-undoing) was even worse than I could have [imagined](http://scripting.com/2020/11/28.html#a141722). Still, it had HBO production values, acting, that part they do so well. I don't know what it is but HBO has a look and feel and the artists know how to be part of it. The story was utterly unbelievably lame. They copied an interesting Law & Order plot, it actually _was_ the plot of an episode of L&O, and stretched it out to six fucking hours. There was one interesting plot twist, no spoilers. Also if they had cut the last episode off before it got ridiculous, I might have had a kind word for it. I've rated it a :fire: over on BingeWorthy. Only the second such show. Somehow they got me to watch to the end. I guess it was kind of relaxing.
