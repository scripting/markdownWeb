![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/06/14/bowling.png)Made good progress on a FeedLand performance issue. The new version is running on [feedland.org](https://feedland.org/). We're getting ready to try it on other systems. On the way I hit a problem with the [wpcom package](https://github.com/Automattic/wp-calypso/tree/trunk/packages/wpcom.js) that implements the WordPress API in Node.js. Apparently the new version depends on [babel/runtime](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@babel/runtime/v/8.0.0-beta.1), but it isn't listed as a dependency in their [package.json](https://github.com/Automattic/wp-calypso/blob/trunk/packages/wpcom.js/package.json) file. I worked around the problem by adding that dependency in wpidentity's [package.json](https://github.com/scripting/wpIdentity/blob/main/package.json) file, and that fixed the problem. Had trouble getting this report in their [issues](https://github.com/Automattic/wp-calypso/issues) section.
