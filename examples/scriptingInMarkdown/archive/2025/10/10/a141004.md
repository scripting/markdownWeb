I know so many people around my age that were never told this simple truth that I heard Steve Jobs say in a [video](https://youtu.be/kYfNvmF0Bqw?si=NYOyLWJRnXuvCktP&t=39) the other day. Paraphrasing -- the people who made the rules you think you have to live by weren't smarter than you. Once you accept this as fact, then if you can find the leading edge you can make it work the way you want it to. You can be one of those people.
