[Rebecca Solnit](https://www.thenation.com/article/archive/the-2000-election-unleashed-disaster-on-the-world-we-cant-let-that-happen-again-in-2016/): "I think of voting as a chess move, not a valentine."

I changed the [header graphic](https://imgs.scripting.com/2025/10/26/deBlasioPolice.png?nodialog) on Scripting News today to raise a question with NYC voters.

[Bill de Blasio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_de_Blasio) was mayor. A funeral for two murdered cops. The police stand at attention, their [backs turned](https://imgs.scripting.com/2025/10/26/deBlasioPolice.png?nodialog) on the mayor. This was a statement about him for sure, but even more about their disdain for the people who elected him, you and me, the people they're sworn to protect. I grew up in NY, and had plenty of experience with NY cops and this kind of disrespect is, from my point of view, who they are. I know they see themselves as martyrs, that's what the demonstration meant. But they also have a job to do and a chain of command.

There's a lot of pent up resentment and you can't just wish it away. The mayor, for better or worse, owns it. I can't imagine they'd have much respect for the Democratic Party nominee. Like it or not, this is the city NYers live in.

I am old enough to remember when [John Lindsay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Lindsay), a liberal Republican, back when there were such things, was the newly elected mayor in 1966 and was greeted by a transit strike, shutting down subways and buses. The unions walked all over him.

I think NYC and NYers will fare better with a [Andrew Cuomo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Cuomo). This isn't the first time I've stood up for him [on this blog](https://daytona.scripting.com/search?q=cuomo). He resigned in disgrace, humiliated, but I never will forget his leadership during the darkest times of Covid. Cuomo, along with [Donald McNeil](https://www.nytimes.com/by/donald-g-mcneil-jr) at the NY Times and [Dr Anthony Fauci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Fauci) were the bright beams of intelligence and compassion.
