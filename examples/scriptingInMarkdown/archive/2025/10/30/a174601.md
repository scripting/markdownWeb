![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2024/12/25/leftfacingsanta.png)Just heard from [Brent Simmons](https://inessential.com/) that the next version of [NetNewsWire](https://github.com/Ranchero-Software/NetNewsWire/issues/3896#issuecomment-3468342615) will support the [Markdown in RSS](http://scripting.com/2022/07/19/152235.html?title=devNotesForMarkdownInRss). I wrote it up on my [daveverse site](https://daveverse.org/2025/10/30/markdown-support-coming-in-netnewswire/), which I edit with [WordLand](https://wordland.social/), which means it has a [special feed](https://wordland.dev/scripting/237777565/rss.xml) that contains [source:markdown](https://source.scripting.com/#1653758422000) elements. You can be one of the first people with markdown-support in your feed, and in doing so, help the bootstrap. If you have questions, post a comment [here](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/issues/334).
