Yesterday I did some research into how weblogs.com, in 1999, tracked changes in the blogs it was following. This was a precursor to feed readers. There was a main output file called changes.xml, a reverse chronological list of sites, not feeds, feeds didn't exist yet.

What the [home page](https://web.archive.org/web/20000620090706/http://www.weblogs.com/) of weblogs.com looked like in June 2000.

A [page](https://web.archive.org/web/20000621141352/http://www.weblogs.com/about) that explained what weblogs are.

A snapshot of [changes.xml](http://scripting.com/publicfolder/misc/changes.xml) from weblogs.com on Dec 13, 2003.

A [search](https://daytona.scripting.com/search?q=changes.xml) of scripting.com for changes.xml.

You can [ask ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/c/68f8f563-0ab8-8322-865a-af0cc88fa35a) to tell you about changes.xml. I did, it was able to put together all the random bits that are now gone, it found them somewhere and I got a much better answer than I would have gotten if somehow all my docs had survived, and most of them did not. This btw is one of the great features of ChatGPT. Truly a miracle.
