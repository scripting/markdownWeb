![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/07/18/leinenkugel.png)The [OPML version](http://scripting.com/index.opml) of this blog, [according to archive.org](https://web.archive.org/web/20041229084558/http://scripting.com/index.opml), goes back to [Dec 28, 2005](http://scripting.com/2005/12/28.html). It [appears](https://web.archive.org/web/20041229084558/http://scripting.com/index.opml) that it hasn't updated since [June 8, 2010](http://scripting.com/2010/06/08.html). On further investigation, we do maintain an [JSON file](http://scripting.com/index.json) for the site, it's just JSONified OPML. That works too. I'm looking for a way to direct posts to the WordPress news river I started last week. You can already easily use WordLand to do that, we have excellent category support, you're one click away from a dialog where you can choose from your categories while writing. You don't have to go anywhere to do that. The JSON file [starts](https://web.archive.org/web/20170718223834/http://scripting.com/index.json) on [July 18, 2017](http://scripting.com/2017/07/18.html) and it's rebuilt every time I post an update to the blog along with the RSS feed. And 2017 was when I did my 180 degree turn back to blogging as I was doing it before we were so rudely interrupted by Twitter.
