A [preview](https://imgs.scripting.com/2025/06/25/linkblogInBlogroll.png) of how a WordLand linkblog works, which is of course a WordPress site, [viewed](https://imgs.scripting.com/2025/06/25/linkblogInBlogroll.png) in my blogroll. When I clicked on the link, I was surprised that it [goes to Poynter](https://www.poynter.org/commentary/2025/trump-says-fudge-vulgarity-news/), and not to the linkblog. But then I remembered that's the point of a linkblog. And it flows through to the feed, and the blogroll software understands. So now I have an end-to-end linkblog.
