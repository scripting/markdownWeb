I was wondering if Saturday's military parade in Washington was going to be the rollout of the Trump junta, and if that meant the [president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generalissimo) would appear in military garb, perhaps subdued so next year's parade they can add more features. So I asked ChatGPT to help.

_President Trump is reviewing the military parade in on a stage in front of the White House in Washington DC. To honor the troops, he’s dressed in a faux military uniform with medals, and a cap that says Make America Great Again. Fighter jets are flying overhead._

When I tried the first time, last night, it got halfway through the drawing then quit, saying it violated their content guidelines. So I went to [meta.ai](https://www.meta.ai/), which also does drawing, and it was willing to do it, and even animated it. I uploaded the result to my [AI group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/1610208569758308) on Facebook. It should be visible to everyone, without an invitation.

I then tried again with ChatGPT and it was willing to do the same task it wasn't willing to do last night.
