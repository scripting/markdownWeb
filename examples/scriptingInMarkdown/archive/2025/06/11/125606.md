Yesterday I [introduced](http://scripting.com/2025/06/10/153445.html) a new feed for great art, 24 times a day. Every hour on the hour.

Today, a version of that feed provides one work of art each day, at midnight, Eastern.

[art.feediverse.org](https://art.feediverse.org/)

[artdaily.feediverse.org](https://artdaily.feediverse.org/)

Why I did this feed. I'm crafting a new information product, and I want to include a random work of art but not every hour, once a day is enough. I thought it was worth a detour to make the feed that I wanted.

The first work of art in the feed on its inaugural issue was [The Countess](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/scripting/artDownloader/main/data/images/FCZ5y6oWQAU8Gum.jpg) from Hans Holbein the Younger, [1526](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/365207). It was chosen at random from a [collection](https://github.com/scripting/artDownloader/tree/main) of 42,473 works of art.
