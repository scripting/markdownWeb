In honor of the Knicks' very successful season, I temporarily put the [paper bags fans](http://scripting.com/images/2023/04/27/knicksFansWithBagsOverHeads.png) at the top of my home page as a reminder to anyone who wants to blame someone on the Knicks for how the season turned out. I think we should all wear the paper bags on our heads as a reminder of how we felt about being Knicks fans [not all that long ago](https://www.nba.com/game/ind-vs-nyk-0042400301). They are always a horrible team to love, whether they're playing for the title, or just trying desperately not to be the worst team in the NBA this season. Love hurts as [some](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hFE2SnliiV0) wise [person](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DhJyjBVTyxU) once [sang](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=soDZBW-1P04).
