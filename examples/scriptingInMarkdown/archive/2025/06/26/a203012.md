[Krugman](https://paulkrugman.substack.com/p/mamdani-and-the-moguls-of-madness) doesn’t understand what’s coming for NYC. And doesn’t understand the leadership [Cuomo](http://scripting.com/2020/07/01/152316.html?title=whatWouldCuomoDo) uniquely provided at the height of Covid. It’s pretty likely what’s [starting](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cvg430dx702o) in NYC will be worse than Covid or 9-11. Funny thing is Krugman _did_ get it, a few weeks ago when the crisis in Los Angeles was peaking. He wrote a [piece](https://paulkrugman.substack.com/p/this-is-not-a-drill) that was terrified, and realistic. The National Guard had been nationalized. Marines were invading California. But now that Calif hasn't been in the news, it's easy to swing back. I bet if the election had happened during the worst of it, Cuomo would've won.
