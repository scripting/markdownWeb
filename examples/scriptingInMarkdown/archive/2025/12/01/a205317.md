![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2022/12/01/metrocard.png)I was able to use my Android phone to get on the NYC subway a few days ago. Turn the phone on, point it at the reader on the turnstyle, and just keep walking. It's that fast and a lot better than with the [MetroCard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MetroCard). Sometimes things _do_ get better.
