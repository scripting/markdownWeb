We love [Pluribus](https://daytona.scripting.com/search?q=pluribus) because it has the features we find irresistible.

1.  AppleTV.
    
2.  The makers of two [previous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breaking_Bad) huge [hits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Better_Call_Saul).
    
3.  An unsung and much loved [star](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhea_Seehorn) in the last hit.
    
4.  An intriguing sci-fi [plot](https://daytona.scripting.com/search?q=pluribus).
    
5.  It’s pretty good adventure type thriller in the first episodes then settles into a slower sexy love story, where the lead character takes us through the mind of someone falling in love and trying to figure out if the other person is worth it.
    
6.  A clever final scene in the [final episode](https://people.com/pluribus-season-1-finale-ending-explained-11873581).
    
7.  A typical long wait for the [next season](https://ew.com/pluribus-season-2-release-will-take-a-while-vince-gilligan-11873854) that we knew was coming.
    

But all this is incidental, what really matters is that we’re all involved, have opinions, and thank goodness it doesn’t actually matter like the other stuff we debate.
