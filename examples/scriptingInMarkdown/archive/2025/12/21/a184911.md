Here's the [transcript](https://chatgpt.com/share/694840eb-18fc-8012-8bef-3e78fad4b77d) of my ChatGPT conversation. One thing it is not good at is being reliable at saving transcripts. I find a lot of times people can't read it. Reminds me, this is the kind of thing [Firefox](https://shownotes.scripting.com/scripting/2025/12/18/whatWouldFirefoxDo.html) could be excellent at. Give it a way for an app to say hey the user asked for a transcript. Here it is. Save it where they're expecting to find it. No reason the browser can't have a JavaScript accessible API, as far as I know there is no rule they can't add functionality there.
