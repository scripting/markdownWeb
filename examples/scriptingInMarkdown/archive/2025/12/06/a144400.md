People using [feedland.org](https://feedland.org/?river=true&screenname=scripting) -- may notice that some old items will appear in your timelines. I just installed a version of [FeedLand](https://docs.feedland.com/gettingstarted.md) on that server that does a better job of figuring out if a feed item has changed. There will be fewer false positives, which makes the software considerably more efficient, and means that you don't have to see things that didn't change. It should settle down fairly quickly, but it may be a little chatty for a while. Still diggin! (Also these changes will come to feedland.com as well.)
