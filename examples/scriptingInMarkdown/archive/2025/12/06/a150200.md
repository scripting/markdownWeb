People seem to like [Telex](https://www.google.com/search?q=telex+wordpress) which makes developing WordPress user interfaces easier, via AI. Software is gradually adjusting this way, putting the AI where the problem is. For example I wanted to do a Google Form a few months ago and the best Gemini could do was tell me what commands to choose. But now if you go to [make a form](https://forms.google.com/), using the Forms app, it [offers to do it](https://imgs.scripting.com/2025/12/06/helloDave.png) via AI.
