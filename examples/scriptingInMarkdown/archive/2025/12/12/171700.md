![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2025/09/21/gorilla.png)In the world of WordLand and FeedLand I can create my own API for my own client. No more living with all the things the Twitter and Bluesky API designers left out or made fragile, or straight out broke. If there's a missing endpoint, I have a talk with the service devs (ie me), they listen and understand, and in an hour or so there's a new freaking endpoint. This is how we did it in the early days, I had all three components needed to move publishing forward: [Manila](https://daytona.scripting.com/search?q=Manila), [my.userland.com](https://daytona.scripting.com/search?q=my.userland.com) and [Scripting News](https://daytona.scripting.com/search?q=%22Scripting%20News%22). Well folks we're back in business again. Enough for a rebooted [writer's web](https://daytona.scripting.com/search?q=%22writer%27s%20web%22). As they say, still diggin! :-)
