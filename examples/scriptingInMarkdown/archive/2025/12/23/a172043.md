![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2025/12/23/veritas.png)Sometimes you think of things 22 years too late, like this time. I wish I had thought of meeting with the [Harvard Crimson](https://daytona.scripting.com/search?q=%22Harvard%20Crimson%22) people in 2003 and made the same offer to them that I had made to NYT the year before, ie we should offer blogs to everyone on staff, and anyone they quote, or basically anyone they want to be writing on the web, which was still a new thing -- and we'd host them alongside the ones we were hosting at the law school. Had we done that there would be a scholarly and intellectual equivalent to Facebook which was also booting up on the _same campus_ at the same time as blogging and podcasting. Love and intellect, that's a good combination for young super-achievers.
