It's not just me that says that our [social web](https://daytona.scripting.com/search?q=%22social%20web%22) is connected by [RSS](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html).

*   [Cory Doctorow](https://doctorow.medium.com/you-should-be-using-an-rss-reader-76aed31151f9) said it.
    
*   [Molly White](https://www.citationneeded.news/curate-with-rss/) said it.
    
*   And last week [Ben Werdmuller](https://werd.io/why-rss-matters/) said it.
    

If they all did it, we'd have all the freedom we could ever want.

So what's holding it up?

Let's make a resolution for 2026, that we the people, will demand that the social networking software we use get in tune with the web, and support two-way RSS.

[I will help](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/issues/347) any and all to get this going.

I'm just like you, if you don't want your online world to be owned by billionaires.

Dave
