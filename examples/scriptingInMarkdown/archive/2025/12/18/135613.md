I have a free account at [Inoreader](https://www.inoreader.com/). I was reminded today that they support dynamic OPML subscription lists, and decided to give it a try.

You can [subscribe](https://www.inoreader.com/opml_subscriptions) to an OPML subscription list. Exactly the same format we used for importing lists. This means I can use the same feed list in two readers. Or I could share my list with everyone who subscribes to my newsletter. I can update the list, and the flow of news to the subscribers changes too.

I tried subscribing to my [podcast list](https://feedland.social/opml?screenname=davewiner&catname=podcasts) in Inoreader.

1.  Put the URL to my podcast list on the clipboard.
    
2.  Go to [this page](https://www.inoreader.com/opml_subscriptions) on the Inoreader site.
    
3.  Click the big blue Subscribe to OPML button.
    
4.  Paste the URL from the first step into the dialog.
    
5.  Enter a folder name and description.
    
6.  For synchronization I recommend the second option, full synchronization.
    
7.  Then click Save, and if it worked, you should see the OPML file in a list.
    

It [worked](https://imgs.scripting.com/2025/12/18/inoreaderList.png). I now can see my podcast updates in Inoreader, exactly as if it was in FeedLand.

And when I add new feeds they show up over there, same when I remove.

It'll be very interesting to see how it changes over time. I'll let you know! ;-)
