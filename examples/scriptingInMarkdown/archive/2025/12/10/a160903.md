Every time I go to the supermarket I'm reminded of how scary the times are. People want to just [live their lives](https://bsky.app/profile/factpostnews.bsky.social/post/3m7ngrmymxk2y), never has that been more understandable, but food prices are a constant reminder for everyone there's good reason to be scared. And if you want to really feel it, imagine what happens if we somehow let Trump flood the economy with dollars. Talk about a recipe for disruption, this time, of our lives, not just some kind of PC or network or game software.
