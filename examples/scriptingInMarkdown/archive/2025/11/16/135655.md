![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2025/11/15/pluribus.png)Spoilers follow. Like [Doc](https://doc.searls.com/2025/11/16/sun-day/), I have trouble getting into TV series, but not [Pluribus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pluribus_\(TV_series\)), probably because it came from the [showrunner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vince_Gilligan) of Breaking Bad and Better Call Saul, two series that I have watched from beginning to end at least three times.

The star of Pluribus, [Rhea Seahorn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhea_Seehorn), was my favorite in [Better Call Saul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Better_Call_Saul), as Kim Wexler. So I expected a continuation of those shows, and in some sense Pluribus _is_ much like them, but the main character Carol, is nothing like Kim -- who is by far the coolest character in any of the shows, and that's saying a lot.

That's okay because the _sweetest_ character yet in a Vince Gilligan comedy (all these shows are comedies btw, even though the plots are super heavy), is Carol's sidekick, Zosia, played by [Karolina Wydra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karolina_Wydra). She's basically the voice of the Others, but the illusion is so alluring, one character in the show has already (understandably) fallen in love with her. And we know that Carol has formed an attachment too, despite her best efforts to hate her. ;-)
