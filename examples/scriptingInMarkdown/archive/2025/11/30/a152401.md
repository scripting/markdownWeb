![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/08/06/hippieBus.png)Hypothetically, if someone were building a headless version of [Frontier](http://scripting.com/2021/01/04/140651.html?title=whatFrontierIsAbout) with help from ChatGPT, they might be thinking about how to build a web app that was a really good shell to start with. If such a person asked what I thought, I would say without a doubt that would be [Drummer](http://drummer.land/). The reason is it was designed for that purpose, because I needed many of the features of Frontier for my work environment, and as you know Frontier was built around the outliner, object database and verb set, back in 1988. Anyway, I also [wrote a doc](https://docserver.scripting.com/drummer/scripting.opml) in April of 2020, that went through all the features of Drummer as a scripting environment. It's as if it were designed for such a hypothetical project. I wrote a [new intro](https://docserver.scripting.com/drummer/scripting.opml#1764514479000) from the point of view of 2025. It might be interesting to write another intro in 2030, knock wood, Praise Murphy, I am not a lawyer and (as far as I know) my mother loves me.
