![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2025/11/24/pocketCastsLogo.png)[Pocket Casts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pocket_Casts) has come up with a [new feature](https://blog.pocketcasts.com/2025/11/24/playlists-a-new-way-to-organize-your-listening/) called Playlists. I was able to figure out how to use it in a few minutes using their [web app](https://pocketcasts.com/playlists). Questions. Is there an open format behind this feature? Can I create a playlist outside of Pocket Casts? Can I use a PC playlist in another app? Pocket Casts comes from Automattic so I would expect the answers to be yes, given how committed they are to the web, and how podcasting is [of the web](http://scripting.com/2025/11/24/141418.html). The most logical open format to use here would be imho [RSS 2.0](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html).
