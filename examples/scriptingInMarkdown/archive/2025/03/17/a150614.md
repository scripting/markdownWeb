I replaced it with a template in the feeder app which was a useful version of the cute little app. Here's a [demo](https://feeder.scripting.com/?template=jsonify&feedurl=http%3A%2F%2Fjeffmilner.com%2F%3Ffeed%3Drss2) of it viewing the contents of a feed in JSON using a special template.
