I took a long drive today on back roads, down toward Poughkeepsie, and on the way back a big pickup swerved into my lane. I had to swerve away from it and hit the brake, and had a bit of a [conniption](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/conniption). Got me thinking. Who was it that did this. Man or woman, young or old, spaced out or thinking about getting a [libtard](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/libtard) to freak out, all kinds of thought. Then I remembered what keeps us safe in situations like this, very few people are going to deliberately try to kill us this way because they would die too. [Mutual assured destruction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutual_assured_destruction). But I'm a programmer, trained to always think of things that could go wrong, then I realized omg, what if it were a [self-driving car](https://thenewswheel.com/this-tesla-drove-itself-to-its-owner-the-video-is-both-fascinating-and-alarming/)? Someone could program it to assassinate a random or specific person, or worse, hack the car remotely, or hack a thousand cars everywhere at the same time to kill people all over the world. I thought we could prevent this by right now requiring there always be a [human sacrifice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_sacrifice) in every car, sort of a hostage to assure good behavior, sometimes.
