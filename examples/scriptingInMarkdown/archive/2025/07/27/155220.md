I wasn't planning on this, but there was a [report](https://github.com/scripting/bingeworthySupport/issues/5) that there was a problem with [BingeWorthy](https://tv.bingeworthy.org/), looked into it and was able to fix it.

Source of problem: When I added a feature that lets you ask ChatGPT to review the program you're looking at, I broke the ability to add a new program to the database. It took about 15 minutes to track down and verify and another few minutes to fix -- and now that important function works again.

As long as I was in there working around, I updated the [Bingeworthy RSS feed](https://tv.bingeworthy.org/rss.xml) to only report program additions. The other events it was reporting just weren't as interesting.

I also added that feed to my [blogroll](https://imgs.scripting.com/2025/07/27/blogrolledBingeworthy.png) on [scripting.com](http://scripting.com/).

I'd say it works a lot better now.
