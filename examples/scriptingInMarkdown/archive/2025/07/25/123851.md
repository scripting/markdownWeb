I got tired of my old sound system, too many wires, a big receiver whose functions I never used, all designed long before the 4-year-old 65-inch OLED screen on top of it all, so I downscaled to a [Sony soundbar](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0BWLCLZPS?ref=ppx_yo2ov_dt_b_fed_asin_title&th=1), figured that was as simple as you could get, for $300, thinking of it as an experiment.

I liked it but then I thought to ask ChatGPT a question I've had for a while. I want a small amp designed for today's music and video, and went through a bunch of options and came up with the [WiiM Home amp](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CGCLXH4H?ref=ppx_yo2ov_dt_b_fed_asin_title&th=1). No speakers, unlike the soundbar, but hooks up to the TV via the ARC connector, and I have plenty of old speakers to try out in this configuration.

I got it yesterday and the setup experience was pretty great and the feature list is totally 2025. Will have more to say for sure.

![Rear view of the WiiM Home amp.](https://imgs.scripting.com/2025/07/25/wiimRearView.png)

PS: It's from a Silicon Valley [tech company](https://www.linkplay.com/aboutus) btw. Nice to see a company just designing nice products and not trying to take over the world.
