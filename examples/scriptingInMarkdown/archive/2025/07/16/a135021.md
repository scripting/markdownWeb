Someday [Manton and I](https://micro.blog/manton/69056237) will make a very nice Markdown editor for Mastodon. Once it's working I'll pitch [Rich Siegel](https://www.barebones.com/company/history.html) to do the same for [BBEdit](https://www.barebones.com/products/bbedit/). Along with the WordPress connection, that should nail it once and for all that Mastodon is a blogging platform. Manton is the right guy to do it, he has all the protocols implemented on micro.blog. All I want is a REST version of what the [MetaWeblog API](https://xmlrpc.com/metaweblog) has been [doing](https://daytona.scripting.com/search?q=MetaWeblog) since [2002](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rssVersionHistory.html). We're turning the clock back to move forward. Trying to undo the damage Twitter did to the web.
