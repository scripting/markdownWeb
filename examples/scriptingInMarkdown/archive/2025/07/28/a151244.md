Everything in ChatGPT is _so_ nice. I just asked it about a random plant I got as a gift, and it gave me a beautiful [one pager](https://imgs.scripting.com/2025/07/28/deltaDawn.png) with everything I would have had to spend time searching for all right there, beautifully laid out, and all the fine UI touches you might think of already in. It's studying us and learning, and picking out the good stuff, at least so far. The web was like this too in the beginning, mind-exploding inventions every day. We called them [mind bombs](http://scripting.com/davenet/2000/08/26/mindBombsForY2k.html). The journalists and social media influencers all just complain, while there is a revolution happening, progress that had slowed to a snail pace, or very often went in reverse, is now coming at breakneck speed. This is as transformative innovation as there has ever been, not that I have much perspective on those that happened before I was invented, but it's as big as the Beatles, the PC, web, mobile.
