![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2023/09/09/poe3.png)Why I need [WordLand](https://wordland.social/). I’m primarily a writer, my podcasts reflect that, so most of the work I do on each podcast is in writing the show notes. I have a [template](https://gist.github.com/scripting/69436e223b212315846f5944e0957504) the writing and audio flow through. Fairly standard stuff, the same approach used by Tumblr and many other blogging systems, including UserLand's Manila and Radio UserLand. Here's an [example](https://shownotes.scripting.com/scripting/2025/06/23/wordpressAndMe.html) of a page rendered through that template. We’re doing similar things with WordPress using themes. The idea of WordLand is to do all the block-oriented work once, outside of the writing environment, then flow my writing through it, far away from the heavy lifting. It’s always how I’ve done my blogging tools. I understand WordPress so far has a steady workflow thru the block editor, but these are workflows for designers and programmers. WordLand is the flow for writers.
