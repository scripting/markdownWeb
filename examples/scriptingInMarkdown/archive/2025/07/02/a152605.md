![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2023/03/12/hamstercage.png)Software internally is mostly pipes connected to other pipes, each adding a specific quality to whatever passes through it. If you have nice standards for what you send through the pipes, you can do more of what you imagine. This is called [orthogonality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthogonality_\(programming\)). [Factoring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Code_refactoring) is when you notice a repeating pattern, give it a name, and a set of things you can do to it, those would be names of pipes. I have to ask ChatGPT what it thinks about this, but I am also asking my human friends. BTW I expect this seems so natural because our minds probably work that way too, internally, below our conscious awareness.
