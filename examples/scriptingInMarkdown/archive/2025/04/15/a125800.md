![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/scripting.com/images/2022/10/31/camel.png)[WordLand 0.5.4](https://github.com/scripting/wordlandSupport/issues/70). New feature, the Timeline. "I can imagine there will be a Timeline for news about WordLand, or a Timeline that contains Scripting News posts. A Timeline for all the people you work with, the people you play chess with. Basically it will be possible to have Timelines that correspond to anything that can be represented in RSS. It's possible to imagine a product where the Timeline is the main display and the editor is the one that pops up."
