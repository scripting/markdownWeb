Just tried an [experiment](https://chatgpt.com/share/67efabbd-1758-8012-a19b-a912cd72944a), I asked ChatGPT to review [ActivityPub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ActivityPub) re [Rules for Standards-makers](https://this.how/standards/). I totally concurred with its conclusions. In any case, it illustrates how ChatGPT can be helpful in designing new formats and protocols, making them more supportable and more useful for interop, which according to Rule 1, is the only reason we make standards.
