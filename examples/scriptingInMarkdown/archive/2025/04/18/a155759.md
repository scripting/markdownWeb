![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/scripting.com/images/2019/05/16/weTryHarder.png)I'm working on a [baseline theme](https://github.com/scripting/wordlandSupport/issues/71) for WordLand-authored sites. I want to show people how to get a good result with WordLand, even if they have plenty of experience with WordPress, but especially if they don't. I want people to look at a user's site and think "Hey I want one of those!" Not too fancy, just get out of the way and let the writer's writing stand out and look great. This is a replay of the work we did on [Manila](https://www.google.com/search?q=site%3Ascripting.com+manila&rlz=1C5CHFA_enUS743US747&oq=site%3Ascripting.com+manila&aqs=chrome..69i57j69i58.4100j0j4&sourceid=chrome&ie=UTF-8) and then [Radio](https://www.google.com/search?q=site%3Ascripting.com+%22radio+userland%22). I hope we're able to start a designer community as vibrant and productive as the one we had a few years ago.
