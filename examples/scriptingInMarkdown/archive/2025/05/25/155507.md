It was so predictable. Shortly after Pete Rose died, baseball [rescinded his lifetime ban](https://sports.yahoo.com/article/calls-escalate-against-rob-manfred-220702792.html), and [now he can](https://www.google.com/search?q=May+13%2C+2025%2C+Manfred+instituted+a+change) be [inducted](https://www.google.com/search?q=pete+rose+hall+of+fame+induction) into the Hall of Fame.

I grew up with a Pete Rose ethos, why shouldn't I go for it all? I feel so sad that he couldn't have gotten the acceptance he so totally deserved. How could we not forgive him? He was Charlie Hustle! :heart:

A role model in so many ways, and like all heroes he had fatal flaws. You may think he set a bad example for kids, but I say the opposite. You don't give kids enough credit.

What he taught was that a man with average athletic ability could will himself to achieve.

He had [more hits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pete_Rose#Records_and_achievements) than any baseball player ever.
