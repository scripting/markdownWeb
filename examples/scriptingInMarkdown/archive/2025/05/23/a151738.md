If you're into Mastodon, you can follow my [daveverse](https://daveverse.org/) blog, the one I write using [WordLand](https://wordland.social/), on Mastodon, using this address: @scripting@daveverse.org. Mastodon preserves links and styling, and doesn't seem to have a size limit. I can edit my posts and you get the updates. So Mastodon kind of informally has become a blogging system, which I totally applaud. It checks a lot of the boxes from my [textcasting](https://textcasting.org/) manifesto. Slowly the world is starting to make sense.
