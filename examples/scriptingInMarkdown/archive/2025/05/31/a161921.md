RSS never had a big corporation acting as a benefactor. Is that why imho RSS hasn't been considered as the backbone of the social web? Probably more likely it's the [Fog Of War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fog_of_war) nature of tech development. We have so many forks of so many things we don't know what anyone else is doing. It's easier to invent your own than figure out what the other thing did. Perhaps ChatGPT et al will change that. Yesterday I used it to get an understanding of how the [content:encoded element](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/issues/320) came to exist and evolved, and you'd think I of all people would know, but I didn't. ChatGPT [did](https://chatgpt.com/share/6839d31f-9910-8012-b324-a0bb3c052f4f).
