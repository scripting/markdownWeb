![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2023/11/30/goodhumortruck.png)Why does Apple invent their own proprietary plugs when everyone else does USB? Even if you don't like to speak ill of Apple, you _know_ why they do it. They want to control who can make add-ons to their products in hardware, just as they do in software. I still buy their products, but I also buy products that use standards, so I can use them on lots of devices. Now, the same thing happens in software from other people and companies. They can choose to use what already exists, or invent their own. Example, I chose MP3 for podcasting, did not invent a new format. If I were to do a social web network, I would use RSS, I wouldn't invent a new format. I want interop. I want to create an open platform, I don't mind making money, but that's not why I do it.
