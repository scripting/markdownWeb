![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2023/02/28/presidentCarter.png)[A note to users](https://this.how/feedland/) of feedland.org and feedland.com. We'll be turning the servers off sometime before the end of March. I haven't chosen the actual date yet. In the meantime, Please [download](https://this.how/feedland/#1739205763000) a copy of your subscription list before the shutdown, it's incredibly easy. You can also [run your own](https://github.com/scripting/feedlandInstall/blob/main/README.md) FeedLand. I started a [thread](https://github.com/scripting/feedlandSupport/issues/245) on the support site if you have questions, remember the comment [guidelines](https://this.how/commentGuidelines/). I've learned a lot from the experience of running FeedLand this way, looking forward to trying some other configurations. :smile:
