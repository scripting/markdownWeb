The key was I [learned](https://bsky.app/profile/bmann.ca/post/3lhluwhk6jk2g) how to do the equivalent of a [View Source](https://pdsls.dev/at/did:plc:dwaduijo6ngdgzmir5vovzrh/app.bsky.feed.post/3lhlwfuq2a22u) on a Bluesky post so I could see how others did it. I tried Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, none of them even understood the question of how to get Bluesky to add the data. I was amazed to learn that each developer has to do it for themselves, that this isn't something they built into their server. Anyway, I'm halfway there.
