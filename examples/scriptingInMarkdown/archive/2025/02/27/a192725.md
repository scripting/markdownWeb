![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2025/02/27/engs.png)Like many other people I love [Severance](https://daytona.scripting.com/search?q=Severance), esp this season, and esp the [most recent episode](https://www.google.com/search?q=severance+season+2+episode+6). My favorite scene in the last episode [took place](https://screenrant.com/severance-season-2-episode-6-why-helena-followed-mark-to-restaurant/) at a Chinese restaurant. Anyway, I was just listening to the latest episode of the [Severance podcast](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/s2e6-attila-with-christopher-walken-and-sarah-bock/id1788381175?i=1000694662565), about this episode, which you should definitely listen to if you like me love the show. They have an interview with [Christopher Walken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Walken) who plays [Burt](https://severance-tv.fandom.com/wiki/Burt_Goodman). Amazing stuff. But even more amazing is that I learned in the podcast that the restaurant mentioned earlier is actually a famous [Kingston restaurant](https://www.engsrestaurant.com/) that just [re-opened](https://www.timesunion.com/hudsonvalley/food/article/engs-chinese-restaurant-kingston-opens-dining-room-20058303.php) after a long hiatus, so I've not yet had a chance to eat there, but I want to, [Eng's](https://www.engsrestaurant.com/). This, after learning that [the diner](https://ny.eater.com/2025/1/27/24350496/severance-pips-phoenicia-diner-catskills) that's in two scenes, one in each season, is the [Phoenicia Diner](https://www.phoeniciadiner.com/), one of my favorite local eating places.
