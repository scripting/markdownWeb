![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/scripting.com/images/2019/09/14/rssCoffeeCup.png)I'm also interested in social networks and [RSS](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html) and have [written](https://daytona.scripting.com/search?q=wordland) a lot about that in the context of WordLand, because it generates RSS, it can connect to any app that understands RSS. But don't get confused that WordLand is somehow a twitter-like silo. There are already too many of those. I want something much simpler and I believe more useful and less spammy and abusive -- _a social network built around RSS._
