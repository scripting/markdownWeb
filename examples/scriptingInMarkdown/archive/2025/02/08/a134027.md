![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2022/05/08/teslaModelY.png)I still haven't decided what to do about my [Tesla Model Y](https://daytona.scripting.com/search?q=tesla), which still is a wonderful car, and I still love driving it [after](http://scripting.com/2021/11/19/140204.html?title=myNewTesla) 3+ years, which is amazing. But the other day it updated, and gave me a pause, if they let Musk hack their computers the way he's hacking ours, should I trust what it installs on the vehicle I drive every day that theoretically has the ability to take control of my car and drive it off the road? I wonder if the people at Tesla would like to reassure people who drive their cars that the updates are safe from hacking by one of his associates? How is security on the Tesla network?
