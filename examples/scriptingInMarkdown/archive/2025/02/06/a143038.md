People might be curious to see the [list of feeds](https://viewfeedlist.opml.org/?url=https%3A%2F%2Ffeedland.social%2Fopml%3Fscreenname%3Ddavewiner%26catname%3Dblogroll) that make up the [Little Feed Reader](https://this.how/feediverse/bluesky/) account [on Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/feediverse.bsky.social). It's dynamic, I can add or remove feeds through a very simple [user interface](https://imgs.scripting.com/2025/02/06/feedlistEditor.png) designed for managing systems like this. It's like the user inteface of a product like WordPress, for designers and developers, producing results that are for users. There are now apps for the [NBA](https://bsky.app/profile/nba.feediverse.org), [Devs](https://bsky.app/profile/devs.feediverse.org) and all [my feeds](https://bsky.app/profile/dave.feediverse.org).
