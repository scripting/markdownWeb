[WordLand](https://wordland.social/) is where we start to boot up a simple social net using only [RSS](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html) as the protocol connecting users. Rather than wait for ActivityPub and AT Proto to get their acts together. I think we can do it with feeds and start off with _immediate_ interop without the complexity of federation. I call it the [feediverse](https://feediverse.org/). It's not a joke, although it may incite a smile and a giggle. And that's ok.
