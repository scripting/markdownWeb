![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/scripting.com/images/2024/10/24/wordlandlogo.png)[WordLand](https://wordland.social/) opened yesterday for everyone to use. There was a deal-stopping bug reported last night, [fixed](https://daveverse.wordpress.com/2025/02/21/bookmarks-opml-not-found-ooops/) provisionally, then for real this morning. Prevented new users from getting started. Really embarassing. If you have trouble, please report. There's a link for Support in the [main menu](https://imgs.scripting.com/2025/02/22/support.png). WordLand is designed to be the kind of editor you use in a social app like Bluesky or Mastodon, but with most of the features of [textcasting](https://textcasting.org/).
