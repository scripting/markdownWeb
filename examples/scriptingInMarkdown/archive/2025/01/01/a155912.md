![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2024/09/10/kittyStamp.png)I wrote a few blog posts in [WordLand](https://wordland.social/) this morning because it was convenient. It's good the same way I like to use a twitter-like app to write first drafts, when all I have to type into is my iPad. I've also started using it on my desktop for short things, but I just [wrote](https://imgs.scripting.com/2025/01/01/wordlandScreen.png) and edited a [complete blog post](https://daveverse.wordpress.com/2025/01/01/gambling-has-infested-sport-in-new-unacceptable-ways/), a response to something [Krugman wrote](https://paulkrugman.substack.com/p/the-plot-to-poison-childrens-minds), about how gambling is destroying sports. And what for? It's like what [Marge said](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FfI2iFvloso) at the end of Fargo. "There's more to life than a little money, you know." It feels like they're feeding the NBA into a [wood chipper](https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/fargo-wood-chipper). What comes out the other side won't imho be recognizable as sport. And here you are, and it's a beautiful day.
