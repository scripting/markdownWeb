[Daytona](https://daytona.scripting.com/search?q=berkeley) is the search engine for Scripting News.

I've wanted to do a rewrite for quite some time, there were a lot of decisions I wanted to redo, and I've learned a lot about databases in the [three years](http://scripting.com/2021/12/) since the first release.

You can [try it out](https://daytona.scripting.com/). There are [docs](https://this.how/daytona/), and a [place](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/issues/317) for questions and comments. The usual caveats apply, esp since it's newly deployed, quickly.

![Screen shot of the new Daytona.](https://imgs.scripting.com/2025/01/15/newDaytonaScreenshot.png)

PS: A list of [problems](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/issues/317#issuecomment-2593188990) I will investigate.
