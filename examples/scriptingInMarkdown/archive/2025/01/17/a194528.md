![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2023/07/28/marshmallowManFromGhostbusters.png)I've come to the conclusion that you can't really use [ChatGPT](https://daytona.scripting.com/search?q=chatgpt) as a programming partner. It doesn't understand the big picture, and if you try to explain it, it probably isn't getting it, and if it does, it will forget it quickly. It's basically fairly stupid, but a way better search engine than Google. Also, it never says it doesn't have enough info to answer the question and then an hour later you figure out the answer it gave you just doesn't work. I still think it's an incredibly useful too, just not as great as I used to think. In a couple of years it has never asked for more information before it answers. That's a clue.
