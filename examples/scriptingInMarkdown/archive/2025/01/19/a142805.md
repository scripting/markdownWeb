When I put an image in the margin of a post I often pause and think about what I want to convey with the image as it relates to the writing that it's next to. In the previous piece the idea was interop. I tried to think of what images I've used in the past, then I thought why don't I just look. I switched into Daytona, entered [interop](https://daytona.scripting.com/search?q=interop), and found [one](https://imgs.scripting.com/2023/03/12/hamstercage.png) I loved, but then kept scanning, and found [another](https://imgs.scripting.com/2024/02/01/runner.png) that I liked even more. Tools are important. Web writers haven't gotten any new tools in a long time. All the tool development has been for other stuff. Let's make tools for users again, as we did in the olden times. Craftwork in software. Playin in the band!
