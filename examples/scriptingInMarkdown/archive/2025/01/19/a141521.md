![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2020/11/27/duck.png)How easy would it be to create a twitter-like app using RSS, and in what ways would it differ from other twitter-like apps. Here's the deal. You need a place to write a posts and a way to read a timeline of posts. That's the basic functionality. To do that with RSS you would start with a blogging app like WordPress, and a feed reader like [FeedLand](https://docs.feedland.com/gettingstarted.md) or [NetNewsWire](https://netnewswire.com/). Manton has integrated the two into a single user interface at [micro.blog](https://micro.blog/). I'm going to approach it in a somewhat differently, not sure yet how it will work, but I'm getting there. And I will offer a way for people to hook in any feed reader they want, thus opening up innovation to tech-curious users. The take-away is that you need to handle inbound and outbound feeds. That's the basics of being twitter-like.
