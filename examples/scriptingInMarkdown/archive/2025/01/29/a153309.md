![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2025/01/29/shanker.png)One more thing then I have to get back to work. There's a great moment in Woody Allen's [Sleeper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleeper_\(1973_film\)) where a time traveler was told that the world was destroyed when [Albert Shanker](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D2fYguIX17Q&t=130s) got hold of a nuclear warhead. If you lived in NYC in the 70s and 80s you knew [Albert Shanker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Shanker) as a NY character like [Al Sharpton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Sharpton) or [Curtis Sliwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curtis_Sliwa). New York [had](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keith_Hernandez) its own semi-celebrities, [people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rudy_Giuliani) who [were](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruce_Morrow) famous [mostly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Breslin) in New York, [but](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bella_Abzug) not [as](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ed_Koch) much [outside](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Lindsay). So Albert Shanker with a nuclear weapon kind of fit his character as a bomb throwing type in city politics in an extreme way. A fitting and comic way for us to end it all. Proof that god has a sense of humor. It was an inside reference only NYers would get. Well in case you didn't know it, back in that time or a little later, Trump was one of those Albert Shanker types. And yes, now again he has all the nuclear weapons. And it still feels like he's only famous in a mediocre Queens real estate way. Isn't that weird?
