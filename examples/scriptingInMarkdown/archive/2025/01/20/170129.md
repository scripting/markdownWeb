There's a [great scene](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dRQtjVzj1bo) in [No Country For Old Men](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Country_for_Old_Men), where a character is facing imminent death, but he's arguing with the character who will kill him, who asks if all your great ideas led to this (his death) how good were the ideas (paraphrasing).

Along those lines.. If Twitter was such a great idea but it led to the death of democracy (for now at least) maybe it wasn't such a great idea. Maybe when we try to reboot we should try something _realllly_ different.

[As they say](https://www.goodreads.com/quotes/5543-insanity-is-doing-the-same-thing-over-and-over-again) -- Insanity is doing the same thing over and over again and expecting different results.
