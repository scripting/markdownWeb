I would like to get on the record.

1.  The only [billionaire-proof](https://daytona.scripting.com/search?q=%22billionaire-proof%22) social network will be based on RSS.
    
2.  The shortest path to being [billionaire-proof](https://billionaireproof.com/) for Bluesky is well thought-out inbound and outbound RSS.
    

We're going to have some interop based on RSS not too far down the road. A network that will only require an RSS feed for entry. It's what we should've built in 2006 and didn't.
