![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2018/08/21/bloatwareBoss.png)I think I'm going to put in my will that ChatGPT should run the Dave Winer persona on all social networks, and my blog, as long as the money lasts. It would tell stories that I would likely tell, take political stands that I would take, draw meta-pictures of my sad and depressed [programmer friend](http://scripting.com/2025/05/09/202442.html) and a cute and [adorable kitty](https://daytona.scripting.com/search?q=%22wordle%20kitty%22) getting into all kinds of trouble. The seasons would come and go, and there would be Dave, still diggin. And of course he would continue to develop software, using some of the greatest tried and true tools, reminding everyone of how great [Frontier](http://scripting.com/2021/01/04/140651.html?title=whatFrontierIsAbout) is -- but -- if only it ran [on Linux](https://daytona.scripting.com/search?q=%22frontier%20on%20linux%22). The long-lived fearless and fully paid-up version of Uncle D.
