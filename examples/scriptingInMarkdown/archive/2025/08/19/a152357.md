I had an idea that [Global Voices](https://globalvoices.org/), at least in concept, could help us prepare in the US for what's coming. I wrote: I'd love to have is an **idea-sharing network** where i could drop this idea in a box named "helping the US stay free" and have it find it's way to the right people in Ukraine who are saying "if only I could talk to people in the US trying to figure this out, we know so much about this!" That's the thing. How to connect know-how with the place that needs to know. To make our resistance more effective use the lessons learned in Ukraine over the last decades.
