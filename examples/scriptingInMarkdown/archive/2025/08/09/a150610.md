![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2023/10/29/grandpaSimpson.png)I did a rewrite of the [FeedCorps page](https://feedland.org/?feedcorps=true) in FeedLand. You get to it through the Reading Lists [sub-menu](https://imgs.scripting.com/2025/08/09/feedCorpsScreenShot.png?nodialog) of the Tools menu. There are three lists in the new version. A lot were false starts, they didn't make the cut. I'm always adding feeds to my blogroll and news.scripting.com. Unfortunately I can't say the same for podcasts, which are not hooked up to my podcast app. I really want a hot connection there. We'll get there. The reading lists feature is going to play a big role going forward in the open social web we're building. BTW, I really like the name [FeedCorps](https://daytona.scripting.com/search?q=feedcorps). I haven't talked about it very much. It's a cause, like peace or freedom. Open those suckas up. Feeds all the way babe.
