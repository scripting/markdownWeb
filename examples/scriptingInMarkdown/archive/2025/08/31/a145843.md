There are two quotes that make me laugh in the [Think Different](http://scripting.com/2025/08/28/140604.html) piece from last week. One of them is [here](http://scripting.com/2025/08/28/140604.html#:~:text=They%20took%20paradise%20and%20blew%20big%20holes%20in%20it%20that%20users%20couldn't%20find%20a%20way%20out%20of.): "They took paradise and.." At first, if you're roughly my age, you read "They took paradise and..." and recognize it as the beginning of a famous line from Joni Mitchell's [Big Yellow Taxi](https://genius.com/Joni-mitchell-big-yellow-taxi-lyrics). The other is a subtle touch of sarcasm, that I usually try not to indulge in, but let pass through as I edited this piece. See if you can [find](http://scripting.com/2025/08/28/140604.html) it.
