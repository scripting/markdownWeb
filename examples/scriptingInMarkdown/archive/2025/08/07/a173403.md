New version of [feedlandSocket](https://github.com/scripting/feedlandSocket). It's now an NPM package you can include in Node projects. The demo is more useful, and there's a [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_Q-ks3uytZI) of what it looks like as it scrolls through the JavaScript console. WebSockets + feeds. A fairly important component of an open social web system.
