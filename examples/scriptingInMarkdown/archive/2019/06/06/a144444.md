[GPS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Positioning_System) is an amazing thing, but there are places it just doesn't work, like what we used to call [The Big Dig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Dig) in Boston. Too many levels of tunnels. GPS can't tell if you're underground or on the surface.
