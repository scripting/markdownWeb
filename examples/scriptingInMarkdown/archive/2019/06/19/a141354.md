The best movie ever, one that I always forget to put on my [list](http://scripting.com/2014/07/16/moviesForMeAt20.html), is [Groundhog Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groundhog_Day_\(film\)). So entertaining, well-done, a love story, a [story](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uw63_YyNsF4) of redemption, a prequel for [Bill Murray's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Murray) [long](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X-PnPrjqkv8) [career](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sU0oZsqeG_s), and a wonderful [Frank Capra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Capra)\-like ending. A perfect movie.
