I'm glad to see a few [#indieweb](https://twitter.com/search?q=%23indieweb) [people](https://twitter.com/ChrisAldrich/status/1141216395545305088) building on OPML. There's a lot you can do with it. For example, the native file format of [LO2](http://littleoutliner.com/) is OPML. So it's really good for editing subscription lists for feed readers. And editing blogrolls. My [listicle](http://storage.littleoutliner.com/users/davewiner/listicles/listicle1.html) tool takes OPML files as input. And [River6](http://scripting.com/2019/06/18.html#a164307), coming soon, does a bunch with OPML. There's also [feedBase](http://feedbase.io/) which will blow your mind if you love sharing subscription lists. Yes there's a lot to discover in OPML-land and more on the way. :boom:
