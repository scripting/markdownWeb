![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/02/08/oklahoma.png)There was a point in the development of [RSS](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html), after a few VCs invested in startups, that the consensus among them (the founders and the investors) was that RSS was theirs, and they told me to my face they weren't going to listen, because I was the old way, and they were the new. (Famous last words.) Even some people who thought they were friends did this, perhaps not so bluntly. Okay now fast forward, all the startups are gone, they were so stupid they failed for many other reasons, not just their arrogance and closed minds. Stupid people. Here's a better rule. Listen by default. Tech is filled with idiotic arrogance. It's a kind of narcissism. I am the only one who can think. Bullshit. Look at all you're building on. The people who created that not only had an idea, _but they were right._ That's a very rare combination. And until you have built something like that, you are _not_ smarter. And once you have, you know other people are smart, because that's what you learn.
