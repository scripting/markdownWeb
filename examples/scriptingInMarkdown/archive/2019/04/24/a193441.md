[Sam Ervin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Ervin) was probably the best character in Watergate. An avuncular country lawyer type, on the verge of senility, but incredibly eloquent all the same. He was one of the last Democrats from the Deep South to serve in the Senate. [Barbara Jordan](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UG6xMglSMdk), also on the Select committee was from Texas, and incredibly eloquent and held the attention of the camera.
