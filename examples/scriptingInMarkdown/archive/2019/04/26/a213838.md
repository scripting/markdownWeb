![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/04/17/roku2.png)I've been steadily building out [my home theater](http://scripting.com/2019/04/17/144031.html). Got a nice [subwoofer](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B0002KVQBA/ref=ppx_yo_dt_b_asin_title_o05_s00?ie=UTF8&psc=1) last week, and a [center channel](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B000V2QVEQ/ref=ppx_yo_dt_b_asin_title_o01_s00?ie=UTF8&psc=1) speaker came today. One thing I've realized is that Netflix has the best sound and picture of any of the other streamers, some of which are really shitty. So I always turn to Netflix to test a new setup. Also HBO has very high quality. Game of Thrones is great for testing, even though the first two episodes of the season have been very disney or hallmark.
