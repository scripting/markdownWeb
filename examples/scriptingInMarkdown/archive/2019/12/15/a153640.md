![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/09/01/mrFrog.png)Are you kidding? Amazon [appropriated](https://www.nytimes.com/2019/12/15/technology/amazon-aws-cloud-competition.html?emc=rss&partner=rss) the small company's trademark -- Elastic? Please, NYT call at least one developer before you run a story like this, or do a [search](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=amazon+elastic&t=h_&ia=web). This is super embarrassing -- for you. Here's the problem with the premise. Amazon has been calling their services Elastic this and that since inception. The first was [EC2](https://aws.amazon.com/ec2/). Elastic Compute Cloud. It was [started in 2006](http://scripting.com/2006/08/24.html#When:8:07:26AM), six years before the [startup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elastic_NV) was founded. It seems to me, unless I'm missing something, that the startup copied Amazon.
