![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/12/08/mailtothefuture.png)I got an email from a longtime reader who wanted to know what became of [Mail To The Future](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=%22mail+to+the+future%22+site%3Ascripting.com&t=h_&ia=web). He said he sent an email to himself twenty years ago and would like to read it.

I feel so irresponsible, when you create a site like that you're pretty much obligated to keep it running. But the contents of the database, if I was any good at backups, is on one of those CDs. I will put the time in at some point to try to restore the app. It should be relatively easy to port to Node. And I can easily export the data out of a root file. I still have [Frontier](http://scripting.com/2021/01/04/140651.html?title=whatFrontierIsAbout) running on my modern iMac. I use it all the time. ;-)

BTW, I still have [the domain](http://mailtothefuture.com/), and put a placeholder page there. And the source code. It's backed up, a little, on [archive.org](https://web.archive.org/web/19991003043305/http://www.mailtothefuture.com:80/public/logon). What I haven't found yet is the all-important data.
