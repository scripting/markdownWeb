Twitter CEO Jack Dorsey [outlines](https://twitter.com/jack/status/1204766078468911106) a plan to fund a small independent team to develop an open and decentralized standard for social media, a standard that Twitter would support. I advocate something different, Twitter already has the bugs and scaling issues solved for a global notification network. Let's add a few APIs and create a new universe. It'll happen a lot faster with much better results imho. Interestingly I recorded a [podcast on just this topic](http://scripting.com/2019/12/03.html#a151621), a week ago today.
