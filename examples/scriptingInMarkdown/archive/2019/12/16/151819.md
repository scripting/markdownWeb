![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/12/16/buitoni.png)A commercial from the 60s or 70s that I only saw a few times.

Camera slowly enters a beautiful office with a distinguished older man behind the desk. The announcer says "And now a word from Guillermo Buitoni, president of [Buitoni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buitoni) Foods." Camera continues in for a close up, and stops. Pauses a couple of seconds.

He says: If you want me to like you...

Another pause:

He says: Buy my pizza.

Logo. Cut.

It was perfect. No product shot. Just hey I'm this [Master of the Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masters_of_the_Universe), and of course you want me to like you. And I've made it realllly simple.

I wish I could find the video to see if it is as good as I remember it.
