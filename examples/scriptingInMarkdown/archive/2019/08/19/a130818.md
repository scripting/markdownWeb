![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2018/01/16/librarianActionFigure.png)As a commercial software developer I had heard about [InfoWorld's review guidelines](http://scripting.com/2019/08/19/infoworldReviewGuidelines.pdf), written in 1994, but had not seen them until yesterday when [Harry McCracken](https://twitter.com/harrymccracken), a former member of their review board, posted an [excerpt](https://twitter.com/harrymccracken/status/1163143998782881793) to Twitter. I asked if I could have a copy of the full manual so I could get them into the archive of my blog, and he kindly provided them. If anyone wants to reboot software reviews this would be a good place to start. In any case it's good to have this [archived](http://scripting.com/2019/08/19/infoworldReviewGuidelines.pdf) for future reference.
