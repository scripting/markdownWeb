The two views: If you live with a creative person, [this scene](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yRRsE0LvUqU) from The Shining will be familiar. At least you'll know other people have to deal with this. On the other hand, if you're a creative person, this scene speaks for your frustration with the world.

Listen to the music, it tells the story from his point of view. Every creative person can relate. Was it Stephen King and Stanley Kubrick pleading with the world to just let them create?
