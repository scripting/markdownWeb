At the same time I'm rediscovering [Sting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sting_\(musician\)) and [The Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Police). I heard them play [Englishman in New York](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d27gTrPPAyk) on [WDST](http://www.radiowoodstock.com/), the excellent Woodstock radio station (I know some don't agree). It's so amazing to listen to music you loved 30 years ago. Somehow all that remains are good memories. They flood in, it's a pure happy [nostalgic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nostalgia) feeling. The way [Don Draper](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=suRDUFpsHus) meant it. A time you were loved. The pain from an old wound. Far more powerful than a memory alone. A time machine. A place where we ache to go again.
