![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/08/08/eighthGrade.png)I recommend [Eighth Grade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eighth_Grade_\(film\)), playing on Amazon. Good movie. You see inside a young girl's life, her self-doubt, relationships with friends, the evolving meaning of cool, trying out the idea of sex, sizing up her own future and past. Cringeworthy in places. I keep thinking about it, a sign of something worthwhile.
