![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2017/12/23/sarahLynn.png)We got a [response](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/issues/128#issuecomment-525762285) to the [braintrust query](http://scripting.com/2019/08/27.html#a133830) for lawyers, from a lawyer. Recall I asked about the risks for a news org that disclaimed up front that they were not using journalistic practices, and only reported on what was obviously true. [Occam's News](Occam's News site:scripting.com). As an example the lawyer uses the assumption that Trump is a [Russian oligarch](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=site%3Ascripting.com+%22Russian+oligarch%22&t=h_&ia=web), and tries to come up with a reason for us saying that. First Occam's News wouldn't have to say why they know it's true that Trump is a Russian oligarch, but if they did, they might use the equivalent of the mathematical [Proof By Contradiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proof_by_contradiction), and start with the assumption that Trump is not a Russian oligarch, and prove that's not possible. It's a perfectly valid way to prove something. Anyway the discussion [continues](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/issues/128#issuecomment-526186621).
