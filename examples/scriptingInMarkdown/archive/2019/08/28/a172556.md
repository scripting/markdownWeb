![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/02/12/boldItalic.png)I was looking around for the next project to work on now that the [Dave-via-email](http://scripting.com/email/) project is done, I hope -- it's the worst kind of software in the world. Talk about small pieces loosely joined. Anyway that's done now, [praise Murphy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murphy%27s_law). I was thinking after reading this [piece](https://www.techdirt.com/articles/20190825/21540442853/protocols-not-platforms-technological-approach-to-free-speech.shtml) about protocols over platforms, which I could not agree with more, that I have an almost-finished JavaScript implementation of [XML-RPC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/XML-RPC), running on client and server. Since it's my second time through this area, I did it better than last time. It's just a matter of writing docs, testing, and getting other people interested/excited, and most important updating the [XML-RPC website](http://xmlrpc.scripting.com/), which hasn't been touched in many years. Might be just the project for my newly powerful [blog](http://scripting.com/2019/08/28.html).
