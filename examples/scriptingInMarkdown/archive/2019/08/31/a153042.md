BTW, for some reason I find [Wired's](https://www.wired.com/) paywall particularly [egregious](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/egregious) even though it's no more limiting than other pubs' paywalls. I think it's because I'd like to link to them as much as I link to any other site. I used up my monthly quota just writing the above bit. Wired is a record, like my blog, of where we came from. That its access is limited, while I understand why, is also limiting access to the record.
