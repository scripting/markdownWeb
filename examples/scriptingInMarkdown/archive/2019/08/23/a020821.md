Today I learned, [via the Daily podcast](https://www.nytimes.com/2019/08/23/podcasts/the-daily/1619-project.html), that Lincoln's plan, after the slaves were freed, was that they would emigrate to another country. He didn't see an America with free blacks and whites co-existing as workable. This isn't what they taught us in grade school.
