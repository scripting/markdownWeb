The [@nbariver](https://twitter.com/nbariver) Twitter feed is [flowing](http://scripting.com/2019/09/15.html#a172245) again.

I'm also getting the [@podc](https://twitter.com/podc) Twitter feed going too. It started in [2007](http://scripting.com/stories/2007/10/04/myPodcatcherIsAPublishingT.html), when podcasting and Twitter [were](http://scripting.com/stories/2007/10/04/myPodcatcherIsAPublishingT.html) both pretty new.

[Rivers](http://scripting.com/2014/06/02/whatIsARiverOfNewsAggregator.html) are like Twitter timelines except made out of RSS feeds, which are distributed all over the internet, not just on Twitter. So there's "nbariver.com" and [@nbariver](https://twitter.com/nbariver). They carry the same information, in the same order (newest first) -- one on my server, and the other on Twitter. Similarly, there's [Podcatch.com](http://podcatch.com/) and [@podc](https://twitter.com/podc).

I know this might be confusing, but it's worth understanding imho.
