Today's [Daily podcast](https://www.nytimes.com/2019/09/18/podcasts/the-daily/harvey-weinstein-lisa-bloom.html) is about Harvey Weinstein. The reporters covering him for the NYT got access to the notes, letters and emails of [Lisa Bloom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisa_Bloom), one of Weinstein's [attorneys](https://nationalinterest.org/blog/buzz/weinstein-may-be-monster-lawyers-who-enabled-him-are-real-villans-81086), detailing the PR and legal campaign to get the story killed. Presumably these techniques worked in the past, but this time the story came out.
