I used my [Apple credit card](https://www.apple.com/apple-card/) to buy the watch. Surprised they didn't streamline that, it shouldn't have required me to enter a number. They know who I am, my identity connects the store and the card. They didn't even acknowledge that I used their card. Apple, who has often set the bar really high for marketing awareness, missed an opportunity for an obvious [high five](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hzQaEtMwBw8). They should also btw give you a discount for using their card, esp in early days.
