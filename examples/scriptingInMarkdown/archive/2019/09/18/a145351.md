Dave Carlick, marketer, a friend for decades. He did the [Cogitank Ergo Sum](https://www.google.com/search?q=) campaign for us, among others. He went on to do Netscape's first website, then became a VC, and a board member lots of places, but we still hang out like we did in the early 80s. Anyway, Dave was one of the first to subscribe to my blog via [nightly email](http://scripting.com/email/), and the other day he told me an [idea I posted](http://scripting.com/2019/09/15.html#a192924) was good. That meant the world to me. For years there has been almost zero feedback to this blog from real-world friends.
