How permalinks work on Scripting News. There's a simple feature in my blog that makes it easy to point to a specific item. If you look at the end of any post you'll [see four icons](http://scripting.com/images/2019/09/24/purplePoundSign.png). The first, a "purple pound sign," is [a](http://scripting.com/2014/02/23/designChallengeParagraphlevelPermalinks.html) link to the item. When you click it, the browser opens the page, with that post at the top. That's the best link to send to friends.
