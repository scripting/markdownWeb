This morning I was listening to [What a Fool Believes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dJe1iUuAW4M), following along in the [lyrics](https://www.google.com/search?q=what+a+fool+believes+lyrics&rlz=1C5CHFA_enUS743US747&oq=what+a+fool+belie&aqs=chrome.1.0l2j69i57j0l3.7618j0j4&sourceid=chrome&ie=UTF-8), and saw that it was [written by](http://scripting.com/images/2019/09/24/arethaWroteTheSong.png) Aretha Franklin! I couldn't imagine [how she would sing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TNIXiqJrhfw) the song. BTW, I've been _exactly_ that kind of fool.
