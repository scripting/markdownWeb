The very last thing I want to watch [on Netflix](https://techcrunch.com/2019/08/29/whats-bill-gates-worst-fear-netflixs-new-docuseries-will-try-to-figure-him-out/) is an ode to [Bill Gates](https://www.google.com/search?q=site%3Ascripting.com+Bill+Gates). Gates was the [Zuck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Zuckerberg) of the 90s. He tried to put our freedom in a Microsoft box and sell it back to us, without the freedom. His empire was brought down by his insistence that PC security was a third party opportunity.
