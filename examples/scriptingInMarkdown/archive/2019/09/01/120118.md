Let’s understand the damage to lives in all dimensions.

1.  Report how many were shot. How many bodies were penetrated with bullets. Not just how many were killed and injured.
    
2.  The survivors’ body condition should be reported. How much pain, how long recovery. Did they have insurance? How disabled?
    

The [NYT reported](https://www.nytimes.com/2019/08/31/us/el-paso-shooting-victim.html#click=https://t.co/m4WHatguhG) on a man who was severely injured in the El Paso massacre 28 days ago.
