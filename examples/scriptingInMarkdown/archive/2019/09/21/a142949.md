BTW, the radical adjustments in the US political system are an obvious response to big changes in the Soviet Union in the 80s and 90s. People thought at the time there would be a [peace dividend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peace_dividend), and that it signalled the [end of history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_End_of_History_and_the_Last_Man). I didn't believe it at the time, I saw both ideas as tempting fate, the same way you brag that your candidate [can't lose](https://money.cnn.com/2016/10/18/news/paddypower-pays-hillary-clinton-bettors/index.html). God hears you and as my dear departed uncle would say goofs on you.
