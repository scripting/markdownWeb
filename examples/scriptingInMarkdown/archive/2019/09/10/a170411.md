I just got a question from a user about how to get the OPML source text from an outline you're editing in [LO2](http://littleoutliner.com/). Two steps. 1. In the File menu, choose _View OPML._ 2. In your browser's File menu, choose _Save Page As._ That's it. You now have a local copy of the file.
