Wouldn't it be great if all the [reporters in tech](https://www.nytimes.com/2019/09/11/opinion/apple-keynote-2019.html) got [together](http://scripting.com/images/2019/09/11/appleJoke.png) to see truly new stuff, without regard of which company it came from, or if it came from a company at all? The big message no one in [the establishment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Establishment) wants to hear (a term that's become precise these days) is that money does not yield progress. Jobs left a [seed](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8rwsuXHA7RA) for you all as a clue. It's _people_ that make the difference, not piles of money. That's the big reboot of news we're waiting for. For our news to stop worshipping money and get down to telling us what's new.
