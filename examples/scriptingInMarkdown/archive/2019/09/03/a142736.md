![](http://static.scripting.com/larryKing/images/2012/10/15/elephant.gif)More and more I use Twitter to write the first drafts of posts that I then write more fully [here](http://scripting.com/2019/09/03.html#a142736) on my blog. It really is becoming the context for my blogging, a very different role from what it played a few years ago. The 280-char limit is a very important change, but I think other things matter too. People are taking Twitter more seriously? Each of us sees [something different](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blind_men_and_an_elephant) of course.
