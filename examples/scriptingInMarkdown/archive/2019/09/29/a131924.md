![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/09/29/myFamilyGrowingUp.png)My friend who loves the [new release](https://variety.com/2019/music/news/beatles-abbey-road-sneak-preview-50th-anniversary-edition-1203307482/) of Abbey Road, which I have bought, insists that I'd for sure hear the difference if I listen on [headphones](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B07574TYWW/ref=ppx_yo_dt_b_search_asin_title?ie=UTF8&psc=1). Which I am doing right now. Sounds lovely. The songs are all so great, and form the musical sound track of my whole life all the way back to [elementary school](https://www.greatschools.org/new-york/queens/2125-Ps-32-State-Street/)! As I said yesterday, and it bears repeating, that all the Beatles albums are deeply ingrained in who I am. All my early life experiences connected with every other period of my life. I started listening to the Beatles early in life and never stopped. But I only hear the Beatles, I only hear Abbey Road on the new mixes. It doesn't sound any different from other mixes. I wish it did, because I'd love to have more Beatles revelations at my age, which also has significance [in Beatles terms](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HCTunqv1Xt4). Heh. But it sounds the same as it always did, full of wonder, like Abbey Road, like the [music of my life](https://www.google.com/search?q=beatles+site%3Ascripting.com).
