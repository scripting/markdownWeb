Braintrust [query](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/issues/130) resolved. [Peter Judge](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/issues/130#issuecomment-527210564) saw immediately what the problem was: no Content-Length [header](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_HTTP_header_fields). _Thank you_ for the help. Here's the postmortem. I'm writing an [XML-RPC](http://xmlrpc.com/) service, the request was being route through [PagePark](http://pagepark.io/). Of course I blamed the one [bit](https://nodejs.org/api/stream.html#stream_stream) of technology that wasn't mine. Heh. Further, the [XML-RPC spec](http://xmlrpc.scripting.com/spec.html) _requires_ that the Content-Length header be [provided](http://scripting.com/images/2019/09/02/contentLengthMustBeSpecified.png) and be correct. If I had followed my own design, I wouldn't have needed the help. Dear Murphy, I am unworthy, I am unworthy.
