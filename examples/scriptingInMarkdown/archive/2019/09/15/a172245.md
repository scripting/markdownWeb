It took hours of digging through various bits of software to figure out that the [nbariver](https://twitter.com/nbariver) Twitter account had been suspended because they thought it was sharing personal information inappropriately. Here's a [screen shot](http://scripting.com/images/2019/09/15/twitterComplaint.png) of the report. Obviously we were not sharing personal information, just [relaying](http://scripting.com/images/2019/09/15/tweetInQuestion.png) a news report from the [Denver Post](https://www.denverpost.com/2019/08/13/michael-porter-jr-adam-silver-cell-phone/?utm_source=feedburner&utm_medium=feed&utm_campaign=Feed%3A+dp-sports-nuggets+%28Denver+Post%3A+Sports%3A+Nuggets%29) that some sharing had taken place. This is interesting because a human obviously would be able to tell the difference, and also it's exactly what we were discussing on Twitter over the last few days, how to automate these things. I did not delete the tweet, it was completely innocent.
