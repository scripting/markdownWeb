![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/09/20/undone.png)Some shows I binge in "savor mode," they're so good and there isn't much to binge on, so I go slow, stop often and savor shows to watch later. [Undone](https://www.metacritic.com/tv/undone) is one of those shows. Two 1/2 hour episodes left. It is of a genre, wild misunderstood-as-crazy super-powered young woman, as much a victim of her powers as master of them. Undone is something like [Russian Doll](https://www.metacritic.com/tv/russian-doll/season-1) and [Killing Eve](https://www.metacritic.com/tv/killing-eve). I'll know more when I finish. But it's one of the best shows ever.
