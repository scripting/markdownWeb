Note to self: A freshly set up Mac has weird mouse behavior. It scrolls when you move the mouse pointer, sometimes. This is how you disable it. In the Accessibility prefs panel, click on _Mouse & Trackpad,_ then _Mouse Options,_ then [uncheck](http://scripting.com/images/2019/03/18/mousePref.png) the Scrolling popup.
