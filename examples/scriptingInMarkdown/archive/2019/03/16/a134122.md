What could we do to prevent the kind of [usurpation](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/usurpation) and abandonment of open formats and protocols so common among the big tech companies? I think there are some valid approaches. Including [this](https://twitter.com/davewiner/status/1106694336215990272) from a thread on Twitter yesterday. "Let's talk about it. I think it involves patents and trademarks. And the equivalent of the GPL for intellectual property. Prevent open shared creations from being used in silos. _You can't import what you won't export._ Something like that."
