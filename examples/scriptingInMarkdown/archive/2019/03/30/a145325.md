I just read a piece on Gizmodo saying that [RSS is better than Twitter](https://gizmodo.com/rss-is-better-than-twitter-1833624929). Then I had a thought, why not turn Twitter into an RSS reader. It's not too many steps. Accept an OPML subscription list. When you add a feed, call [IFTTT](https://ifttt.com/) and say "map the output of this feed onto this Twitter account." When a feed disappears from the list, delete the IFTTT script. I'm not sure if IFTTT's API will let you do this. OTOH you could probably set up a [River5](https://github.com/scripting/river5) installation to do it, but it'd be more work. :-)
