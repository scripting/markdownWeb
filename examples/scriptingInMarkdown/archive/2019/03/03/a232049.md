I was asked how I feel about my new [Subaru Forester](https://www.subaru.com/vehicles/forester/index.html). I love it. It's a sweet car all around, but the _best_ thing about it is it fits me. For my whole life I've been driving cars that were too small. I'd have to [contort](http://thesaurus.land/?word=contort) myself to get in and out. Not the Forester.
