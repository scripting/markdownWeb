I've been emailing with my old friend [Dave Carlick](http://scripting.com/davenet/stories/IntroducingDaveCarlick.html) about customized searching. I've also been [writing](http://scripting.com/2019/10/09/143957.html) about it here. I'm not sure I've made my point, but now I have an excellent example that might shed some light. Dave, take note.

*   I just searched Google for [Old School](https://www.google.com/search?q=).
    
*   It came back with pointers to a 2003 [movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_School_\(film\)). A Facebook [group](https://www.facebook.com/OldSchoolMovie/). A [dictionary](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/old-school) definition. The [urban dictionary](https://www.urbandictionary.com/define.php?term=old%20school) version. And on and on.
    
*   But I have a product named [Old School](https://github.com/scripting/oldSchoolBlog). I've written about it on my blog a [bunch](https://www.google.com/search?q=site%3Ascripting.com+) of times. If Google knew I was the author of this blog, it would also know that when I search for Old School, I want to see my product first, because in all likelihood that's what I'm looking for.
    

That's what I mean about associating the searcher with the searcher's blog. Think of it as SEO for a person.

PS: Here's another one. I just searched for Dave Carlick. Google asks if I [meant](http://scripting.com/images/2019/10/14/didIMeanCarlock.png) Dave Carlock. That's crazy. I've been friends with Dave Carlick for 30-plus years. If it read my blog it would know that. It's so wrong. In fact, if I type Carlock it should know I meant Carlick.

PPS: Google is so crazy that when I [restict the search](https://www.google.com/search?q=Dave+Carlick+site%3Ascripting.com&rlz=1C5CHFA_enUS743US747&oq=Dave+Carlick+site%3Ascripting.com&aqs=chrome..69i57j69i64l2.1191j0j4&sourceid=chrome&ie=UTF-8) to my site, it still asks if I meant Carlock, even though there are references to Carlick _on the site I'm searching_ going back to the [1990s](http://scripting.com/davenet/stories/IntroducingDaveCarlick.html). And the only refs to [Carlock](https://www.google.com/search?safe=off&rlz=1C5CHFA_enUS743US747&sxsrf=ACYBGNTwZ7Hn9FWhmqTjXuOfyD9HmpV29Q:1571062782317&q=Dave+Carlock+site:scripting.com&spell=1&sa=X&ved=0ahUKEwif6Lz4-JvlAhXlmeAKHQcNByEQBQguKAA&biw=1390&bih=1182) are this post. That is just plain wrong.
