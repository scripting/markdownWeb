![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/09/30/metsCap.png)Yesterday was the 50th anniversary of the [1969 Mets World Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1969_World_Series) win. I was 14. Lived in [Flushing](https://www.google.com/maps/place/29-31+170th+St,+Flushing,+NY+11358/@40.769637,-73.7970707,17z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m5!3m4!1s0x89c28a80780e9c3b:0xa8668e9b256999a4!8m2!3d40.769637!4d-73.794882), mowed the lawns of [Mets](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=site%3Ascripting.com+mets&t=h_&ia=web) players. Loved them when they lost, was rewarded for the trust. I’ve believed in miracles my whole life thanks to the example of the 1969 Mets.
