If it ever happens that browsers refuse to browse [HTTP](http://this.how/googleAndHttp/) sites, it's going to create a huge outage. Think about [Yahoo Groups shutting](https://www.vice.com/en_us/article/8xwe9p/yahoo-groups-is-winding-down-and-all-content-will-be-permanently-removed) down in December and all the history that will go offline as a result. The web is huge, deep and [old](http://scripting.com/autowebdocs/whatisautoweb_119.html). No platform has existed without breakage as long as the web.
