![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/10/01/25.png)WIth [Big 25](http://scripting.com/2019/10/07.html) coming up, it's great to hear from people who were reading in the early years. For example, David Boroditsky checked in yesterday [on Twitter](https://twitter.com/boroditsky/status/1179081346943078400). He sent a link to an email he sent me, which I [posted](http://scripting.com/frontier/stories/DavidBoroditsky.html) to the blog, in Oct 1996, two years after the start. He talks about the non-web features of [Frontier](http://scripting.com/2021/01/04/140651.html?title=whatFrontierIsAbout). Sometimes I forget that Frontier and the blog were two sides of the same coin in the early days. All the early blogging and RSS software ran in Frontier.
