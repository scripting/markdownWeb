Why people like [AOC](https://twitter.com/AOC) are so important -- she doesn't depend on the established news orgs to transmit what she says. She goes [direct](http://scripting.com/stories/2009/03/19/theRebootOfJournalism.html). This was the [goal](http://scripting.com/2015/10/12/itsStillSourcesGoDirect.html) of blogging. And it's being achieved.
