Jay Rosen and I used to have a podcast entitled [Rebooting the News](http://rebootnews.com). Since then it's gotten worse, as Jay explains in this [Twitter thread](https://twitter.com/jayrosen_nyu/status/1082105442820595713). Today it needs more than a [reboot](http://scripting.com/stories/2009/03/19/theRebootOfJournalism.html). More like a tear-down and rebuild. Back in the podcast days I wasn't a believer in what they call "citizen journalism." Now I think it's the only thing that can save us.
