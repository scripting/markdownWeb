![](http://scripting.com/2015/02/17/blog.png)[James Ball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Ball_\(journalist\)) writing in the [Columbia Journalism Review](https://www.cjr.org/opinion/no-facebook-shouldnt-fund-journalism.php) says tech companies should not fund journalism, for the same reasons I've been giving for decades. But he misses one. Tech has created a [level playing field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Level_playing_field) where you and I have equal chance to write the news as any comfortable [Church of The Savvy](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=%22church+of+the+savvy%22) pundit. Journalism wants money from tech so they can keep pretending they are the source of truth for the masses, when their lazyness and corruption have made our lives worse. We need an [Indivisible](https://indivisible.org/) for journalism. Journalism should compete with tech, as distasteful as it may be for them to create a level playing field where all of us can report the news. Tech had the guts to give all of us an equal voice. Journalism must match that.
