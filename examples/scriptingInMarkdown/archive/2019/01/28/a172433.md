![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/01/28/lincoln.png)[Podcast](http://scripting.com/2019/01/28/whyAOCIsWrongAboutTechMonopoliesKillingJournalism.m4a): I tweeted [earlier](https://twitter.com/davewiner/status/1089912273622183937) that I don't agree with [AOC](https://twitter.com/AOC) about tech monopolies killing journalism. I believe they should have competed, created a _news Facebook_ of their own, any one of them could, and until they offer the level playing field that tech offers, they can't get our help. There's actually a lot to this. I scratch the surface in this 15-minute rambling (sorry) podcast.
