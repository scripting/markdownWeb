I've been taking pictures of my countryside explorations, and found that my top of the line [iPhone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IPhone_XS) isn't doing a good enough job, so I thought I'd try dusting off the old [Canon EOS 5D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canon_EOS_5D) my father left me, and see what it can do. I went though some of the pictures, the quality is amazing, esp for a camera that's over ten years old. Here's a small bit of a [picture](http://scripting.com/images/2019/05/02/lafayetteStreet2011.png) I took on [Lafayette St](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Bleecker+St+%26+Lafayette+St,+New+York,+NY+10012/@40.7259294,-73.9968293,17z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m5!3m4!1s0x89c259855fdd5293:0xd39ec45d9866811e!8m2!3d40.7259294!4d-73.9946406) in NYC. It does beautiful work.
