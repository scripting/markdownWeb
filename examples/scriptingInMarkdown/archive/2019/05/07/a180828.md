![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/05/07/fdBox.png)Living in Manhattan, with no car, I got used to FreshDirect where you order groceries via a [website](https://freshdirect.com/), and they deliver what you ordered the next day. You develop a [list](http://scripting.com/images/2019/05/07/freshDirectList.png) of items you buy regularly, and shopping is a matter of reviewing that list, and re-ordering the things you need more of. Very efficient, and I rarely forgot basic items, because they floated to the top of the list. Now I'm shopping in grocery stores, basically walking around through their warehouse in realspace, doing the job FD employees do in NY, and while I like the experience in general, I often forget to restock things I've run out of. Which makes me wonder why stores don't adopt the FreshDirect user interface, let me shop for basic stuff from home via the web, even if they can't deliver.
