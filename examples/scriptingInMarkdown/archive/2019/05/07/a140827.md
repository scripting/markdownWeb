With the help of [ouchiko](https://github.com/ouchiko), [jmgs](https://github.com/jmgs) and [tommy-carlier](https://github.com/tommy-carlier), yesterday's [JavaScript challenge](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/issues/116) is solved. I learned that there's a built-in [browser function](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/issues/116#issuecomment-489985995) that does exactly what was called for, [scrollIntoView](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Element/scrollIntoView). Makes sense, in 2019 this is something every app shouldn't have to do itself. This will be part of a new app I'm working on.
