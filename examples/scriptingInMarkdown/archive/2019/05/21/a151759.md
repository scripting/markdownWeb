![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/05/21/stillAVirgin.png)Once a long time ago I wrote on this blog that we should have fewer humans, and the rage it provoked was awesome, and imho totally wrong. As we consume the life-sustaining ability of our planet, if we had [fewer people](http://scripting.com/images/2010/08/29/virgin.jpg), we'd consume it at a slower rate. I don't think there's any great reason to overpopulate the planet, esp when we're so resource-constrained. BTW, I'm a mystic, i.e. I believe in the mystery of it all. :-)
