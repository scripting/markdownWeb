Suppose the [worst person in the world](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C-fLC1dJi78) has a great idea, but you aren't listening because you don't like this person. In normal times you would be the main loser. But if the idea could help us avert the climate or constitutional meltdowns, we all lose because of your pride. That's why I try to listen to smart people even if it hurts my ego.
