The town of Woodstock, where I live, is [working on a law](https://hudsonvalleyone.com/2019/05/18/woodstocks-short-term-rental-regs-set-for-passage/) to limit short-term vacation rentals. When I was shopping for a house here, I used the AirBnBs. But now that I own property here, I have conflicted views. I understand the reason they want the law is to have more housing available for full-time residents to rent.
