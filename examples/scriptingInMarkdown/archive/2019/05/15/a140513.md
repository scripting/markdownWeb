I used to sit in my hot tub in Calif, looking up at the [live oak trees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Live_oak), and tried to convince myself that I owned them. I said to one tree, "I own you." The tree laughed: "You just got here. I have been here a hundred years, and I will be here hundreds more after you're gone."
