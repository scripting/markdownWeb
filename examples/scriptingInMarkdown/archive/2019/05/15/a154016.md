Yesterday [Howard Weaver](http://howardweaver.com/styled-12/index.html), a long-time friend, journalist, asked "What is an open identity server?" I was able to answer the question [in a tweet](https://twitter.com/davewiner/status/1128674591201689602). Briefly it's the part of Twitter that knows you're @howardweaver and I'm @davewiner. I need that in my software too. Rather than build my own, and make users establish a new "identity" for my apps, e.g. -- [Little Outliner](http://littleoutliner.com/), I just use Twitter's. That's the open part.
