People say [Tuca and Bertie](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZybYIJtbcu0) is the next level of Bojack Horseman and it is that for sure. For example, in T&B there are people and animals, as in Bojack, but [plants](http://scripting.com/images/2019/05/09/plantAsACharacter.png) also have intelligence and speak and have legs and can walk and go places (and are teenagers). Also they include little bits of non-animated [human stuff](http://scripting.com/images/2019/05/09/humanInTuca.png) in it. And the snarky Easter eggs are even better. But it's also got a bit of [Studio Ghibli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Studio_Ghibli) in it and trippy [Spirted Away](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spirited_Away) type stuff. I love the [actors](https://deadline.com/2019/05/tiffany-haddish-ali-wong-tuca-and-bertie-interview-tribeca-1202605920/). I'm only on Episode 4 and savoring every moment.
