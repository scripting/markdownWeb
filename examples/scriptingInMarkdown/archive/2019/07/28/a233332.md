![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/07/28/religiousFreedom.png)Finished [this book](https://www.amazon.com/Island-Center-World-Manhattan-Forgotten/dp/1400078679) today. Lessons learned, the Dutch started the bootstrap of American culture by colonizing Manhattan, and aren't given enough credit for it. [Peter Stuyvesant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Stuyvesant) was not much loved in the colony for good reason. [Adrian Vanderdonk](https://www.dutchgenealogy.nl/donck-adriaen-van-der/) was the visionary. We tend to think of colonization of the US as starting with the British, but our legacy also comes from the Netherlands. Growing up in [Flushing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flushing,_Queens) I was aware of the [Dutch presence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flushing,_Queens#Dutch_colony). They had [farms](https://hiddenwatersblog.wordpress.com/2016/11/11/bownepd/), [houses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Bowne_House) and civic buildings dating back to the 1600s all around our neighborhood. The [Flushing Remonstrance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flushing_Remonstrance) was prior art for the Bill of Rights.
