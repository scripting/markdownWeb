![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/07/30/hillaryBoxer.png)[Years and Years](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Years_and_Years_\(TV_series\)), a six-part miniseries on HBO [starring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Years_and_Years_\(TV_series\)#Cast_and_characters) [Emma Thompson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Thompson), is short-term science fiction, starting from today and going forward about twenty years. Centers on a complicated British family. It'll probably look very dated in five years. They don't imagine much that isn't already well underway. It's well-done enough that it pulled me through all six episodes, the first five I [binged](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binge-watching), in time to watch the finale last night. The message, you're really rich if you have a loving family, and everything else is bollocks.
