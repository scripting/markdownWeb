![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/07/07/theAffair.png)I gave up on [Designated Survivor](http://scripting.com/2019/07/05.html#a211151), I even tried skipping ahead to the third (Netflix) season, where they say the f-word and there are naked butts, but it's just like [House of Cards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Cards_\(American_TV_series\)), idiotic. But the [set](https://torontolife.com/culture/movies-and-tv/how-designated-survivor-built-a-replica-oval-office-in-toronto/) for the Oval Office is much better. After looking through HBO, I landed on [The Affair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Affair_\(TV_series\)) on Showtime. Finally something with an adult plot, and HBO style. One of the stars is [McNulty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominic_West) from The WIre and his asshole [father-in-law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Doman) is the police chief who [tormented](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Rawls) McNulty. And the sex is not gratuitous, it's the plot. [Montauk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montauk,_New_York) is very photogenic as is McNulty's [lover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruth_Wilson). It got [high ratings](https://www.metacritic.com/tv/the-affair), I'm in episode 3 of season one and there are four seasons, so I'm hopeful.
