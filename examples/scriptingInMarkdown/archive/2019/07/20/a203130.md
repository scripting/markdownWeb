I was 14 years old in 1969, at summer camp, on a trip to the [Newport Folk Festival](https://www.setlist.fm/festival/1969/newport-folk-festival-1969-13d6e58d.html), when the [moon landing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apollo_11) happened. I remember being part of a huge group of people huddled in front of a [VW minibus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volkswagen_Type_2) with a small [B&W TV](https://www.google.com/search?safe=off&rlz=1C5CHFA_enUS743US747&biw=1133&bih=940&tbm=isch&sa=1&ei=BnszXfjjGaSq_QaLmLegDQ&q=portable+b%26w+tv+sixties&oq=portable+b%26w+tv+sixties&gs_l=img.3...12523.13721..14115...0.0..0.0.0.......0....1..gws-wiz-img.0H8gj3AGQbY&ved=0ahUKEwj4_tq_rMTjAhUkVd8KHQvMDdQQ4dUDCAY&uact=5) on top, and somehow we all saw the landing. Goosebumps.
