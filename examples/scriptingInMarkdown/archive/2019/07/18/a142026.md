I can't believe I agree with [Thomas Friedman](https://www.nytimes.com/2019/07/16/opinion/trump-2020.html). We ought to pick the most boring and coherent presidential candidate -- the most plain and uncontroversial person on the stage. Nominate him or her, and then get ready to fight. I agree with Friedman, the things they agreed to in the debate were _insane._ They handed the election to Trump. We need to grab it back. My two picks: Klobuchar or Inslee.
