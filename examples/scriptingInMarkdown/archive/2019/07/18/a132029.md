I am having an allergic reaction to the idea that those monsters at Trump [rallies](https://www.newsobserver.com/news/politics-government/article232776672.html) are Americans. I desperately want to join with others to say that the America I was born in doesn't belong to any one race or religion. I think every few generations we have to renew this very basic idea, that the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) is a [union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E_pluribus_unum), out of many, one.
