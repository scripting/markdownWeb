![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/07/18/greatDictator.png)[Amy Klobuchar gets it](https://www.npr.org/2019/07/17/742818947/minnesota-sen-amy-klobuchar-turns-to-humor-to-distinguish-herself-among-candidat). Trump is funny. He's a [caricature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caricature), a funny [impersonation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YqyQfjDScjU) of a [strong man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benito_Mussolini). People who love him think he's hilarious. Entertaining. I've been saying [this](http://scripting.com/2016/04/20/1203.html) [since](http://scripting.com/2016/04/28/1224.html) [2016](http://scripting.com/liveblog/users/davewiner/2016/01/09/0815.html). If you want to counter-program the rallies, which the Democrats, if they had an ounce of ambition, would have done from the start, what you need are old-style standup comedians. Unfortunately the good ones are dead. Joan Rivers (who [liked](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nN2C6ctpOhg) Trump, she was a real [asshole](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j-5Q7yuaXjM), she didn't just play one on TV) or [Don Rickles](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qofdWFv7m0U), [Rodney Dangerfield](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MecU2keW54I), [George Carlin](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KLODGhEyLvk). But there must be others. Get [Bill Hader](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LH_-9hD2xBY) to do a Joan Rivers impersonation. Or [Kate McKinnon](https://www.glamour.com/story/kate-mckinnon-best-celebrity-impressions-on-saturday-night-live). Then you can leave the [Democratic nominee](https://elizabethwarren.com/) to talk about health care, securing the borders, the basic values of America. Let Trump compete with the comedians, which is appropriate because that's what he is.
