He might have been talking about the management of the 2019 Knicks. They have lots of [cap space](https://www.spotrac.com/nba/cap/), the profits from giving up on Kristaps Porzingas, which was sad (and it turns out delusional), but it's also power, even if they didn't get KD or Kawhi -- and _of course_ they're [pissing](https://twitter.com/TommyBeer/status/1145705086871625733) it away. We need this team to be sold. Please anyone, my friend's cat even, could run this team better.
