It's funny how Microsoft's [old slogan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Where_do_you_want_to_go_today%3F) makes so much more sense today than when they were using it. _Where do you want to go today?_ Back then the computer world didn't really have the concepts of places to go. But after the net, that's what we all do all the time, go places.
