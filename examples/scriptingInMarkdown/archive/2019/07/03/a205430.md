![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/07/03/kentBrockman.png)There was a time when every woman I met was named Kim. It's happening again, only this time they're all named Maryanne or Marianne or Maryann. There have been four so far. Just got an email from a person working on a project that I'm also working on. Of course, her name is Marianne. My dear departed [uncle](http://crimson1.scripting.com/kenKiesler) used to say he was a member of the [Church of Non-functional Probabilities](http://scripting.com/2007/01/02.html#brockmansQuestion). Things would happen to him that could not be predicted by any legit [probability distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probability_distribution). This is an example of a message from god. I was doubting His or Her existence. I don't doubt it now. And further I think my dear uncle in heaven is having a good laugh at his still-living nephew. If you can read this, Ken, I miss you! :boom:
