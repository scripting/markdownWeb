I finished [The Power Broker](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=site%3Ascripting.com+%22The+Power+Broker%22&t=h_&ia=web), and there's absolutely no mention of [Jane Jacobs](https://www.vanityfair.com/culture/2017/04/jane-jacobs-citizen-jane-documentary) in the story. He told the story of the East Tremont neighborhood in the Bronx that was wiped out by the [Cross Bronx Expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross_Bronx_Expressway), I thought that was the prelude to the [Lower Manhattan Expressway](https://www.curbed.com/2016/5/4/11505214/jane-jacobs-robert-moses-lomex), but nothing, zip, as if [it never happened](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_78_in_New_York#Lower_Manhattan_Expressway).
