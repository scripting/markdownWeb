![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2018/12/10/christmasTree.png)Twitter [clarifies](https://twitter.com/TwitterSupport/status/1199777312054493184) its inactive account policy. My concern was [linkrot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Link_rot). Twitter accounts are websites, we point to them and include them, as I point to the new Twitter policy above. Jack was around when the web was very young. The idea is that we're creating a record. It's not ephemeral. It should survive us. It shouldn't matter if a site is active. Now we know that _users care_ about preserving the web. Twitter is part of that.
