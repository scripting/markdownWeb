Good morning coffee fans!

This is something I used to do in the [early days](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=site%3Ascripting.com+%22morning+coffee+notes%22&t=h_&ia=web) of the blog. When I started work in the morning, I'd create a section for the first notes of the day. It's nice because it leaves room at the top of the page as the day goes on, and I don't have to shuffle the morning stuff off to new places.

Morning Coffee Notes was also the name of [my first podcast](http://morningcoffeenotes.com/), in the summer of 2004.

Want to see and hear my first podcast? Scroll down to [6/11/2004](http://scripting.com/images/2019/11/26/mcnFirst.png).

Later that year I started another podcast with Adam Curry called [Trade Secrets](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=site%3Ascripting.com+%22trade+secrets%22&t=h_&ia=web). It was a cool name for a podcast, It would be cool even today with the glut of podcasts because it's a [double entendre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double_entendre), and inviting if you're into secrets that are traded.

Now I forgot why I started today's MCN section.

I'll catch up on my next coding break. :boom:
