I am what you might think of as a white Russian. My grandparents on my father's side came from the eastern side of the Urals. But I was born in the US of A. Maybe that's why the favored drink for the night after Thanksgiving is a White Russian, which also happens to be the favorite drink of [Jeff Lebowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Big_Lebowski).

So, while [turkey soup](http://scripting.com/2019/11/30/142215.html) is simmering...

A very easy drink to make, and love.

1.  Ice cubes.
    
2.  Kahlua.
    
3.  Vodka.
    
4.  Heavy cream.
    

Sip.

Yum.
