I have a test version of [TweetSucker](https://duckduckgo.com/?q=TweetSucker+site%3Ascripting.com&t=h_&ia=web) running on a server. It reads my previous day's tweets at midnight and sends them to me in an email. The body of the email is a sequence of HTML paragraphs. Attached is an OPML file ready to be loaded into Electric Outliner. Here's an [example](http://scripting.com/2019/11/12/tweets.opml) of the OPML file. First impression: I tweet a lot.
