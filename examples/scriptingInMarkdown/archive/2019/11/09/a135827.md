![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/11/09/porzingas.png)I couldn't bring myself to watch the Knicks game last night with the Mavericks with their new star, and former Knick, Kristaps Porzingas, who for a few years was the embodiment of hope for the team. I was sure the Knicks would be blown away. What a surprise to wake up to find out _[the Knicks won](https://nypost.com/2019/11/09/ntilikina-gem-in-win-over-porzingis-could-be-knicks-turning-point/)._ I don't think anyone was humiliated, but there's a sadness when you realize how great the team could be now if KP hadn't demanded a trade, had found a way to work with the Knicks.
