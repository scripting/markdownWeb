People should simply use their names in the space where you put your name in a tweet. After a couple of years of playing with it, I find it's confusing to put messages there. An example. [David Weinberger](https://twitter.com/dweinberger) is someone I would read every [tweet](http://scripting.com/images/2019/11/22/tweetexample.png) of, but sometimes miss because he's endlessly creative with his twitter name. I believe this is called [cognitive overload](https://www.teachingenglish.org.uk/article/cognitive-overload). My brain screams "too much" and I move on to the next tweet.
