It’s a good thing Google [discontinued](https://killedbygoogle.com/) their RSS reader product. Had it continued, they would likely be torturing [RSS](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html) the same way they are [HTML](https://developers.google.com/amp) and [HTTP](http://this.how/googleAndHttp/). Had they kept it they could have siloed podcasting, for example.
