One great use for ChatGPT, simple recipes.

If you try looking for a recipe on Google they take forever to get to the point, and pop up all kinds of offers when all you wanted was a checklist of ingredients and steps.

For example, [how to make hard-boiled eggs](https://chatgpt.com/share/c9b39a6b-ad4b-4931-9f7e-93c6c7685c03).

Try doing the [same thing](https://www.google.com/search?q=how+to+make+hard+boiled+eggs) on Google to see what I mean.
