[Matt says](https://ma.tt/2024/07/6-6/) some interesting new stuff is coming from Automattic now that [WordPress 6.6](https://wordpress.org/news/2024/07/dorsey/) is out. Something for writers? Perhaps something that moves WordPress into a space adjacent to twitter-like systems? They just added support for [Threads](https://www.threads.net/@davew) to WordPress, so now you can [crosspost](https://jetpack.com/social/) from a blog to a thread. Haven't tried it yet. They also have a new [identity system](https://blog.gravatar.com/2024/06/03/profiles-as-a-service/) built around Gravatar, announced in early June. It'll be interesting to see what they come out with. I wonder if there is a developer ecosystem building on this, and if they have an evangelism program. I have my own vision of how these things should work.
