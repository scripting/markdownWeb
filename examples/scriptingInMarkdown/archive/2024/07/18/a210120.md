If the world doesn't know you did something you might as well not have done it. This is what [Doug Engelbart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_Engelbart) learned, and what we learned in his aftermath. He is known for inventing the mouse, because that's the one thing he invented everyone knows about. He also developed software that pioneered using a computer to [organize your ideas](https://web.stanford.edu/class/history34q/readings/Engelbart/Engelbart_AugmentIntellect.html). For the most part people don't know about that because (I guess) most people don't organize their work?
