![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2024/07/02/spiced.png)My favorite soft drink these days is [Coke Cherry Zero](https://preview.us.coca-cola.com/products/coca-cola-flavors/cherry-zero-sugar). It's got a really nice taste. Went to the store today, they only had one 12-pack, but they also had a 12-pack of [Coke Spiced Zero](https://www.coca-cola.com/us/en/brands/coca-cola/products/coca-cola-spiced). I decided to give it a try. I had a can at lunch and it's weird but okay I guess, but the belches are strongly flavored and that's _realllly_ weird. It took me a while to figure out where this [belch-breath](https://www.prevention.com/health/a32925734/why-do-my-burps-smell/) was coming from. It tastes like I took some very strong medicine. The weirdest soft drink I've ever had. But it kind of grows on you. I'll try another one and let you know how it goes.
