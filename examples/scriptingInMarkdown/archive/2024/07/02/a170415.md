I post stuff to [Facebook](http://scripting.com/2021/09/17/153104.html?title=facebookIsAtLeastEightThings) that could get inundated with hateful comments. I have a pretty good idea upfront which ones those are. I go ahead and post them. At the first sign of flameage I turn comments off, with a [simple explanation](https://www.facebook.com/dave.winer.12/posts/pfbid0YQnqZuksa8nEHgCsEcvyJ5krLniGoFCrRRB2UHmjsDGosFycKorpskY9KuokgQkNl?comment_id=393461023707630). "I turned off comments here. I didn't want to have a discussion about this, it's just an observation." I think this is totally legit and more people should do it. Really frees you up to say what you see.
