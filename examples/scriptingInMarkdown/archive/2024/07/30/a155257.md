I said on [Thursday](http://scripting.com/2024/07/25.html) that Biden's speech last Wednesday had the potential to be a [Gettysburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gettysburg_Address) address, if it the United States turns back toward democracy and government of the people, by the people and for the people. Biden has the potential of being as great a president as Lincoln. Let that settle in for a moment. I get goosebumps when I think about it. If he hadn't taken that stand, he could have become known as the US equivalent of [Kaiser Wilhelm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilhelm_II#Life_in_exile).
