Here's a screen shot of the new software that came out today, the blogroll feature on [Scripting News](http://scripting.com/).

It's a [post](https://www.manton.org/2024/03/13/more-updates-from.html) on [Manton's blog](https://www.manton.org/), viewed in the blogroll on my site, talking about stuff on my site.

As we used to say in the Old School Blogosphere: "Watching them watch us, watch them watching us, etc, etc."
