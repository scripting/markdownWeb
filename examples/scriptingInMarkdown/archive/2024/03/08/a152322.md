There's a problem on [news.scripting.com](https://news.scripting.com/) and the [scripting.com](http://scripting.com/) home page. On a mobile device, sometimes you have to click several times on a tab to get it to switch. It doesn't seem to happen in desktop browsers. Yesterday I finally got it to reproduce on my own machine, when emulating an iPad Mini in Chrome on my desktop. I added debugging code and set breakpoints, and had a theory that tooltips were interfering, which turned out not to be the case, but now that I can reproduce it here, I really want to get this solved. If you have development experience, or just like a good technical puzzle, [here's the thread](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/issues/287#issuecomment-1983757115). Help much appreciated.
