![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/08/17/doctorNick.png)Manton Reece gives Facebook the [benefit](https://www.manton.org/2024/03/20/threads-optin-vs.html) of the doubt. I have at times been that optimistic. And there are good well-intentioned people at every bigco. The problem is, when you get to the top, they don't actually give a F about any of this. They like to keep their users where they are. Right now Facebook is hoovering up people who are looking for something new in the Twitter space. So it helps to encourage people to believe that there will be a way out if they want to try something else. But everyone knows for real that that _isn't_ what's going to happen. This is in the tech playbook. When you're growing, you want everything to be open. When there aren't any more users to get from other places, well, that was a [nice idea](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y8Rsretl-qQ&t=32s).
