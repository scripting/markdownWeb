![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2024/03/22/wordperfectdisk.png)[Back in the 80s](https://mastodon.social/@davew/112139000153008269) before many of you were born, you could buy a word processing program, which was basically a text editor, and you could use it to write and then when it came time to send that writing to other people, you would print it. And get this the printer could take its input from any of those writing tools. There were no [tiny little text boxes](https://imgs.scripting.com/2024/03/22/tinyLittleTextBox.png). The printers didn’t come with their own editors, you had choice and therefore there was lots of competition. Amazing, right?
