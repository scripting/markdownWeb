I saw these [Sony buds](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09YL76VSR?ref=ppx_yo2ov_dt_b_product_details&th=1) advertised and I had to try them. They're now my favorite way to listen to music and podcasts. Most ear buds in my experience don't do very well with bass, and I love music with a strong beat. Sony makes great inexpensive headphones.
