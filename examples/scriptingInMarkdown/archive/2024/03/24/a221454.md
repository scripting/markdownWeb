So I went ahead and moved [xmlrpc.com](https://xmlrpc.com/) to a HTTPS server. The [other day](http://scripting.com/2024/03/22/031933.html) I forgot to mention that style sheets might not be readable when you move from HTTP to HTTPS, leading to [this striking breakage](https://imgs.scripting.com/2024/03/24/httpsBreaksSitesThanksGoogle.png) I saw when I first got to look at the site in its new location. And now thanks to Google and the EFF, I get to spend time debugging something that worked just fine in 1998 and every year since then. Should I send them the bill for my time? Fuckers.
