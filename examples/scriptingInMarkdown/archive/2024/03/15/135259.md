We've noted this before.

Established facts about Trump eventually lose their currency, reporters forget and report it as big news next time they see it happening.

Trump's [fealty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fealty) to Putin for example. How could a reporter forget that? Yet they seem to.

Two moments to bring you back.

![Picture 1.](https://imgs.scripting.com/2024/03/15/trumpBowsToPutinWhoGlowsWithPride.png)

![Picture 2.](https://imgs.scripting.com/2024/03/15/whatKindOfMonsterIsThis.png)

There are so many of these pictures.

Remember we tried letting Trump play president, we shouldn't have survived it, and in a lot of ways we didn't.

Collectively we're like the main character in [The Sixth Sense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sixth_Sense) (no spoilers, but if you have seen it you know what I mean).

So please, dear reporters and editors, try to factor actual proven facts into the context of your reporting.
