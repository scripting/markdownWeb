![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2023/04/24/cheshireCat.png)Maybe the thing I'm most [proud](https://imgs.scripting.com/2024/03/15/jayGraberInBlogroll.png) of is that the [blogroll](https://blogroll.social/) can host [Jay Graber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jay_Graber), CEO of Bluesky, because they [support](https://openrss.org/blog/bluesky-has-launched-rss-feeds) outbound [RSS](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html). I can also follow [Eugen Rochko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugen_Rochko), founder of Mastodon, for the same reason. And of course [Matt Mullenweg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Mullenweg), founder of Automattic, whose WordPress supported RSS from inception. Bluesky, Mastodon and WordPress, in the same social web. One of those [Golden Spike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_spike) moments.
