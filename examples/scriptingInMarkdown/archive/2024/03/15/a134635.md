This is a nit, but it bugs me anyway. I'd love to know why Threads, which in every way is a modern JavaScript app running in a web browser, uses [urls that begin with www](https://imgs.scripting.com/2024/03/15/exampleOfAThreadsUrl.png). In 2024. There's no harm in it, it's just there was a consensus a long time ago that the _www_ part was not necessary.
