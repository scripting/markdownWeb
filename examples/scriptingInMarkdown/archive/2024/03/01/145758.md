In a new product I'm working on, I'm including links to the blogs of the social media apps I use. I notice that most of them have one. This is why:

1.  You can put a title on a post on a blog.
    
2.  You can include links (invariably announcements have links).
    
3.  You can edit posts.
    
4.  Simple text styling.
    
5.  Blogs have feeds.
    

So I'm looking for the official blogs of the social apps I use -- Mastodon, Bluesky, Twitter, Threads.

If so, what's the feed URL?

Here's a [place](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/issues/294) to comment.

PS: It seems dumb to have two places for writing. Also I bet Bluesky wrote their own blogging software, which is why there's no feed for their blog, which is awkward because Bluesky itself has feeds. As a programmer, I don't think I could maintain both codebases, and i'm pretty sure the blog is the one that'll get abandoned. Update: they do [have a feed](https://github.com/scripting/Scripting-News/issues/294#issuecomment-1973571777) for their blog. At some point there won't be a distinction between blogging and social media-ing. Remember the [tiny little text boxes](http://scripting.com/2024/01/14/031201.html) have to go. :smile:

PPS: Twitter's blog has a [really shitty title](https://blog.x.com/en_gb/blog.rss), it shows up in the list as English (UK). That should be fixed to something like Twitter Blog. I know how this happens, obviously they're maintaining more than one language, so the title is given from their point of view. As a reader, it has to make sense in a list of feeds from different publishers. Here's a [screen shot](https://imgs.scripting.com/2024/03/01/howCouldYouTellThatsTwitter.png) to illustrate.
