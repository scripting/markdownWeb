If we want even a modest reboot of blogging, as a community thing, we're going to have to start with something like blogrolls. I wrote a bit [yesterday](http://scripting.com/2024/02/28.html#a154126) about how blogrolls came about, then weblogs.com and how that led to RSS and feed readers. This time, we don't have to wait for feeds to show up, they're already here. It should happen more quickly.
