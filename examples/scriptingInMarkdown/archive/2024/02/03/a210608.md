People are really gushing about the Apple goggles, now that they have them in their hands. I still can't get psyched about it. I was late to getting an [Apple Watch](http://scripting.com/2016/04/10/1175.html), and now I [wear it](https://imgs.scripting.com/2024/02/03/appleWatch2024.png) every day, can't imagine not wearing it. So maybe I will get one eventually. But my vision is weak, and I kind of doubt it'll work for me. And I would much prefer if Apple had poured their vast resources and creativity into making mass transport work in the Bay Area instead, as a prototype for what might be possible in the rest of the world. _That_ I would cheer, unconditionally.
