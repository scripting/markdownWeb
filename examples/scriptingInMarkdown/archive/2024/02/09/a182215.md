If you have a new trend in tech, the absolute [worst way](https://www.citationneeded.news/review-read-write-own-by-chris-dixon/) to promote it is as a replacement for existing layers of tech with huge [installed bases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Installed_base#:~:text=Installed%20base%20of%20a%20product,a%20particular%20product%20or%20technology.). You can't win that kind of comparison. Installed bases generally don't disappear, and if they do, it happens very slowly. You almost certainly don't have the replacement, but you might have a useful product that would appeal to people, so be smart and explain it that way, if you just happen to [boil the ocean](https://chat.openai.com/share/2ba3a8fb-8951-4c23-b955-b32199f3ebe6) with it, you can thank your luck because that's what actually happened.
