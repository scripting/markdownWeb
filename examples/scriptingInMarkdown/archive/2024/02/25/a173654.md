There's a [Beatles song](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bJVWZy4QOy0), by John Lennon, that was written by cribbing promotional items from a 19th-century [circus poster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Being_for_the_Benefit_of_Mr._Kite!#Background). It's kind of like asking ChatGPT to give you some random phrases and presenting that as your creativity. I think I'm going to [try that now](https://chat.openai.com/share/4aafde35-2903-48e3-88c0-bcd8725ac702). I asked ChatGPT to write a poem using ideas from a random song written by John Lennon. It chose Imagine. I asked that it write a poem using the ideas in Day Tripper. And finally I asked it to write a song based on the story of The Matrix.
