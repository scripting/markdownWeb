"When your house is on fire there aren’t two sides." A few people misunderstood. In this [analogy](http://scripting.com/2024/10/24.html#a204037) there are no arsonists. There is the house and there is fire. If you were reporting on this situation, you don't need to find out what motivates the fire, the only important thing is that if not checked it will destroy the house.
