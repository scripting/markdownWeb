![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2022/09/21/mets.png)I'm really proud of [what John Gruber said](https://daringfireball.net/linked/2024/10/07/scripting-news-30-years) about me as a blogger in his Daring Fireball [yesterday](https://daringfireball.net/linked/2024/10/07/scripting-news-30-years): "Winer is rightfully renowned for his technical achievements — outliners as an application genre, RSS in general, and RSS in the specific context of podcasting in particular — but what’s kept me reading Scripting News for the entirety of Scripting News’s 30-years-and-counting run is his writing. He has such a distinctive writing voice that is impossible to imagine in any medium other than the web. But I think that’s because he helped define what writing not just on the web, but for the web, even meant."
