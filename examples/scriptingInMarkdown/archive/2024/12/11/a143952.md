Just added to my todo list -- add the option to use the [WordPress REST interface](https://github.com/WP-API/node-wpapi) in place of the [WPCOM interface](https://github.com/Automattic/wpcom.js), this will give [WordLand](https://wordland.social/) the ability to edit WordPress sites anywhere, not just on wordpress.com. When I made the choice to go with WPCOM I didn't have ChatGPT to look at the other options, I was surprised to find that WordPress actually had a good JavaScript API. It doesn't look like the conversion will be too bad. It's obviously better to be able to work with all WordPress sites.
