I frequently have trouble remembering the names of things I want to use in my writing. I've come to rely on ChatGPT for help. So I [wrote](https://chatgpt.com/share/674f36d9-9990-8012-8971-a2019ac8808b) to ChatGPT: "I'm trying to remember the name of the stone that has the key to understanding a language." To which it replied: "You're likely thinking of the [Rosetta Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosetta_Stone). It was crucial in deciphering Egyptian hieroglyphs because it featured the same text written in three scripts: Greek, Demotic, and hieroglyphic. The Greek text provided the key to understanding the other two scripts."
