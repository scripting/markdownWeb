BTW, as a longtime speaker of English _and_ programmer, I think the term [refactoring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Code_refactoring) is re-dundant. The term comes from mathematics where you simplify a statement without altering its truth. Factoring is a repetitive process. You factor, and then factor some more if you can.
