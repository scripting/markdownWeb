I have lived on the [San Andreas](https://www.woodsidetown.org/189/Geotechnical-Reports-Soils-Reports) and [Hayward](https://earthquakes.berkeley.edu/hayward/hayward_tours.html) faults, was in the [Valley](https://www.google.com/maps/@37.4424535,-122.1906001,3a,75y,195.92h,90.44t/data=!3m6!1e1!3m4!1sG9O9pttfboV1BMp2aQGbPw!2e0!7i16384!8i8192?entry=ttu) for the [Loma Prieta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1989_Loma_Prieta_earthquake) quake -- and I have to say this was a real earthquake. It wasn't very long, but it had a real jolt. Scared the shit out of me. I'm still very sensitive to this kind of disaster.
