I hate paywalls tied to subscription. I’m never going to subscribe to a Philadelphia news org, but based on [Jay’s recommendation](https://bsky.app/profile/jayrosen.bsky.social/post/3kqnxdwej3s2c) I might pay $1 on my [EZ Pass for News](http://scripting.com/2022/10/04/130438.html?title=howEzpassForNewsWouldWork) to read [this story](https://www.inquirer.com/opinion/commentary/civil-war-movie-about-journalism-20240421.html), esp since I saw the [Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_War_\(film\)) movie. I just had a thought, I might subscribe to a Philadelphia news org for a week or two, given that the Knicks are playing their NBA team right now in the first round of the playoffs. I really want to know everything there is to know about this faceoff. See, I want to pay for journalism here, but journalism hasn't been willing to sell it to me, at any price. They've never gotten the basic truth of: "The customer is always right." Really important point and true in every way.
