Question: What's between a [tiny little text box](https://imgs.scripting.com/2024/04/14/tinylittletextboxexample.png) and a full-blown content management system?

Answer: A full-featured text editor with a social media feel to it without the limits of twitter-like systems.

That's what [textcasting](https://textcasting.org/) is for, to identity the essential features. This editor supports them.

100% built on WordPress. Why reinvent all the good stuff that's been debugged and scaled and has all that support in the world. As I like to say, one way of doing something is better than two, no matter how much better the second way is.

PS: A [seven-minute podcast](http://scripting.com/2024/04/13/biggerThanATinyLittleTextbox.m4a) that explains. :smile:

PPS: And don't forget about [the blogroll](https://blogroll.social). It's part of the puzzle too.
