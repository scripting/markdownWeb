![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2021/02/11/frescaBlackberryCitrus.png)Back in the old days, during a great sport event, we'd post our feelings, pro or con, to Twitter. I [observed](https://twitter.com/davewiner/status/1782032706903195993) as follows: "Let's do something great with our lives! In the meantime I miss the role that twitter used to play and never will play again. It was the place to go to say 'How about those Knicks!' when they win a game like the one they won last night. Not no mo." [Betsy Devine](http://scripting.com/2005/01/27.html#When:9:35:10AM) was the first to like this. I felt heard.
