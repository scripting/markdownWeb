Quick [video demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=46eVHcVYPFY) of [meta.ai](https://meta.ai/). This is a demo of just one feature, its ability to recalc drawings as you edit the prompt that defines the picture. As you can tell from the demo I love it because it's new, creative, super fun to use and to watch the result. And lovely to see this much progress so quickly. You really should watch it [on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=46eVHcVYPFY) so you can see how what I type relates to the image in real-time. It's like subtitles inverted, with a very knowledgeable, creative and high bandwidth computer network behind it. Living in the future.
