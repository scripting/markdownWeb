Then I asked where he went to high school and it said Bronx Science. But by then I was pretty sure he didn't, so I went to Google and meta.ai, and neither knew where he went to high school. So I asked on Twitter, Mastodon, [Blue Sky](https://mastodon.social/@davew/112321321415008740) and Threads. Not sure why I even care! Oh well. :smile:
