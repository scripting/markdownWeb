![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2021/06/14/strongman.png)BTW, there's a lot of talk about new ways to communicate that don't depend on silos that could be sold to billionaires. One way to do it is with my product [FeedLand](https://docs.feedland.com/gettingstarted.md). It manages news streams for feeds you subscribe to. I think every publication should share news from feeds they depend on, experts they quote, other news sources they read. We can build our own networks this way. The advantage of this approach is that it is truly decentralized and not at all complicated to use. Here's the [news stream](https://news.scripting.com/) I provide for readers of my blog. It's the most popular feature on the site. With FeedLand you can [create your own](https://docs.feedland.com/newsproducts.md).
