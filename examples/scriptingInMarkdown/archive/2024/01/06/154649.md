_Caveat: This very likely already exists._

Random idea for my friends at Automattic. How about a theme for a WordPress site that's good for technical subjects.

It should have good default styles for code. Handle screen shot images gracefully. Basically a way of using WordPress for documenting the kinds of things we write up on GitHub because their editor is designed to work well for technical stuff.

Just thinking out loud. A good test case would be the [RSS 2.0 spec](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html), which doesn't look too great after years of no-updates (more than 20 years) and browser slippage?

It started life as a Manila site, transitioned to WordPress, and now I believe it's a static site. At one time it looked good in WordPress. It could totally use the kind of treatment [Doc's blog](https://doc.searls.com/) got, but obviously it's serving a different purpose.
