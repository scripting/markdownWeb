I got this idea [from Philip Bump](https://bsky.app/profile/pbump.com/post/3kj7q6wejuk23). :smile:

"Draw a three-panel comic strip in the style of Mary Worth. Panel one should show Bull Mancuso, a waste management consultant in New Jersey, at a Starbuck's in Newark, ordering a huge coffee drink. In panel two he's teaching a class on organized crime in New Jersey at [Staten Island Community College](https://www.csi.cuny.edu/). In panel three he slips on a banana peel on the steps of the Capitol and falls down. There is no dialogue on any panel."
