![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2024/01/18/visionPro.png)I've been wrong about many of Apple's products, but not the ones that made the company -- the Apple II and Mac, though I was somewhat skeptical of the iPhone because I couldn't write software for it, but I did [get one](http://scripting.com/stories/2007/06/30/initialReviewOfIphone.html) on the day they came out in [June 2007](http://scripting.com/2007/06.html), and never used my Blackberry again. Anyway, the [goggles](https://techcrunch.com/2024/01/17/i-spent-the-morning-with-the-apple-vision-pro/) they just [started](https://www.techmeme.com/240117/p38#a240117p38) demoing to selected reporters and analysts looks like a product they released because they invested billions in it, had no idea what it's used for, and were overlooking the ability of the human body to actually _use_ such a thing, and couldn't consider writing it off because so much had been made of this, esp since the current management has been coasting on innovation done by [Steve Jobs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Jobs), and hadn't released anything that wasn't completely predictable since his [passing in 2011](http://scripting.com/stories/2011/10/06/myThoughtsOnSteveJobs.html). Their lack of confidence in their own product overwhelms any positive reviews coming out from the privileged press, who we know in advance to discount, these are the press people Apple can count on to not say a negative word, to preserve their access at least, if not because they are complete fans. It reeks of a loser product. I write these things partially so I can be proven ridiculously wrong when I get one myself in two weeks and can't believe all the things I can do with it.
