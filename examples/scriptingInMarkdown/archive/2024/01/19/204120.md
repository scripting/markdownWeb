Draw a nine-panel comic strip. Panels one through three should show [Bull Mancuso](https://www.google.com/search?q=%22Bull+Mancuso%22), a [fishery management consultant](https://chat.openai.com/share/0d0ec2ca-2846-4088-90ed-d5ee1bba5970) in Oregon at a [Peet's Coffee](https://www.peets.com/pages/store-locator?stockist-query=Portland,%20OR,%20USA) in Portland, ordering and enjoying a huge [Chai ice tea](https://www.peets.com/products/iced-chai-latte) drink. In panels four through seven she's teaching a class on [abortion rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abortion_in_the_United_States) in [Portland State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portland_State_University). In panels eight and nine she does a _hilarious_ [faceplant](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bm9bbT8CIG8) while skiing at [Mt Hood Ski Bowl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Hood_Skibowl). There is no dialogue on any panel.
