[Johannes Ernst](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jernst/), host of [FediForum](https://fediforum.org/), proposes a [session](https://www.threads.net/@johannes.ernst/post/C2fqDkVPoMx) that looks at how a product like [Ghost](https://twitter.com/Ghost) might support [ActivityPub](https://www.google.com/search?q=site%3Ascripting.com+ActivityPub). A nicely pragmatic way to look at it. I wrote a thread in response [saying](https://www.threads.net/@davew/post/C2hpsEysa7L) what's called for is a Busy Developer's Guide To ActivityPub, with a matching open source client and server to test against.
