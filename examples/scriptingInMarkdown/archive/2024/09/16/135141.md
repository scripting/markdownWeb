I have a linkblogging tool crossposts to Bluesky, Mastodon, Threads, Twitter, WordPress and RSS.

Every time I publish a link it goes to all those places,

[The RSS feed](http://data.feedland.org/feeds/davewiner.xml) is doing a lot of the work.

*   Links from the RSS feed show up on my [Links page](http://scripting.com/?tab=links) and in the [nightly emails](http://scripting.com/email/).
    
*   The RSS feed is also used to connect to Threads, going through micro.blog. I am able to [give](https://micro.blog/account/feeds) it the [URL](http://data.feedland.org/feeds/davewiner.xml) of the linkblogging feed, and then click on [Add Threads](https://imgs.scripting.com/2024/09/16/addthreads.png) -- and that's it. When I post something to the feed it shows up on Threads.
    

This feature is tucked away in a corner of micro.blog and I suspect most people don't know it's there. It's sort of a Grand Central station for moving stuff around among the twitter-like systems. If you're a micro.blog user, its [Feeds page](https://micro.blog/account/feeds) is where you set it up. [Screen shot](https://imgs.scripting.com/2024/09/16/microblogScreen.png).
