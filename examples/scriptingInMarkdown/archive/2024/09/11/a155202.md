![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2020/07/16/parrot.png)The most encouraging thing about last night's debate was that the moderators were journalists. When Trump repeated his most egregious lie about murdering babies being legal in certain states, moderator Muir confidently, almost derisively, said that there is no such thing. And when Trump said he didn't admit he lost the election, and then when challenged said in a snotty way he was being sarcastic, Muir plainly that wasn't true either. It was like the Monty Python [dead parrot](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4vuW6tQ0218) [sketch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dead_Parrot_sketch). ABC News was the last place you'd expect real journalism to surface, maybe the NYT et al will follow their lead.
