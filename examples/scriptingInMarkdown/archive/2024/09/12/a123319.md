![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2024/07/16/succession.png)Someday an election will be like Game of Thrones or Succession. A campaign would be a season. There would be character development, arcs, twists, revelations, unforseen events, [cameos](https://www.cnn.com/2024/09/11/politics/taylor-swift-kamala-harris-endorsement/index.html), acts of god. Near the end of the season you have the election, with the following episodes the plotting, intrigue, and undermining of democracy, and at the end of the season the inauguration of a new president. Each year you have more michegas, until four years later you go through the election all over again. People would really study the candidates, and would have reasons they like one character over another. And maybe the [patriarch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dYqqW3c2mBU) doesn't die, and decides to run again. This is where we're heading, we might already be there.
