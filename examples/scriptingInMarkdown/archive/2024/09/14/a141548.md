BTW the [Wikipedia page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MetaWeblog) for the MetaWeblog positions it as a replacement for the Blogger API, but it's an extension of it. You could use MetaWeblog to publish to Blogger sites, but it also supported features that Blogger didn't have, that were in our blogging software, Manila.
