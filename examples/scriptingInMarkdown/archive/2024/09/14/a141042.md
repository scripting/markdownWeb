I'd like to ask for a picture of [Chuck Berry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chuck_Berry), and get something nice and be able to put him in a scene with the [Wordle Kitty](https://www.google.com/search?q=site%3Ascripting.com+%22wordle+kitty%22). That seems pretty harmless. And the news industry could hardly object, they didn't invent Chuck Berry, or own the copyright of the picture of him in Wikipedia.
