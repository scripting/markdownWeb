[September 2004](http://scripting.com/2004/09.html) was the month when podcasting became a real community thing. [Twenty years ago](http://scripting.com/2024/06/17/152838.html). I did eight podcasts that month. The mail list, which Adam started, was going strong. This is the month where [podcasting got its name](http://scripting.com/2013/04/07/howPodcastingGotItsName.html), thanks to some brilliant creativity from Dannie Gregoire. Until then we were calling them "audio blog posts" or some variant thereof. You can see it in this [Google Trends](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?date=all&q=podcast) graph. I started a [special feed](http://scripting.com/podcast0/rss.xml) to echo my programs from 2004, I even got it registered in [Apple's podcast](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/dws-podcast0-feed/id1752527298) database. There will be two podcasts in the feed today because I missed the one from yesterday. _Still diggin!_
