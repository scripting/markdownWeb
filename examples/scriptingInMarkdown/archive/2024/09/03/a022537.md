Today, in 2024, AI cannot [create art](https://kottke.org/24/09/can-ai-make-art). But a human being [can](http://scripting.com/2024/08/28/190028.html) [use](http://scripting.com/2024/09/01/165153.html) AI [to](http://scripting.com/2024/08/28/190028.html) [create](http://scripting.com/2024/08/20/142602.html) art. It's a medium, like paint and canvas except it's not static. It gets new skills all the time. It gives me the ability to create in a way I've never been able to before. I can't wait to see what it can do in a few months or years.
