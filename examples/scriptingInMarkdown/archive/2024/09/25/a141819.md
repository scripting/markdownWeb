They have two more games to play with the Braves, but there's a [hurricane](https://imgs.scripting.com/2024/09/25/atlantaWeather.png) headed [toward](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?lat=33.7485&lon=-84.3915) Atlanta. Do the Mets stay in Atlanta to ride out the storm, or head back to NY? It's far more weird than I have time to explain right now.
