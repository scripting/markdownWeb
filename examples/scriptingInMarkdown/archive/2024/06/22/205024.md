[Jon Stewart said](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CuDcTyCq8nc&t=560s) something interesting — and imho correct, that news is about the past.

When they cover things that haven’t happened, that’s not news.

He said news should be like [jury duty](https://www.google.com/search?q=site%3Ascripting.com+%22jury+duty%22). Rigorous and decisive.

That makes total sense, and gives a pretty good idea of how far off course we are.
