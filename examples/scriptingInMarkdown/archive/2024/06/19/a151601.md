Technical note. I made a [mistake](https://imgs.scripting.com/2024/06/19/datesoff.png) in converting local time to GMT in the code that builds the feed. My times were off by four hours. So I corrected them. And now instead of three items in the feed, according to my own feed software, there are now six. I bet all the [other](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/dws-podcast0-feed/id1752527298) systems will do the same thing because the guid is a function of the creation date of the item, and they changed. Oh well. At least the [RSS version](http://scripting.com/podcast0/rss.xml) only has three items. I think I should change how the guid works.
