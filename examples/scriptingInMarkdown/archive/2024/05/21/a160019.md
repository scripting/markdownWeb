I found a fairly painless way to transcribe voicemails using Google Docs. 1. Open a text document. 2. Choose [Voice Typing](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/4492226?hl=en) from the Tools menu. 3. Play the voice memo over the speaker of your iPhone. 4. That's it. Google transcribes it into your document. It would be better if Apple offered that as an option in the Share menu, but they don't.
