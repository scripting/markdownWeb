If you want a new perspective on the election, two recommendations.

*   Greg Sargent interviewed political consultant [Joe Trippi](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/dont-be-fooled-heres-how-we-know-rfk-jr-is-tacitly/id1728152109?i=1000653431136), who explains why third parties could make all the difference in the election.
    
*   Chris Lydon interviewed [Richard Slotkin](https://radioopensource.org/american-disorder/) about the four major stories of American politics.
    

Both very illuminating and immediately influenced my thinking.
