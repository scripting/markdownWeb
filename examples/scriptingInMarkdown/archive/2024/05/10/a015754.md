Pretty sure I understand why Jack Dorsey is [disappointed](https://www.piratewires.com/p/interview-with-jack-dorsey-mike-solana?data-crypto-dot-news) with Bluesky. The mistake they made at Twitter was taking responsibility for enforcing decorum, which is completely diseconomic. A fully decentralized system is very different. That was why he funded Bluesky, to make a social web that wouldn't have at its center a company responsible for content.
