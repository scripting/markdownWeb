![](https://imgs.scripting.com/2019/07/02/knicks.png)Since I write so [much](https://www.google.com/search?q=site%3Ascripting.com+knicks) about the Knicks here, I need to tell you that [the Knicks won](https://www.nba.com/news/knicks-sixers-takeaways-game-6-2024-nba-playoffs) their first round playoff series against the [Philadelphia team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philadelphia_76ers) last night. Almost all playoff series that aren't sweeps are intense, but this one was especially so. So we're on to the [next round](https://fox59.com/sports/pacers/schedule-released-for-pacers-knicks-playoff-series/), starting Monday, back in NYC, against the Indiana Pacers, an excellent team this year. And Doc Searls, who is also a Knicks fan, now lives in Indiana, so he is somewhat justified in believing the world revolves around him. I've always had that sense about Doc.
