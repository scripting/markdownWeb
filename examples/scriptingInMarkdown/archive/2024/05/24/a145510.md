There's this great [scene](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7mzY1sdiFSU&t=53s) in MoneyBall where [Billy Beane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billy_Beane) is talking to [David Justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Justice) in the batting cage. Justice reminds Beane how much he's paying him, and Beane says I'm going to tell it to you straight -- half your salary is being paid by the Yankees. Think about it. They're paying all that money for you to play _against_ them. That's how I feel about Fox. How much will it cost to get them to STFU. Whatever it is it's worth it.
