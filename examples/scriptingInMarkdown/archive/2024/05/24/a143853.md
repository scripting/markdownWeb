If you're part of the indieweb community and work in Node.js, you should know about the [reallysimple package](https://www.npmjs.com/package/reallysimple). It flattens the interface for the most common feed formats. It's designed to be super easy to use. Give it a URL and it returns a consistent JSON structure. More people would develop feed-using apps if they knew about this package. I just want more people to develop around feeds.
