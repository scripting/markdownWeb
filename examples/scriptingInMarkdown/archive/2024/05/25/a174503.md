As you may know, over the years I extend [RSS](https://cyber.harvard.edu/rss/rss.html) by adding elements to the [source namespace](http://source.scripting.com/). The latest is [source:self](http://source.scripting.com/#1716308936000) whose value is the canonical URL for the feed. This was a useful addition in Atom, which I [supported](https://github.com/scripting/reallysimple/blob/main/worknotes.md#61522-by-dw----v049) in [reallySimple](https://github.com/scripting/reallysimple/blob/main/README.md) in 2022, and I wanted to include the value without having to add another namespace to [my feed](http://scripting.com/rss.xml) and feeds produced by my products, so reallySimple now supports both. Small move forward for [interop](https://www.google.com/search?q=site%3Ascripting.com+interop).
